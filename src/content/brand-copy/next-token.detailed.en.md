Next Token | 词元之外 is a video podcast about AI technology, products, entrepreneurship, and creativity. It brings first-hand experience and perspectives from the Chinese-speaking world into a wider conversation about what AI is changing and what deserves attention next.

A “next token” is a generative model’s next prediction. The Chinese name, 词元之外, points to the people, industries, products, society, and culture beyond that prediction. The program connects technical developments with hands-on use and real-world practice, while making room for different experiences and judgments.

Its flagship show, Next Token Weekly, brings four co-hosts together for a weekly roundtable on consequential AI news, sharing observations, hands-on experience, and different judgments through video, audio, and show notes. Explore the program at nexttoken.tv.
