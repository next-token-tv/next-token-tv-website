Genesis Academy is a space for learning and collaboration on floors 9 and 10 of Yuandian Building, in Wudaokou’s AI Yuandian Community. It brings students, founders, scientists, company leaders, and investors together around three activities: Study, Social, and Sale.

The academy provides hands-on training for university students, connects companies and founders with resources, and supports product launches and early-stage projects.
