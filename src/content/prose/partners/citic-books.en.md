Beijing Chaoyue, CITIC Books’ first flagship store, is located on the ninth floor of Chaoyang Joy City in Beijing. Spanning nearly 4,000 square metres, it brings together more than 40,000 titles and 100,000 books, alongside reading, family activities, exhibitions, art, dining and lifestyle offerings.

Envisioned as an “ideal living community in the sky,” this cultural space welcomes the city to read and spend time together. Beijing Chaoyue provides the recording venue for Next Token Weekly.
