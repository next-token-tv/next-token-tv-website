Next Token Weekly is the flagship weekly show from Next Token | 词元之外. Its four co-hosts meet around the past week’s consequential AI news, spanning technology, products, entrepreneurship, creativity, and real-world impact.

The show does not try to read every headline. It starts with hands-on use, product practice, and industry observation to compare what actually changed, what was only noise, and which signals deserve to be followed.

There is no permanent host and no assigned point of view. A rotating duty host threads each episode together while disagreements stay on the table; video, audio, and show notes preserve the conversation in complementary forms.
