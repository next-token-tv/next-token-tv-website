## Positioning

An AI video creator known publicly as Foyege, with the handles X and Instagram @Foyege24 and the Bilibili account name 弗曳哥 (Fu Ye Ge), focusing on Harry Potter fan videos made with AI tools.

## Career and representative work

Foyege uses @Foyege24 on both X and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/foyege24/), where the profile bio reads "AI is best！"; the Bilibili account goes by 弗曳哥. According to the fan-run [Malfoid archive](https://malfoid.me/zh/creators/foyege) that catalogs the work, the creator describes themselves as an AIGC technology blogger, a VSC Creator, founder of INFINITY, and an exclusive director signed with Libtv.

The representative work is the Harry Potter fan series In Another Universe (《在另一个宇宙》), released as numbered parts and built around 马尔芙 (Draconia Malfoy), a female version of Draco Malfoy, in a love story with the boy who lived, with English audio. The series, standalone fan videos, and illustrations are archived on the Malfoid site; series episodes are also published on YouTube and other platforms under the "FOYEGE ORIGINAL" prefix.

## Mention in the show

Foyege does not appear in episode 008. In that episode's chapter "AI 短剧：个人与小团队的创作机会" ([AI short dramas: creative opportunities for individuals and small teams](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-04)), Guizang tells the story of the viral gender-swapped Malfoy series: a Russian artist first drew a gender-swapped Malfoy image, "然后那个就叫，他们叫 Foyege 还是，我忘了那个拼音那个 Foyege……是一个创作者，中国创作者" ([it was called — they called it Foyege, I forgot the spelling — a creator, a Chinese creator](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-08c0ca7b752c9d251e57)), who then "他自己捏了一个，用 AI 捏了一个性转马尔福的脸" (crafted a gender-swapped Malfoy face with AI). Guizang says Foyege turned Harry Potter into a teen campus drama in the vein of Twilight, that "现在是三集还是四集，他每一集在 Instagram 上点赞有一百多万" ([it is three or four episodes now, each with over a million likes on Instagram](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-9c24cf2f57ad9ddece00)) and that [exposure on X should be over a million per episode](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-85493dd4f493a5ef64ea). These distribution figures and episode counts are Guizang's retelling in the episode and have not been independently verified for this entry; the hosts also use the case to argue that individual creators still need taste. Yang Pan compares the AI-generated face to Android 18.
