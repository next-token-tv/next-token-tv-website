He created CodePilot, a desktop client for AI agents, and open-source Skills for tasks such as making presentations. Through “歸藏的AI工具箱” and AIGC Weekly, he shares tools and working methods. His approach to a one-person business emphasizes the role of design judgment, context, and workflows in the quality of the finished work.
