## Positioning

Co-founder of the cryptocurrency exchange Binance, commonly known as CZ, who served as Binance's chief executive officer for years.

## Career and representative work

Changpeng Zhao was born in 1977 in Jiangsu, China, moved to Canada with his family, and studied computer science at McGill University. According to the English Wikipedia article, he started his career developing trade-matching software for a subcontractor of the Tokyo Stock Exchange, then spent four years building futures trading software at Bloomberg Tradebook; in 2005 he moved to Shanghai and founded the technology company Fusion Systems. He entered the cryptocurrency industry around 2013, joined the early team at Blockchain.info, and served as chief technology officer of OKCoin for under a year.

In July 2017 Binance launched, raising about 15 million US dollars in an initial coin offering; per the English Wikipedia article, within eight months of launch, by April 2018, Binance had become the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange by trading volume. In 2022, Zhao invested 500 million US dollars through Binance in Elon Musk's acquisition of Twitter.

In November 2023, following US Department of Justice allegations that he violated the Bank Secrecy Act and other laws, he resigned as Binance CEO and pleaded guilty; in April 2024 he was sentenced to four months in prison and completed the sentence in September of the same year. In October 2025, US President Donald Trump pardoned Zhao. These dates follow the English Wikipedia article.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Changpeng Zhao?

Changpeng Zhao is the co-founder of the cryptocurrency exchange Binance, commonly referred to by his initials CZ; see [the founder's post on the Binance blog](https://www.binance.com/en/blog/from-our-ceo/421499824684901276).

### Why did CZ step down as Binance CEO in 2023?

Per the English Wikipedia article, in November 2023 the US Department of Justice alleged violations of the Bank Secrecy Act and other laws; he resigned as CEO and pleaded guilty, was sentenced to four months in prison in April 2024, completed the sentence in September 2024, and was pardoned by the US President in October 2025.

### What is the Binance incident discussed in episode 008?

In that episode the hosts discuss the reported 6.3-billion-dollar theft from Binance, retell how CZ progressively updated the amount on X, and connect the incident to compromised employee computers, personal security, and supply-chain risk; these details are the participants' retelling — see "Mention in the show" above.
