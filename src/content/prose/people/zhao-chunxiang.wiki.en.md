## Zhao Chunxiang

Zhao Chunxiang is an independent creator who develops AI products and writes. His personal site, [chunxiang.ai](https://chunxiang.ai/), describes him as "a programmer, writer, and AI practitioner." He is the founder of [Laper](https://laper.ai/), an AI screenwriting tool; in Laper's founder letter he describes himself as a former unsuccessful screenwriter who later became a developer.

## Background and notable work

His signature product is [Laper](https://laper.ai/), an AI writing system for screenwriters and novelists: automatic screenplay formatting, script-structure visualization, real-time collaboration built on Loro CRDT, AI-generated storyboards and character portraits, and an "AI Script Doctor" that diagnoses structure, pacing, and character issues. Laper is positioned as not replacing the author — the site says it "does not write the story for you" — and its stated aim is to do the pre-production work for every film that might one day be made; the site footer notes it is "100% Built by Claude Code".

His site chunxiang.ai also lists Bellybook, an AI food journal; Vibe Coding (chunxiang.space), a Chinese full-stack course that starts from zero; and several essays and short fiction. Awards listed on the site for 2025 include GeekPark IF 2025 Developer of the Year, a ByteDance Jimeng AI UI/UX Design Gold Award, and a Bronze Award in the Xiaohongshu Independent Developer Competition.
