## Positioning

A Chinese science-fiction writer whose works include The Three-Body Problem; he has also worked as a computer engineer. His name appears as Cixin Liu in English translations.

## Career and representative work

Per the English Wikipedia article, Liu Cixin was born on June 23, 1963, in Beijing, grew up in Yangquan, Shanxi, and graduated from the North China University of Water Resources and Electric Power; his occupations are science-fiction writer and computer engineer. He is a nine-time winner of China's Galaxy Award, won the 2015 Hugo Award for Best Novel for the English translation of The Three-Body Problem, and received the 2017 Locus Award for Death's End; he is also a winner of the Chinese Nebula Award.

His representative work is the Remembrance of Earth's Past series, in which The Three-Body Problem depicts humanity's contact with the Trisolaran civilization, followed by The Dark Forest and Death's End; the "mental seal" (思想钢印) mentioned in the episode is a concept from this series.

## Mention in the show

Liu Cixin does not appear in episode 008. In that episode's chapter "Agent 会怎样影响人的判断？" ([how agents affect human judgment](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-07)), Yang Pan says he recently saw a news item: "说这个说刘慈欣又领先 AI 多少年，说这个有一个模型的权重参数，就只在那个所有权重参数里改某一个位置的值，某一个点的值，改完以后，然后那个模型就变邪恶了" ([the headline was about how far ahead of AI Liu Cixin is again — researchers changed the value at a single position among all of a model's weight parameters, and the model turned evil](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-4808fbcf13e10aa9c819)). Orange adds "就是 260 亿参数改三个" ([three parameters out of 26 billion](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-f58215d53b1458fc7c30)) and relays the question of how those three were found; Yang Pan continues, "然后说那个思想钢印也说刘慈欣领先" ([the mental seal also shows Liu Cixin ahead](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-45ccc01524ff97a0ad95)), referring to the Three-Body setting of implanting beliefs directly into human brains. These remarks are the participants' retelling of a news report; the details of the underlying model experiment have not been independently verified for this entry. Elsewhere in the chapter the hosts cite works such as Pantheon and Cyberpunk 2077 as analogies; those are the hosts' own remarks.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Liu Cixin?

A Chinese science-fiction writer whose works include The Three-Body Problem; per the English Wikipedia article he won the 2015 Hugo Award for its English translation and previously worked as a computer engineer.

### Which books make up the Three-Body series?

The Remembrance of Earth's Past trilogy consists of The Three-Body Problem, The Dark Forest, and Death's End.

### What is the "mental seal" mentioned on the show?

The mental seal (思想钢印) is a concept from the Three-Body novels in which technology implants a specific belief directly into a human brain. In episode 008, Yang Pan brings it up while discussing the report about a model turning evil after a few weight values were changed, calling Liu Cixin ahead of AI again; it is the participants' analogy.
