## Khazix (数字生命卡兹克)

Khazix (Chinese: 数字生命卡兹克, GitHub account KKKKhazix) is a Chinese AI content creator. He runs the WeChat official account "数字生命卡兹克" and created [AIHOT](/en/wiki/products/aihot), a website that aggregates AI industry news.

## Background and notable work

- AIHOT: according to [the site's about page](https://aihot.news/about), AIHOT watches a large set of AI sources, scraping, merging, scoring and curating their output into a daily AI digest every morning, free and without registration; it also offers RSS, API and MCP subscriptions. The about page identifies the site's operator as 数字生命卡兹克 and marks the project as running "since 2026".
- Open-source AIHOT engine: [the GitHub repository KKKKhazix/AIHOT](https://github.com/KKKKhazix/AIHOT) publishes the site's engine and framework under the MIT license. The README says in the first person that "AIHOT 是我做的一个 AI 热点网站" (AIHOT is an AI news site I built) and describes the author as a designer by training rather than a professional developer, with the code rewritten together with AI.
- Open-source AI skills: his GitHub account also hosts [khazix-skills](https://github.com/KKKKhazix/khazix-skills), a collection of AI skills described as "数字生命卡兹克开源的 AI Skills 合集" (including khazix-writer and others), and [human-writing](https://github.com/KKKKhazix/human-writing), a general writing-and-revision skill described as making AI-written Chinese read like a specific person.

Details of this entry are based on the site, its about page, and the GitHub repositories' own descriptions; no sufficiently sourced account of his personal career is available, so none is given.

## Mentioned in the show

Khazix does not appear in the episodes quoted on this page; the following are the hosts' remarks. In the [RSS, independent blogs and open content](/weekly/005/transcript#chapter-06) chapter of Weekly #005 ([Chinese transcript](/weekly/005/transcript#chapter-06)):

- Yang Pan, listing the sources each host maintains, says "卡兹克不都对外发布嘛" (doesn't Kazike publish his sources openly?), and adds "你看卡兹克不是发布个 AIHOT.news 吗" (you see, Kazike released AIHOT.news), saying he himself plans to publish his curated AI sources as RSS (see [that line](/weekly/005/transcript#quote-090a602af9cf1d5a15bd)).
- Xiangyang Qiaomu says that when he built his RSS reader he had to provide feeds "提供卡兹克等等这些朋友的这个 RSS" (from Kazike and these friends) for subscription (see [that line](/weekly/005/transcript#quote-871c3aac328f2c52603e)); he also asked an agent to find and summarize the style of a Kazike article, but it "找不着" (could not be found), and Yang Pan adds that Kazike's content exists "只在公众号上" (only on WeChat official accounts) (see [that line](/weekly/005/transcript#quote-e43a23c7777c172ec79d)).

The discussion is about the lack of RSS in Chinese content and official accounts being closed to agents: the hosts see Kazike as one of the few Chinese AI creators publishing sources and RSS openly, while his articles mainly live inside WeChat official accounts, where agents cannot reach them.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Khazix (数字生命卡兹克)?

A Chinese AI content creator who runs the WeChat official account "数字生命卡兹克" and built the AI news aggregation site AIHOT. See [the AIHOT about page](https://aihot.news/about).

### What is AIHOT?

AIHOT (AIHOT.news) is an AI news site built by Khazix. It scrapes, merges and curates a large set of AI sources and publishes a daily AI digest each morning, with RSS, API and MCP subscriptions available. See [aihot.news](https://aihot.news/).

### What has Khazix open-sourced?

On GitHub ([KKKKhazix](https://github.com/KKKKhazix)), he has published the AIHOT site engine and framework, plus skills repositories such as khazix-skills and human-writing.
