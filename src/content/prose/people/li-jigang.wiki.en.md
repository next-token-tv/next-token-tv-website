## Li Jigang

Li Jigang is an independent creator known in the Chinese AI community for prompt (Prompt) writing. His Lisp-style prompts, including Hanyu Xinjie (《汉语新解》, New Explanations of Chinese Words) and Roundtable Discussion, spread widely from around 2024, published through GitHub and the WeChat public account "Write Prompt." He is not to be confused with other people sharing the name, such as university teachers.

## Career and notable work

The following draws on his own published material and verifiable third-party coverage:

- **Lisp-style prompts**: His [GitHub Gists](https://gist.github.com/lijigang) collect a series of Lisp-style prompts organized around a "sword name / sword intent" structure, such as Roundtable Discussion Prompt (a truth-seeking structured dialogue), Business Structure (analyzing companies), and Evolutionary Notes; his Emacs configuration code there dates from 2019 to 2021.
- **Hanyu Xinjie**: A Claude prompt released in 2024 that makes the model give a sharp reinterpretation of a Chinese word and render it as an SVG card; it was reposted and remixed extensively across the Chinese AI community. The prompt is signed "作者: 李继刚" (author: Li Jigang), and his [GitHub repository write-prompt](https://github.com/lijigang/write-prompt) ("prompts published by the Write Prompt public account, archived here") includes it. Third-party write-ups on Tencent Cloud's developer community and elsewhere have collected and discussed the series, reporting that his prompts were first shared mostly on Jike (即刻) before he opened the Write Prompt public account.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Li Jigang?

A creator known in the Chinese AI community for prompt writing; his representative works include the Lisp-style prompts Hanyu Xinjie and Roundtable Discussion, published via GitHub ([gist.github.com/lijigang](https://gist.github.com/lijigang)) and the Write Prompt public account.

### What is Hanyu Xinjie?

A Claude prompt Li Jigang released in 2024 that reinterprets a Chinese word from a sharp new angle and renders it as an SVG text card; after release it was widely reposted and remixed in the Chinese AI community. See his [write-prompt repository](https://github.com/lijigang/write-prompt).

### Where can I read Li Jigang's prompts?

Two first-party channels on GitHub: his [Gists](https://gist.github.com/lijigang) (Roundtable Discussion, Business Structure, and others) and the [write-prompt repository](https://github.com/lijigang/write-prompt), the archive of prompts published by the Write Prompt public account.
