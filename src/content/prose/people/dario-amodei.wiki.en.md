## Dario Amodei

Dario Amodei is an American artificial intelligence researcher and entrepreneur who co-founded the AI company Anthropic and serves as its chief executive officer (CEO). Anthropic develops the [Claude](/en/wiki/products/claude) family of large language models; he previously served as vice president of research at OpenAI. See the [Anthropic](/en/wiki/brands/anthropic) entry for the company.

## Career and milestones

- He holds a bachelor's degree in physics from Stanford University and a doctorate in biophysics from Princeton University, where he received a Hertz Foundation Fellowship in 2007; in 2000 he was a member of the U.S. Physics Olympiad team.
- He worked at Baidu from November 2014 to October 2015, then at Google; he joined OpenAI in 2016 and was vice president of research before leaving.
- In 2021 he co-founded Anthropic with his sister Daniela Amodei and other former senior members of OpenAI; Anthropic's leadership page lists him as Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer.
- In October 2024 he published the essay “Machines of Loving Grace” on how AI could improve human welfare; in January 2026 he published a follow-up essay, “The Adolescence of Technology,” on the risks of powerful AI.
- In September 2026 he publicly called for the technology industry to slow the pace of AI development, saying Anthropic would allow independent evaluators permanent access to its models to assess the company's safety commitments.

These milestones follow [Anthropic's leadership page](https://www.anthropic.com/company/leadership) and the [English Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dario_Amodei).
