## Jeff Atwood

Jeff Atwood is an American software developer (born 1970, per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Atwood)) best known as the author of the programming blog Coding Horror, started in 2004. He co-founded the programmer Q&A site Stack Overflow in 2008 with Joel Spolsky and later co-founded the open-source forum software Discourse.

## Background and representative work

**Coding Horror (2004).** Per English Wikipedia, Atwood started the Coding Horror blog in 2004, writing about programming and human factors, and remains its owner and writer.

**Stack Overflow (2008).** He and Joel Spolsky co-founded Stack Overflow in 2008; the site grew into the Stack Exchange network. Per English Wikipedia, Stack Overflow was sold to Prosus for 1.8 billion dollars in 2021.

**Discourse (2013).** Per English Wikipedia, Atwood announced Civilized Discourse Construction Kit, Inc. on February 5, 2013, building the open-source discussion software Discourse out of frustration with existing forum software; he stepped down as CEO on February 1, 2023, becoming Executive Chairman. The product lives at [discourse.org](https://www.discourse.org/).

**Atwood's Law.** "Any application that can be written in JavaScript, will eventually be written in JavaScript." The phrase comes from his Coding Horror post "All Programming Is Web Programming," published August 14, 2009 — see [the post](https://blog.codinghorror.com/all-programming-is-web-programming/) — where he writes that writing Photoshop, Word, or Excel in JavaScript makes "zero engineering sense," but that audience and distribution make such things happen anyway.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Jeff Atwood?

An American software developer, author of the Coding Horror blog (started in 2004), and co-founder of Stack Overflow (2008, with Joel Spolsky) and Discourse (2013); see [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Atwood).

### What is Atwood's Law?

The line from his August 14, 2009 Coding Horror post "All Programming Is Web Programming": "any application that can be written in JavaScript, will eventually be written in JavaScript." See [the post](https://blog.codinghorror.com/all-programming-is-web-programming/).

### Who founded Stack Overflow?

Jeff Atwood and Joel Spolsky co-founded it in 2008; the site grew into the Stack Exchange network and was sold to Prosus for 1.8 billion dollars in 2021 (per English Wikipedia).

### What is Discourse, and how is Jeff Atwood connected to it?

Discourse is the open-source discussion software built by Civilized Discourse Construction Kit, Inc., which Atwood announced in February 2013; see [discourse.org](https://www.discourse.org/). He is a co-founder and became Executive Chairman in February 2023 (per English Wikipedia).
