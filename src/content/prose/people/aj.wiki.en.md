## AJ, initiator of WaytoAGI

AJ is the initiator of [WaytoAGI](/en/wiki/brands/waytoagi), an open community for learning AI. According to the [official introduction](https://about.waytoagi.com/) (Chinese), the community serves Chinese-speaking AI learners and practitioners, maintains a free and open knowledge base, and organizes co-learning sessions, city chapters, swap meets, and hackathons.

## Background and WaytoAGI

The [official introduction](https://about.waytoagi.com/) records that after AJ first used ChatGPT in 2022, he kept collecting good articles and tools into a Feishu document. In 2023 the document was made public, and readers began contributing and correcting it, gradually turning a personal notebook into a co-built open knowledge base and learning community. The number of city chapters, partner universities, and events changes over time; for the current picture, see the [official introduction page](https://about.waytoagi.com/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who founded WaytoAGI?

WaytoAGI was initiated by AJ. The [official introduction](https://about.waytoagi.com/) (Chinese) records the path from his personal Feishu notes in 2022 to the public, co-built knowledge base starting in 2023.

### What is WaytoAGI?

It is a Chinese-language AI learning community and open knowledge base, with the stated mission of helping more people learn AI with fewer detours. The knowledge base is free; some training camps and enterprise services charge fees. The entry points are [waytoagi.com](https://www.waytoagi.com/) and the [official introduction page](https://about.waytoagi.com/).
