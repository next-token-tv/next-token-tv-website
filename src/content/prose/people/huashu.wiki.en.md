## Huashu

Huashu is a creator known for building products with AI and for his open-source agent Skills, under the GitHub account [alchaincyf](https://github.com/alchaincyf). His profile README describes him as "a creator who can't write code" who turns ideas into products with AI and writes up his projects as tutorials and "orange books." His positioning is that of an independent creator in the AI era: building products while distilling his methods into reusable open-source tools.

## Background and notable projects

His GitHub bio reads "AI Native Coder. Ship products without writing code." and lists three signature projects: nuwa-skill, huashu-design, and darwin-skill.

Notable projects include:

- [nuwa-skill](https://github.com/alchaincyf/nuwa-skill): "distills" a person's mental models, decision heuristics, and expression style into a Skill that can be invoked repeatedly — the repo tagline asks why your next distilled employee should be a colleague. Derived persona skills such as zhangxuefeng-skill and naval-skill were generated with nuwa.
- [huashu-design](https://github.com/alchaincyf/huashu-design): an HTML-native design skill for Claude Code, aimed at prototypes, slides, animations, and infographics.
- [darwin-skill](https://github.com/alchaincyf/darwin-skill): a system that lets Skills evolve on their own, iterating through evaluate → improve → test → keep or roll back.
- Tooling projects: huashu-chrome (lets an agent drive Chrome with your existing logins), huashu-mac-use (operates Mac native apps that have no API), huashu-art-motion (art-style animation written in code), and FanBox (a cockpit for coding agents).
- [huashu-skills](https://github.com/alchaincyf/huashu-skills) is the index of all his open-source Skills, which the repository describes as dozens of flagship, persona, and built-in skills.

Beyond open source, he documents his project experience as tutorials and "orange books" published at [bookai.top](https://bookai.top) (linked from his GitHub profile). His profile also mentions AI-built products such as the "kitten fill-light" photo app (小猫补光灯).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Huashu?

Huashu (GitHub: [alchaincyf](https://github.com/alchaincyf)) is a creator who builds products with AI and authors open-source agent Skills such as nuwa-skill, huashu-design, and darwin-skill; he calls himself a creator who can't write code.

### What is nuwa-skill?

An open-source skill generator that distills a person's mental models, decision methods, and expression style into a reusable Skill; persona skills like zhangxuefeng-skill and naval-skill were generated with nuwa. See the [nuwa-skill repository](https://github.com/alchaincyf/nuwa-skill).

### Where can I get Huashu's open-source Skills?

Each Skill has its own repository under his [GitHub profile](https://github.com/alchaincyf), and [huashu-skills](https://github.com/alchaincyf/huashu-skills) is the master index with categorization and installation instructions.
