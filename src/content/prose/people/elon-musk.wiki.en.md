## Elon Musk

Elon Musk is an American entrepreneur who serves as chief executive officer (CEO) of Tesla and SpaceX and is the largest individual shareholder of both companies. He is known for reusable rockets and commercial spaceflight at SpaceX, electric vehicles at Tesla, acquiring the social platform [X](/en/wiki/products/x), and founding the AI company xAI, which merged into SpaceX and now operates under the SpaceXAI brand (see [SpaceXAI](/en/wiki/brands/xai)).

## Career and milestones

- In 1995 he co-founded the software company Zip2, acquired by Compaq in February 1999; in 1999 he co-founded the financial services company X.com, which merged with Confinity to form PayPal, acquired by eBay in 2002.
- In May 2002 he founded the space technology company SpaceX and serves as its CEO and chief engineer; the company is known for the reusable Falcon rockets, the Starlink satellite internet constellation, and crewed spaceflight.
- In 2004 he led Tesla's Series A investment round and became chairman of its board, becoming CEO in 2008. Tesla was founded in 2003 by Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning.
- He co-founded OpenAI in 2015 and left its board in 2018.
- In 2022 he acquired the social platform Twitter and renamed it [X](/en/wiki/products/x).
- In July 2023 he founded the artificial intelligence company xAI, which develops the [Grok](/en/wiki/products/grok) model family; in 2026 xAI became a subsidiary of SpaceX.
- From January to May 2025 he was Senior Advisor to the President in the second Trump administration and de facto head of the newly created Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE), leaving the administration in May 2025 to return to managing his companies.

These milestones follow the [English Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elon_Musk).
