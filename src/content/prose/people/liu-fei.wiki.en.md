## Liu Fei

Liu Fei is a content creator and product manager who hosts the Chinese technology podcast [San Wu Huan](https://podcasts.apple.com/cn/podcast/id1475113228) (三五环), publishing under the name 刘飞Lufy. He also co-hosts the business podcast Banchatie (半拿铁) with Xiao Lei.

## Background and representative work

- **San Wu Huan**: The [Apple Podcasts page](https://podcasts.apple.com/cn/podcast/id1475113228) lists it as a technology talk podcast hosted by Liu FeiLufy. In the podcast description he describes himself as having "a product management career spanning the entire Web 2.0 era — half in small and mid-sized companies and startups, half at DiDi and Alibaba" — and he runs the WeChat public account 刘言飞语. He lives in Hangzhou.
- **Banchatie**: The [Apple Podcasts page](https://podcasts.apple.com/cn/podcast/id1615939013) credits the business podcast to Xiao Lei and Liu Fei as co-hosts.

## Mention in the show

Liu Fei did not take part in either of the two recorded episodes. In Weekly #002's chapter "iPhone Duo launch: folding screens and new interaction questions," [Xiangyang Qiaomu mentions in the Chinese transcript that Liu Fei walked through the whole history of folding screens on a podcast episode](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-8a64cb3ad3d232b72c80), which leads into a discussion of claims that Apple's folding device "reinvented" the folding screen. This is a host relaying Liu Fei's public content.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the San Wu Huan podcast?

San Wu Huan is a Chinese technology talk podcast hosted by Liu Fei, updated on Apple Podcasts and other platforms, built around conversations with friends from different industries and driven by his personal curiosity. The entry point is the [Apple Podcasts page](https://podcasts.apple.com/cn/podcast/id1475113228).

### Who is Liu Fei?

Liu Fei is a content creator with a product management background. By his own account he has worked through the Web 2.0 era at small and mid-sized companies and startups as well as DiDi and Alibaba; he hosts San Wu Huan and runs the WeChat public account 刘言飞语.

### What other podcasts does Liu Fei take part in?

He co-hosts the business podcast Banchatie (半拿铁) with Xiao Lei; see the [Apple Podcasts page](https://podcasts.apple.com/cn/podcast/id1615939013).
