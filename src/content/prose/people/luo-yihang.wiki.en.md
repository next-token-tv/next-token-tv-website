## Positioning

Founder of PingWest, with a background in technology journalism and media. See [source information](https://www.pingwest.com/author).

## Career and representative work

PingWest's official introduction states that it was founded in 2012 in Silicon Valley, later moved its headquarters back to Beijing, and maintains offices in Silicon Valley, Hangzhou and Haikou; it positions itself as a tech content platform for innovators with verticals including PinWest Global (品玩 Global), Guixingren (硅星人), PinChe (品驾) and Bu Keguan Lab (不客观实验室). According to an archived copy of PingWest's official team contact page (January 2017 snapshot), Luo Yihang (Thomas) is listed as "CEO & 总编辑" (CEO & editor-in-chief). This entry's "founder" wording follows the previously published metadata; the sources verified in this batch are the role listing and the platform introduction above. Per the PingWest site footer, the platform is operated by Beijing Pinxi Interactive Technology Co., Ltd. (北京品西互动科技有限公司). No reliable sources for further personal history were verified, so none is recorded.

Thomas Luo does not appear in the episode quoted on this page. The transcript writes the name as "骆一航", the same spoken name; the spending and ARR figures are the hosts' retelling and have not been independently verified. See [episode 007's chapter](/weekly/007/transcript#chapter-06), linked transcript is in Chinese.

## Common confusions

- Thomas Luo vs. PingWest: the former is the person; the latter is the tech media outlet he runs, with no separate brand entry on this wiki.
- "骆一航" vs. "骆轶航": the episode 007 transcript transcribes the name as "骆一航"; both spellings sound identical and this entry treats them as one reference.
