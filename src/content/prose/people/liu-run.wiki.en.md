## Positioning

Founder of Runmi Consulting, business adviser and author who shares business and management ideas. See [source information](https://www.run2me.com/).

## Career and representative work

According to his [official website](https://www.run2me.com/), Liu Run is a business strategy adviser and bestselling author of the "5 分钟商学院" (5-Minute Business School) and "底层逻辑" (The Underlying Logic) book series. He founded Shanghai Runmi Management Consulting and Shanghai Runmi Culture and Media in Shanghai; the businesses cover strategy consulting, executive training, strategy workshops, private-board meetings and the "问道中国 / 问道全球" study tours, plus an AI practice focused on connecting AI to enterprise workflows. His content channels include the WeChat public account "刘润", Bilibili and Douyin accounts "刘润商学", the Runmi Reading Club livestream and the "进化岛" community; since 2021 he has held his annual speech "进化的力量" in Shanghai each October. Per the Dedao App official site's people section, he also teaches courses on Dedao. His website does not list prior employment, so this entry records none.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Liu Run?

A business strategy adviser and bestselling author, founder of Runmi Consulting, known for the "5-Minute Business School" and "The Underlying Logic" series; see his [official website](https://www.run2me.com/).

### What is Runmi Consulting?

The umbrella for Shanghai Runmi Management Consulting and Shanghai Runmi Culture and Media, founded by Liu Run in Shanghai, covering strategy consulting, training, study tours and content communities; see the [official introduction](https://www.run2me.com/).
