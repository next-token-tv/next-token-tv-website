## Positioning

Co-founder of Dify, an AI application development platform, focused on AI product design, product architecture, and commercialization.

## Career and representative work

According to [his QCon Beijing 2026 speaker page](https://qcon.infoq.cn/2026/beijing/speaker/10244), Yan Junchen is a co-founder of Dify.AI with over ten years of experience in product architecture and commercialization, spanning online education, OTA, e-commerce retail, and life sciences; he produced the "OpenClaw 生态实践" (OpenClaw ecosystem in practice) track at that conference. Dify is an AI application development platform for developers.

## Mention in the show

Yan Junchen does not appear in episode 008. In that episode's chapter "录音硬件、跨端兼容与安卓设备" ([recording hardware, cross-device compatibility, and Android devices](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-13)), while talking about Oppo's foldable phone, Orange says "那个你说那个手机，N6，我们还没有装吧，他用的就是" ([that phone, the N6 — he is the one using it](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-631be5410d3ececeb3a8)), and Yang Pan replies "君晨不是" ([isn't that Junchen](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-541986302eeee8a68df1)). In context, the discussion is about Yan Junchen using the Oppo foldable: Orange says the phone is "非常好，就是比那个 Duo 还要薄，然后又轻" ([very good — thinner and lighter than the Duo](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-db1c3765ee62cbb72bfa)) and "他已经用了有几个月了吧。就看不到折痕" ([he has used it for a few months now; the crease is invisible](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-53aa3ae540963961b11f)), and Guizang adds "关键他同时还用苹果手机，他主力机变成了 N" ([the key point is he still uses an iPhone, yet the Oppo has become his main phone](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-b928805f420ddbf76c78)). These are the hosts' retelling of his experience and their own impressions.
