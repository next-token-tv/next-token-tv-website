## Chen Baoyi (陈抱一)

Chen Baoyi is a technology video creator who publishes unboxing and hands-on videos on [Bilibili](https://space.bilibili.com/3530725). His content centers on the Xiaomi ecosystem, covering Xiaomi phones, the SU7, smart-home products and HyperOS; he also runs "Xiaobao Tongxue" (小抱同学), an account posting short tech clips.

## Background and notable work

Videos on his Bilibili account include "【首发】小米澎湃OS4全家桶体验！万物乱连？" (first look at the Xiaomi HyperOS 4 family experience), "【开箱】新一代小米SU7的100种玩法！" (100 things to do with the new Xiaomi SU7) and "【开箱】2024年了！小米智能家居成了吗？" (has Xiaomi's smart home come together in 2024?), demonstrating how Xiaomi's "Human × Car × Home" ecosystem connects through its system features. Related entries: [Xiaomi](/en/wiki/brands/xiaomi) and [HyperOS](/en/wiki/products/hyperos).
