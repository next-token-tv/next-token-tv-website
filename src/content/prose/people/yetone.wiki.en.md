## yetone

yetone is an open-source developer whose GitHub profile is [github.com/yetone](https://github.com/yetone). His representative projects include Avante (avante.nvim), an AI coding plugin for Neovim, and the cross-platform translation tool NextAI Translator.

## Career and representative work

- **[Avante (avante.nvim)](https://github.com/avante-corp/avante.nvim)**: an AI coding plugin that lets you "use your Neovim like using Cursor AI IDE", hosted under the avante-corp organization and pinned on his [GitHub profile](https://github.com/yetone).
- **[NextAI Translator](https://github.com/nextai-translator/nextai-translator)**: a translation browser extension and cross-platform desktop application, also pinned on his profile. The project README states that after receiving a trademark ownership warning from OpenAI, the project and product were renamed to nextai translator (previously openai-translator).
- **[Magpie](https://github.com/yetone/magpie)** ([usemagpie.ai](https://usemagpie.ai), launched 2026): a menu bar model aggregator whose repository tagline reads "Every agent's model. One place." — pointing Codex at DeepSeek, Claude Code at Kimi, and connecting coding agents to models of your choice.
- **[Cumora](https://github.com/yetone/cumora)** ([cumora.ai](https://cumora.ai), launched 2026): a cross-platform team chat application "where agent teams gather", with AI agents as first-class teammates over cloud or bring-your-own (Claude Code / Codex) brains.

## Mention in the show

yetone does not appear in the episode quoted on this page. In episode 010's chapter "持续迭代与开源生态：代码之外还能留下什么" (Continuous iteration and the open-source ecosystem: what can be left behind beyond code), which discusses continuous iteration and ecosystems around open-source projects, Yang Pan says "那个 yetone 不就是吗？对吧？那个每天发无数个版本，这个谁也跟不上" (isn't that yetone? He ships countless versions every day — nobody can keep up) ([see the line](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-3ee901e07c8d300fe26e) in the Chinese transcript). Xiangyang Qiaomu then says "但他应该也是搞生态，因为他一个人的话，他做的东西非常好，我试过，非常好。但是他一个人没有形成生态，别人一些中转站或者什么，出于利益都就会开始攻击他。因为你抢了别人利益啊" (but he is probably also building an ecosystem — on his own his work is very good, I have tried it, very good — but as an individual he has not formed an ecosystem, and relay services and the like will attack him over their interests, because you have taken their business) ([see the line](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-6bb48548d9b9cd9195ae)). "Shipping countless versions every day" is Yang Pan's description of his release cadence, "his work is very good" is Xiangyang Qiaomu's experience as a user, and the account of being attacked is Xiangyang Qiaomu's own claim; these are the show participants' observations and personal accounts of release cadence and ecosystem dynamics around open-source projects, and do not represent any statement by yetone himself.
