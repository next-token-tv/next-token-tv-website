## Brad Qiang (Brad 强)

Brad Qiang is a co-founder and community lead of the [Yizhi Yanhua Community](/en/wiki/brands/yizhi-yanhua) and a developer. According to the self-description on the Yizhi Yanhua AI podcast page on Xiaoyuzhou, the community was founded in 2023 and is "a high-quality, hardcore-oriented AI community" running more than 40 vertical AI groups and the Yizhi Yanhua AI podcast, with content also published through the WeChat official account "一支烟花AI".

## Background and notable work

His GitHub account ([ccc7574](https://github.com/ccc7574)) carries the display name "Brad 强" and lists his location as Shanghai. Open-source projects he has published include fireworks-skill-memory, a persistent per-skill experience memory for Claude Code that distills session lessons into KNOWLEDGE.md files and injects them on the next use, and wechat-radar, a WeChat chat intelligence dashboard that aggregates group-chat signals, topics, links and trends.
