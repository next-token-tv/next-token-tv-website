## Xiang Zheng (相征)

Xiang Zheng is the founder and host of the Chinese-language podcast 大内密谈 (Midnight Talk); listeners and fellow podcasters commonly call him "Xiangye" (相爷).

## Career and representative work

According to his [Apple Podcasts creator profile](https://podcasts.apple.com/cn/multi-room/6753197297), 大内密谈 is a pan-cultural podcast produced by "Shenye TanTan" (深夜谈谈) covering music, film, travel and other topics, and "founder and host Xiang Zheng is one of the earliest creators of Chinese-language podcasts". The same page lists other shows from his team, including 米地研究所, 高端边缘多余的十大男神, 空岛 landing on air, and 枕边风 Whisper & Roar. The page also quotes him comparing recording a show to cooking, aiming for "guoqi" (wok hei) in the content.

大内密谈 regularly invites creators from different fields as guests. The [episode page](https://www.xiaoyuzhoufm.com/episode/6747ffb7c3b2a2f3340a95ae) on Xiaoyuzhou shows that vol.1244, "作为乙方的创作者，困境与救赎。" (As a creator on the agency side: predicament and redemption), was hosted by Xiang Zheng with [Zhongqing](/en/wiki/people/zhongqing), Xiaobaimian, and Tang Hanxiao as guests.

## Mention in the show

Xiang Zheng does not appear in the episode quoted on this page. In episode 010's chapter "水墨交互：复刻一个作品与做出第一个作品" (Ink-wash interaction: recreating a work and making a first work), Orange retells a podcast episode she listened to, recorded by [Zhongqing](/en/wiki/people/zhongqing) together with Xiang Zheng. Orange quotes Zhongqing's view: "他就说人类一直是说，AI 会帮我们做很多这种我们不想做的事情，让我们去享受生活，做很有创造力的事情" (he said humanity keeps saying that AI will do the things we do not want to do so we can enjoy life and do very creative things) ([see the line](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-e16ee850ae2c6260612c) in the Chinese transcript), and then "他说这是放屁，他说你真实一点吧，AI 做的就是最有创造力的事情" (he said that is nonsense — face reality, what AI makes is the most creative thing) ([see the line](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-590a59fad49316289b8a)). The transcript does not name the podcast; public sources confirm that 大内密谈 has episodes with Zhongqing as guest and Xiang Zheng as host (such as vol.1244), but the specific episode Orange heard cannot be confirmed. These are Zhongqing's views as retold by Orange; Xiang Zheng is mentioned in the transcript only as the host of that recording, which does not represent any statement by Xiang Zheng himself. See [the chapter in the Chinese transcript](/weekly/010/transcript#chapter-08).
