## Positioning
Host of the Lex Fridman Podcast, with research interests in human–AI interaction and robotics. [Official source](https://lexfridman.com/). English Wikipedia describes him as an American computer scientist and podcaster who has hosted the interview podcast named after himself since 2018, speaking with notable figures from science, technology, sports, the arts, and politics.

## Career and representative work
Lex Fridman Podcast: an interview show whose podcast page describes topics spanning technology, history, philosophy, physics, AI, robotics, business and more; see the [podcast page](https://lexfridman.com/podcast/).

Research and publications: his website lists work under Research & Publications, including DeepTraffic (a reinforcement learning system for multi-agent dense traffic navigation), Arguing Machines (a framework for human supervision of black-box AI systems), and the MIT Advanced Vehicle Technology Study (a large-scale naturalistic study of human interaction with driving automation).

## Mention in the show
Lex Fridman does not appear in the episode quoted on this page. In episode 007's chapter "更大胆的任务：播客转写、Slack 与微信整理" (Bolder tasks: podcast transcription, Slack, and WeChat clean-up), Xiangyang Qiaomu says he now dares to take on "particularly bold" jobs: Lex Fridman has some 400–500 episodes and the official site lacks subtitles for some of them, so he had AI take the site's subtitles where available, download and transcribe the missing videos, translate everything into Chinese, and turn it all into a website ([see the line](/weekly/007/transcript#quote-aa0f52904f98d4fb554d) and [this line](/weekly/007/transcript#quote-194fdd8d1baa66b25243) in the Chinese transcript). The "400–500 episodes" figure is the host's own count; the actual episode list and subtitle availability are on the official site.

## Common confusions
- Lex Fridman vs. the Lex Fridman Podcast: this entry is the person; the show is the interview program he hosts, and this wiki has no separate entry for it.
- Mentioned is not the same as being a guest: he appears in Next Token transcripts only because the hosts discuss transcribing his show, not as a recording participant.
