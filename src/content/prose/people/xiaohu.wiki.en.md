## Xiaohu

Xiaohu is an AI news creator. He founded [Xiaohu.AI](https://xiaohu.ai/) (Chinese), which describes itself as "a platform built by Xiaohu dedicated to publishing AI news, tutorials, courses, tools, and open-source project case studies, where most of the content is carefully selected and personally tested by Xiaohu."

## Background and representative work

Xiaohu publishes on [Xiaohu.AI](https://xiaohu.ai/), covering AI news, tutorials, and hands-on tool reviews from China and abroad, with a focus on products and content he has tested himself.

## Citation in the show
