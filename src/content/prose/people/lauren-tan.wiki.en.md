## Positioning

Software engineer known online as poteto, working on AI products and the React compiler. See [source information](https://github.com/poteto).

## Career and representative work

Her [GitHub profile](https://github.com/poteto) (handle poteto, registered in 2012) states "Software Engineer @xai-org & @react compiler core team" — a software engineer at xAI and a member of the React compiler core team — and lists more than eighty public repositories. The React [team page](https://react.dev/community/team) also lists her as Lauren Tan in the Compiler working group. The two official sources differ on her employer as of their last edits (the React page says "Engineer at Cursor"; her GitHub bio says xAI), so this entry records both statements rather than picking one. Her X handle is also poteto.

## Mention in the show

Lauren Tan does not appear in the episode quoted on this page. In episode 007's chapter "Omarchy、旧手机与重写软件的可能性" (Omarchy, old phones, and the possibility of rewriting software), Guizang retells that someone flashed the Linux distribution inside Grok Bot to [Omarchy](/en/wiki/products/omarchy) ([see the line](/weekly/007/transcript#quote-175a9ff7651adf1dbc74) in the Chinese transcript) and that "Grok Bot 那个人很感兴趣，说 @poteto。艾特那个人，说我们想了解这个细节" (the Grok Bot person was interested, @-mentioning that person and asking for the details) ([see the line](/weekly/007/transcript#quote-2563de5f754183baae93)). @poteto is this entry's public handle; the @-mention is the host's retelling of a social-media exchange that this entry has not verified. [Grok Bot](/en/wiki/products/grok-bot) is an xAI product.
