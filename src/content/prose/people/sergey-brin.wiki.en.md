## Sergey Brin

Sergey Brin is an American entrepreneur and computer scientist, co-founder of [Google](/en/wiki/brands/google). He was born in Moscow, Soviet Union, on August 21, 1973, and moved to the United States with his family in 1979 (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergey_Brin)). He earned his computer science and mathematics degrees at the University of Maryland and then entered graduate study at Stanford.

## Career and representative work

- Stanford and Backrub: Brin met Larry Page at Stanford. They built Backrub, a search engine that ranked pages using links; per English Wikipedia, an early version ran on the Stanford website starting in August 1996.
- Founding Google: in September 1998, the two founded [Google](/en/wiki/brands/google), which started from a garage in Menlo Park, California (recorded both on Google's company history page and on English Wikipedia).
- President of Alphabet: after the 2015 restructuring into Alphabet, Brin served as president of Alphabet; in December 2019 he and Page announced they were stepping down from Alphabet's management, with Google CEO Sundar Pichai becoming CEO of Alphabet (per English Wikipedia).
- Returning to AI research: per English Wikipedia, in 2023 Brin came out of retirement to contribute to AI research at Google, including work related to the Gemini models.

## Common confusions

- Brin vs. Page: the two co-founders are often mentioned together. Before stepping down, Brin was president of Alphabet and Page was CEO of Alphabet; Google has been led by Sundar Pichai since.
- Backrub vs. Google: Backrub was the early name of the search project that later became Google.
- Google vs. Alphabet: after the 2015 restructuring, Google is a subsidiary of Alphabet; the positions Brin and Page stepped down from were at the Alphabet level.

## Mention in the show

Brin is not a participant in the show. Episode 011 uses his return to discuss how founders can affect collaboration across research teams.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Sergey Brin?

Co-founder of [Google](/en/wiki/brands/google), which he founded with Larry Page in 1998; he served as president of Alphabet from 2015 to 2019 and returned to AI research at Google starting in 2023.

### How did Brin meet Larry Page?

The two met at Stanford, where Brin was in graduate school; they went on to build Backrub together and found Google.

### What was Backrub?

An early search engine built by Brin and Page that ranked results by the links between pages; per English Wikipedia, an early version ran on the Stanford website from August 1996 and it later became Google.

### What role did Brin hold at Alphabet?

He served as president of Alphabet after the 2015 restructuring and stepped down in December 2019, with Google CEO Sundar Pichai becoming CEO of Alphabet (per English Wikipedia).

### What has Brin been doing since returning to Google?

He came out of retirement in 2023 to contribute to AI research at Google, including work related to the Gemini models (per English Wikipedia); public updates can be followed through the projects he joins and company announcements.
