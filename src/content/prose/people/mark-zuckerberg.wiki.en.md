## Mark Zuckerberg

Mark Zuckerberg is the founder, chairman, and chief executive officer of [Meta](/en/wiki/brands/meta). According to Meta's official leadership page, he started the company in 2004 as Facebook and is responsible for setting its overall direction and product strategy, leading the design of Meta's services and the development of its core technology and infrastructure. He is known for building Facebook from a campus social network into a global company spanning Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and other apps, and for steering the company toward its "metaverse" positioning in 2021.

## Career and milestones

[Meta's leadership and governance page](https://investor.atmeta.com/leadership-and-governance/) states that Zuckerberg studied computer science at Harvard University before relocating the company to Palo Alto, California; he appears on both the management and board listings, with Robert M. Kimmitt, Lead Independent Director since March 2020, serving as the independent board role in that structure.

Major company events during his tenure include:

- **Instagram acquisition**: in April 2012, Facebook acquired Instagram for approximately US$1 billion in cash and stock.
- **WhatsApp acquisition**: the roughly US$19 billion purchase of the mobile messaging app WhatsApp was announced in February 2014 and completed on October 6, 2014.
- **Rename to Meta**: on October 28, 2021, at Facebook Connect, the company announced it would rename from Facebook, Inc. to Meta Platforms, pivoting toward virtual and augmented reality as the "metaverse."
- **AI moves**: the company launched the Meta AI assistant and the open-weight Llama model family, and in June 2025 invested US$14.3 billion for a 49% stake in [Scale AI](https://scale.com/about), with Scale AI co-founder [Alexandr Wang](/en/wiki/people/alexandr-wang) becoming Meta's Chief AI Officer and leading Superintelligence Labs.

Company history and deal figures are taken from the [Wikipedia article on Meta Platforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta_Platforms) and the official pages above.

## Frequently asked questions

### What role does Mark Zuckerberg hold at Meta?

He is Meta's founder, chairman, and chief executive officer (Founder, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer); see [Meta's leadership page](https://investor.atmeta.com/leadership-and-governance/).

### What is the relationship between Meta and Facebook?

The company was started as Facebook in 2004 and was renamed Meta Platforms on October 28, 2021; Facebook is one social app owned by Meta, alongside Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, and Threads.

### When did Mark Zuckerberg found Facebook?

In 2004. Per [Meta's leadership page](https://investor.atmeta.com/leadership-and-governance/), he studied computer science at Harvard University and then relocated the company to Palo Alto, California.

### Which companies has Meta acquired?

Per the [Wikipedia article on Meta Platforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta_Platforms): Instagram in April 2012 for about US$1 billion, and WhatsApp — announced in February 2014 and completed on October 6 that year — for about US$19 billion.

### What is Mark Zuckerberg's relationship with Alexandr Wang?

In June 2025, Meta invested US$14.3 billion for a 49% stake in Scale AI, and Scale AI co-founder Alexandr Wang joined Meta as Chief AI Officer leading Superintelligence Labs; see [Scale AI's site](https://scale.com/about) and the [Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandr_Wang). The show's phrase "acquired Alexandr Wang's company" is the hosts' summary.
