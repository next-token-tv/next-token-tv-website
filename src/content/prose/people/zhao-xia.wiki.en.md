## Positioning

Zhao Xia, known as "Simon" (西蒙), is the founder of [GCORES](/en/wiki/brands/gcores) (机核), a games-culture creator with a design background.

## Career and representative work

Per the [Chinese Wikipedia entry on GCORES](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9C%BA%E6%A0%B8), GCORES was founded by Zhao Xia and Jiang Yao; Zhao Xia started it in 2010 as an amateur internet radio run by console-game players, and the business was incorporated in 2014 as 机核（北京）文化传媒有限公司, with the entry referring to Zhao Xia as the person in charge. The [GCORES site](https://www.gcores.com/jobs) states that since 2010 the company has been dedicated to sharing gamers' lives and discussing games culture. Simon's design background is recorded in a user-contributed interview published on GCORES on 18 December 2024, Director BK's 《机核创始人：现在遇到所有问题都是钱从哪来？》 ("GCORES founder: every problem we run into now is where does the money come from?"): he worked in the clothing industry from 2006 to 2014, including stints at Kappa and FILA, before leaving to commit to GCORES with Jiang Yao. GCORES also incubated the clothing brand [GCORES Industries](/en/wiki/brands/gcores-industries). Simon has long published on GCORES under the name "Simon"; "Simon" and "Zhao Xia" are the same person.

## Mention in the show

Zhao Xia does not appear in the episode quoted on this page. In episode 009's chapter "创始人、组织与消费" (Founders, organizations, and consumption), Guizang mentions that Zhongqing's new podcast "采访了那个机核的 CEO，那个对吧？西蒙" (interviewed the CEO of GCORES, right, Simon), and says of Simon "他很少技术，他是个设计师出身" (he is not very technical; he comes from a design background), noting "他其实还有个服装品牌叫吉考斯（工业）嘛" (he also has a clothing brand, GCORES Industries) ([see the line](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-b971729eaffca7f92fd6) in the Chinese transcript). Guizang further retells that since [OpenClaw](/en/wiki/products/openclaw), Simon's team has automated merch selection, ad buying, reporting, and analytics tracking, and that "一个一个小公司带来的生产力的提升是巨大的" (for small companies the productivity gain is huge) ([see the line](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-8924ce119be20399d645)). "CEO of GCORES" is Guizang's wording; public sources record Zhao Xia as the founder. The automation details are the hosts' retellings and do not represent statements by Zhao Xia on the show. See [the chapter in the transcript](/weekly/009/transcript#chapter-05).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Zhao Xia?

The founder of [GCORES](/en/wiki/brands/gcores), known as "Simon" (西蒙). Per the Chinese Wikipedia entry, GCORES was founded by Zhao Xia and Jiang Yao, with Zhao Xia starting it in 2010.

### Are Simon and Zhao Xia the same person?

Yes. "Simon" is the name Zhao Xia uses on GCORES; the Chinese Wikipedia entry on GCORES records that the apology article published by GCORES' person in charge, Zhao Xia, was authored under the name Simon.

### When was GCORES founded?

Zhao Xia started GCORES in 2010 as an amateur internet radio; the business was incorporated in 2014 as 机核（北京）文化传媒有限公司.

### What is GCORES Industries?

A clothing, accessories, and lifestyle brand incubated by GCORES; the show also mentions Simon "having a clothing brand called GCORES Industries (吉考斯工业)".
