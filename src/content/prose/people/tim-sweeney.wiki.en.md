## Positioning

Tim Sweeney is an American video game programmer and businessman, the founder and CEO of [Epic Games](/en/wiki/brands/epic-games) and the creator of Unreal Engine.

## Career and representative work

Per the [English Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Sweeney), Sweeney was born in 1970 and studied mechanical engineering at the University of Maryland. He started out as Potomac Computer Systems, distributing the shareware game ZZT that he developed himself, then renamed the company Epic MegaGames and grew the business with Mark Rein. Following the success of the 1998 first-person shooter Unreal, the Unreal Engine he developed for the game was licensed by multiple other game makers, and the company relocated to North Carolina and changed its name to Epic Games in 1999. Epic later expanded around Fortnite and digital distribution: the Epic Games Store launched in 2018 and expanded to mobile in 2024, and in 2020 Sweeney led Epic's legal challenge to Apple's App Store policies. He is the principal creator of [Unreal Engine](/en/wiki/products/unreal-engine), the core of Epic's game development platform.
