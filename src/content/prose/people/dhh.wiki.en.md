## David Heinemeier Hansson (DHH)

David Heinemeier Hansson (DHH) is the creator of Ruby on Rails, the creator of Omarchy, and a co-owner of 37signals. His personal site also lists roles as an author, racing driver, investor, and Shopify board member.

## Representative work and relationships

DHH created Ruby on Rails in 2003 and describes the framework and its ecosystem on his personal site. He also created Omarchy, which he describes as an opinionated Linux system built while searching for a different kind of personal computer for the age of agents. Related entries include [Ruby on Rails](/en/wiki/products/ruby-on-rails), [Omarchy](/en/wiki/products/omarchy), and the [Omacom Foundation](/en/wiki/brands/omacom). 37signals is the company behind Basecamp and HEY; DHH says he owns it with Jason Fried. These role descriptions come from [his personal profile](https://dhh.dk/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is David Heinemeier Hansson, also known as DHH?

DHH uses the initials of David Heinemeier Hansson. He created Ruby on Rails and Omarchy and co-owns 37signals, as described in his [personal biography](https://dhh.dk/).

### What books has David Heinemeier Hansson written?

He co-authored REWORK, REMOTE: Office Not Required, It Doesn’t Have to Be Crazy at Work, and Getting Real with Jason Fried. His [author biography](https://dhh.dk/) links to the books.

### Has DHH raced at the 24 Hours of Le Mans?

Yes. His biography records appearances beginning in 2012 and a class win with Aston Martin in 2014. That was a class victory rather than an overall win; see his [racing biography](https://dhh.dk/).
