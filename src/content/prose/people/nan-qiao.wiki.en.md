## Nan Qiao (南乔)

Nan Qiao is named in the opening thanks of Next Token Weekly episode 002. That episode was recorded at AGI Bar in Shanghai on September 12, 2026, with the duty host Xiangyang Qiaomu delivering the thanks on behalf of the show.

## Thanks in the show

In Weekly #002's opening chapter "上海开场：本周模型、Agent 与硬件" (Shanghai opening: this week's models, agents, and hardware) of the [Chinese transcript](/weekly/002/transcript#chapter-02), Xiangyang Qiaomu says: "首先感谢大聪明、南乔，邀请我们在上海的 AGI Bar，录节目" ("First, thanks to Da Congming and Nan Qiao for inviting us to record the show at AGI Bar in Shanghai") ([paragraph](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-beaa798a9959760c2231)). Nan Qiao appears in this thanks alongside [Cyber Zen](/en/wiki/brands/cyber-zen) ("Da Congming"), and the thanks relates to inviting the show to record at [AGI Bar](/en/wiki/brands/agi-bar) in Shanghai.

## Verifiable information

The transcript does not state Nan Qiao's identity, role, or organization, and Nan Qiao did not take part in either of the two recorded episodes. Beyond the transcript itself, no further publicly verifiable information about Nan Qiao is available, so this page records only the thanks given in the show.
