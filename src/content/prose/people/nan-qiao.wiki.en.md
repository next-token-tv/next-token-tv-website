## Nan Qiao (南乔)

Nan Qiao is named in the opening thanks of Next Token Weekly episode 002. That episode was recorded at AGI Bar in Shanghai on September 12, 2026, with the duty host Xiangyang Qiaomu delivering the thanks on behalf of the show. According to information provided by Next Token, Nan Qiao is a person in charge of [AGI Bar](/en/wiki/brands/agi-bar); this statement is not corroborated by independent public sources, and existing public reporting on who founded and runs AGI Bar does not directly match it (see below).

## Thanks in the show

In Weekly #002's opening chapter "上海开场：本周模型、Agent 与硬件" (Shanghai opening: this week's models, agents, and hardware) of the [Chinese transcript](/weekly/002/transcript#chapter-02), Xiangyang Qiaomu says: "首先感谢大聪明、南乔，邀请我们在上海的 AGI Bar，录节目" ("First, thanks to Da Congming and Nan Qiao for inviting us to record the show at AGI Bar in Shanghai") ([paragraph](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-beaa798a9959760c2231)). The episode's [show notes on Xiaoyuzhou](https://www.xiaoyuzhoufm.com/episode/6aa68040492687f6aad92b15) likewise thank "上海 AGI Bar 提供录制场地，感谢赛博禅心与南乔邀请" (Shanghai AGI Bar for the recording venue, and Cyber Zen and Nan Qiao for the invitation). Nan Qiao appears in these thanks alongside [Cyber Zen](/en/wiki/people/cyber-zen) ("Da Congming"), and the thanks relates to inviting the show to record at AGI Bar in Shanghai. Nan Qiao did not take part in either of the two recorded episodes, and the show itself does not state Nan Qiao's identity, role, or organization.

## Who runs AGI Bar in public reporting

Public reporting credits the founding of AGI Bar to Song De: according to "海淀AI人群图鉴" (a profile of Haidian's AI community by Jiazi Lightyear), Song De is better known by the nickname "Da Congming" and as the person behind the WeChat account "赛博禅心" (Cyber Zen); in early 2025 he began planning a bar and named it AGI Bar, which opened on Zhongguancun Startup Street in Beijing on June 1, 2025 ([republished on Tencent News](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260205A066B000)). Several event reports likewise refer to him as AGI Bar's principal ("主理人"). "Nan Qiao" and "Da Congming" appear as two different names in the episode 002 thanks, and neither the AGI Bar website nor the public reports cited above mention the name Nan Qiao. The "person in charge" statement on this page therefore reflects only information provided by Next Token; whether it refers to the Beijing venue, the Shanghai venue, or AGI Bar overall, and how it relates to the principal role, is not documented in public sources.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Nan Qiao?

Nan Qiao was thanked in the opening of Next Token Weekly episode 002, alongside "Da Congming" (Cyber Zen), for inviting the show to record at AGI Bar in Shanghai. According to information provided by Next Token, Nan Qiao is a person in charge of AGI Bar; as of September 2026 this is not corroborated by independent public sources, and public reporting names Song De (better known as "Da Congming," the person behind the Cyber Zen WeChat account) as AGI Bar's founder and principal.

### What is AGI Bar?

AGI Bar is an in-person gathering space for AI practitioners and founders. It started on Zhongguancun Startup Street in Beijing in June 2025 and later added a venue in Shanghai, operating as an AI store by day and a bar by night, hosting product launches and industry events. See the [AGI Bar entry](/en/wiki/brands/agi-bar).
