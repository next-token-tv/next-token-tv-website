## Luo Yonghao

Luo Yonghao (born July 9, 1972; widely known as "Lao Luo") is a Chinese technology entrepreneur and interview-show host. He founded the blogging site Bullog, the Laoluo English training school, and the smartphone company Smartisan; returned to public attention in 2020 with the livestream commerce brand Jiaogepengyou ("Make a Friend"); announced the AR startup Thin Red Line in 2022; and began producing the interview program Luo Yonghao's Crossroads (《罗永浩的十字路口》) in August 2025.

## Career

Drawn from Chinese Wikipedia and public reporting:

- **Early years and teaching**: Born in Helong, Yanbian, Jilin, to an ethnic Korean family, he dropped out in his second year of high school. In 2001 he joined New Oriental in Beijing as a GRE lecturer; students' bootleg recordings of his humorous asides spread online as "Lao Luo Quotations," making him one of China's early internet celebrities and placing him on Baidu's annual search rankings in 2005 and 2006. He resigned in June 2006.
- **Bullog and the English school**: On July 31, 2006, he launched the blogging site Bullog, which gathered writers such as Han Han and Li Yinhe; it was shut down about three years later. He then founded his own English training school.
- **Smartisan (2012–2019)**: He announced in 2012 that he would build a smartphone and founded Smartisan, releasing its first phone, the Smartisan T1, in 2014. The company fell short commercially; in 2019 the Nut (Jian Guo) phone business was sold to ByteDance. Luo was listed as a dishonest debtor by a court over unfulfilled obligations, and he publicly put his personal debt at around 600 million yuan in 2020.
- **Jiaogepengyou livestreaming (2020–2022)**: On March 26, 2020, he announced Douyin as his exclusive livestream commerce platform and held his first livestream on April 1. Livestream sales became his main source of debt repayment; in September 2020 he said on the show Rock & Roast that 400 million of his 600 million yuan debt had been repaid. From June 13, 2022, he quit Weibo and other social platforms, and joined Taobao livestreaming that October.
- **Thin Red Line (2022– )**: On July 10, 2022, he announced on the Jiaogepengyou livestream that his new company was named Thin Red Line, focused on AR, calling it his "last startup." Per a 36Kr report in October 2022, the company raised nearly 400 million yuan in angel funding led by Meituan Longzhu.
- **Luo Yonghao's Crossroads (2025– )**: Per Chinese Wikipedia, he began the interview program Luo Yonghao's Crossroads in August 2025, holding three-to-five-hour in-depth conversations with one industry figure per episode.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Luo Yonghao?

A Chinese technology entrepreneur and interview-show host: a former New Oriental English teacher ("Lao Luo Quotations"), founder of Bullog and of Smartisan, co-founder of the livestream commerce brand Jiaogepengyou, founder of Thin Red Line, and since August 2025 the host of Luo Yonghao's Crossroads.

### How much did Luo Yonghao owe, and has he repaid it?

In September 2020 he said publicly on Rock & Roast that 400 million of roughly 600 million yuan in debt had been repaid, mainly through livestream selling; his repayment progress has been covered repeatedly by media, and binding debt and enforcement details are a matter of public court records.

### What is Luo Yonghao's Crossroads?

An interview program he began producing in August 2025, featuring three-to-five-hour in-depth conversations with one industry figure per episode; see [Chinese Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%BD%97%E6%B0%B8%E6%B5%A9).

### What happened to Smartisan?

Smartisan released the T1 in 2014 but fell short commercially; in 2019 the Nut phone business was sold to ByteDance and Luo entered his debt-repayment phase. The Smartisan phone business did not continue.

### What is Thin Red Line?

The company Luo announced on July 10, 2022, initially focused on AR as a next-generation computing platform.
