## Shengyu Liu (刘胜与)

Shengyu Liu is a machine learning systems engineer at [DeepSeek](/en/wiki/brands/deepseek). According to [his homepage](https://interestinglsy.github.io/), he has worked at DeepSeek-AI in Hangzhou since April 2025, focusing on machine learning systems (MLSys) and kernel design and optimization.

## Background and notable work

The following is based on the account on [his homepage](https://interestinglsy.github.io/).

- Education: he entered the Turing Class at the School of EECS, Peking University in September 2021 (September 2021 to July 2025), advised by Associate Professor Xin Jin; his research areas are machine learning systems (MLSys), machine learning compilers, and distributed systems.
- Supercomputing: as team leader of the Peking University Supercomputing Team, he led the team to first place at the 10th ASC student supercomputer competition and second place at SC23.
- Open source and research: he built [swiftLLM](https://github.com/interestingLSY/swiftLLM), a research-oriented inference system of roughly 2,000 lines of code that claims vLLM-comparable performance; papers listed on his homepage include DistServe (OSDI 2024), LoongServe (SOSP) and RLHFuse (NSDI 2025) in LLM serving systems.
- Work at DeepSeek: his homepage records, in the team's voice, the releases of DeepSeek-V3.2, the DeepSeek-V4 preview and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, as well as DeepSelect, an efficient top-k implementation for DeepSeek Sparse Attention; it also describes [FlashMLA](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/FlashMLA), an efficient MLA decoding kernel, as delivering 5–15% speedups on compute-intensive tasks and up to 660 TFlops on an NVIDIA H800 SXM5 GPU.
