## Positioning

A mathematician working in Fourier analysis and geometric measure theory, best known for proving the Kakeya set conjecture in three dimensions together with Joshua Zahl.

## Career and representative work

According to [her personal website](https://sites.google.com/view/hongwang/home) and the Chinese Wikipedia article, Hong Wang was born in 1991 in Guilin, Guangxi, China. She earned a bachelor's degree at Peking University in 2011, an engineer's degree from École Polytechnique and a master's degree from the University of Paris-Sud in 2014, and a mathematics PhD at MIT in 2019 under Larry Guth. She was a postdoctoral member at the Institute for Advanced Study from 2019 to 2021 and an assistant professor at UCLA from 2021 to 2023, then joined the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences at New York University, where she became a professor in 2025; since September 2025 she has also been a permanent professor at IHÉS in France.

Her research covers Fourier analysis, restriction and decoupling theory, fractal geometry, and Kakeya-type problems. In February 2025 she and Joshua Zahl posted a preprint on arXiv giving a complete proof of the Kakeya set conjecture in three dimensions; earlier, with Kevin Ren in 2023, she resolved the Furstenberg sets estimate in the plane.

Per the Chinese Wikipedia article, her honors include the 2022 Maryam Mirzakhani New Frontiers Prize, the 2025 Salem Prize and Ostrowski Prize, and the 2026 Clay Research Award; in July 2026 she shared the Fields Medal with Yu Deng, John Pardon, and Jacob Tsimerman, becoming the first Chinese woman to receive the medal and the third woman in its history. The names of that year's co-laureates follow the [official IMU page](https://www.mathunion.org/imu-awards/fields-medal/fields-medals-2026).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Hong Wang?

A mathematician in Fourier analysis and geometric measure theory and a professor at the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences at New York University, known for proving the three-dimensional Kakeya set conjecture with Joshua Zahl in 2025; see [her personal website](https://sites.google.com/view/hongwang/home).

### What is the Kakeya set conjecture?

The Kakeya set conjecture is a well-known problem in geometric measure theory about the smallest volume of sets (Kakeya sets) that contain a unit line segment in every direction. Per the Chinese Wikipedia article, Hong Wang and Joshua Zahl posted a preprint on arXiv in February 2025 giving a complete proof in three dimensions.

### What awards has Hong Wang received?

Per the Chinese Wikipedia article, she received the 2022 Maryam Mirzakhani New Frontiers Prize, the 2025 Salem Prize and Ostrowski Prize, and the 2026 Clay Research Award, and in July 2026 she was awarded the Fields Medal.
