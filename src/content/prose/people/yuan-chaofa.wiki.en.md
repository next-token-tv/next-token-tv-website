## Chaofa Yuan (袁朝发)

Chaofa Yuan is an LLM (large language model) engineer and technical blogger who has long written under the pen name "chaofa用代码打点酱油" (roughly "chaofa does odd jobs with code") and runs [his personal blog at yuanchaofa.com](https://yuanchaofa.com/). He describes himself as "业余野生视频博主，专业大模型算法工程师" (an amateur video creator by night, a professional LLM algorithm engineer by trade). Per a monthly recap on his blog dated June 2026, articles and videos from then on are signed with his real name, 袁朝发.

## Background and notable work

The following is based on [his blog](https://yuanchaofa.com/):

- Blog topics include agent system design (such as Harness Engineering and a series on prompt caching in agent systems), hand-writing LLM components from scratch (self-attention, RoPE, DPO) and paper readings (such as DeepSeek's MLA and R1, and a technical breakdown of Kimi K2.5).
- Beyond writing, he publishes technical videos on Bilibili and produces the podcast 《逃逸速度》 (Escape Velocity).
- The blog offers RSS subscription and pages such as LLMs-Zero-to-Hero.

No reliable public account of his employer or education is available, so none is given.
