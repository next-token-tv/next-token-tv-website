## Fangbo Tao (陶芳波)

Fangbo Tao is the founder of Mindverse (心识宇宙), working on artificial intelligence and digital selves. The transcript of the show renders his given name as 陶方波, a homophone variant; his public materials write it as 陶芳波.

## Background and notable work

- Career: according to [an event introduction page by Peking University's Intelligent Computing and Sensing Lab](https://ics.pku.edu.cn/xwtz/xxyd/f6244c1e2bf2494d89448455e97eae42.htm), Dr. Fangbo Tao is a data mining and AI specialist who has done research and development at Microsoft Research (US), Facebook Research, NASA and the neural-symbolic lab of Alibaba DAMO Academy; the same page says that at Alibaba he led the building of what it calls the first commercial photonic cognitive computing system on the market.
- Mindverse (心识宇宙): the same page introduces him as founding Mindverse (Mindverse.ai) as founder and CEO in 2022, in a batch of the Hangzhou Future Sci-Tech City overseas high-level talent innovation and entrepreneurship program; [the company site](https://www.mindverse.ai/) describes its mission as "We Create AI as Your Digital Self".
- Second Me: Mindverse's open-source project [mindverse/Second-Me](https://github.com/mindverse/Second-Me) (created in March 2025, Apache-2.0 license) is described as an open-source prototype for training your own AI self: a locally trained and hosted AI avatar that preserves your data and can join a decentralized network to connect with others.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Fangbo Tao?

The founder of Mindverse (心识宇宙); he previously did research and development at Microsoft Research (US), Facebook Research, NASA and Alibaba DAMO Academy's neural-symbolic lab. See [the PKU lab's introduction page](https://ics.pku.edu.cn/xwtz/xxyd/f6244c1e2bf2494d89448455e97eae42.htm).

### What is Second Me?

An open-source "AI self" framework released by Mindverse in 2025: you train and host an AI avatar with your own memories and context locally, and it can connect to others over a network. The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/mindverse/Second-Me).
