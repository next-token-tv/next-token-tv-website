## Positioning

## Career and representative work

"Kuaidao Qingyi" (快刀青衣) is the name he uses publicly, and verifiable public information about him is limited: a September 2026 event news page from the Shanghai University of International Business and Economics introduces him with the titles "得到联合创始人、AI 学习圈主理人" (Dedao co-founder, host of the AI learning circle) in a roundtable panel about bringing AI products into enterprises. Dedao is a knowledge-service platform founded by Luo Zhenyu, whose official site lists platform and course information; the AI learning circle is Dedao's community column around learning AI, with Kuaidao Qingyi as its host. Beyond these, no reliable sources for his personal history or legal name were verified, so this entry records none; the titles come from his introduction at a public event, and this wiki has not verified a personal website or social media account of his.
