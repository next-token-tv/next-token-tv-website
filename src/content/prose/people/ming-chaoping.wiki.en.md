## Positioning

Chaoping Ming (Leon Ming, Chinese name Ming Chaoping) is the founder and CEO of [YouWare](/en/wiki/products/youware), focused on AI creation tools; per an HKUST Entrepreneurship Center event page, his work centers on Renoise, an AI video creation platform.

## Career and representative work

Per the speaker introduction on the [HKUST Entrepreneurship Center event page](https://ec.hkust.edu.hk/events/zhenfund-hkust-startup-talk-building-whats-next-ai-zhengexiaoyuanxing) for a ZhenFund × HKUST startup talk scheduled for 25 November 2026, Chaoping Ming is the founder and CEO of YouWare, "with a current focus on Renoise, an AI-powered platform exploring new ways to create video". He was previously a founding member of ByteDance's CapCut team and held product roles at OnePlus and Moonshot AI, where he worked on Kimi. [YouWare](/en/wiki/products/youware) is an AI agent workspace for research, presentations, reports, spreadsheets, and building websites and applications; [Renoise](/en/wiki/products/renoise-ai) is a video and image creation tool supporting multiple generative models and agent workflows.

## Mention in the show

Chaoping Ming does not appear in the episode quoted on this page. In episode 009's chapter "Harness 与插件生态" (Harness and the plugin ecosystem), Xiangyang Qiaomu says: "你看那个小明他们的 [Renoise](/en/wiki/products/renoise-ai) 就是很聪明，它底层也是 Hyperframes" (Xiao Ming's Renoise is smart — it also builds on Hyperframes), adding that it aggregates the models people cannot be bothered to configure into an MCP ([see the line](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-2f16bb6bca60bb7c0c9f) in the Chinese transcript). Yang Pan adds "所以小明他们这一波，感觉还行" (so this wave from Xiao Ming's team looks fine) ([see the line](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-b1124707e21b6cabf564)), and Orange adds that he thinks "小明他们那个就是做同款的东西嘛。就是让大家去给大家灵感" (Xiao Ming's team makes a similar product — giving people inspiration) ([see the line](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-adcafb397075c8d00fed)). The show's "Xiao Ming" (小明) refers to the team behind Renoise; combined with the HKUST event page's description of Renoise as Chaoping Ming's focus, this points to this entry's subject. The comments on Renoise's product design are the participants' views, not statements by Chaoping Ming.
