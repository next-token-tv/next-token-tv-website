## Positioning

Co-founder and chief executive officer of the smartphone brand [OnePlus](/en/wiki/brands/oneplus), publicly known as Pete Lau, and — per the English Wikipedia article — chief product officer (CPO) at [Oppo](/en/wiki/brands/oppo).

## Career and representative work

Per the English Wikipedia article, Pete Lau was born on May 5, 1975, in Hanchuan, Hubei, China, and graduated from Zhejiang University. He started at Oppo as a hardware engineer, later directed Oppo's Blu-ray division, and rose to vice president; in that period he helped bring the CyanogenMod Android build to the Oppo N1 smartphone. In November 2013, after more than a decade at Oppo, he resigned.

In December 2013 he and Carl Pei co-founded OnePlus. The company's first phone, the OnePlus One, was announced in April 2014 and went on sale online in June 2014, earning praise for its specifications, performance, design, and aggressive pricing; since OnePlus had no factory of its own at the time, the device was manufactured in Oppo's facilities. Per the English Wikipedia article, Lau later also serves as chief product officer at Oppo.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Pete Lau?

Pete Lau (Liu Zuohu, 刘作虎) is the co-founder and CEO of OnePlus; per the English Wikipedia article he also serves as chief product officer at Oppo.

### Who founded OnePlus?

OnePlus was co-founded by Pete Lau and Carl Pei in December 2013; its first phone, the OnePlus One, was announced in April 2014 — see the [English Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pete_Lau).

### What is Pete Lau's relationship with Oppo?

He resigned from Oppo in November 2013 to found OnePlus, whose early phones were manufactured in Oppo's facilities; per the English Wikipedia article he later serves as Oppo's chief product officer.
