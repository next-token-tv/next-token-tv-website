## Liang Wenfeng (梁文锋)

Liang Wenfeng is the founder of [DeepSeek](/en/wiki/brands/deepseek) and a co-founder of the quantitative investment firm High-Flyer (幻方量化). [DeepSeek](/en/wiki/brands/deepseek) started within the High-Flyer group in May 2023; its DeepSeek-V3 (December 2024) and DeepSeek-R1 (January 2025) releases drew worldwide attention.

## Background and notable work

- Early life and education: born in 1985 in Wuchuan, Guangdong (a county-level city administered by Zhanjiang); per the [Zhanjiang government profile](https://www.zhanjiang.gov.cn/zsj/gkmlpt/content/2/2155/post_2155953.html), he ranked among the top gaokao scorers in the Zhanjiang region. He entered Zhejiang University in 2002 at age 17 and earned a bachelor's degree in electronic information engineering in 2007 and a master's in information and communication engineering in 2010.
- Quantitative investing: per his [Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liang_Wenfeng), from 2013 he co-founded investment management companies in Hangzhou with classmates; in February 2016 he co-founded High-Flyer (Ningbo High-Flyer Quantitative Investment Management), and in 2019 founded High-Flyer AI, by which time the fund managed over 10 billion yuan and had invested heavily in GPU clusters for research.
- Founding [DeepSeek](/en/wiki/brands/deepseek): in May 2023 he announced that High-Flyer would pursue artificial general intelligence and launched DeepSeek. Per his [Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liang_Wenfeng), High-Flyer holds 99% of DeepSeek and Liang personally holds 1%.
- Notable releases: [DeepSeek](/en/wiki/brands/deepseek) released DeepSeek-V3 in December 2024 and the reasoning model DeepSeek-R1 in January 2025; the latter drew global attention with markedly lower training and usage costs.
- Public activities in 2025: he attended a symposium hosted by Premier Li Qiang on January 20, 2025, and a symposium with private-sector enterprises at the Great Hall of the People on February 17, 2025; in August 2025 he was named to TIME's 100 Most Influential People in AI and Fortune's 100 Most Powerful People in Business.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Liang Wenfeng?

The founder of [DeepSeek](/en/wiki/brands/deepseek) and a co-founder of the quantitative investment firm High-Flyer.

### What is Liang Wenfeng's educational background?

Zhejiang University: a bachelor's in electronic information engineering (2007) and a master's in information and communication engineering (2010); he enrolled in 2002.

### What is the relationship between High-Flyer and DeepSeek?

Per his [Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liang_Wenfeng), DeepSeek was launched by High-Flyer in May 2023; High-Flyer holds 99% of DeepSeek and Liang personally holds 1%. High-Flyer's quant business revenue and accumulated compute formed an important basis for DeepSeek's early work.

### When were DeepSeek V3 and R1 released?

DeepSeek-V3 in December 2024 and DeepSeek-R1 in January 2025.
