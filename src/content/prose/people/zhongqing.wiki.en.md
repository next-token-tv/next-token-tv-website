## Positioning

A podcast creator and one of the hosts of [Meanders](https://fuyoutiandi.com/) (《蜉蝣天地》), an interview show that he and Hanyang take turns (or co-) hosting.

## Career and representative work

According to [the Meanders website](https://fuyoutiandi.com/), the show is an interview podcast hosted alternately (or jointly) by Hanyang and Zhongqing. Its guests span AI engineering, pharmaceutical research, architecture archaeology, comedy, birdwatching, and other fields, and the show frames each conversation around the question "What did you actually see?" (你到底看到了什么？).

## Mention in the show

Zhongqing does not appear in episode 008. In that episode's chapter "AI 制药：从效率提升到创造增量" ([AI pharma: from efficiency gains to new value](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-06)), Guizang recommends an episode: "这个推荐一个播客，就之前那个「重轻」主持，那个《蜉蝣天地》，他采访了一个做药学的，做医药研制的。那个有个两三个小时，基本上听完你就会对你自己的未来健康非常乐观。" ([a podcast recommendation — an episode of Meanders hosted by Zhongqing, interviewing someone in pharmaceutical research; it runs two or three hours, and after listening you feel very optimistic about your future health.](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-472379e97fac6c093cfb)) Guizang retells the guest's argument: thanks to protein splicing, CRISPR, and AlphaFold, researchers can design arbitrary protein structures and targeted drugs, with phase-3 clinical trials as the bottleneck, and "因为那个是 19 年、20 年成熟的。所以在 30 年左右会有一大波新药上市" (those tools matured in 2019-2020, so a wave of new drugs should arrive around 2030). Guizang himself flags "我可能不准确" (I may have this wrong); the retelling is Guizang's summary of that episode, not the words of Zhongqing or the guest.
