## Sam Altman

Sam Altman is an American entrepreneur and investor who co-founded the artificial intelligence company OpenAI and has served as its chief executive officer (CEO) since 2019. During his tenure as CEO he oversaw the launch of ChatGPT in November 2022, which became one of the most prominent products of the AI boom. See the [OpenAI](/en/wiki/brands/openai) entry for the company and the [GPT](/en/wiki/products/gpt) entry for its model family.

## Career and milestones

- In 2005 he co-founded Loopt, a location-based mobile app, and led it as CEO until the company was acquired by Green Dot Corporation in March 2012. He studied at Stanford University for two years before leaving to work on startups.
- He joined the startup accelerator Y Combinator in 2011 and was its president from February 2014 until 2019.
- He co-founded OpenAI in 2015, then a non-profit research organization, and became its CEO in 2019.
- The landmark moment of his tenure is the release of [ChatGPT](/en/wiki/products/chatgpt) in November 2022.
- In November 2023, OpenAI's board removed him as CEO; after strong pushback from employees and investors he was reinstated within five days, and a new board was formed. OpenAI published its review findings on March 8, 2024, stating the review did not find grounds for removal and that he would rejoin the board as CEO.

These milestones follow the [English Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Altman) and OpenAI's own announcements.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Sam Altman's role at OpenAI?

He has been OpenAI's chief executive officer (CEO) since 2019 and rejoined the board of directors as CEO in March 2024, per [OpenAI's announcement](https://openai.com/index/review-completed-altman-brockman-to-continue-to-lead-openai/).

### Is Sam Altman a founder of OpenAI?

Yes, he is one of the co-founders. He was among the original founders when OpenAI was created in 2015 and became CEO in 2019.

### What did Sam Altman do before OpenAI?

He co-founded Loopt in 2005 (acquired by Green Dot in 2012), joined Y Combinator in 2011, and served as its president from 2014 until 2019.

### Was ChatGPT launched while Sam Altman was CEO?

Yes. ChatGPT launched in November 2022, more than three years into his tenure as CEO.

### What happened with OpenAI's board in 2023?

On November 17, 2023, the board removed Altman as CEO. After employees and investors pushed back, he was reinstated within five days and a new board was formed. OpenAI's review published on March 8, 2024 found his conduct did not mandate removal.
