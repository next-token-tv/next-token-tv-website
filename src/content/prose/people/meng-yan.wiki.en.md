## Positioning

An investment-education creator and one of the founders of [Youzhiyouxing](https://www.youzhiyouxing.cn) (有知有行), who has long written the "Meng Yan Investment Journal" (孟岩投资实证) series on long-term, value-oriented investing.

## Career and representative work

According to [his first-person essay on the Youzhiyouxing site](https://www.youzhiyouxing.cn/materials/181) (published in December 2020), Meng Yan entered Beihang University's computer science program in 1997, joined Sun Microsystems after graduating, and later joined Microsoft. In 2006 he co-founded Caibangzi (财帮子), a community for fund investors to manage their accounts, which grew to roughly one million users. In 2015 he founded Qieman (且慢), a fund advisory service that combined investor education with a "fund following" model; in September 2017 he began publishing the weekly "Meng Yan Investment Journal," recording his real portfolio and investment thinking. In late 2019 he and his partners left Qieman and founded Youzhiyouxing. In the essay he also writes that he began studying investing in 2005 and tracking his own performance from 2009.

Youzhiyouxing is an investment-education brand for general audiences, offering courses such as the Youzhiyouxing Investment Primer alongside related products, with an emphasis on staying invested for the long term.

## Mention in the show

Meng Yan does not appear in episode 008. In that episode's chapter "能力越来越普及，信任为什么仍然重要？" ([as capabilities spread, why trust still matters](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-10)), the hosts bring up Meng Yan twice while discussing brands and trust. Yang Pan says "包括那个「有知有行」。孟岩他们。孟岩对吧？其实也是一样。他们无论就是媒体平台怎么变，对吧？互联网、移动互联网还是 AI，对吧？他的品牌在那儿，是吧？那些人都跟着。" ([Take Youzhiyouxing — Meng Yan and his team. Whatever happens to media platforms — the web, mobile, or AI — the brand is there, and the people follow.](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-68648450e8ed7ba5cd11)) Orange adds "对孟岩他们那个长尾非常猛。他们那个「有知有行」，现在有小一半流量都是来自播客。就是因为建立了信任，所以大家信你这个品牌。" ([their long tail is strong — a sizable share of Youzhiyouxing's traffic now comes from podcasts; because they built trust, people trust the brand.](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-e16acb361a69f6edebd4)) The podcast-traffic share is Orange's statement in the episode and has not been independently verified for this entry. Yang Pan adds that such brands existed in other forms before podcasts, and trust carries over as platforms change.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Meng Yan?

An investment-education creator and one of the founders of Youzhiyouxing; per [his own essay](https://www.youzhiyouxing.cn/materials/181), he previously founded Caibangzi and Qieman and has published the weekly "Meng Yan Investment Journal" since September 2017.

### What is Youzhiyouxing?

An investment-education brand founded by Meng Yan and his partners in late 2019, offering courses such as the Youzhiyouxing Investment Primer and advocating long-term investing; see the [Youzhiyouxing website](https://www.youzhiyouxing.cn).

### What did the hosts say about Meng Yan in episode 008?

While discussing brands and trust, Yang Pan used Meng Yan and Youzhiyouxing as an example of a brand whose audience follows it across platform shifts, and Orange said a sizable share of Youzhiyouxing's traffic comes from podcasts; these are the participants' statements — see "Mention in the show" above.
