## Jensen Huang

Jensen Huang (formally Jen-Hsun Huang; Chinese name 黄仁勋) is the founder, president, and chief executive officer (CEO) of NVIDIA. He co-founded the company in 1993 with Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem and has served as its president, CEO, and a board member since the company began. See the [NVIDIA](/en/wiki/brands/nvidia) brand page for the company and its products.

## Career and milestones

- He worked as a microprocessor designer at Advanced Micro Devices and later as director of CoreWare at LSI Logic, before co-founding NVIDIA on April 5, 1993 with Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem.
- Under his leadership NVIDIA expanded from graphics chips (GPUs) into systems spanning data centers, accelerated computing for artificial intelligence, and a software ecosystem built around CUDA.
- He holds a BSEE from Oregon State University and an MSEE from Stanford University.
- He is a member of the National Academy of Engineering and was appointed to the President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology in 2026. His industry honors include the SIA's Robert N. Noyce Award, the IEEE Founder's Medal, and the Dr. Morris Chang Exemplary Leadership Award.

These details follow [NVIDIA's official bio](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/bios/jensen-huang) and the [English Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nvidia).
