## Haixin (海辛)

Haixin (online handle "海辛Hyacinth") is an AI video producer and digital artist, known for AI-generated moving-image works such as Cats Working in the Forbidden City (《故宫猫猫上班记》) and To Dear Me (《To Dear Me》).

## Background and notable work

The following is based on the episode notes of the interview show Crossing (《十字路口Crossing》, [episode page on Xiaoyuzhou](https://www.xiaoyuzhoufm.com/episode/67cd91020766616acdb292ea)):

- Background: she started as a medical student, moved into film and video production, and worked as a director focusing on new technologies at Shanghai Media Group (SMG), before becoming a leading AI art creator.
- Notable works:
  - Cats Working in the Forbidden City: an AI-generated series featuring cats; per the episode notes it has been viewed over 100 million times.
  - To Dear Me: per the episode notes, winner of the best film in the AI section of the Beijing International Film Festival.
  - Light Lulls Her to Sleep (《枕着光的她》): the AI animated music video for the CCTV Spring Festival Gala of the Year of the Dragon (2024).
- Community involvement: per the episode notes, she co-founded the collectives "整活编辑部" and Demo Inn and advises AI video companies.
