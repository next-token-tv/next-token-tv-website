## Tim (Pan Tianhong, Tim Pan)

Tim, whose real name is Pan Tianhong, is the founder and CEO of Mediastorm (影视飓风), a Chinese video team and channel producing filmmaking, science-communication, and tech content under its operating company Hangzhou Xingao Media. It is one of the most influential video teams in mainland China and, per the Chinese Wikipedia article, was named a Bilibili Top 100 Creator for seven consecutive years from 2019 to 2025.

## Background and notable work

According to the Chinese Wikipedia article, Pan Tianhong was born in 1996 in Hangzhou, Zhejiang. He became interested in digital gadgets in middle school and started making videos in 2014 while in high school, creating the Mediastorm channel on YouTube, Youku, and Bilibili. After graduating from high school in 2015 he studied film at the University of Kent in the UK, and in 2018 he returned to China, set up a studio, and co-founded Hangzhou Xingao Media with Sheng Jiacheng and Yu Zhihao.

Signature projects include sending a camera and notes carrying audience wishes into the stratosphere by hydrogen balloon in late 2019 — the video went viral, the channel passed one million followers, and the team made the Bilibili Top 100 list for the first time; and a 2021 partnership with Insta360 to jointly buy a satellite, launched on a Long March 2D rocket on January 15, 2023, whose wide-angle camera captured panoramic footage of Earth. Beyond the main channel, Hangzhou Xingao Media runs the channels "亿点点不一样", "一步一部 Rolling", and "飓多多 StormCrew", plus a TVC advertising business. In October 2024, in the interview show "扬声" produced by Xinhua Net and Tencent Video, he said Mediastorm's 2024 revenue reached the order of 100 million RMB with profits in the tens of millions.

On October 14, 2026, Pan's father Pan Shuimiao, president of YTO Express, became chairman of Hangzhou Xingao Media, with Pan Tianhong remaining the company's legal representative and largest shareholder (Chinese Wikipedia; 36Kr).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who founded Mediastorm?

Mediastorm (影视飓风) was founded by Pan Tianhong (Tim), who is also the founder and CEO of its operating company Hangzhou Xingao Media, according to the Chinese Wikipedia article.

### What is Tim's educational background?
