## Positioning

Yao Jingang writes about AI marketing, SEO / GEO, content systems and growth. He is the creator of GEOFlow; his [project introduction](https://github.com/yaojingang/GEOFlow/wiki/%E4%BD%9C%E8%80%85%E4%B8%8E%E9%A1%B9%E7%9B%AE) provides his background and project links.

## Career and representative work

The following follows his self-maintained [GEOFlow project page](https://github.com/yaojingang/GEOFlow/wiki/%E4%BD%9C%E8%80%85%E4%B8%8E%E9%A1%B9%E7%9B%AE): he is the author of five marketing books including "AI营销：从SEO到GEO" (AI Marketing: From SEO to GEO), worked in marketing, brand and market roles at Zhonghe Group, TAL Education and Yuanfudao, and founded Liehe Technology, whose AI course platform and tools claim to have served over 10 million users (his own figure, not an independent audit). His [GitHub profile](https://github.com/yaojingang) (handle yaojingang) reads "AI Marketing · GEO · EdTech · Business · AI Entrepreneurship", with the blog laoyao.cn and the same-name X account. His flagship project [GEOFlow](https://github.com/yaojingang/GEOFlow) is an open-source GEO operations system for corporate websites that links trusted knowledge, AI content production, quality gates, human review, multi-site distribution and analytics into one chain, licensed under AGPL-3.0, with its site at [geoflow.me](https://www.geoflow.me).

## Mention in the show

Yao Jingang does not appear in the episode quoted on this page and is referred to as "金刚" (Jin, "King Kong"). In episode 007's chapter "Agent 流量、个人网站与开放接口" (Agent traffic, personal websites and open interfaces), Xiangyang Qiaomu says "金刚他做了一个。流量统计就是包含这些能力的。它加上日志分析，加上页面里嵌入的 JS 访客分析，把它合在一起" (Jin built a traffic statistics tool with exactly these capabilities, combining server log analysis with page-embedded JS visitor analytics) ([see the line](/weekly/007/transcript#quote-af0116525cd7e9cf3cf4) in the Chinese transcript), that it is "基于 Umami 开源来改的" (adapted from the open-source Umami) and may first be used by himself and friends ([see the line](/weekly/007/transcript#quote-7b1f7eddfc944a1032c0)), and that its backend showed "六十多种 AI Agent" (over sixty kinds of AI agents) ([see the line](/weekly/007/transcript#quote-45edd18f2bba2befbde9)). The tool's shape and figures are the host's retelling; no first-hand publication by Yao Jingang was verified. See [episode 007's chapter](/weekly/007/transcript#chapter-04), linked transcript is in Chinese.

## Common confusions

- "金刚" vs. Yao Jingang: the show's nickname refers to this person, not to other public figures called "Jin Gang".
- GEOFlow vs. GEO: the former is his open-source project; the latter is the practice of generative engine optimization that his content covers, and the two are not interchangeable.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Yao Jingang?

An author focused on AI marketing, SEO/GEO and content systems, and creator of the GEOFlow open-source project; see his [self-described project page](https://github.com/yaojingang/GEOFlow/wiki/%E4%BD%9C%E8%80%85%E4%B8%8E%E9%A1%B9%E7%9B%AE).

### What is GEOFlow?

An open-source GEO operations system for corporate websites covering knowledge management, AI content production, quality review, multi-site distribution and analytics, licensed under AGPL-3.0; see the [repository](https://github.com/yaojingang/GEOFlow) and [official site](https://www.geoflow.me).

### What is the "Jin Gang" traffic analytics tool mentioned on the show?

Per Xiangyang Qiaomu's retelling in episode 007: a tool combining server log analysis with page JS visitor analytics to identify AI agents visiting websites, adapted from open-source Umami and planned for personal use first; no first-hand publication verified; see [the chapter in the transcript](/weekly/007/transcript#chapter-04).
