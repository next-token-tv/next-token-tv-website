## Lenny Rachitsky

Lenny Rachitsky is a creator focused on product, growth and careers: he writes [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/) and hosts the interview podcast [Lenny's Podcast](/en/wiki/products/lennys-podcast). In product circles he is usually just called Lenny.

## Background and notable work

- Lenny's Newsletter: per [its about page](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/about), it is a deeply researched advice column about building product, driving growth, and advancing your career, aimed at product leaders, founders and ambitious builders, with a large accompanying Slack community.
- Lenny's Podcast: an interview show in which Rachitsky talks with world-class product leaders and growth experts to uncover concrete, actionable and tactical advice; the show page frames its themes as Product, Career and Growth.
- How I AI: a show in the Lenny’s Podcast Network hosted by Claire Vo, covering practical AI workflows; see the [launch announcement](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/announcing-a-brand-new-podcast-how).


## Mentioned in the show

Rachitsky does not appear in the episodes quoted on this page; the following is a host's relay. In the [cloud computers, your own computers and server management](/weekly/005/transcript#chapter-07) chapter of Weekly #005 ([Chinese transcript](/weekly/005/transcript#chapter-07)), Orange, discussing OpenAI's personal agent [Dots](/en/wiki/products/dots) and its head Thibault Sottiaux, says: "Dots 上了个 Lenny 的播客然后那讲了一下他们的那规划，就说这个 Dots 的未来就是它要管一堆电脑的" ("Dots went on Lenny's podcast and talked about their plans: Dots' future is managing a bunch of computers") (see [that line](/weekly/005/transcript#quote-183c33529c971ee3ac88)).

The matching public episode is [OpenAI's Head of ChatGPT: We're entering a new era of AI (again)](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/openais-head-of-chatgpt-were-entering), published on Lenny's Podcast on October 4, 2026, with Tibo Sottiaux as the guest; the episode page's description of Dots matches the host's relay, but the specific plans about "managing a bunch of computers" are in the episode's paid full text; the episode itself is the authoritative source.
