## Positioning

Tianyi Cui (Chinese name Cui Tianyi) is a member of the DeepSeek Harness team ("DeepSeek Harness 组") at [DeepSeek](/en/wiki/brands/deepseek). He uses his real name on Weibo, where his profile tagline reads "Agent = Model + Harness".

## Career and representative work

His [Weibo account](https://www.sina.cn/media/7542534202) (display name 崔添翼, registered in December 2020) pins a self-introduction that reads: "感谢 @微博AI 邀请入驻微博，我是 DeepSeek Harness 组的崔添翼" — "I am Tianyi Cui of the DeepSeek Harness team". The profile is based in Hong Kong, and his posts cover the open-sourcing and iteration of DeepSeek Harness, [DeepSeek](/en/wiki/brands/deepseek) model releases, and team hiring. Beyond this Weibo account, little public career material is available, so this entry records only the verifiable facts above. [DeepSeek Harness](/en/wiki/products/deepseek-harness) is the open-source harness (agent runtime) released by DeepSeek, and "Agent = Model + Harness" is the framing the team uses for how agents are composed.

## Mention in the show

Tianyi Cui does not appear in the episode quoted on this page. In episode 009's chapter "Harness 与插件生态" (Harness and the plugin ecosystem), Guizang describes how the [DeepSeek Harness](/en/wiki/products/deepseek-harness) team reached out: "其实之前（崔）添翼加我了，但他比较忙" (Tianyi had added me before, but he is rather busy) ([see the line](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-d6c7ef56d581b4718464) in the Chinese transcript). In the chapter "Agent 与个人上下文" (Agents and personal context), Xiangyang Qiaomu retells: "因为添翼说了，它也不是说更强是吧？它只是说这个能组合出不同的 Agent 的" (Tianyi said it is not that it is stronger; it just combines different agents) ([see the line](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-cdcb2ef06db1a46e250b)). Both are the hosts' retellings of his words and actions; he did not take part in the recording, and the retold views have not been confirmed with him.
