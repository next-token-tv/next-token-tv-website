Xiangyang Qiaomu is an AI creator and serial entrepreneur who previously worked as an AI product manager in ByteDance's monetization business. He turns product experience into tool reviews, prompting techniques, and practical workflows, exploring how complex AI capabilities can become useful in everyday work.

He co-authored AI Leadership with Yao Jingang and shares work on generative engine optimization (GEO), AI marketing, and content engineering. Through “向阳乔木推荐看,” his websites, and open-source projects, he emphasizes hands-on testing, reusable methods, and retaining independent judgment when working with AI.
