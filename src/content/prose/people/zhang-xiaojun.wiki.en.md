## Zhang Xiaojun (张小珺)

Zhang Xiaojun is the host of the podcast Zhang Xiaojun Jùn (《张小珺Jùn｜商业访谈录》), a former tech journalist and the founder of the studio "语言即世界" (Language is the World). The show features long conversations of two to seven hours per episode, focusing on people and frontline information in China's tech industry, and is available on [Apple Podcasts](/en/wiki/products/apple-podcasts) and other platforms.

## Background and notable work

- Journalism: according to the show's [official Apple Podcasts description](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/id1634356920), she previously specialized in in-depth reporting on China's tech industry and has won 13 domestic and international journalism awards, including three consecutive Society of Publishers in Asia (SOPA) awards from 2022 to 2024.
- Zhang Xiaojun Jùn: produced by the studio she founded, the show releases weekly episodes of two to seven hours, with guests spanning AI, major tech companies, venture capital and well-known figures.
