## Li Nan

Li Nan is the founder of [Angry Miao](/en/wiki/brands/angry-miao), a hardware brand that designs mechanical keyboards, gaming mice, and other desktop peripherals with a premium, lifestyle-tech positioning. Before that, he was CMO and senior vice president of Meizu and created the Meilan (魅蓝) sub-brand.

## Background and products

At Meizu: according to the Chinese Wikipedia article, Li Nan was appointed CMO and senior vice president on June 20, 2018, in charge of marketing and e-commerce, and announced his departure from Meizu on Weibo on July 18, 2019. A 36Kr report describes him as one of the "three Meizu veterans" and the founder of Meilan, Meizu's youth-oriented sub-brand.

As a founder: the Angry Miao official store says the brand was founded in 2019. The brand's first product was the Cyberboard, a mechanical keyboard with an LED matrix display that built a following among enthusiasts through its industrial design and high price point; the product line later expanded into lifestyle-tech categories including the CYBERBLADE true wireless earphones (released in 2022) and the Infinity Mouse gaming mouse (released in March 2025, the brand's first mouse, known for hot-swappable magnetic batteries). In April 2026 the company followed up with the AM Infinity .97, tuned for Asian hand shapes. The same report quotes his stated approach: startups cannot compete with large companies on technical depth or scale, so they are better off building product identity around subcultures and young consumers.

Li Nan remains active on social media, where he frequently comments on the consumer electronics industry. The Angry Miao team, including Li Nan, has held an ask-me-anything session on the r/angrymiao subreddit.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who founded Angry Miao?

Angry Miao was founded by Li Nan, formerly CMO and senior vice president of Meizu; the Angry Miao official store says the brand was founded in 2019.

### What role did Li Nan hold at Meizu?

Per the Chinese Wikipedia article, he was appointed Meizu CMO and senior vice president in June 2018, in charge of marketing and e-commerce, and announced his departure on Weibo in July 2019; 36Kr describes him as the founder of the Meilan sub-brand.

### What does Angry Miao make?

Premium desktop peripherals and lifestyle-tech products, including the LED-matrix Cyberboard keyboard, CYBERBLADE true wireless earphones, and the Infinity Mouse gaming mouse series; see the [Angry Miao website](https://www.angrymiao.com/).
