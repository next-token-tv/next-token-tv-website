## YURI

YURI (尤栗) is a digital singer created by AI.TALK under Hanqing Studio, also described as a digital human or virtual singer: her appearance, voice and video are generated with AIGC technology and do not correspond to any real performer. AI.TALK is the AI content brand founded by [Hanqing](/en/wiki/people/hanqing) (Zhao Hanqing), and YURI is the AI-native singer he built. The official MV description calls her a "digital singer" and states that the work was produced by AI.TALK, a brand under Hanqing Studio, using AIGC.

## Works and public appearances

- **The MV "SURREAL（超现实）"**: released by AI.TALK on Bilibili on June 3, 2025, under the title "AI音乐MV「SURREAL（超现实）」数字歌手Yuri"; the official description reads "数字歌手Yuri演绎AI原生MV「SURREAL（超现实）」" (the digital singer Yuri performs this AI-native MV). According to the introduction in the Yi Lun AI interview, the MV surpassed 12 million views within a month.
- **Brand singles**: according to the Yi Lun AI interview and videos published by AI Talk, YURI has released singles in collaboration with brands including The North Face and vivo.
- **The "超现实AI音乐会" (Surreal AI concert)**: held in Shanghai in September 2026, co-hosted by the 2026 Inclusion Conference on the Bund and YURI. The [official concert video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4q-eUoeKvRk) describes it as "首场AI歌手商业演唱会" (the first commercial concert headlined by an AI singer), with AI imagery supported by Jimeng AI and real artists such as Fang De Mao (房东的猫) taking part.

Orange says "它已经不用那种剪映这样的工具了，它完全是自己搭的一套基于代码的一套逻辑东西，包括上次那个演唱会，他们要做一个非常炫酷的粒子特效" (the team no longer uses tools like CapCut; they built their own code-based pipeline, including for the dazzling particle effects at the last concert) and "用各种声音模型，它都没有那么好的粒子效果，最后就是用 HTML 搞定的" (no sound model produced particle effects that good; they finally did it with HTML) ([see the lines](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-0ce5a861396faa0d6947) and [here](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-b1f28867f7277f214428)). These are personal observations by the participants Xiangyang Qiaomu, Yang Pan, Guizang and Orange; neither Hanqing nor AI.TALK took part in the recording or commented on the show.

## Common confusions

- YURI vs. "AI idol": YURI is a digital singer created by AI.TALK whose appearance and voice are AIGC-generated and correspond to no real person; "AI idol" is a broader label, and the [Hanqing entry](/en/wiki/people/hanqing) classifies YURI as an AI-native singer.
- YURI vs. Hanqing: YURI is a virtual persona; [Hanqing](/en/wiki/people/hanqing) (Zhao Hanqing) is the real creator behind her, and the two are not the same entity.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is YURI?

A digital singer and virtual persona created by AI.TALK under Hanqing Studio; AI.TALK founder Hanqing (Zhao Hanqing) is her creator, and the official MV description calls her a "digital singer" whose work is produced with AIGC.

### Is YURI a real person?

No. YURI is a virtual singer generated with AIGC technology; there is no real performer behind her, and her appearance, voice and video are all AI-generated.

### When was the YURI MV "SURREAL" released?

AI.TALK released it on Bilibili on June 3, 2025; see the [official video page](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1La7hzCEiy). According to the Yi Lun AI interview, the MV surpassed 12 million views within a month.

### What is the surreal AI concert?

A concert co-hosted by YURI and the 2026 Inclusion Conference on the Bund in Shanghai in September 2026; the official concert video calls it "the first commercial concert headlined by an AI singer", with real artists such as Fang De Mao taking part.

### What does episode 010 say about YURI?

Discussing language models and video generation, the hosts use the Hanqing team's YURI videos as an example: Yang Pan says the latest YURI video was "made purely with APIs", and Orange says the team "built its own code-based pipeline"; see [the chapter in the transcript](/weekly/010/transcript#chapter-14) (Chinese). These are the participants' observations; the Hanqing team did not take part in the recording.
