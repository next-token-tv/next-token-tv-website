## Thibault Sottiaux (Tibo)

Thibault "Tibo" Sottiaux is a Belgian software engineer and product executive at [OpenAI](/en/wiki/brands/openai), where he leads the company's core product and platform organization. He previously led Codex, OpenAI's AI coding agent, and is commonly known as Tibo.

## Background and notable work

The following is based on [his English Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thibault_Sottiaux) and the sources cited there:

- Before joining OpenAI, he spent several years at Google, working on Google Maps and later on machine learning infrastructure for research at Google DeepMind.
- He joined OpenAI in 2024 as an engineering lead for Codex, the agentic coding system the company released in 2025 (see [Codex](/en/wiki/products/codex)).
- In May 2026, OpenAI consolidated its product organization under president Greg Brockman, merging the ChatGPT, Codex and API teams into a single core product group; Sottiaux was given responsibility for that group, covering the API, agent infrastructure, enterprise products, ChatGPT and Codex.
- Among Codex users, he became known for announcing resets of subscription usage quotas.
