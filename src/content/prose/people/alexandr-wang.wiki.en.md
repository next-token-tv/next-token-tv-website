## Alexandr Wang

Alexandr Wang is the Chief AI Officer of [Meta](/en/wiki/brands/meta) and has led the company's Superintelligence Labs since 2025. He was previously known for co-founding the AI data company Scale AI in 2016 and serving as its chief executive officer for years, becoming one of the world's youngest self-made billionaires at age 24 in 2021.

## Career and milestones

According to the [Wikipedia article on Alexandr Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandr_Wang), he was born in January 1997 in Los Alamos, New Mexico, worked at Quora and Addepar, briefly attended the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), and left to start his company. In 2016, he co-founded Scale AI with Lucy Guo; [Scale's site](https://scale.com/about) describes the company's mission as developing "reliable AI systems for the world's most important decisions," providing high-quality training data, annotations, and RLHF for frontier models, with headquarters in San Francisco.

In June 2025, Meta announced an investment of US$14.3 billion for a 49% stake in Scale AI; Wang became Meta's Chief AI Officer and head of the newly formed Superintelligence Labs, stepping down as Scale AI's CEO while remaining on its board (see the [Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandr_Wang)). Francis deSouza subsequently became Scale AI's chief executive officer (see [Scale's site](https://scale.com/about)).
