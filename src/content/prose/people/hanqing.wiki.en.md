## Hanqing (汗青)

Hanqing is the founder of the AI media brand AI.TALK and the creator of Yuri (尤栗), an AI-native singer. He appears under the full name Zhao Hanqing (赵汗青) in public talks and media interviews; the self-description of his YouTube channel reads "I'm HQ, the founder of AI.TALK."

## Identity and work

- **AI.TALK**: The [AI Talk YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCEC4_kBaGfVicaAEPuzfq-A) describes him as the brand's founder, working on IP and interactive storytelling built with AIGC. The interview show《易论AI》introduces its guest the same way: "赵汗青。AI.TALK 创始人，Yuri 的构建者" (Zhao Hanqing, founder of AI.TALK and builder of Yuri).
- **Yuri (尤栗)**: An [AI-native singer created by Hanqing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VVuIoDDKleg). According to the《易论AI》introduction, Yuri's first MV, "SURREAL," surpassed 12 million views within a month; official AI Talk videos show singles made with brands including The North Face and vivo.
- **Surreal AI Concert**: Held in Shanghai in September 2026, jointly presented by the 2026 INCLUSION·Conference on the Bund and YURI. The [official concert video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4q-eUoeKvRk) calls it the first commercial concert featuring AI singers, with AI visuals supported by Jimeng AI and creative collaborations with human artists including Landlord's Cat (房东的猫).

Hanqing has also given a [TEDxPetalingStreet talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ollRs70uHo), "Can AI Creativity Move Us?," credited to Zhao Han Qing.

## Mention in the show

Hanqing did not take part in either of the two recorded episodes. Weekly #002 was recorded in Shanghai on September 12, 2026. In the chapter “从代码到实物：3D 打印带来的创作空间” (From code to physical objects: the creative space opened by 3D printing) of the [Chinese transcript](/weekly/002/transcript#chapter-28), while discussing the pace of embodied AI in the physical world, Xiangyang Qiaomu says "昨天我们都看汗青的音乐会了" ("yesterday we all went to Hanqing's concert"), adding that embodied, physical-world AI still develops rather slowly ([paragraph](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-1d0d7282c49bdffcdc1e)). This is a host describing attending Hanqing's concert, consistent with the public record that the Surreal AI Concert was held in Shanghai in September 2026.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Hanqing?

Hanqing is the founder of the AI media brand AI.TALK and the creator of the AI-native singer Yuri (尤栗); in public talks and interviews he appears under the name Zhao Hanqing (赵汗青).

### Who or what is Yuri?

Yuri (尤栗) is an AI-native singer (AI idol) created by Hanqing. According to the《易论AI》introduction, her first MV "SURREAL" surpassed 12 million views within a month; she has released brand-collaboration singles with The North Face and vivo, and in September 2026 she co-presented the Surreal AI Concert with the Conference on the Bund.

### Did Hanqing appear on Next Token?

No. He did not take part in either of the two recorded episodes; in Weekly #002, Xiangyang Qiaomu mentions that he and the other hosts attended Hanqing's concert, and comments on the pace of embodied AI.
