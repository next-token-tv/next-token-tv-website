## Positioning

A product creator known publicly as kalasoo, active in the VibeCafe and [Vibe Friends](/en/wiki/brands/vibe-friends) AI creator communities and involved in organizing related events.

## Career and representative work

In the [VibeCafe](https://www.vibecafe.cn/) community, Yin Ming posts regularly as 阴明 (@kalasoo), sharing product updates and polls and writing long recaps of the "Vibe Thursday" (疯狂星期四) events, such as edition 68; the community belongs to the same Vibe creator ecosystem as [Vibe Friends](/en/wiki/brands/vibe-friends).

## Mention in the show

Yin Ming does not appear in episode 008. In that episode's chapter "AI 短剧：个人与小团队的创作机会" ([AI short dramas: creative opportunities for individuals and small teams](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-04)), Yang Pan says "昨天那个，你像阴明他们做那个 Vibe Friends，他们做的黑客松就是做游戏。" ([Yesterday — like how Yin Ming and his crew run Vibe Friends: their hackathon was all about making games.](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-33296d41121c944d7e58)) It is Yang Pan's retelling of Yin Ming's community activity, referring to a Vibe Friends hackathon themed around game creation.
