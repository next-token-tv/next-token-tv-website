## Positioning

A historian, philosopher, and bestselling author, best known for Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind, who speaks and writes regularly on technology and society.

## Career and representative work

According to [his official website](https://www.ynharari.com/about/), Yuval Noah Harari was born in Israel in 1976 and received his PhD from the University of Oxford in 2002; he has lectured at the Department of History of the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. His books include Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind (published in 2011, English edition 2014), Homo Deus: A Brief History of Tomorrow (2015), 21 Lessons for the 21st Century (2018), and Nexus: A Brief History of Information Networks from the Stone Age to AI (2024). Per his website, his books have sold 55 million copies in 65 languages. In 2019 he co-founded the social impact company Sapienship with Itzik Yahav.

## Mention in the show

Harari does not appear in episode 008. In that episode's chapter "Agent 会怎样影响人的判断？" ([how agents affect human judgment](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-07)), Orange says "哎，但是那个写人类简史那个，我不是发了一篇文章嘛，赫拉利他就说其实 AI 已经掌握了人类文明的这个秘密，就是人类文明是构建在语言体系上的嘛。然后 AI 其实根本就不需要进入到具身智能里去反对人类，就是它只要影响一下人们的思想，就可以。" ([the author of Sapiens — I shared an article of his; Harari says AI has already grasped the secret of human civilization: civilization is built on language, so AI does not need embodied intelligence to oppose humanity — it only needs to influence how people think.](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-79a6c6cb13927387a096)) It is Orange's retelling of an article by Harari. Guizang and Yang Pan then build on the point, arguing that if everyone uses the same model or a handful of models, slight steering could shift most people's judgments; those extensions are the hosts' own views. The hosts also connect the remark to the Anthropic agent experiment and the GEO discussion earlier in the chapter, again as their own commentary.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Yuval Noah Harari?

A historian, philosopher, and bestselling author best known for Sapiens; per [his website](https://www.ynharari.com/about/) he received his PhD from the University of Oxford in 2002 and has lectured at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.

### Which books has Harari written?

His books include Sapiens, Homo Deus, 21 Lessons for the 21st Century, and Nexus, the last of which examines AI through the history of information networks; see [his website](https://www.ynharari.com/about/).

### How was Harari cited in episode 008?

Orange retells an article by Harari as saying that human civilization is built on language, so AI can influence civilization by shaping human thought without embodied intelligence; the retelling is a participant's summary and the further discussion belongs to the hosts.
