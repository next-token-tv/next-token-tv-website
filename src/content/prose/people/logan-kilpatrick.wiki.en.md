## Logan Kilpatrick

Logan Kilpatrick works at [Google DeepMind](/en/wiki/brands/google-deepmind) as the Product Lead for Google AI Studio and the Gemini API, leading the team dedicated to enabling developers worldwide to build with Gemini, Veo, Imagen and more. This is the role stated on his [official Google author page](https://blog.google/authors/logan-kilpatrick/), whose title reads "Product Lead, Google AI Studio and the Gemini API". His X account is [@OfficialLoganK](https://x.com/OfficialLoganK).

## Related products

- **[Google AI Studio](/en/wiki/products/google-ai-studio)**: a web-based developer tool for experimenting with Gemini models, testing prompts and building AI applications, at [aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com/).
- **Gemini API**: the official developer interface to the [Gemini](/en/wiki/products/gemini) model family, documented at [ai.google.dev](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs). Per his official author page, the team he leads is dedicated to enabling developers worldwide to build with Gemini, Veo, Imagen and more.
