## Tom Huang

Tom Huang is a co-founder of [OpenDesign](/en/wiki/products/open-design), an open-source AI design workspace that uses agents to create and iterate on prototypes, websites, and other design assets. His X handle is [@tuturetom](https://x.com/tuturetom).

## Career and representative work

According to his [founder interview on Okane Land](https://okaneland.com/studio/tom-huang/), before becoming a founder he worked on agent platforms at ByteDance, including Coze and Aily. The interview describes OpenDesign as an open-source, local-first design tool licensed under Apache 2.0, and records his account of how the team builds with AI: humans write the contracts, agents write the implementation, and validation decides what survives, with humans owning contracts, security boundaries, and taste.

A [write-up of an OpenDesign livestream](https://blog.qiaomu.ai/2026-06-06-ckkgvc) published in June 2026 by Xiangyang Qiaomu describes Open Design as a design tool driven by local agents that detects the user's existing coding agents and uses them as the design engine, and says the team treats the number of contributors to the open-source project, rather than stars, as its north-star metric.

## Mention in the show

Tom Huang does not appear in the episode quoted on this page. In episode 010's chapter "持续迭代与开源生态：代码之外还能留下什么" (Continuous iteration and the open-source ecosystem: what can be left behind beyond code), Xiangyang Qiaomu says "就像之前不是问过这个小黄，然后 OpenDesign 嘛？我觉得这个东西好像没有壁垒，我问他说这好像没有壁垒，别人也都可以抄。他说他是因为他开源，吸引了很多提贡献者" (I asked Xiao Huang before, about OpenDesign — it seemed to have no moat; I asked him and he said it was because it is open source, which attracted many contributors) ([see the line](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-b084c5c2a0dd9e856d79) in the Chinese transcript), and continues: "然后形成了一个生态。所以呢，他就不怕别人抄，因为你只能抄我的代码，你抄不了我的生态，抄不了我这个团队花费足够的心力，持续地去迭代它" (an ecosystem formed, so he is not afraid of being copied — you can copy my code, but not my ecosystem, nor the effort my team keeps putting into iterating on it) ([see the line](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-64ec7f37c8a54cd77118)). The transcript only says "Xiao Huang" (小黄) without a full name; the context — the discussion is about OpenDesign — and Xiangyang Qiaomu's blog write-up of an OpenDesign livestream point to this entry's subject. The account of open source attracting contributors and forming an ecosystem is Tom Huang's view as retold by Xiangyang Qiaomu, while "this may be one path" is Xiangyang Qiaomu's own judgment; neither is an official statement by OpenDesign or any other party. See [the chapter in the Chinese transcript](/weekly/010/transcript#chapter-13).
