## Yubo (玉伯)

Yubo is the founder and CEO of [YouMind](/en/wiki/products/youmind), an AI creation tool. According to the guest introduction on episode 74 of the podcast 会友镖局 (Huiyou Biaoju), before founding YouMind he served as vice president of product at [ByteDance](/en/wiki/brands/bytedance) and as product lead for Yuque at Ant Group. He has long worked in product and engineering team management and has built AI products with a small team since founding YouMind.

## Career and milestones

- **Yuque at Ant Group**: he served as product lead for Yuque, a document and knowledge-base product operated by Ant Group.
- **ByteDance**: he served as vice president of product at ByteDance.
- **YouMind**: he founded and leads YouMind. The official site describes the product as "your AI studio for turning inspiration into your own work," covering the collection of articles, videos, and podcasts, AI-assisted understanding and idea connection, and the creation of articles, slides, images, videos, and webpages; it is operated by MIND MOTOR PTE. LTD. and offers iOS, Android, and macOS (beta) apps plus a browser extension (see the [YouMind website](https://youmind.com/)).

Role information above is taken from the podcast page's guest introduction; the page does not list specific years of service.
