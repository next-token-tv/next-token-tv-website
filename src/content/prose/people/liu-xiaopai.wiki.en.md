## Positioning
Liu Xiaopai is an AI product builder and founder of Raphael AI. His [Jike profile](https://m.okjike.com/users/065922A7-2523-4C26-AB2B-DDFF60443E2C) shares product and AI coding experiences, while his [GitHub profile](https://github.com/liuxiaopai-ai) presents open-source development work. The two accounts corroborate each other: his Jike bio reads "Raphael AI 创始人" (Raphael AI founder), and the blog field of his GitHub profile points to raphael.app.

## Career and representative work
Raphael AI: a free AI image generation product at [raphael.app](https://raphael.app/). According to his pinned Jike post, it was the first product he released after starting his company, grew from zero to over one million monthly active users within a month, and was run by him alone (his own account, not an independent audit).

Open source: his GitHub shows projects including Ralph Desktop (a visual controller for AI coding agents that helps brainstorm requirements and execute tasks through persistent iteration) and Raphael Publish (a Markdown typesetting engine for WeChat official accounts).

The "tenfold goal" advice is the hosts' retelling of Liu Xiaopai's public statements, not a statement by him on the show.

## Common confusions

- raphael.app vs. other AI tools named Raphael: the product referenced here is the service at the domain raphael.app.
