## Zhang Yiming (张一鸣)

Zhang Yiming is the founder of [ByteDance](/en/wiki/brands/bytedance), the company behind Toutiao, [Douyin](/en/wiki/products/douyin) and its international version [TikTok](/en/wiki/products/tiktok). He co-founded ByteDance with Liang Rubo in March 2012 and stepped down as CEO and then chairman in 2021, handing both roles to Liang Rubo.

## Background and notable work

- Early life and education: born in 1983 in Longyan, Fujian; he entered Nankai University in 2001, studying microelectronics and software engineering, and graduated in 2005.
- Early career: per his [Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Yiming), in 2006 he became the fifth employee and first engineer at the travel search site Kuxun, promoted to technical director about a year later; in 2008 he worked briefly at Microsoft and then joined the startup Fanfou; in 2009 he founded the real-estate search site 99fang.com, his first company.
- Founding [ByteDance](/en/wiki/brands/bytedance): in March 2012 he and Liang Rubo founded ByteDance in a Beijing apartment; in August 2012 the company launched Toutiao, a personalized news feed app that reached more than 13 million daily users within two years, and in 2014 Sequoia Capital led a 100 million dollar investment.
- [Douyin](/en/wiki/products/douyin) and [TikTok](/en/wiki/products/tiktok): ByteDance launched the short-video app Douyin in 2016 and the international version TikTok in September 2017; in August 2018 it acquired Musical.ly for about 800 million dollars and merged it into TikTok. By late 2018 ByteDance was valued at 75 billion dollars, surpassing Uber.
- Stepping down: he announced his resignation as CEO in May 2021 and completed the handover as chairman on November 4, 2021, with co-founder Liang Rubo succeeding him in both roles. Per Reuters, he retains over 50% of ByteDance's voting rights.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Zhang Yiming?

The founder of [ByteDance](/en/wiki/brands/bytedance), whose most representative products are Toutiao, [Douyin](/en/wiki/products/douyin) and [TikTok](/en/wiki/products/tiktok).

### What does Zhang Yiming do now?

He stepped down as ByteDance's CEO in May 2021 and as chairman in November of the same year, succeeded by Liang Rubo; per Reuters, he retains over 50% of the company's voting rights.

### When was ByteDance founded?

March 2012, founded by Zhang Yiming and Liang Rubo in Beijing.

### What did Zhang Yiming do before ByteDance?

Per his [Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Yiming), he was Kuxun's first engineer (later technical director), worked briefly at Microsoft, joined Fanfou, and founded 99fang.com in 2009.
