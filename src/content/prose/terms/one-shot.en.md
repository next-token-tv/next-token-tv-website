## Definition

In the show’s AI creation and coding discussions, one shot means obtaining a usable result from an initial request rather than through repeated follow-up revisions.

## Usage and distinctions

Success still needs an acceptance criterion: running, meeting requirements and being ready to release are different thresholds. This usage is distinct from one-shot learning, which concerns learning from a single example. Here it describes the interaction workflow.
