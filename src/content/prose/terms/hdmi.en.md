## Definition

HDMI is an interface for carrying digital video and audio between devices such as computers, televisions and displays.

## Usage and distinctions

Available picture formats depend on the source, display and cable together. The presence of an HDMI connector does not establish support for every feature, and an audiovisual interface should not be treated as a general-purpose device power connection.
