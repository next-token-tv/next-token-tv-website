## Definition

Frame generation synthesizes additional images to increase displayed frame rate and perceived motion smoothness.

## Usage and related concepts

Generated display frames do not imply equally frequent game-state updates, nor do they necessarily increase resolution. DLSS includes frame-generation technology, but the general concept is not identical to that product suite.
