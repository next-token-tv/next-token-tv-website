## Definition

Galgame commonly refers to games centered on interactions with female characters, often using dialogue, relationships and branching stories.

## Usage and distinctions

The category overlaps with visual novels but is not synonymous with them or with adult games. The episode invokes the format to describe a character interaction experience, rather than categorizing every AI chat product as a game.
