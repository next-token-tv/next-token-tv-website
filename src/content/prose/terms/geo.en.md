## Definition

Generative engine optimization (GEO) is an emerging practice of adjusting and organizing content so that generative engines are more likely to retrieve, cite and surface it in AI-generated answers. The concept spread from the research paper "GEO: Generative Engine Optimization", published in November 2023.

## How it works

Generative engines retrieve external content and synthesize answers. The paper's experiments found that writing adjustments such as adding citations, authoritative phrasing and statistics were associated with a higher chance of content being included in answers; which tactics work in which products is still being studied and tested in practice.

## Usage

GEO comes up in content operations and AI product discussions — for example, making product docs or review articles easier for AI assistants to cite, or analyzing how one's content appears in AI answers.

## Related and confusable terms

GEO shares goals with search engine optimization ([SEO](/en/wiki/terms/seo)) but targets a different surface: SEO competes for rankings on search results pages, while GEO competes to become a cited source in AI-generated answers.
