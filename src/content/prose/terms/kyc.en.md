## Definition

KYC refers to processes for understanding and verifying a customer’s identity and background, commonly within financial customer due diligence.

## Usage and distinctions

The episode uses KYC to describe a platform identity check. That conversational usage does not mean every account check is a complete financial KYC process. Required evidence and verification steps depend on the service and context.
