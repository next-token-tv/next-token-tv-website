## Definition

Software as a service (SaaS) is a software delivery model in which applications are hosted by the vendor in the cloud and used over the network on a monthly or annual subscription, with no local installation or maintenance. It is one of the main service models of cloud computing.

## Meaning and usage

Because SaaS revenue is subscription-based and recurring by nature, the industry tracks health with metrics such as [ARR](/en/wiki/terms/arr), churn and renewal rates. Multi-tenant architecture, continuous automatic upgrades, and per-seat or usage-based pricing are common traits.

## Usage

Everything from office collaboration suites to customer-relationship management is now widely delivered as SaaS, and consumer subscription software fits the same model. The term also comes up in discussions of cloud transition and the subscription economy.

## Related and confusable terms

Most SaaS is [B2B](/en/wiki/terms/b2b), but there is plenty of consumer-facing [B2C](/en/wiki/terms/b2c) subscription software — the difference lies in the customer type. "SaaS" names a delivery model while "cloud computing" names the underlying resource model, and the two are often used interchangeably in casual talk.
