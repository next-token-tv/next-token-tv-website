## Definition

In AI applications, deep research involves multiple rounds of searching, reading, analysis, and synthesis rather than a single search result.

## Usage and related concepts

It may name a workflow or a product feature. Combining search APIs with models can support such a workflow, but citations still need checking against conclusions; implementations differ between products.
