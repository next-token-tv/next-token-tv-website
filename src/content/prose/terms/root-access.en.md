## Definition

Root commonly denotes the superuser identity on Unix-like systems, with administrative privileges unavailable to ordinary users.

## Usage and related concepts

Modern systems also constrain access with capabilities and sandboxes. Obtaining root, unlocking a bootloader, and installing another system are distinct operations whose availability depends on the device and its security model.
