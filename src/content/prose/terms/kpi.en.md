## Definition

A key performance indicator (KPI) is a quantifiable measure an organization selects to gauge progress toward an important objective — for example monthly active users, customer churn rate or gross margin.

## Meaning and usage

Good KPIs tie directly to strategy, are few in number, and can be measured regularly and objectively. The classic failure modes are tracking a wall of vanity metrics, or gaming the number in ways that distort real behavior — which is why choosing the right indicator matters more than adding more of them.

## Usage

Operating reviews, performance evaluations and growth analyses all assign KPIs to different teams. Status meetings routinely ask how the month's KPIs are tracking.

## Related and confusable terms

KPIs are most often confused with OKRs (objectives and key results): OKR is a goal-management framework, while KPIs are the measurement tools inside it, so the two operate at different levels. A KPI is also not just any statistic — only measures tied to a key objective and reflecting performance earn the name.
