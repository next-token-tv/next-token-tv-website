## Definition

A first-person shooter puts the camera at the player character’s viewpoint and makes aiming and shooting central to play.

## Related concepts

In a genre label, FPS stands for first-person shooter. In graphics performance, FPS can also mean frames per second, a measure of how frequently the display updates.
