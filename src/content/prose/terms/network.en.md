## Definition

A network is a set of computers and devices connected by communication links and protocols to share resources and exchange data. By reach, networks are classified into LANs, MANs and WANs.

## How it works

Devices are linked over wired or wireless media and communicate through a layered protocol stack: IP handles addressing and routing, while TCP and UDP provide end-to-end transport. Switches and routers decide how packets travel from one end to the other.

## Usage

A home Wi-Fi setup is the smallest network; corporate intranets and the interconnection of [servers](/en/wiki/terms/server) in a data center are the everyday objects of network engineering; connect the world's networks together and you get the [internet](/en/wiki/terms/internet).

## Related and confusable terms

A network is any collection of interconnected devices at any scale and topology; the internet is the one particular network that spans the globe; [Wi-Fi](/en/wiki/terms/wi-fi) is only one wireless access technology for a network, not the network itself.
