## Definition

Visual effects (VFX) refers to creating or altering imagery in post-production through compositing, computer-generated imagery (CGI) and related techniques beyond what was captured on set. It is widely used in film, advertising and game cinematics.

## How it works

A typical pipeline includes green-screen keying and compositing, motion tracking, 3D rendering and set extension: live-action footage and computer-generated elements are aligned, lit and blended within the same space to produce a seamless picture.

## Usage

Scenes that are hard or costly to shoot in reality—explosions, creature characters, fantastical cities—are commonly delivered with VFX. It also underpins feature animation and virtual production workflows.

## Related and confusable terms

VFX is often discussed alongside [3D](/en/wiki/terms/3d): 3D computer graphics are one of the core technologies for producing VFX, but VFX also includes keying and compositing methods that never involve solid modeling.
