## Definition

Show notes are text materials published alongside a podcast episode, typically covering key points, guest information, timestamps and related links that help listeners understand, search and revisit the episode.

## How it works

Show notes ship with the episode audio: the web version can be indexed by search engines, and the RSS feed carries links to them; timestamps let listeners jump straight to the segments they care about.

## Usage

Listeners use show notes to find tools, articles and guest profiles mentioned in an episode; creators also rely on them as episode archives for later retrieval and citation.

## Related and confusable terms

Show notes are text material built around a single episode, a different job from [RSS](/en/wiki/terms/rss) as a distribution format: RSS delivers updates to subscribers, while show notes make the episode itself legible.
