## Definition

Agent-to-agent collaboration occurs when multiple agents exchange information, delegate tasks, or combine results. The episode gives the example of travelers’ personal agents collecting preferences and agreeing on meeting times and locations.

## Related concepts

There are several ways to implement agent collaboration. The Agent2Agent Protocol is one option. The travel example describes the behavior without identifying the protocol used.
