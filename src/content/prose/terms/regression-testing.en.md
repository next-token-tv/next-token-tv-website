## Definition

Regression testing checks whether changes to software or its environment have unintentionally affected existing behavior.

## Usage and related concepts

It differs from confirming that a particular defect is fixed. Regression checks may revisit existing login, navigation, and submission flows. Automatically clicking controls is insufficient unless the expected outcomes are also checked.
