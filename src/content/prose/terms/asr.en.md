## Definition

Automatic speech recognition (ASR) is the technology that transcribes spoken audio into text automatically, also commonly called speech-to-text (STT).

## How it works

Earlier systems modeled acoustics and language separately, while modern end-to-end models map audio features directly to text sequences, coping better with casual speech, accents and noise. Word error rate (WER) is the standard accuracy metric.

## Usage

Meeting and interview transcription, video captioning, voice keyboards, voice assistants and customer-service analytics all start with an ASR system.

## Related and confusable terms

[Text-to-speech (TTS)](/en/wiki/terms/tts) runs in the opposite direction: ASR turns speech into text, TTS turns text into speech, and the two are often paired in spoken dialogue systems.
