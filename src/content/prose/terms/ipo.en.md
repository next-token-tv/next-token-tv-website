## Definition

An IPO generally refers to a company’s first sale of shares to the public.

## Usage and distinctions

In the episode, releasing a product before an IPO describes a company milestone. Offering shares and obtaining an exchange listing are related but distinct, and not every route to public trading is an IPO. The term alone does not establish a company’s plans.
