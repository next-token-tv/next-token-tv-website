## Definition

Open source describes software distributed under a license permitting use, inspection, modification, and redistribution of its source code.

## Usage and related concepts

Source visibility alone does not make software open source; rights and obligations depend on its license. Open model weights, open-source software, and a publicly available API are distinct forms of access.
