## Definition

4K describes image resolutions around four thousand pixels wide: consumer UHD commonly uses 3840×2160, while digital cinema also uses 4096×2160.

## Usage and related concepts

Resolution describes pixels in an image, while frame rate describes frames per second. “4K at 120 frames” combines two dimensions; neither figure alone determines overall image quality or experience.
