## Definition

Frame rate is the number of video or animation frames displayed or processed per second, commonly expressed as FPS.

## Usage and related concepts

Frame rate, resolution, and display refresh rate describe different properties. In games, rendered, generated, and displayed frames may differ; a high displayed rate does not guarantee equally frequent game-state or input updates.
