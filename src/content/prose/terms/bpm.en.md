## Definition

Beats per minute (BPM) is a unit for measuring musical tempo—the number of beats occurring in one minute; higher values mean a faster pace. It turns the vague notion of "fast or slow" into a precise number.

## How it works

Given a BPM, the duration of one beat follows directly (60 divided by BPM gives seconds per beat). Metronomes and music software generate a steady pulse from this value so performances and productions stay aligned.

## Usage

BPM appears in score tempo markings, metronomes, DAW projects and DJ sets. Electronic music genres are often described by their characteristic BPM ranges.

## Related and confusable terms

BPM measures tempo, while time signatures such as 4/4 describe how beats are grouped—different dimensions. In music software, BPM works together with [MIDI](/en/wiki/terms/midi) sequences to determine how fast notes play.
