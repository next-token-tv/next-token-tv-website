## Definition

Rendering computes final images from scenes, source media, and effects, producing still images or successive video frames.

## Usage and related concepts

In 3D work, geometry, materials, and lighting determine the image; video work also involves compositing and effects. Producing editing instructions and rendering the resulting frames are different stages with different performance limits.
