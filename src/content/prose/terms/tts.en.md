## Definition

Text-to-speech (TTS), also called speech synthesis, is the technology that converts written text into audible speech output.

## How it works

Classic pipelines normalize the text first (numbers, abbreviations, pronunciations), generate speech features with an acoustic model, and reconstruct the waveform with a vocoder; newer neural TTS systems are largely end-to-end and can clone a voice from a short audio sample.

## Usage

Audiobooks and podcast voiceover, accessibility features like screen reading, voice assistants and support bots, and batch voiceover for games and video are all typical TTS applications.

## Related and confusable terms

[Automatic speech recognition (ASR)](/en/wiki/terms/asr) is the inverse process: TTS generates speech from text, while ASR extracts text from speech.
