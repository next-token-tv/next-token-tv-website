## Definition

A digital certificate associates a public key with information about a subject, supporting authentication through signatures and a chain of trust.

## Usage and related concepts

Root certificates sit at the trust-anchor end of a chain; intermediate and end-entity certificates have different roles. Certificates are normally public, while private keys are not. Certificates, passwords, and private keys are different objects.
