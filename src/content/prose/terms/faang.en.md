## Definition

FAANG groups Facebook, Amazon, Apple, Netflix, and Google using their initial letters.

## Usage and related concepts

The shorthand appears in technology and recruiting discussions; it is not a company or a unified employer. It may persist after corporate names or structures change. Context determines whether it refers to businesses, jobs, or stocks.
