## Definition

The chief technology officer (CTO) is the executive in charge of a company's technology strategy and engineering organization, setting the technical direction and leading the R&D team that builds the product, usually reporting to the CEO.

## Meaning and usage

At an early-stage company the CTO often writes code and sets the architecture personally; as the company grows, the role shifts toward technology choices, team building and cross-department collaboration. The CTO differs from the CIO (chief information officer): the former faces the product and technology capability, the latter more the company's internal systems.

## Usage

The title appears in tech company launches, architecture reviews and engineering job postings. Public statements by a CTO are also read as signals of where a technology company is heading.

## Related and confusable terms

The [CEO](/en/wiki/terms/ceo) runs the whole company while the CTO runs technology — a difference of scope, not of rank, since the CTO sits in the same executive team. At a ten-person startup, the CTO is often simply the technical co-founder.
