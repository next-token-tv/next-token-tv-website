## Definition

Save Time emphasizes reducing the time needed for a task; Kill Time usually means passing time. The episode uses the contrast to discuss utility and entertainment.

## Usage and related concepts

This is not a formal product taxonomy. One activity can save effort while also being enjoyable to spend time on; the experience of vibe coding depends on the user’s purpose.
