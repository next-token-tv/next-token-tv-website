## Definition

RSI describes a loop in which a system improves itself and uses the resulting capability to support further improvement.

## Usage and distinctions

The key question is whether an improvement feeds into the ability to improve again, rather than merely revising an answer. Human-led upgrades, task-level self-correction and stronger recursive claims should be distinguished. Forecasts in a discussion are not evidence that the capability has been achieved.
