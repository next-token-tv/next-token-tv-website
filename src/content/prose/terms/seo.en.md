## Definition

Search engine optimization (SEO) is the practice of improving content quality, technical structure and backlinks so that pages get indexed and ranked better in the organic (non-paid) results of search engines.

## How it works

Search engines decide where pages appear through crawling, indexing and ranking, and SEO addresses each step: publishing content that answers search demand, improving page structure and loading performance, and earning references from other sites. Google's SEO Starter Guide frames the goal as making a site easy for both users and search engines to understand.

## Usage

SEO comes up when planning websites, running content operations or reviewing marketing spend — for example, choosing keywords, tuning page titles and meta descriptions, or analyzing where search traffic changes come from.

## Related and confusable terms

SEO targets organic rankings in search engines. Its counterpart is generative engine optimization ([GEO](/en/wiki/terms/geo)), an emerging concept that instead asks whether content gets cited in AI-generated answers.
