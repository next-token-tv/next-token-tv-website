## Definition

The internet is a global system of computer networks: different networks are interconnected through the TCP/IP protocol suite and forward packets to one another, which is what carries services such as the web, email and streaming.

## How it works

The internet has no single owner; it is built on common open standards. Every device has an IP address, routers forward packets hop by hop between networks, and the domain name system translates memorable names into IP addresses.

## Usage

People use the internet to browse sites, send email and watch streams; for developers, "deploying to production" means making your [server](/en/wiki/terms/server), in an [IDC](/en/wiki/terms/idc) or in the cloud, reachable by the public over the internet.

## Related and confusable terms

The [network](/en/wiki/terms/network) is the concrete system of interconnected machines; the World Wide Web is only one service running on top of the internet; "the cloud" is a way of consuming data center resources reachable over the internet — see [cloud computing](/en/wiki/terms/cloud-computing).
