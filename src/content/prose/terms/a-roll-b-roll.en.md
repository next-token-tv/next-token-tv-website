## Definition

A-roll carries the main narrative, while B-roll supplies supporting or illustrative footage. The labels describe editorial roles, not image quality.

## Usage and distinctions

In an interview, the speaker often carries the story, while product close-ups, surroundings or demonstrations provide supporting images. An editor can keep the interview audio while showing B-roll. These roles depend on the story rather than a fixed rule about faces versus other images.
