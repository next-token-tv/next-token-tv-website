## Definition

A mod is a player- or third-party-made modification of a video game, adjusting its content, mechanics or presentation; both the practice and its output are commonly called mods.

## How it works

Mods use the editors, tools or open interfaces a game provides to change its assets and logic, ranging from adding an item or tweaking numbers to reworking the gameplay or producing a full "total conversion".

## Usage

Modding communities keep games alive: DOTA began as a custom map for Warcraft III, and many developers treat mod tools as part of their creation ecosystem.

## Related and confusable terms

A mod is a modification of a game, while [roguelike](/en/wiki/terms/roguelike) is a game genre—the two are not on the same dimension; when a game engine ships under an open license such as [GPL](/en/wiki/terms/gpl), distributing its mods must follow those terms as well.
