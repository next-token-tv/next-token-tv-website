## Definition

SOTA (state of the art) refers to the best publicly known result or the most advanced method on a given task. Saying a model "achieves SOTA" usually means it matches or beats the best publicly reported score on recognized benchmarks.

## How it works

SOTA standing is measured by published scores on authoritative benchmarks and is refreshed as new models ship. It is a relative, time-varying label rather than a fixed title, so claims should be read together with the specific benchmark and test conditions they refer to.

## Usage

Papers, model release pages and technical press use SOTA to signal leadership; in engineering it offers a quick sense of where a method stands within its class.

## Related and confusable terms

A [benchmark](/en/wiki/terms/benchmark) is the measuring instrument; SOTA is the ranking the measurement produces. Leading on a specific benchmark does not mean a model is best on every task or in every real-world setting.
