## Definition

A smart home uses connected devices, sensors, and control systems for remote operation, status feedback, or household automation.

## Usage and related concepts

Lights, outlets, speakers, and appliances can react to shared conditions. Interoperability standards help devices work together, but capabilities depend on device support, connectivity, and control methods. Smart-home automation does not always require an AI model.
