## Definition

Business-to-business (B2B) describes companies that sell products or services to other companies or organizations rather than to individual consumers — think enterprise software, cloud services and industrial goods.

## Meaning and usage

B2B transactions typically involve long decision chains and high deal sizes, lean on dedicated sales teams and multi-year contracts, and are judged by buyers on return on investment, reliability and support. Related labels such as B2C and B2B2C describe businesses aimed at other customer types.

## Usage

SaaS, cloud computing and professional services are largely B2B industries. Conversations about customer acquisition cost, sales cycles and enterprise contracts usually start by clarifying whether the business is B2B.

## Related and confusable terms

B2B is the counterpart of [B2C](/en/wiki/terms/b2c), which sells directly to individual consumers and leans on brand and conversion. Many [SaaS](/en/wiki/terms/saas) companies run B2B and B2C motions side by side, and the playbooks differ.
