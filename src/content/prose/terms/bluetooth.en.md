## Definition

Bluetooth is a family of wireless connectivity technologies used for audio, device data exchange, and other communication scenarios.

## Usage and related concepts

Bluetooth Classic and Bluetooth LE address different needs. Bluetooth support does not imply support for every audio or data function: capabilities depend on implementation and profiles, independently of Wi-Fi support.
