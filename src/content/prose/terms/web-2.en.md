## Definition

Web 2.0 is a concept popularized by O'Reilly Media around 2004 for the phase of the web marked by user-generated content, participatory communities, and websites acting as platforms.

## How it works

Pages turned from static documents into read-and-write platforms: users publish on blogs, wikis and social networks, sites expose [APIs](/en/wiki/terms/api) for third parties to combine data, and [RSS](/en/wiki/terms/rss) feeds and tags let information be aggregated and reorganized.

## Usage

Products driven by participation—blogs, Wikipedia, social networks and video communities—are all built on the Web 2.0 idea.

## Related and confusable terms

Web 2.0 describes a shift in how the web is used and what products look like, not a specific technology; open [APIs](/en/wiki/terms/api) were one of its common implementation tools, but one names an era and an idea while the other names an interface technique.
