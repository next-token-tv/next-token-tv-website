## Definition

An aha moment is a sudden realization or experience of a product’s value: the point at which its usefulness becomes clear.

## Usage and distinctions

Teams can examine actions associated with users discovering value. An action’s correlation with retention does not establish causation, and an impressive demonstration is not the same as sustained usefulness.
