## Definition

RSS is an XML-based format for syndicating web content: a site organizes titles, summaries and links into a feed file, and subscribers poll that file with a feed reader to pick up updates. The RSS 2.0 specification is maintained by the RSS Advisory Board.

## How it works

A site publishes an RSS file at a fixed URL and updates it as content changes; the reader requests the file at a set interval, notices new entries and presents them. The whole process is pulled by the user and does not depend on platform recommendation algorithms.

## Usage

RSS is used to subscribe to blogs, news and podcasts. Podcast distribution is built on RSS: episode audio and links to [show notes](/en/wiki/terms/show-notes) reach every podcast client through the feed.

## Related and confusable terms

RSS and Atom are two coexisting syndication formats: Atom was standardized by the IETF in RFC 4287, the two are semantically close, and most feed readers support both.
