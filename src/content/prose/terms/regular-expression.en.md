## Definition

A regular expression describes a text-matching pattern for searching, extracting, replacing, or validating strings.

## Usage and related concepts

Patterns can specify character sets, repetition, and positions. Syntax varies between implementations. Regular expressions work well for structured text operations; they are not a substitute for understanding natural-language meaning.
