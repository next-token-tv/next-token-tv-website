## Definition

Infrastructure is the combined foundation of hardware, software and services that supports applications and business operations — compute, storage, networking, facilities and the operational practices around them. In speech and in technical writing it is often shortened to infra.

## How it works

Infrastructure work means keeping [servers](/en/wiki/terms/server), storage and the [network](/en/wiki/terms/network) available in a predictable way: procurement and racking, configuration management, monitoring and alerting, capacity planning. After the cloud arrived, part of this work moved into consoles and code.

## Usage

"Working on infrastructure" can mean building your own facilities and networks, or defining resources on [cloud computing](/en/wiki/terms/cloud-computing) platforms with tools like Terraform; in AI companies, infrastructure often refers specifically to standing up and maintaining GPU clusters and training platforms.

## Related and confusable terms

Infrastructure is the umbrella term for the underlying resources and systems; an [IDC](/en/wiki/terms/idc) is its physical-facility part; a [neocloud](/en/wiki/terms/neocloud) is a class of provider that packages GPU infrastructure and rents it out.
