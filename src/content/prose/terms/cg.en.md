## Definition

Computer graphics (CG) covers techniques for creating, manipulating, and displaying two- and three-dimensional graphics with computers.

## Usage

Animated films and games use CG to create characters, environments, and motion. A 3D production typically models and animates a scene before rendering images from its materials, lights, and camera.

## Related concepts

CGI (computer-generated imagery) refers to the imagery itself. CG covers the broader field. [VFX](/en/wiki/terms/vfx) can use CG assets and also includes techniques such as tracking and compositing live-action footage.
