## Definition

Embodied AI refers to AI agents that perceive, act and learn through a physical body interacting directly with their environment — the idea that intelligence emerges from a perceive-decide-act loop rather than from disembodied computation alone.

## How it works

A typical embodied system closes the loop between sensing (vision, touch and other modalities), decision-making (often involving large multimodal models) and execution (robot arms, legged platforms, dexterous hands), trained and deployed in simulation or the real world.

## Usage

Industrial and household robotics and warehouse automation are the main application areas; a large body of research trains skills in simulation first and then transfers them to real robots.

## Related and confusable terms

Contrast with [computer use](/en/wiki/terms/computer-use): computer use gives a model a "body" in digital interfaces, while embodied AI centers on physical embodiment; both share the perceive-decide-act loop.
