From Dots and Instinct to games, software rewrites and small hardware: four co-hosts share hands-on experiences with personal agents, memory, permissions, open ecosystems and multi-model workflows.

Recorded on October 7, 2026, at Wenyu River Park in Beijing. The conversation covers personal agents as assistants or digital counterparts; email, WeChat and RSS; building games and replacing editor internals; software distribution and open-source licensing; ESP32 and voice-input hardware; and multi-model workflows.
