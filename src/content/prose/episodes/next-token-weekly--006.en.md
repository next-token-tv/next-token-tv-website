Recorded in Beijing on October 16, 2026, with Xiangyang Qiaomu, Guizang, Orange and Yang Pan.

## Discussion

The conversation starts with AI reverse engineering and writing a macOS mouse driver, then explores phishing, permissions and closed app ecosystems. Hands-on stories about Muse, Grok Bot and Dots lead into multi-agent collaboration, skills, memory, AI video and the attention economy.

How much work do personal agents actually complete? What remains valuable when software and media become easier to produce?
