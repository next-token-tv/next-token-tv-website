## 🎧 Overview

A single HTML file can hold more than 20 3D models, along with animations, textures, and a soundtrack. At 8 MB, it can even run on a phone. Guizang shares what happened when he used Fable 5.2 for a game and an interactive ink painting. Scenes and camera angles came together quickly. Making combat feel right took more rounds of feedback. As AI raises the starting point for creative work, what does good judgment look like? What can creators build on once the work itself becomes easier to copy?

The episode opens with Jensen Huang’s departure as NVIDIA’s president, CEO, and board member. The four hosts share memories of NVIDIA and discuss what its leadership transition could mean for AI compute and gaming GPUs. They then consider how language connects games and robotics, compare their experiences with Gemini 4 Carbon, and revisit the instructions they give models as those models become more capable.

A trip planned with Instinct brings the conversation to personal agents. An agent can remember preferences, send timely reminders, or help coordinate a shared itinerary. Each useful action can earn more trust and access, which raises questions about accounts and sensitive data. The hosts close with Seedream 5.5, Jimeng’s creative tools, recent purchases, and how Next Token Skill finds passages from the show.

## 💡 Questions from the episode

- **Where does a designer’s judgment show up?** Given ten plausible options, the ability to select one and explain why it works still shapes the product.
- **What can creators build when copying becomes easier?** Obsidian and OpenDesign lead into a discussion of continued iteration, audience trust, distribution, and open-source contributors.
- **How much access should a personal agent get?** Shared maps and travel reminders lead to questions about account access, sensitive information, and when a product should ask for permission.
- **When should an image model preserve details or try something new?** The hosts discuss visual consistency for commercial images and the room they want models to explore when testing a new style.
