What new interactions could iPhone Duo and a foldable screen enable? Meanwhile, Astra can operate a computer and use tools to complete tasks. Does that count as AGI?

Starting with hands-on GPT-6 Astra experiments in 3D modeling and Next Token's podcast production workflow, the conversation explores the distance between completing tasks and general intelligence. DeepSeek Flash and Harness raise another question: as models become faster and more capable, do testing, delivery and human judgment become the bottlenecks?

The second half turns to foldable interfaces, consistent design standards, watches, personal context, music and image editing, voice input, and whether models really become less capable over time.

## In this episode

- Does using a computer and completing real tasks count as AGI?
- Where do human judgment and delivery still matter?
- Why do agents need an always-on computer?
- What interaction problems should foldable screens solve?
- How should personal context and hardware respect privacy?

Recorded at AGI Bar in Shanghai on September 12, 2026. Xiangyang Qiaomu hosts, joined by Guizang, Orange, Yangpan and guests 南乔 and 赛博禅心. Experiences, opinions and predictions reflect the participants' views, not independent verification of product capabilities. Consult official sources for current features and availability.
