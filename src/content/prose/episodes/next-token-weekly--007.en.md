## 🎧 About this episode

DeepSeek answers fast enough to change the experience of waiting for a response. Search APIs bring Chinese information into personal agents. Browser games become more complete, and an old TV gets a new launcher. Starting from these hands-on experiences, we discuss how AI is changing software, content and the devices around us.

From console-game ports and custom editing tools to Codex and Grok Bot in the living room, there is more we can build ourselves. Alongside that freedom come questions about vulnerabilities, certificates and API keys: how should individuals and teams respond?

In the second half, we ask whether vibe coding saves time or makes creating things enjoyable in its own right. Transcribing hundreds of podcasts, catching missed Slack discussions, organizing WeChat contacts and connecting a voice device to a personal agent all lead to a bigger question: with enough intelligence and tokens, what would you try?

## 💡 Questions to take away

**Can speed change what we expect from software?**

The discussion moves from inference speed to search and reading documents aloud. Shorter waits may change whether people need to think about the steps behind a tool at all.

**Why is the opportunity in AI games bigger than games that use AI at runtime?**

PokeBonk, console ports and browser distribution suggest a different cost structure: use intelligence to create something that people can enjoy repeatedly. Taste, experience and distribution still matter.

**Can security keep pace with lower barriers to building?**

The speakers discuss reported vulnerabilities and leaked credentials, then return to everyday habits: managing passwords, recovering them after changing devices and deciding what permissions to give an agent.

**Is vibe coding work, or a new kind of creative game?**

Buying tokens can feel like spending on a game. The conversation considers the pleasure of building, alongside the value of asking good questions, developing judgment and working with people.

**What would you do with enough intelligence and tokens?**

The practical examples encourage a bigger starting point: describe the result you want and give the agent room to complete it.

## 🧩 Concepts

- **Token:** A basic unit of model input and output; discussed in relation to speed, usage and production costs.
- **API:** An interface that lets software call a capability, such as search.
- **Personal agent:** An AI assistant using personal context, memory and tools to act on tasks.
- **Harness:** The surrounding system for tool calls, task loops and context management.
- **Vibe coding:** Building software through repeated natural-language collaboration with AI.
- **Skill:** Reusable task instructions and resources for an agent, potentially including scripts and assets.
- **Save time / kill time:** Two experiences discussed in the episode: efficiency and enjoying the activity itself.
