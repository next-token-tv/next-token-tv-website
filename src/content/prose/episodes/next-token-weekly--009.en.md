DeepSeek Harness | Obsidian | Personal agents | AI security | Steam | Kindle | M5Stack

## In this episode

From DeepSeek Harness and its plugin culture to personal agents, AI security and game distribution, the four hosts discuss what changes when more people can build software—and how useful work finds an audience.

Plugins give users more ways to adapt an agent to their own habits. The discussion moves from migrating Obsidian plugins to playful extensions, memes and communities outside the usual technology circles. Openness creates room for experimentation while leaving discovery and moderation as ongoing challenges.

The hosts also explore personal agents that delegate tasks across tools, and the value of retaining context, preferences and finished work. They discuss model efficiency, data-center costs and AI security before turning to games: making a working product is only one step toward making something enjoyable and helping people find it.

## Topics

- DeepSeek Harness, plugins and community-led creativity
- Personal agents, task routing and persistent context
- Model efficiency, infrastructure costs and security
- Game development, discovery and distribution
- Personalized tools, audio workflows and hands-on experiments
- iPad mini, second-hand Kindle devices and programmable hardware
