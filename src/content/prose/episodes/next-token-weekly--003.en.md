What happens when a model does not chat, but makes the same small decision over and over? And why does working with agents suddenly make an ultrawide monitor sound tempting?

We begin with Jev: filtering memories, tagging contacts, semantic queries and spotting advertising. These are workflows built around frequent judgments rather than conversation. A key distinction runs through the discussion: matching an output type does not guarantee a correct decision.

We then turn to multimodal tools, local models and creative work: using GPT-6 Astra to help produce training data, making promotional videos and music with code, and the very practical problem of projects filling up a hard drive.

Browsers, chats and task lists compete for screen space. From that everyday friction, we explore voice input, multiple accounts, persistent computers, cloud Macs and enterprise data boundaries. Better capabilities do not remove the need for a workable environment and human oversight.

Recorded online via Tencent Meeting on September 19, 2026. Experiences, opinions and predictions reflect the participants’ views, not independent verification of product capabilities.

## Questions from the episode

- Where do high-frequency decisions fit better than chat?
- Why are structured output, accuracy and “no hallucinations” different claims?
- How do small models and multimodal tools fit into creative work?
- Do agents make an ultrawide necessary—and what about storage?
- What boundaries should govern a persistent agent’s accounts and data?
