## What Douyin is

Douyin is the short-video and livestreaming platform operated by [ByteDance](/en/wiki/brands/bytedance), launched in China in September 2016. ByteDance's official [company page](https://www.bytedance.com/en/) lists Douyin among its China-market products and records that it "launches in China and quickly becomes a popular short-video sharing platform". The same company's short-video product for international markets is [TikTok](/en/wiki/products/tiktok); the two run separately for different markets.

## What it does and where it stops

Douyin's core is watching and publishing short videos plus livestreaming, with an interaction loop among creators, viewers, and live sessions. According to the Chinese Wikipedia [article on Douyin](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%8A%96%E9%9F%B3), the platform started as a music-oriented short-video community, and in March 2020 it introduced group buying, bringing local merchants' deals in restaurants, hotels, and retail into livestreams and short videos. ByteDance's official materials also list Douyin E-commerce as its own line, showing that commerce has become a core part of the platform.

Douyin mainly serves the mainland China market; account registration, content rules, and the conditions for local-life services follow the [official site](https://www.douyin.com/) and in-app terms. Content- and commerce-related features change with regulation and product strategy, so availability should not be inferred from the product's name alone.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is Douyin's official website?

The official site is [douyin.com](https://www.douyin.com/) for browsing on the web. For the app, use the official site or the official publisher listing in each app store rather than third-party download sites.

### When did Douyin launch?

Douyin launched in China in September 2016. ByteDance's official [company page](https://www.bytedance.com/en/) records the timing, and the Chinese Wikipedia [article](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%8A%96%E9%9F%B3) gives September 20, 2016 as the launch date.

### How do Douyin and TikTok relate?

Both come from ByteDance but serve different markets: ByteDance's site lists Douyin as a China-market product, while TikTok is its short-video product for international markets. Features, content ecosystems, and operating rules do not carry over directly between them.

### Can you shop on Douyin?

Yes. The Chinese Wikipedia [article](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%8A%96%E9%9F%B3) records that Douyin introduced group buying in March 2020, integrating local merchants' deals into livestreams and short videos, and ByteDance's official materials list Douyin E-commerce separately. For current entry points and rules, check the app and the [official site](https://www.douyin.com/).
