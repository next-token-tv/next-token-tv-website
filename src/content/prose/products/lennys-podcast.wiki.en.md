## What Lenny’s Podcast is

Lenny’s Podcast is an English interview podcast hosted by [Lenny Rachitsky](/en/wiki/people/lenny-rachitsky), titled in full "Lenny’s Podcast: Product | Career | Growth". Each episode is a conversation with product leaders, founders and growth experts, distilled into actionable advice on product, careers and growth. The show lives on the same Substack account as Lenny’s Newsletter, and its [show page](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/podcast) sits under the newsletter’s domain. The [Apple Podcasts listing](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/lennys-podcast-product-career-growth/id1627920305) credits Lenny Rachitsky as the artist and categorizes the show under Technology, Business and Entrepreneurship.

## Where to listen and related shows

- Podcast apps: the show is subscribable in Apple Podcasts and other major clients; the RSS feed is served by Substack.
- Official pages: the show page lists episodes and guests one by one, and each episode page carries a description.
- Other shows by the same host: Lenny Rachitsky also hosts How I AI, focused on how people use AI tools (see [his site navigation](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/about)).
- Relationship to the newsletter: Lenny’s Podcast and Lenny’s Newsletter are created by the same person. The Newsletter is an advice publication on product and growth with free and paid subscriptions; see [the site](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/).

## FAQ

### What kind of show is Lenny’s Podcast?

An English interview podcast hosted by Lenny Rachitsky covering Product, Career and Growth, with guests drawn from product leadership, founders and growth experts; see the [show page](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/podcast).

### Where can I listen to Lenny’s Podcast?

For free on podcast clients such as Apple Podcasts, or episode by episode on the [official show page](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/podcast).

### Does Lenny’s Podcast cost money?

The podcast itself is free on podcast clients. The newsletter on the same account, Lenny’s Newsletter, uses free and paid subscriptions, and some content is reserved for paying subscribers; see [the site](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/).

### Who has been on Lenny’s Podcast?

Guests are mostly product and management leaders, such as OpenAI’s Tibo Sottiaux on the October 4, 2026 episode. The full episode and guest list is on the [official show page](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/podcast).
