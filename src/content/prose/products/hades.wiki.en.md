## Hades

Hades is an action role-playing roguelike developed and published by Supergiant Games. The player controls Zagreus, son of Hades, in repeated attempts to escape the Greek underworld and reach Mount Olympus, receiving help from the gods along the way. The game entered early access in December 2018 and reached version 1.0 on September 17, 2020, launching on macOS, Windows and Nintendo Switch (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hades_%28video_game%29)). The official page is [supergiantgames.com/games/hades](https://www.supergiantgames.com/games/hades/).

## Release timeline

- December 2018: early access begins
- September 17, 2020: version 1.0 on macOS, Windows and Nintendo Switch
- August 2021: PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox One and Xbox Series X/S
- March 2024: iOS through Netflix Games

(Timeline per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hades_%28video_game%29).)

## Gameplay and subject matter

Set in the Greek underworld, each escape attempt runs through a series of procedurally arranged rooms where players fight with weapon attacks, dashes and spells. After a death, the run restarts — and resources earned along the way upgrade Zagreus, unlock new weapons and abilities, and improve the odds for the next attempt. The story follows Zagreus and his relationships with the gods of the underworld; combat, story progression and character growth are woven into each loop.
