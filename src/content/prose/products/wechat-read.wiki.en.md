## What WeRead is

WeRead (微信读书) is an e-reading app operated by [Tencent](/en/wiki/brands/tencent). The official App Store introduction describes it as “WeChat’s official reading app,” offering a large catalog of quality books along with reading data and note-taking; its iOS version went live on the App Store on August 27, 2015. The official introduction lists licensed books with rankings and curated lists, in-book discussions with other readers, audiobook features (full-text read-aloud, book summaries, and audiobooks), and reading statistics.

Its connection to WeChat is that both belong to Tencent and share the WeChat account system; the WeChat platform itself (messaging, official accounts, mini programs) is covered in the [WeChat](/en/wiki/products/wechat) entry, and the company’s input tool in the [WeChat Input Method](/en/wiki/products/wechat-input-method) entry.

## Usage and entry points

Most readers use the mobile app; the official web version is at [weread.qq.com](https://weread.qq.com/), with the bookstore, rankings, and a web reader, plus device-to-device features such as sending books to the phone. Membership, payments, and audiobook entitlements follow what is shown in the app and on official channels.

Shelves, notes, and reading data are personal account data: no public official entry point exists for bulk-exporting whole books as epub/pdf. When automating access to your own account data, follow the user agreement and avoid handing account credentials to untrusted third-party tools.

Weekly #006 devoted a chapter to this: “整理几百本微信读书藏书：这叫“假提效”，还是收纳的快乐？” (Organizing hundreds of WeRead books — fake productivity or the joy of tidying?). Yang Pan said he [put all the books from his WeRead shelf onto a new channel on his personal website](/weekly/006/transcript#quote-d3c4655c73b44780898f). Xiangyang Qiaomu asked why he did not [use the WeRead CLI directly](/weekly/006/transcript#quote-b115d57b3c73fe45982c), Yang Pan answered [that it “can only read, not write”](/weekly/006/transcript#quote-41b7aa025fc1e12e1fef), and Guizang [agreed](/weekly/006/transcript#quote-c2b87ba6afd5911c45c9). Yang Pan then described the full workflow: as a paid annual member he had an agent pull the several hundred books on his shelf and categorize them, and after confirming the categories [used Computer Use on his Mac to operate the WeRead app and re-shelve every book accordingly](/weekly/006/transcript#quote-d4b289d3f6c4c0251474). Orange’s verdict was [that this was “classic fake productivity”](/weekly/006/transcript#quote-070fdce3a7b5b658d694), and he later called the exercise mere tidying with no ROI — a disagreement about whether agent-driven shelf organizing is worth it, not a judgment of the product’s features. ## Frequently asked questions

### Where is the WeRead web version?

The official web version is [weread.qq.com](https://weread.qq.com/) — sign in to browse the bookstore and rankings and use the web reader; the mobile app offers the full feature set.

### Which platforms does WeRead support?

Confirmed official entry points are the mobile app (the iOS version went live on the App Store in August 2015) and the web version at [weread.qq.com](https://weread.qq.com/). For other clients, rely on the official download channels.

### Can I export WeRead notes and books?

No public official entry point exists for bulk-exporting whole books as epub/pdf. In the show, Yang Pan’s approach was to have an agent read the shelf data and categorize it, then use Computer Use to operate the app; third-party export tools are unofficial and carry account-security and terms-of-service risks to weigh yourself.

### How much does WeRead cost?

WeRead offers memberships and other paid entitlements, and audiobook or book availability varies with membership status; current prices and entitlements follow what is shown in the app and on official channels.

### What is the relationship between WeRead and WeChat?

WeRead is a reading app under Tencent, described in the official introduction as “WeChat’s official reading app,” using the WeChat account system. For the WeChat platform itself, see the [WeChat](/en/wiki/products/wechat) entry.
