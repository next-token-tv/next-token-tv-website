## What is Instagram

Instagram is Meta's social platform centered on photo and video sharing and social interaction. The official page frames its purpose as "Bringing you closer to the people and things you love," with an emphasis on expression, creation, and a safe community ([official overview page](https://www.meta.com/instagram/)). For the company's business, AI, and hardware, see the [Meta entry](/en/wiki/brands/meta).

## Founding and acquisition

Instagram was created by Kevin Systrom and Mike Krieger and launched on iOS on October 6, 2010; the Android version followed on April 3, 2012. On April 9, 2012, Facebook announced it would acquire Instagram for approximately one billion dollars in cash and stock, and the deal closed on September 6, 2012 ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram)). Since then Instagram has sat alongside [Facebook](/en/wiki/products/facebook), WhatsApp, Messenger, and Threads in Meta's social-app product line.

## Usage and boundaries

Instagram's core is posting photos and videos, browsing other people's content, and interacting; specific feature sections and naming change with versions and regions. Resources for creators and businesses (creator tools, business accounts, verification subscriptions, and more) are reachable from the entries on the [official overview page](https://www.meta.com/instagram/); for day-to-day questions, refer to the [Instagram Help Center](https://help.instagram.com/). Compared with [Threads](/en/wiki/products/threads), which centers on public text conversations, Instagram's content is image- and video-centric.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is Instagram's official website?

The site is [instagram.com](https://www.instagram.com/), the product overview is at [meta.com/instagram](https://www.meta.com/instagram/), and help documentation is at [help.instagram.com](https://help.instagram.com/).

### What kind of platform is Instagram?

Instagram is Meta's social platform centered on photo and video sharing, launched on October 6, 2010 ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram)).

### Which company owns Instagram?

Facebook acquired Instagram in 2012, and it now belongs to Meta Platforms; Facebook, Inc. was renamed Meta on October 28, 2021 ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram)).

### How do Instagram and Threads differ?

Both are Meta's social apps: Instagram centers on photo and video sharing, while Threads centers on text sharing and public conversations. See the [Threads entry](/en/wiki/products/threads) for its positioning.
