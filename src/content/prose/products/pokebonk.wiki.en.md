## Definition

PokéBonk is an unofficial browser-based survivor game that uses Pokémon characters; opening [pokebonk.com](https://pokebonk.com/) is enough to play in the browser, and the site offers a multilingual interface including English. It is a fan work with no connection to Nintendo or The Pokémon Company.

## Usage

As a pure browser game, it is playable instantly on desktop or mobile browsers, with no client to install and no account required; pages and saves are handled in the browser. The experience depends on the actual device.

## Gameplay

As described in episode 007, PokéBonk plays like a Vampire Survivors-style survivor game: characters fire skills automatically while the player steers them to dodge and defeat ever-larger monster hordes, gaining numeric growth through levels and items. Specific mechanics are best checked in the game itself.

## Related and confusable terms

PokéBonk is not an official [Pokémon](/en/wiki/products/pokemon-games) game and is not a licensed product. It is likewise unrelated to Vampire Survivors, the best-known representative of the survivor genre — the similarity is one of gameplay type only.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this an official Pokémon game?

No. PokéBonk is an unofficial fan game; its use of Pokémon characters does not imply an official release or partnership.

### Where can I play PokéBonk?

Open the official site, [pokebonk.com](https://pokebonk.com/), and play in the browser; both desktop and mobile browsers work, with no installation or login.

### What kind of game is PokéBonk?

It is a browser-based survivor game: characters attack automatically while the player handles positioning, growing stronger through levels and items.
