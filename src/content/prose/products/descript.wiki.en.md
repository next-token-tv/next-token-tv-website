## What Descript is

Descript is an audio and video editor that, per its official description, "lets you edit by editing text." Recordings and uploads are transcribed automatically, and editing the transcript edits the media — deleting a word deletes that moment of video, and moving a sentence moves the footage with it. A traditional timeline remains available for finer control.

## Usage and boundaries

- **Workflow**: covers recording, editing, refining, and sharing, plus repurposing finished cuts into clips and translated versions.
- **AI features**: Underlord is the official AI editing agent, with the official example of turning "a raw recording into a tight cut in minutes"; other tools include Studio Sound noise removal and voice enhancement, filler-word and retake removal, eye contact correction, AI avatars and voice cloning, captions, translation, and clip creation.
- **Platforms**: the desktop app supports macOS 14 (Sonoma), Windows 11, and newer; a web editor also exists at [web.descript.com](https://web.descript.com/). Downloads are on the [official download page](https://www.descript.com/download).
- **Agent and developer interface**: the official "API + MCP" offering lets AI assistants drive the editor — the MCP page lists [Claude](/en/wiki/products/claude) (app, web, or Claude Code), [ChatGPT](/en/wiki/products/chatgpt) (via its app directory), and any MCP-speaking client. Nothing needs to be installed or configured as a server, and AI-based edits consume the same AI credits as in-app usage. The supported scope belongs to the [official MCP page](https://www.descript.com/mcp).
- **Pricing**: a free plan is available, with more features and quotas on paid subscriptions; current plans belong to the [official pricing page](https://www.descript.com/pricing).

Yang Pan turns this into a pattern judgment: [the old model was putting agents inside software, and going forward software should become an agent plugin](/weekly/005/transcript#quote-8bef4b9d3f8a34c4f954) — [turning itself into a harness for agents](/weekly/005/transcript#quote-78ef3e13ccc0b6f81cc1). ## Frequently asked questions

### What is Descript?

An audio and video editor that edits media through its transcript: after automatic transcription, editing the text edits the footage. Official entry point: [descript.com](https://www.descript.com/).

### Is Descript free?

A free plan is available (no credit card required), with more features, media hours, and AI credits on paid subscriptions; current plans belong to the [official pricing page](https://www.descript.com/pricing).

### Which platforms does Descript support?

The desktop app runs on macOS 14 (Sonoma), Windows 11, and newer, with a web editor as an alternative; downloads are on the [official download page](https://www.descript.com/download).

### What AI features does Descript have?

The official page lists the Underlord AI editing agent, Studio Sound noise removal, filler-word and retake removal, eye contact correction, AI avatars and voice cloning, captions, translation, and clip creation.

### Can AI agents drive Descript?

Yes — the official "API + MCP" offering: after adding Descript as a connector via the [MCP page](https://www.descript.com/mcp), Claude, ChatGPT, and any MCP-speaking client can drive Underlord to edit, publish, and export, with no server to install.
