## What is Facebook

Facebook is Meta's mainstream social platform; the official page describes it as helping you "connect with friends, family and communities of people who share your interests" ([official product page](https://www.meta.com/facebook/)). The platform launched as TheFacebook on February 4, 2004, founded by Mark Zuckerberg together with Eduardo Saverin, Dustin Moskovitz, Andrew McCollum, and Chris Hughes; on October 28, 2021, the company operating Facebook was renamed Meta Platforms ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook)). For the company's business, AI, and hardware, see the [Meta entry](/en/wiki/brands/meta).

## History and opening up

Facebook was initially restricted to students of Harvard College and later expanded to other North American universities; in 2006 it opened registration to everyone at least 13 years old with a valid email address ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook)). This path from campuses to the general public shaped the product form built around real identities and a friend graph.

## Main features

The sections highlighted on the official product page are:

- **Groups**: finding and joining communities built around interests.
- **Watch**: video content.
- **Marketplace**: buying and selling between people.

Beyond these, specific features and naming — the Feed, Stories, live video, and others — change with versions and regions; for current behavior, refer to the [Facebook Help Center](https://www.facebook.com/help/).

## Relationship to other Meta products

Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, and Threads form Meta's social-app product line; the apps keep separate brands and accounts while sharing infrastructure and the advertising business at the company level ([Meta entry](/en/wiki/brands/meta)). [Instagram](/en/wiki/products/instagram) was acquired by Facebook in 2012, and [Threads](/en/wiki/products/threads) is Meta's platform centered on text sharing. For Meta's AI assistant and models, see the [Meta entry](/en/wiki/brands/meta).

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is Facebook's official website?

The site is [facebook.com](https://www.facebook.com/), and the product overview is at [meta.com/facebook](https://www.meta.com/facebook/).

### What is the relationship between Facebook and Instagram?

Both are Meta's social apps. Instagram was created by Kevin Systrom and Mike Krieger; on April 9, 2012, Facebook announced it would acquire Instagram for approximately one billion dollars, and the deal closed on September 6, 2012 ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instagram)). See the [Instagram entry](/en/wiki/products/instagram) for the product itself.

### When did Facebook launch?

TheFacebook launched on February 4, 2004, initially only for Harvard students, and opened to the general public aged 13 and above in 2006 ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook)).

### How do Facebook and Instagram differ?

Facebook centers on friend-based social networking and communities (Groups, Marketplace, and more), while Instagram centers on photo and video sharing; their positioning and content formats differ. See the [Facebook entry](/en/wiki/products/facebook) and the [Instagram entry](/en/wiki/products/instagram).
