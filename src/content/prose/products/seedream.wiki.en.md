## Seedream

Seedream is the family of image generation and editing models developed by the Seed team at [ByteDance](/en/wiki/brands/bytedance), supporting text prompts and reference images. Versions advance by number, and the team publishes a dedicated page for each, such as Seedream 4.0 (whose official page describes a single unified architecture for generation and editing with output up to 4K resolution, see the [official page](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seedream4_0)) and Seedream 5.5.

## Seedream 5.5

The official page describes Seedream 5.5 as "a multimodal image generation model with greater creativity and more precise execution". Its main capabilities include:

- Multi-turn editing: per the official page, it makes precise, targeted changes across multiple edits while keeping unedited regions "intact at the pixel level".
- Transparent output: native support for transparent and semi-transparent backgrounds, with RGBA layers suited to stickers, lettering, and other layered assets.
- Realism: the official page says lighting is more natural, materials finer, and skin textures more refined.

The try-out entry point is on the [official Seedream 5.5 page](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seedream5_5).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Seedream?

The family of image generation and editing models developed by the Seed team at ByteDance, supporting text prompts and reference images; each version has its own material on the [Seed official pages](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seedream5_5).

### What are the features of Seedream 5.5?

Per the official page: unedited regions stay intact at the pixel level across multi-turn edits, native support for transparent and semi-transparent backgrounds (RGBA), and more realistic lighting and materials.

### How does Seedream 5.5 differ from Seedream 4.0?

The official pages describe the two versions separately: 4.0 unified generation and editing in one architecture with output up to 4K (see the [official page](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/seedream4_0)), while 5.5 emphasizes more precise multi-turn editing and transparent-background output. The full comparison belongs to the official material.

### How is Seedream related to Jimeng?

Seedream is a model family; Jimeng is the image and video creation tool owned by ByteDance. In episode 010, Xiangyang Qiaomu mentions trying "the Jimeng 5.5" inside Jimeng, and Orange adds that Jimeng now operates image models through agents by default; the models actually available belong to the Jimeng product.
