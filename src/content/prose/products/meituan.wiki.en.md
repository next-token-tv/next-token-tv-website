## Meituan

Meituan is an internet platform for local life services, founded in March 2010 and listed on the Main Board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in September 2018. Officially, it describes itself as “A Tech-driven Retail Company” following a “Retail + Technology” strategy, with the mission “We help people eat better, live better.” Consumer services run through apps such as Meituan Waimai (food delivery) and Dianping, covering everyday scenarios from food delivery to in-store consumption.

## Usage and boundaries

Meituan’s main entry points are mobile apps such as the Meituan app; the [official website](https://www.meituan.com/) provides company information, download links, and merchant services. Products and services on the platform are fulfilled by merchants and the delivery network, with prices and rules varying by region and service; the current in-platform terms govern.
