## What Taopiaopiao is

Taopiaopiao (淘票票) is an internet ticketing platform under Alibaba Pictures, offering information and seat-selection booking for films and live entertainment. The platform launched as “Taobao Movie” (淘宝电影) in late 2014; in November 2015, Taobao Movie was injected from Alibaba Group into Alibaba Pictures; on May 16, 2016, the platform was officially renamed “Taopiaopiao,” with its scope expanded from movies to live events, sports, and the wider entertainment business. The mobile entry point is the [official App Store page](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id566813949).

## Use and boundaries

Through the Taopiaopiao app and partner channels, users look up films and showtimes, select seats, and buy tickets for movies and other live entertainment. Ratings, reviews, and ticket prices on the platform are managed by its operator; booking rules and refund policies are governed by the in-app terms. Taopiaopiao belongs to the Alibaba product family alongside [Alipay](/en/wiki/products/alipay) and [Taobao](/en/wiki/products/taobao).
