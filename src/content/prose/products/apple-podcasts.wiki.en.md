## What Apple Podcasts is

Apple Podcasts is [Apple](/en/wiki/brands/apple)'s podcast platform: it refers both to the podcast app on iPhone, iPad, Mac and other Apple devices and to the catalog and distribution system behind it, with the web player at [podcasts.apple.com](https://podcasts.apple.com/). Apple was an early driving force of podcasting — iTunes 4.9, released in June 2005, added podcast playback and a show catalog — and in June 2021 Apple launched paid channel subscriptions for creators.

## Usage and boundaries

- Listening: discover, follow and listen to shows, with progress synced across Apple devices; features listed on the official page include video podcasts, episode transcripts (searchable, with paragraph-level navigation), Enhance Dialogue voice clarity, playback speed with per-show settings, and CarPlay support.
- Platform coverage: the app is built into Apple's systems (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV and visionOS devices); it is also usable in web browsers and in the car through CarPlay.
- Creators: shows are submitted and managed, paid subscriptions configured, and analytics reviewed through [Apple Podcasts Connect](https://podcasters.apple.com/); submission requirements and review rules are in Apple's official creator documentation.

For listeners, the app and the catalog are free; for creators, Apple handles billing and hosting for paid subscriptions.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Apple Podcasts?

Apple's podcast app and platform for discovering, following and listening to podcasts, built into iPhone, iPad, Mac and other Apple devices, with a [web player](https://podcasts.apple.com/).

### Is Apple Podcasts free?

The app and the catalog are free to use; since June 2021 creators can set up paid subscriptions and channels, so some shows or episodes require payment to unlock.

### Is there an Apple Podcasts app for Android?

There is no Android app. Android users can listen through a web browser at [podcasts.apple.com](https://podcasts.apple.com/).

### How do creators get a show onto Apple Podcasts?

Shows are submitted by RSS through [Apple Podcasts Connect](https://podcasters.apple.com/), where content, subscriptions and analytics are managed; requirements are in Apple's official creator documentation.
