## Definition

Google Analytics is Google’s analytics service for websites and apps, used to understand traffic sources, user behavior, and conversions. Officially it is positioned as a tool to "get a complete understanding of your customers across devices and platforms", with the tools offered "free of charge" and a paid enterprise tier, Analytics 360, alongside the standard version. See the [official page](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/).

## How it works

After a tracking tag is deployed on a site or app (it can be paired with Google Tag Manager), data accumulates in one property, and reports cover the customer journey across sites and apps throughout its lifecycle. The official page lists integrations with Google Ads, Search Ads 360, Display & Video 360, Google Cloud, and Search Console, plus insights powered by Google’s machine learning. The current version is Google Analytics 4 (GA4): per the [official support page](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11583528), standard Universal Analytics properties stopped processing data on July 1, 2023, and GA4 "has replaced Universal Analytics".

## Usage

It fits site and app owners measuring traffic, behavior, and marketing conversions — especially teams advertising on Google who need attribution and cross-platform reporting. Personal sites can also use it to observe where visits come from.

## Related and confusable terms

GA4 and Universal Analytics are successive generations, and the older one no longer processes data; the free standard tier and Analytics 360 are two commercial tiers of the same service. Google Analytics is hosted by Google, which distinguishes it from self-hostable open-source analytics such as [Umami](/en/wiki/products/umami), covered on this site.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is GA4?

GA4 (Google Analytics 4) is the current version of Google Analytics. Google’s support page states that it replaced Universal Analytics, whose standard properties stopped processing data on July 1, 2023 (with a one-time processing extension for 360 properties to July 1, 2024). See the [official migration note](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11583528).

### Is Google Analytics free?

The official product page states the tools are offered "free of charge"; a paid enterprise tier, Analytics 360, adds advanced commercial tooling. Tiers and terms are described on the [official page](https://marketingplatform.google.com/about/analytics/).

### How is Google Analytics different from Umami?

Google Analytics is a hosted commercial service deeply integrated with Google’s advertising ecosystem; Umami is an open-source analytics tool that can be self-hosted and is officially positioned as cookie-free and non-tracking. The Umami entry on this site covers the contrast.
