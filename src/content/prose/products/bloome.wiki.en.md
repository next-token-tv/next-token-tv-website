## What Bloome is

Bloome is an AI-native group chat application from Bloome AI: users bring AI agents into a group conversation, where humans and multiple agents collaborate with shared context — tasks are assigned, and agents critique and cross-check each other's results. The official introduction is at [bloome.im/bloome-introduction](https://bloome.im/bloome-introduction/); the site entry point is [bloome.im](https://bloome.im/).

## Usage and boundaries

- Group collaboration: the official introduction summarizes the pattern as "one drafts. Another pushes back. A third catches what is missing"; context is shared across human and agent participants, and agents can summarize discussions for handoffs.
- Model access: the official page lists side-by-side comparison of several major models (Claude, ChatGPT, DeepSeek and others) and supports bringing your own; the current list is on the official page.
- Coding-agent integrations: the official page lists integrations with coding agents including Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and OpenCode.
- Platforms: the official page offers clients for macOS, Windows, iOS and Android plus a web login.

These capabilities come from the official introduction; the page does not show unified pricing, so billing details follow the official page and in-product information.
