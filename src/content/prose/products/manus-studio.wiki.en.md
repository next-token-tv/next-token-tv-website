## What Manus Studio is

Manus Studio is the desktop workspace from [Manus](/en/wiki/products/manus). Its official page describes it as a new workspace where "you and AI build together": creating and editing websites, videos and games alongside your own local files and tools, with hands-on controls to shape the details. It belongs to the same product family as the Manus web app, mobile clients and [Manus Cue](/en/wiki/products/manus-cue), but with a different form and entry point.

## Uses and boundaries

By the official page, Manus Studio works directly with local files, folders and tools, so projects can start from work you already have; there is also a Computer Use capability where Manus operates in its own workspace on your computer while you stay in yours. Agents sync across devices: the page describes taking your agents from Cue and picking up on desktop.

For platforms, the official page offers downloads for macOS (Apple Silicon) and Windows (via the Microsoft Store). As with Manus itself, billing follows the [official pricing page](https://manus.im/pricing); local file permissions and the review of generated results remain with the user.

## FAQ

### What is Manus Studio?

Manus's desktop workspace for creating and editing websites, videos and games with local files and tools, with agents syncing across the web app and Cue. See the [official page](https://manus.im/desktop).

### Which platforms does Manus Studio support?

The official page offers downloads for macOS (Apple Silicon) and Windows (via the Microsoft Store); system requirements follow the official download page.

### How does Manus Studio relate to Manus Cue?

They are different products in the Manus family: Studio is the desktop workspace, while Cue is another Manus product (see the [Manus Cue](/en/wiki/products/manus-cue) entry). The official page describes agents syncing between Cue and desktop; show participants also drew the same distinction.

### How much does Manus Studio cost?

The desktop page does not display pricing; Manus subscriptions and plans are on the [official pricing page](https://manus.im/pricing).
