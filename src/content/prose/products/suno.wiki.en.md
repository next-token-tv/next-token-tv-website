## What Suno is

Suno is an AI music product that generates complete songs from text prompts. Per the [official website](https://suno.com/), a single prompt can produce a song with vocals, lyrics, and full production in under a minute, across genres; users can also describe a genre, mood, or theme, or supply their own lyrics. The product runs on the web and on mobile apps.

## Usage and plans

The plans listed on the official site are: Free — 10 songs per day, no subscription required; Pro — 500 songs per month with commercial rights, royalty-free music, priority generation, a Song Editor, stem separation, and audio uploads up to 8 minutes; Premier — 2,000 songs per month plus Suno Studio (described by the site as a web-based generative audio workstation combining traditional DAW tools with AI generation), multitrack editing, MIDI export, and Persona Voices. Songs can be published to the Suno community. Plan details and prices are governed by the [official site](https://suno.com/). For models, the site's FAQ names v5.5 as its leading model; model information changes quickly, so check the official site before relying on it.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Suno and where is its website?

Suno is an AI music product that generates complete songs with vocals, lyrics, and production from text prompts. The website is [suno.com](https://suno.com/).

### Is Suno free?

There is a free tier: the official site lists a Free plan with 10 songs per day. Higher limits and features like the Song Editor and stem separation are in the paid plans (Pro, Premier); details on the [official site](https://suno.com/).

### Can songs generated with Suno be used commercially?

The Pro and Premier plans include commercial rights per the official site; the free tier does not. The binding terms are the official terms of service and plan descriptions.

### Has Suno V6 been released?

Weekly #002's quick-updates segment reported that Suno V6 had been released — the participant's relay of news at the time. The currently offered models and capabilities are governed by [suno.com](https://suno.com/).
