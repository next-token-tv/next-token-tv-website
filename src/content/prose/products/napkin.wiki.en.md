## What Napkin AI is

Napkin AI is an online tool that turns text into visual content. Its own FAQ describes it as "a visual communication platform that helps you turn ideas into visuals and presentations." It has two main product lines: Napkin Visuals converts text into diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps, infographics, and data charts, while Napkin Slides builds complete presentation decks. The product is provided by Napkin AI and lives at [napkin.ai](https://www.napkin.ai/).

## How it works and where it fits

Napkin is not prompt-driven image generation: you paste or import text, click the Autospark icon to generate a set of candidate visuals, then pick and edit one. The official site says colors, fonts, layouts, and icons are all editable, with export to PNG, PDF, PPT, or SVG. Visual generation runs in the browser (app.napkin.ai), with a mobile app for viewing and editing presentations. Visuals can be created in 60+ languages, and team features include shared Teamspaces, real-time co-editing, and commenting.

One distinction matters when placing this product: generative image models output bitmaps, while Napkin outputs structured visuals that keep being editable and scale without loss — the same distinction behind the vector-graphics point raised in [the show's discussion](#discussion-in-the-show). The free plan and the paid Plus and Pro plans differ in AI credits, export formats such as PPT and SVG, and branding options; current plan details belong to the [official site](https://www.napkin.ai/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What does Napkin AI do?

Napkin AI turns text into editable visuals: diagrams, flowcharts, mind maps, infographics, data charts, and presentations. The input is text rather than prompts, and the results can be edited piece by piece before export. The official entry point is [napkin.ai](https://www.napkin.ai/).

### Is Napkin AI free?

There is a free plan that, per the official site, includes weekly AI credits, unlimited editing, and PNG and PDF export; the paid Plus and Pro plans add more AI credits plus PPT and SVG export and custom branding. Check the [official site](https://www.napkin.ai/) for current plan details.

### What export formats does Napkin support?

The official site lists PNG, PDF, PPT, and SVG; PPT and SVG export are features of the paid plans.

### How is Napkin different from AI image generation?

Napkin produces structured, editable diagrams with SVG among its export formats, while AI image generation models output pixel images that rarely suit print layouts or later editing. That distinction is why the Weekly #003 discussion brought it up.
