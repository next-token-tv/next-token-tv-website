## Definition

4399 is a Chinese-language online games website where titles run directly in the browser without installing anything. The site's own title covers "小游戏, 4399小游戏, 小游戏大全, 双人小游戏" (mini games, two-player games and more), the footer copyright starts in 2004, and the operator is based in Xiamen. The main entry is [www.4399.com](https://www.4399.com/), with mobile entries such as [4399.cn](https://www.4399.cn/).

## How it works

Games are organized into categories such as action, puzzle, sports, adventure, racing and two-player, and start with a click. The site also runs weekly charts, featured picks and "newly launched" lists. After signing in, players get favorites and a play history. Beyond browser mini games, the site maintains an H5 games section (h.4399.com), a web-game area (web.4399.com) and links to WeChat mini games.

## Usage

The typical use is playing a short casual round in the browser during a break, or sharing a two-player game on one screen; most titles are simple, single-session games. On phones, official mobile applications such as the 4399 game box carry the same content.

## Related and confusable terms

"4399" is often used as shorthand for "casual browser games" in general — episode 007 uses it that way — but it names this specific site and its operator. The "4399 game box" is the mobile application, a separate entry point from the website. Searching the number 4399 also returns unrelated results such as currency conversions and postal codes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to download anything to play 4399 games?

No — the web version runs in the browser on click. Mobile users can go through official entries such as [4399.cn](https://www.4399.cn/).

### What is the 4399 game box?

It is the mobile application entry to 4399's games. See the official [4399.cn](https://www.4399.cn/) page linked from the site footer for the current download and usage options.
