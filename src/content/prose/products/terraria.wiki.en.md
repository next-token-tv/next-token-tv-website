## What Terraria is

Terraria is a two-dimensional sandbox adventure game developed and published by Re-Logic. It was first released for Windows on May 16, 2011, and has since expanded to desktop, console, and mobile platforms. The official tagline is "Dig, fight, explore, build! Nothing is impossible in this action-packed adventure game," and the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/105600/Terraria/) describes the game as blending "elements of classic action games with the freedom of sandbox-style creativity": players fight for survival, fortune, and glory in a destructible, buildable, randomly generated world.

Re-Logic is known for long-term free content updates. According to the official Terraria Wiki (terraria.wiki.gg), Terraria is one of the world's most sold video games, with over 70 million sold copies as of May 2026, and the game is a one-time purchase with free updates afterward. The same wiki records that Terraria won Steam's "Labor of Love" award, announced in January 2022.

## Gameplay and mods

Terraria supports both single-player and multiplayer: you can explore caves, mine ore, build houses, and fight bosses, or play cooperatively and competitively. There is no subscription or consumable monetization — a one-time purchase grants subsequent updates. Current prices and platform versions live on the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/105600/Terraria/) and other official channels.

For mods, [tModLoader](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1281930/tModLoader/) on Steam is a free DLC: a community-driven mod loader published by Re-Logic that makes it possible to make and play Terraria mods. One of the largest and best-known community mods is Calamity: per [its own wiki](https://calamitymod.wiki.gg/wiki/Calamity_Mod_Wiki), it is a large content mod adding many hours of endgame content, dozens of enemies, harder difficulty modes, and a new class, maintained by a community team. Mods run alongside the vanilla game; they do not change what the base game is.

## Frequently asked questions

### What type of game is Terraria?

Terraria is a 2D sandbox adventure game by Re-Logic that combines exploration, building, mining, and combat. It was first released for Windows on May 16, 2011, and later expanded to desktop, console, and mobile. The official description is on the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/105600/Terraria/).

### Does Terraria have multiplayer?

Yes. The official wiki records that both single-player and multiplayer are supported, with multiplayer ranging from cooperative play to competitive PvP. Setup references are available via the [tModLoader Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1281930/tModLoader/) and the official wiki.

### How do I install Terraria mods?

Mods are installed and played through [tModLoader](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1281930/tModLoader/), a free DLC on Steam: a community-developed mod loader published by Re-Logic that requires you to own Terraria. Mods are available from Steam Workshop and other officially supported channels.

### What is the Calamity mod?

Calamity is a community mod for Terraria. Its own wiki describes it as a large content mod that adds many hours of endgame content, dozens of enemies, several harder difficulty modes, and a new class. It is played through tModLoader and maintained by a community team; it is not official Re-Logic content.

### How much does Terraria cost?

The game is a one-time purchase, and content updates afterward are free; the official wiki notes the price generally ranges between US$5–20. Current prices per platform are shown on the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/105600/Terraria/) and other official channels.
