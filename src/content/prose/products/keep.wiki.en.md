## Keep

Keep is a fitness app developed by Beijing Calorie Technology Co., Ltd. (北京卡路里科技有限公司), with the official slogan “自律给我自由” (self-discipline gives me freedom) and a mission of using technology to get more people to exercise. The app offers workout courses to follow along, activity tracking for running and cycling, calorie data, and a community; a Keep store sells fitness equipment and gear, and the official site notes data syncing with smart hardware.

## Usage and boundaries

Keep’s core scenarios are everyday fitness: following workout courses, recording outdoor runs or rides, and checking calories and weight data. Some courses and features may require a membership; membership benefits and prices belong to the in-app terms and the [official website](https://www.gotokeep.com/).

## Discussion in the show

In Weekly #004’s chapter “Muse Charm, Phones, and the Entry Point for AI,” [Orange says he uninstalled Keep because voice broadcasts interrupted with ads and wrote his own cycling app in the Chinese transcript](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-ceee33869e13137cf9f5), which records his rides and announces distances; he uses the example to discuss personal development and system permissions in the iPhone ecosystem. This is personal experience and choice, not an overall assessment of Keep.
