## What Pika is

Pika is an AI creative tools platform whose official site is [pika.art](https://pika.art/), self-described as "AI creative tools. Built for Creatives," spanning AI video, AI image, and AI audio. Tools listed on the official pages include Video Studio (from short clips to 3-minute multi-shot pieces), Color Grade, Extend Video, Pika Soundtrack, and Character Studio, alongside integrated entries for third-party models such as Seedance and MiniMax H3.

## Company background and boundaries

Pika was founded in 2023 by Demi Guo and Chenlin Meng, both former PhD students in Stanford's Artificial Intelligence Lab; in November 2023 the company raised $55 million in a round led by Lightspeed Venture Partners, coinciding with early access to Pika 1.0 (per TechCrunch). The product is delivered through the web, with a developer entry at [dev.pika.art](https://dev.pika.art) offering an API membership program for generative media models. Features, available models, and pricing change with releases; the [official pricing page](https://pika.art/pricing) is the reference.

One boundary to keep in mind: the third-party models integrated into Pika each carry their own capabilities and licensing, and the usage terms for generated content are governed by the platform and model providers.

## Discussion in the show

In Weekly #001's chapter “语音与多模态模型为什么仍然难用” (Why speech and multimodal models remain hard to use), while discussing how awkward multimodal and content-production models are to use, [Guizang said Pika had picked a good direction — a multimodal platform that, beyond LLMs, "includes all the video models and image models"](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-7ae6153c2d7e9f578145) in the Chinese transcript. [Yang Pan recalled that "Pika was originally built by a Chinese woman,"](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-aa5bd3347f8920c6349b) and [Guizang added that "they have started selling wrappers now"](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-889438612758d7a891f5). These were the participants' comments at the time; against the official pages, Pika does now integrate multiple third-party models on its platform.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Pika?

An AI platform for creatives ([pika.art](https://pika.art/)) offering video generation, image tools, soundtrack features, and integrated access to multiple third-party video and image models.

### Who founded Pika?

Pika was founded in 2023 by Demi Guo and Chenlin Meng, both former PhD students in Stanford's Artificial Intelligence Lab (per TechCrunch).

### How much does Pika cost?

Pika offers subscription-based access through its website; rates are on the [official pricing page](https://pika.art/pricing). There is also a developer API membership program ([dev.pika.art](https://dev.pika.art)).

### Is Pika the same as Pikachu?

No. Pika is the name of an AI video creation platform and is unrelated to the Pokémon character Pikachu or the pika animal; searching "pika" often surfaces these naming collisions.
