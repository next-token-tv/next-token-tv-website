## Definition

Apple TV hardware is a family of television set-top boxes, including Apple TV HD and Apple TV 4K, for media playback and supported applications and games. Processors, ports, and capabilities vary by generation; the model number identifies the specific device.

## Related concepts

The box is a device, the [Apple TV streaming subscription](/en/wiki/products/apple-tv) is a content service, and the Apple TV app provides a viewing interface. Buying hardware and subscribing to content are separate actions. A streaming catalog does not describe the games a device supports.
