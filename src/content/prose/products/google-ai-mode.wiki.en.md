## What Google AI Mode is

Google AI Mode is an AI search mode inside [Google](/en/wiki/brands/google) Search. On March 5, 2025, Google announced AI Mode in an official blog post as a Search Labs experiment, initially available in the United States to Google One AI Premium subscribers; at Google I/O in May 2025 the company announced a full rollout to U.S. users, followed by expansion to more countries and languages.

AI Mode runs on a custom version of Gemini and targets complex questions that need exploration, comparison, and reasoning: you can ask multi-part questions in one go and keep digging with follow-ups. It uses a "query fan-out" technique — issuing multiple related searches across subtopics concurrently and combining the results into one response — and includes relevant web links, drawing on Google's own sources such as the Knowledge Graph and real-time information.

## Uses and differences

In supported regions, Google Search shows an AI Mode tab; switching to it turns search into a conversation. Current availability is described in the [official Google announcement](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/ai-mode-search/).

Two concepts are easy to mix up: AI Overviews is the AI summary shown above regular search results, which you do not actively choose; AI Mode is a separate search mode producing full responses with follow-ups. The other is the [Gemini](/en/wiki/products/gemini) assistant app, which is a standalone application, whereas AI Mode lives inside Google Search itself.

## Discussion in the show

Episode 008, chapter 3 ([Reddit and AI search: content gets used, where did the traffic go?](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-03) in the Chinese transcript), centers on what AI Mode means for content sites. Guizang relays what Reddit's CEO said on an earnings call: Google's AI Mode and other Google AI features had brought Reddit no incremental traffic, and that pessimistic statement, on top of daily active users missing expectations, sent Reddit shares sharply down the next day. Details Orange relays include that nearly all of Reddit's traffic came from Google SEO, that users stopped clicking through to the original site, that ["AI Mode sends no traffic — not to us, not to anyone"](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-773677b55c4424cf75e0) (a quote he attributes to Reddit's CEO), and that AI Mode was costing Reddit roughly a million visits per week while reducing Google's own search ads. Yang Pan looks at it from the source side: keep your own site and blog in shape and your content ends up in AI Mode anyway — "I don't need the traffic, I need the awareness, I need the information" — which is his reason for doing GEO. These figures and statements are participants' retellings; the transcript gives no primary source, so verify independently before citing.

## Frequently asked questions

### How is AI Mode different from AI Overviews?

AI Overviews is the short AI summary above regular search results, launched in the U.S. in May 2024; AI Mode, announced March 5, 2025, is a separate search mode that uses query fan-out to produce fuller responses with follow-up questions. Both belong to Google Search, but they have different entry points and interactions.

### How do I use Google AI Mode?

In supported regions, switch to the AI Mode tab on the Google Search results page and continue asking follow-up questions like a conversation. Whether the tab appears depends on region, language, and account; go by the [official Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/ai-mode-search/) and what your search page actually shows.

### Is Google AI Mode free?

AI Mode is a free feature of Search. At launch in March 2025 it was a Search Labs experiment limited to Google One AI Premium subscribers in the U.S.; from May 2025 it opened to all U.S. users. No paid subscription is required for AI Mode itself.

### Does AI Mode really send no traffic to websites?

Google states that AI Mode responses include web links; Reddit, however, said on an earnings call that AI search brought it no traffic increase, and episode 008 discusses the "AI Mode sends no traffic" claim and its effect on content sites and search ads. The impact on any specific site varies with content type, so it cannot be generalized.

### Where is AI Mode available?

AI Mode started in the United States and has been expanding to more countries and languages since 2025. The [official Google announcement](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/ai-mode-search/) carries the up-to-date list of supported countries and languages.
