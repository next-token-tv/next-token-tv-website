## Definition

Snapdragon is the mobile computing platform brand of [Qualcomm](/en/wiki/brands/qualcomm), covering processing, graphics, and connectivity. It is used primarily in smartphones and also extends to other computing devices such as PCs. The first Snapdragon chip shipped in November 2007. The brand is tiered by market segment: the 8 series targets flagship smartphones, the 7 series launched in 2018 between the 6 and 8 series, and the 6 and 4 series cover mid-range and entry segments. The brand also includes the Snapdragon X line for PCs, among others. See the [official page](https://www.qualcomm.com/snapdragon/smartphones).

## Product lines

Snapdragon platforms are sold to device makers as SoCs (systems on a chip) that integrate CPU, GPU, ISP, and modem components, so a single Snapdragon generation appears in phones from many brands. Flagship naming evolved from numeric generations to the 8 series (such as the 8 Elite), while the 7, 6, and 4 series serve the upper-mid, mid-range, and entry markets. Specifications vary widely between models, so purchase decisions should rely on the specific phone and the [official materials](https://www.qualcomm.com/snapdragon/smartphones).

## Related concepts

Snapdragon is a product brand of Qualcomm and should be distinguished from the company entry [Qualcomm](/en/wiki/brands/qualcomm). MediaTek's Dimensity ([Dimensity](/en/wiki/products/mediatek-dimensity)) is the competing smartphone chip family and is frequently compared with Snapdragon in SoC reviews. Performance changes noticeably between generations, so comparisons should target specific models.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the relationship between Snapdragon and Qualcomm?

Snapdragon is the mobile computing platform brand of Qualcomm, applied to its chip products for smartphones, PCs, and other devices. See the [Qualcomm entry](/en/wiki/brands/qualcomm) for the company.

### How do the Snapdragon 8, 7, 6, and 4 series differ?

They are product lines tiered by market segment: the 8 series targets flagship smartphones, the 7 series (launched in 2018) sits between the 6 and 8 series, and the 6 and 4 series cover mid-range and entry devices. Models within one series also differ in performance; check the [official page](https://www.qualcomm.com/snapdragon/smartphones) and the specific phone's materials.

### What is Snapdragon X?

Snapdragon X is the product line within the Snapdragon brand for PCs and other computing devices, alongside the 8/7/6/4 series for smartphones. See the [official page](https://www.qualcomm.com/snapdragon/smartphones).

### How do I choose between Snapdragon and Dimensity?

They are the smartphone chip families of Qualcomm and MediaTek respectively, with corresponding tiers (flagship against flagship, mid-range against mid-range). No conclusion fits all phones: real-world results within a generation depend on the specific model, device tuning, and thermal design. Third-party reviews of the specific phone are the best reference.
