## What YouTube is

YouTube is a platform for watching, creating, and sharing video, owned by [Google](/en/wiki/brands/google); its stated mission is "to give everyone a voice and show them the world" (see the [official about site](https://about.youtube/)). It is both an on-demand video and live-streaming service for viewers and a publishing and channel-management platform for creators.

YouTube was founded on February 14, 2005, by Chad Hurley, Jawed Karim, and Steve Chen. Google announced the acquisition in October 2006 and closed the deal that November for US$1.65 billion in stock; YouTube has operated as a Google (Alphabet) subsidiary since.

## Main product surfaces

Per the [official about site](https://about.youtube/), YouTube's main surfaces include:

- **Watching and creating**: video viewing, uploads, and live streaming on web and mobile, with YouTube Studio for creators to manage content and analytics;
- **YouTube Shorts**: the short-video product, introduced in 2020 and rolled out globally from 2021;
- **YouTube Premium**: the ad-free subscription; **YouTube Music**: the music streaming service, launched in 2018;
- **YouTube TV**: the US live-TV subscription, introduced in 2017;
- Plus YouTube Kids, developer APIs, and the advertising business.

Regional availability varies by surface; official pages are authoritative.

## FAQ

### Who owns YouTube?

YouTube is a subsidiary of Google (Alphabet). It was founded in 2005 and acquired by Google in 2006.

### When was YouTube founded, and when did Google buy it?

YouTube was founded on February 14, 2005. Google announced the acquisition in October 2006 and closed it in November 2006 for US$1.65 billion in stock.

### What are YouTube Premium and YouTube Music?

YouTube Premium is the subscription that removes ads and adds benefits such as background play; YouTube Music is the music streaming service launched in 2018. They can be subscribed to separately or together; prices and benefits are on the official pages.

### Where do creators upload and manage videos on YouTube?

Creators upload videos, check analytics, and manage channels through [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com/); simple uploads can also be done directly on the YouTube website or mobile apps.
