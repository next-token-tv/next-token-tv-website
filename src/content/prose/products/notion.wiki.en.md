## What Notion is

Notion is a collaboration platform that puts documents, wikis, project management, and databases in one workspace, which it now markets as an "AI workspace." Pages are built from composable blocks and can combine databases, forms, and multiple calendars; the official product line also includes Notion Calendar plus AI features such as Notion AI, Agents, AI Meeting Notes, and Enterprise Search. The product is provided by Notion, the website is [notion.com](https://www.notion.com/), and the same workspace serves personal note-taking and team collaboration alike.

## Usage and boundaries

There are several ways in: the web app at [notion.com](https://www.notion.com/); the [desktop download page](https://www.notion.com/desktop), which offers installers for macOS (Universal, Apple Silicon, and Intel) and Windows (x64 and ARM64); and mobile apps reachable from that page. Pages can be published as public websites or forms, which distinguishes Notion from purely local document tools.

On plans, the official pricing page lists Free, Plus, Business, and Enterprise tiers, priced per member per month with a yearly discount; the Free and Plus tiers are labeled as including a trial of Notion AI, while the Business tier lists Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes, and Enterprise Search. Rates and feature breakdowns change, so the [official pricing page](https://www.notion.com/pricing) is the reference. Notion also offers Connections to external tools such as Slack and Google Drive, and third-party clients can read and write Notion content through integrations.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Notion, and what is it used for?

Notion is a collaborative workspace: pages and databases for writing documents, building wikis, and managing projects and tasks, plus Notion Calendar and AI features such as Notion AI and Agents. Individuals use it for notes and personal organization; teams use it for collaboration and publishing public pages.

### Where is the Notion website and web app?

The official site is [notion.com](https://www.notion.com/); after signing in you can use the web app. Desktop and mobile downloads are on the [official download page](https://www.notion.com/desktop).

### How do I download the Notion desktop or mobile app?

The [official desktop page](https://www.notion.com/desktop) provides installers for macOS (Universal, Apple Silicon, Intel) and Windows (x64, ARM64); mobile apps are also reached from there. Note that Notion has no official Linux desktop app.

### Is Notion free, and how is it priced?

There is a free tier (Free), paid Plus, Business, and Enterprise tiers billed per member per month with a yearly discount, and custom pricing for Enterprise. Current rates and per-tier features are on the [official pricing page](https://www.notion.com/pricing).

### What is Notion AI, and how is it charged?

Notion AI is the built-in AI feature set, alongside Agents, AI Meeting Notes, and Enterprise Search in the product line. Which AI features each plan includes, and whether they cost extra, are stated on the [official pricing page](https://www.notion.com/pricing).
