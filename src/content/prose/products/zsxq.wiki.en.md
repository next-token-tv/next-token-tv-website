## What Zsxq is

Zsxq (知识星球, "Knowledge Planet") is a paid-community platform: a creator (the "star owner") sets up a circle called a "planet," members pay to join, and the owner publishes text, images, files, and audio there while the content accumulates over time. The official slogan is "connect with one thousand true fans," echoing Kevin Kelly's one-thousand-true-fans idea of the creator economy. The platform is operated by Shenzhen Dacheng Tianxia Information Technology Co., Ltd.

## Features and usage

The official site lists these capabilities: create a paid planet in a minute, with WeChat Pay support and payouts available three days after receiving payment; content can be tagged, marked as highlights, and given assignments, with keyword search and multiple filters; communities need no chat-group invites and have no member-count cap, with multi-role permissions for admins and guests, and an owner dashboard with management data. Apps cover iOS and Android, with a HarmonyOS version available. Each planet's price is set by its owner; the platform's pages list no uniform rate.

## Discussed on Next Token

Episode 008 (recorded November 1, 2026), in the ["capabilities spread, why does trust still matter?"](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-10) chapter of the Chinese transcript, Xiangyang Qiaomu used Zsxq as an example of turning trust into revenue: a book blogger who had spent years building readers who trusted the quality of the writing could, when transforming in the AI era, ["open a Zsxq planet and, immediately, everyone follows."](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-b1ff3cbbaf1a9523268b) In his view, readers' trust in content quality migrates across platforms, and a paid community is the direct container for that trust. This is a participant's view.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Zsxq?

Zsxq is a paid-community platform: a creator opens a planet, members pay to join, and the creator publishes text, images, files, and audio that accumulate into a searchable knowledge base.

### How much does Zsxq cost?

Each planet's price is set by its owner and paid on joining; creating a planet is free, and the platform's pages list no uniform rate. Payouts are available three days after payment is received; see each planet's page and the official notes for specifics.

### How is Zsxq different from a WeChat group?

Per the official site, Zsxq needs no group invites, has no member cap, and lets owners tag content, mark highlights, assign homework, and search by keyword, so the content stays put instead of being washed away by chat. A WeChat group is instant messaging whose content is hard to archive.

### What content formats does Zsxq support?

Text, images, files, and audio, with tags, highlights, assignments, keyword search, and multiple filters.
