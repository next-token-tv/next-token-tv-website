## What Weibo is

Weibo (also known as Sina Weibo) is a microblogging social platform launched in August 2009. According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sina_Weibo), it was launched by Sina Corporation on August 14, 2009 and is one of the biggest social media platforms in China; the operator, Weibo Corporation, is based in Beijing and was carved out of Sina for a Nasdaq listing (ticker WB) in March 2014. Its role is publishing, reading, and discussing public content.

## What it does and where it stops

Weibo's core is the public content feed: users post short updates and repost, comment, and like them, follow accounts of interest, and a large share of public discussion and real-time trending topics happens on the platform, with the trending-topics list (热搜) among its signature entry points. Both a web version and mobile apps are offered, with [weibo.com](https://weibo.com/) as the web entry.

Weibo is at once a public-expression channel and a marketing and distribution channel, so advertising and sponsored content are mixed into the feed; account rules and content policies follow the platform's published terms. Browsing public content does not require signing in, but full features need an account.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is Weibo's official website?

The web entry point is [weibo.com](https://weibo.com/), where signing in unlocks the full features; the mobile app should be downloaded from official publisher listings in each app store.

### When did Weibo launch?

Sina launched Weibo on August 14, 2009, per [Wikipedia's account](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sina_Weibo). In March 2014, Weibo was carved out of Sina and listed on Nasdaq under the ticker WB.

### How does Weibo relate to Sina?

Weibo was launched by Sina Corporation and is operated by Weibo Corporation; after the 2014 spinoff listing it is an independent public company headquartered in Beijing.

### Where can I see Weibo's trending topics?

The trending list is visible in the signed-in web version and in the app; it is one of the platform's public-topic entry points, changes in real time, and should be read as displayed on the platform.
