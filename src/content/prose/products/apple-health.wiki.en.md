## What Apple Health is

Apple Health is [Apple](/en/wiki/brands/apple)'s built-in app for bringing together health and fitness data: Apple's official page describes it as a central, secure place to see medications, sleep, activity and more, with features developed in collaboration with clinical experts. Data comes from iPhone and [Apple Watch](/en/wiki/products/apple-watch) sensors as well as manual entries, third-party apps and compatible devices.

## Devices and data boundaries

The Health app spans iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple Vision Pro and Mac, with the iPhone at the center: data collected on the watch and other devices is viewed in the Health app on iPhone. Watch-side health sensing is covered in the [Apple Watch](/en/wiki/products/apple-watch) entry.

On privacy, Apple's page states that health data is encrypted on the device and reachable only with the passcode, Touch ID or Face ID; when two-factor authentication is on, iCloud-synced data is encrypted end to end. Third-party apps can read health data only after the user grants access item by item in system settings.

## FAQ

### Which devices run Apple Health?

Apple's page lists iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple Vision Pro and Mac. The features available on each device differ; see the [official page](https://www.apple.com/health/).

### Is Apple Health data secure?

Apple's page states that health data is encrypted on the device and accessible only with the passcode, Touch ID or Face ID, and that iCloud-synced data is end-to-end encrypted when two-factor authentication is enabled. Third-party apps need explicit, per-category permission to read health data.

### Does Apple Watch data end up in the Health app?

Yes. Heart rate, sleep and activity data collected by the [Apple Watch](/en/wiki/products/apple-watch) is viewed alongside the Health app on iPhone; see the Apple Watch entry for model and sensor details.

### Can third-party AI assistants connect to Apple Health?

Reading health data requires system-level permission granted by the user. Show participants observed that some of the personal agents they used at the time could not yet connect to Apple Health; whether a given assistant can connect depends on that product's own official documentation.
