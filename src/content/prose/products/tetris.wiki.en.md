## What Tetris is

Tetris is one of the best-known puzzle games: pieces (officially called Tetriminos) fall continuously, and players rotate and arrange them so completed lines clear for points. The official site, [tetris.com](https://tetris.com/), calls it "one of the most iconic and beloved video games in history," credits "Tetris Game Design by Alexey Pajitnov," and notes the game was created in 1984. The trademark and copyright belong to Tetris Holding, licensed to The Tetris Company.

## Gameplay and official versions

The core rule is clearing lines: the falling shapes are limited, the player decides where they land, a filled line disappears, and a stack reaching the top ends the game. Implementations and modes have varied for decades, but this core has stayed stable.

On the official side, [tetris.com](https://tetris.com/) offers free online play in the browser; the site also lists licensed products including mobile apps, console and PC titles (such as Tetris Forever, Tetris The Grand Master 4 - Absolute Eye, and Tetris Effect: Connected), a board game, and merchandise. For browser play, go to [tetris.com](https://tetris.com/); availability per platform belongs to the official site.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who created Tetris, and when?

Tetris was designed by Alexey Pajitnov and created in 1984, per the credit and description on the official site, [tetris.com](https://tetris.com/).

### Where can I play Tetris online for free?

The official website, [tetris.com](https://tetris.com/), offers browser play with no installation.

### What is the relation between Tetris and 俄罗斯方块？

They are the same game: Tetris is the original English name, while "俄罗斯方块" (Russian blocks) is the common Chinese name.

### What official versions of Tetris exist?

The official site lists licensed products including the browser version, official mobile apps, console and PC titles such as Tetris Forever, Tetris The Grand Master 4 - Absolute Eye, and Tetris Effect: Connected, plus a board game and merchandise. Purchase and download entry points per platform are on [tetris.com](https://tetris.com/).
