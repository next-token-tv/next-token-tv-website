## What Vampire Survivors is

Vampire Survivors is a time-survival roguelite by poncle. It entered Steam Early Access on December 17, 2021 and released version 1.0 on October 20, 2022, with poncle as both developer and publisher (see the [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1794680/Vampire_Survivors/)). Weapons attack automatically; the goal is to survive incoming enemy waves as long as possible. Defeated monsters drop experience gems, level-ups let you pick among weapons and passives, and gold funds permanent upgrades. As summarized on its [Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vampire_Survivors), a maxed-out weapon paired with a specific maxed passive can evolve into a stronger final form — the basis of the community's weapon "combination" guides. Runs have soft time limits such as 15, 20, or 30 minutes.

## Platforms and versions

Per [poncle's website](https://poncle.games/), the game is available on Steam, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, iOS, Android, and the Epic Games Store, as Vampire Survivors+ on Apple Arcade, and in a VR version on the Meta Store. Notable additions: local co-op in August 2023, the Adventures mode in November 2023, and online play in October 2025; poncle also released the card-based spin-off Vampire Crawlers. The game won BAFTA Games Awards for Best Game and Game Design on March 30, 2023 (per Wikipedia). Prices and content vary by platform and store.

## Frequently asked questions

### What kind of game is Vampire Survivors?

A time-survival roguelite: weapons fire automatically while you dodge enemy hordes, collect experience, and pair weapons with passives to survive as long as possible. See the [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1794680/Vampire_Survivors/).

### Which platforms is it on?

Steam, Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, iOS, Android, the Epic Games Store, Apple Arcade (as Vampire Survivors+), and a VR version on the Meta Store — see [poncle's website](https://poncle.games/).

### How does weapon evolution (the "combination chart") work?

A fully upgraded weapon combined with a specific maxed passive evolves into a final form when you open chests. Specific pairings are best confirmed in the in-game collection and official channels; community charts are player compilations.

### How much does Vampire Survivors cost?

Prices differ by platform and region, with frequent sales and content variants; check the store page for your platform, such as [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1794680/Vampire_Survivors/).
