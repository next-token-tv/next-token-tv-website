## Definition

Meanders (蜉蝣天地, Fuyou Tiandi) is a Chinese long-form interview podcast hosted by Hanyang and [Zhongqing](/en/wiki/people/zhongqing). Framed as asking "the most interesting people what they saw after venturing alone into uncharted territory", it brings on guests from fields such as history, science, art, sports and AI research for conversations that often run two to three hours. Episodes are published through the official site [fuyoutiandi.com](https://fuyoutiandi.com/) and on platforms including Apple Podcasts and Spotify.

## Discussed on Next Token

In episode 008, in the chapter "AI 制药：从效率提升到创造增量" ([AI drug development: from efficiency gains to creating new value](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-06); Chinese transcript), Guizang recommended an episode featuring a pharmaceutical researcher and relayed its gist: protein design and DNA editing (CRISPR) together with tools like AlphaFold have greatly improved the ability to make targeted drugs, while the bottleneck remains the unavoidable clinical-trial stage. He said listening to it left him far more optimistic about future health outcomes. These remarks are the participant's relay of an episode and his personal takeaway.
