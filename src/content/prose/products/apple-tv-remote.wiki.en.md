## What the Apple TV remote is

The Apple TV remote is the physical remote [Apple](/en/wiki/brands/apple) ships with the [Apple TV](https://www.apple.com/apple-tv-4k/) set-top box; Siri-enabled models are officially called the Siri Remote. The first-generation Siri Remote launched in October 2015 with the fourth-generation Apple TV, replacing the earlier Apple Remote. The current third-generation Siri Remote was announced in October 2022 and ships with Apple TV 4K, switching charging from Lightning to USB-C.

## Features and use

The official product page lists:

- Siri voice: the remote has a dedicated Siri button; Apple says voice recognition supports up to six family members, with tailored recommendations per person.
- Playback control: the click pad handles play, pause, fast-forward, rewind, and volume.
- iPhone as the remote: the official page says an iPhone can control Apple TV from Control Center or the Lock Screen, and can help locate a missing Siri Remote.
- Charging: as of October 2026, the official specs for both Apple TV 4K configurations on sale note "Siri Remote with USB-C connector"; for the current lineup, see [Apple's product page](https://www.apple.com/apple-tv-4k/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Are the Siri Remote and the Apple TV remote the same thing?

"Apple TV remote" is the general term; Siri-enabled models are officially named Siri Remote. Historically, Apple TV also shipped with the voice-less Apple Remote.

### How does the Siri Remote charge?

The current third-generation Siri Remote charges over USB-C and has shipped with Apple TV 4K since October 2022; earlier models charged over Lightning.

### Can an iPhone work as an Apple TV remote?

Yes. The official page says an iPhone can play, pause, skip, and adjust volume from Control Center or the Lock Screen, and can help find a lost Siri Remote.

### What if the remote stops working or needs re-pairing?

Follow Apple’s troubleshooting and pairing guides for Apple TV remotes on the [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/) site; steps differ by remote generation, so identify your model first.
