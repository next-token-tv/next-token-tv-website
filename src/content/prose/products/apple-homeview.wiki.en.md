## What HomeView is

Apple HomeView is a smart home display from [Apple](/en/wiki/brands/apple), positioned by the company as keeping “the whole home connected, entertained, and in the know. All in one place.” Per the official product page, it features a 7-inch Liquid Retina display with True Tone; the domed aluminum base uses a stainless steel hinge to adjust the display angle. A wall-mounted option supports both hardwired and plug-in installations for places like the entrance or hallway.

On availability, the official product page shows HomeView going on sale starting November 13, 2026, with pre-order available on the page; no price is listed, and purchasing information follows the [official product page](https://www.apple.com/apple-homeview/).

## Features and usage

For audio, video, and communication, HomeView includes dual full-range drivers with a downward-firing woofer, Spatial Audio, and multi-room AirPlay; the 12MP Center Stage camera keeps everyone in frame during video calls, four beamforming microphones reduce background noise, and Intercom and Find My are supported. Siri, powered by Apple Intelligence, serves as the personal assistant, and the device runs Apple Music, News, Audiobooks, and compatible third-party apps.

Smart home control is a core scenario: the built-in Home app acts as the dashboard, showing up to nine camera feeds, controlling lights and temperature, detecting smoke and carbon monoxide alarm sounds, and aggregating notifications with Apple Intelligence. On accessories, Apple says HomeView works with thousands of accessories bearing the “Works with Apple Home” logo, requiring HomeKit or Matter devices sold separately; an Apple Home Collection of branded accessories (camera, thermostat, door lock) is marked “coming later” on the official page.

For families, the official page says the device recognizes up to six people and delivers a personalized experience for each; guests can control accessories, check the weather, set timers, and play music but cannot access anyone’s personal information. On privacy, camera and microphone feeds are processed in the A18 chip’s Secure Exclaves, which Apple says neither the operating system nor Apple can access. For the HomeKit framework itself, see the [HomeKit](/en/wiki/products/homekit) entry.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Apple HomeView?

HomeView is Apple’s smart home display: a 7-inch Liquid Retina screen integrating Siri powered by Apple Intelligence, audio and video playback, Center Stage video calling, and smart home control through the Home app.

### When does Apple HomeView go on sale?

The official product page shows November 13, 2026, with pre-order available on the page; after release, purchasing runs through [apple.com](https://www.apple.com/apple-homeview/), the Apple Store app, and select retailers.

### How much does Apple HomeView cost?

The official product page does not list a price. Pricing and configuration follow the [official product page](https://www.apple.com/apple-homeview/) and retailer listings.

### Which accessories does Apple HomeView support?

Apple says it works with thousands of accessories bearing the “Works with Apple Home” logo; accessories must be HomeKit or Matter devices and are sold separately. The Apple Home Collection (camera, thermostat, door lock) is officially marked “coming later.” See the [HomeKit](/en/wiki/products/homekit) entry and the official page for details.

### Can Apple HomeView be wall-mounted?

Yes. A wall-mounted option supports hardwired and plug-in installations for places like the entrance or hallway; the standard version sits on a hinged base that adjusts the display angle.

### Is personal information safe on HomeView?

Per the official page, the device recognizes up to six people and personalizes for each, while guests cannot access members’ personal information; camera and microphone feeds are processed in the A18 chip’s Secure Exclaves, which neither the operating system nor Apple can access. Accessing personal content requires the paired iPhone/iPad on the same Wi-Fi, recently unlocked, and a face scan.
