## Amazon shopping marketplace

Amazon is an online shopping marketplace for consumers. The company describes its mission as being "Earth's most customer-centric company," and frames its retail business around selection, value, and convenience: buyers order on sites such as [amazon.com](https://www.amazon.com/), and goods come from both Amazon's own retail operation and third-party sellers, who join through official entry points such as Seller Central. Amazon's businesses span retail, logistics, cloud services, and more; the cloud business is carried by the separate brand [Amazon Web Services](/en/wiki/brands/aws). Business structure, site coverage, and program names change, so the [official company overview](https://www.aboutamazon.com/what-we-do) is the reference.

## Prime membership and shopping features

Prime is Amazon's paid membership program. Core benefits listed on the official page include fast, free delivery up to same-day, Prime Video streaming, members-only deal events, and add-ons such as grocery delivery, Amazon Music, and Amazon Photos. Membership pricing is time-sensitive: as of September 2026, the US official page lists $14.99 per month or $139 per year, with different pricing in other countries; check the [official Prime page](https://www.amazon.com/prime) for current rates.

On AI shopping, Amazon offers assistants such as Alexa for Shopping inside its own ecosystem; external agents, by contrast, are not free to place orders on customers' behalf. The episode 004 discussion of Amazon blocking Muse from ordering is exactly this friction between the platform and outside agents; see below.

## Frequently asked questions

### What kind of platform is Amazon?

Amazon is a consumer shopping marketplace offering both first-party retail and third-party sellers, with businesses extending into logistics, cloud services, and more. The company-level overview is on [About Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/what-we-do).

### What does an Amazon Prime membership include?

Core benefits on the official page include fast, free delivery up to same-day, Prime Video streaming, members-only deal events, plus grocery delivery, Amazon Music, and Amazon Photos. The full list is on the [official Prime page](https://www.amazon.com/prime).

### How much does Amazon Prime cost per month?

As of September 2026, the US official page lists $14.99 per month or $139 per year, with separate pricing for student and discounted plans. Membership prices and benefits vary by country and change over time; the Prime official page is the reference.

### How do I cancel an Amazon Prime membership?

The official page notes you can cancel anytime, and membership is managed from the Prime section of your Amazon account settings. Members who paid but have not used benefits may be eligible for a refund of the current period, per the official page.
