## What Lovart is

Lovart is an AI design agent built by Evoken (演语科技), the AI application company founded by [Chen Mian](/en/wiki/people/chen-mian). Its official site is [lovart.ai](https://www.lovart.ai/), where the product is positioned as a design agent: you describe a brand and its needs in conversation, and it produces design assets such as logos, packaging, social content, and video ads.

Lovart is one of three product lines from the same company, alongside [Liblib](/en/wiki/products/liblib), an AI image-creation and model-sharing community, and [LibTV](/en/wiki/products/libtv), an AI video creation tool — an image community, a design agent, and a video tool respectively.

## Release timeline

The following is Chen Mian's own account in the LateTalk episode 175 interview (July 2026, republished by Chwang):

- Before Lovart, the team built Xingliu (星流), an AI design product whose node-based image workflow attempt failed in 2024; the team then pivoted to agents.
- Lovart's beta launched in May 2025 and the official version in July of that year. The official release included ChatCanvas: select a region on the canvas and let the agent revise and edit it through natural language.

Current features and plans are on the [official site](https://www.lovart.ai/).

## Using it and pricing

The official pricing page lists a free plan and a paid Pro plan; plan differences and current prices are on the [official pricing page](https://www.lovart.ai/pricing). As a design agent, its output is design assets rather than finished engineering work; what sets it apart from general assistants is that its tools and generation are organized around design workflows. Feature details follow the official site and in-product documentation.
