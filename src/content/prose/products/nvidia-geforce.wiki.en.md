## Definition

GeForce is NVIDIA’s brand of graphics processors for gaming and personal computing (the performance market), first introduced on August 31, 1999. The official GeForce page presents it through desktop cards (such as the GeForce RTX 50 Series), gaming laptops, G-SYNC displays, the GeForce NOW cloud gaming service, and drivers and companion software. See the [official page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/).

## How it works

GeForce hardware comes as desktop graphics cards and laptop GPUs, paired with official drivers and software such as the NVIDIA App, with AI features like DLSS layered on top (the official page presents RTX AI PCs alongside DLSS and Reflex). Specifications of any specific model are governed by the [official materials](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/).

## Usage

Primary uses are gaming and personal creation: game rendering, acceleration in content-creation software, and local AI applications in the RTX AI PC context. GeForce NOW extends GeForce to devices without a discrete GPU by streaming from the cloud.

## Related and confusable terms

GeForce is the consumer GPU brand and should be distinguished from the company entry [NVIDIA](/en/wiki/brands/nvidia) and from the data-center and professional product lines — this site’s [DGX Spark](/en/wiki/products/nvidia-dgx-spark) belongs to that other context. In naming, the GTX suffix has been used since the GeForce 200 series in 2008, and the RTX branding debuted with the GeForce 20 series announced in August 2018 ([Wikipedia: GeForce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GeForce)); GeForce NOW is a cloud gaming service, not a card model. For the GPU term itself, see this site’s [GPU entry](/en/wiki/terms/gpu).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do GeForce RTX and GTX differ?

They are successive naming lines within the GeForce brand: the GTX suffix has been used since the GeForce 200 series (2008), and the RTX branding began with the GeForce 20 series announced in August 2018. The official product page currently presents the GeForce RTX 50 Series; see the [official page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/).

### Where do I download GeForce Experience?

NVIDIA’s official companion software for PC gamers and creators is now the NVIDIA App; the download entry is on the [official page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/software/nvidia-app/). The page makes no explicit successor claim, so installation and features should be taken from it directly.

### What is GeForce NOW?

GeForce NOW is NVIDIA’s cloud gaming service, officially framed as letting players "experience the power of GeForce on any device" — no local graphics card required, gameplay streamed from the cloud. See the [official page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/).
