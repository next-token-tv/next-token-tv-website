## What Hongguo is

Hongguo Short Drama (红果短剧, often shortened to 红果 / "Hongguo") is a free short-drama viewing app from Douyin Group under ByteDance. The [App Store (China) listing](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id6451407032) names Beijing Bimo Liuxiang Technology Co., Ltd. as the seller and files the app under Entertainment. It centers on free drama viewing, with short dramas across urban, romance, workplace, and fantasy (xianxia) genres, plus films, TV series, novels, audiobooks, and comics.

## Features and use

Per the official app description, Hongguo offers a single-column immersive vertical feed with personalized recommendations, plus genre-organized "theater" browsing; viewing features include comments, one-tap series following, upcoming-show reservations, picture-in-picture, playback speed, and episode selection. The app also offers subscription memberships and in-app purchases; current forms and prices follow the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id6451407032) and the app itself. For content producers, Hongguo runs listing and promotion mechanisms, with creator-side rules on its official channels.

## Discussion in the show

In Weekly #001's chapter “AI 短剧把 Token 消费带进新的内容市场” (AI short dramas bring token consumption into a new content market), the panel put Hongguo at the center of the AI short-drama industry. Guizang said that [swiping through Douyin or Hongguo, you find hit AI short dramas almost every week, at very high quality](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-44958efa4a8671429878). Comparing platform mechanics, he argued that [Hongguo does have monthly-vote and pay-per-quantity mechanisms, but remains too institution-facing — listing on Hongguo means ad-buying workflows built for studios](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-d3a69472cd439e9a6a7d), against his imagined "Qidian for short dramas." [Yang Pan expected the bigger next market to be global, beyond Hongguo](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-54b23c6a793110050d08); Guizang noted overseas resistance to AI dramas, and Juzi pointed to TikTok as the main overseas channel. These are participant observations and predictions about the industry, not product statements from Hongguo.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Hongguo Short Drama?

It is a free short-drama app from Douyin Group (ByteDance), focused on discovering and watching vertical short dramas, with films, TV series, novels, audiobooks, and comics as well. See the [official App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id6451407032).

### Is Hongguo free?

The official positioning is free viewing, supported by advertising and memberships; the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/cn/app/id6451407032) also lists subscription and in-app purchase items. Which titles are fully free and which need a membership is shown inside the app.

### How does Hongguo relate to Douyin?

Hongguo is launched by Douyin Group within the ByteDance family, but it is a standalone app that does not depend on the Douyin client for download or use.
