## All-In Summit

All-In Summit is an in-person event hosted by the team behind the [All-In podcast](/en/wiki/products/all-in). The summit features conversations on business, technology and society. Event details are available on the [official site](https://allin.com/summit).

## Discussion in the show

In Next Token Weekly #003’s chapter “用 GPT-6 Astra 造数据、训练小模型” (training small models with GPT-6 Astra), Guizang mentions a phone-call clip involving NVIDIA’s Jensen Huang from an All-In event earlier that week and [asks for it to be played](/weekly/003/transcript#quote-beb2c5083e938a67f78e); Orange adds that the clip had spread online. The mention is part of the hosts’ live discussion, not a description or endorsement of the event. See the [episode 003 chapter](/weekly/003/transcript#chapter-12) in the Chinese transcript.
