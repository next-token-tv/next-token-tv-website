## The TRAE product line

TRAE is an AI productivity product line from ByteDance. Its international site is [trae.ai](https://www.trae.ai/) and its China site is [trae.cn](https://www.trae.cn/), with the tagline "The Real AI Engineer." The line has two main products: TraeCode, positioned as an AI coding engineer for software development, and TraeWork, positioned as a professional AI work assistant for office tasks. The two are separate products with their own entry points and clients.

## Product forms and boundaries

- TraeCode is an AI coding IDE installed from the Trae websites, aimed at understanding, generating, and editing code and carrying out development tasks.
- TraeWork offers a [web version](https://work.trae.ai/) and desktop clients for macOS and Windows, with three modes: Work for non-developers handling documents, data, and presentations; Code for coding, debugging, and repository workflows; and Design for generating, editing, and delivering design work. The official [quick-start documentation](https://docs.trae.cn/work_trae-work-web-and-desktop-quickstart) describes text or voice instructions, attachments such as .pptx and .xlsx, GitHub and Feishu integrations, MCP, skills, and rules; cloud-mode tasks started on one desktop client sync automatically with the same account.
- TRAE has a free tier alongside paid plans; plan contents and prices change, so check the [official pricing page](https://www.trae.ai/pricing) for current options.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is TRAE?

TRAE is ByteDance’s AI productivity product line, tagline "The Real AI Engineer," comprising TraeCode for software development and TraeWork for office work. International site: [trae.ai](https://www.trae.ai/); China site: [trae.cn](https://www.trae.cn/).

### What is the relationship between TRAE and TraeWork?

TraeWork is a separate product within the TRAE line, not the same software: TraeCode targets coding while TraeWork targets office tasks and has its own [web version](https://work.trae.ai/) and desktop clients. The show’s hosts also explicitly distinguish the two.

### Where are TRAE’s official site and download entry points?

International users should use [trae.ai](https://www.trae.ai/); users in China use [trae.cn](https://www.trae.cn/). TraeWork’s web version is at [work.trae.ai](https://work.trae.ai/) (work.trae.cn in China), and desktop clients are available from the official download pages.

### Is TRAE free, and how is it priced?

TRAE has a free tier plus monthly subscription plans that differ in model access, concurrent cloud tasks, and quotas. Current plans and prices are on the [official pricing page](https://www.trae.ai/pricing).

### Is TRAE a ByteDance product?

Yes. TraeWork’s official Chinese site describes it as an AI work platform launched by ByteDance, and the enterprise offering is provided through ByteDance’s BytePlus.
