## What Pinduoduo is

Pinduoduo is an e-commerce platform founded in September 2015 in Shanghai, operated by Shanghai Xunmeng Information Technology Co., Ltd. and founded by Colin Huang (Huang Zheng); the official [website](https://www.pinduoduo.com/) calls itself a pioneer of "new e-commerce". At the group level, Pinduoduo belongs to PDD Holdings — officially described as "a multinational commerce group that owns and operates a portfolio of businesses" and listed on Nasdaq (ticker PDD); [Temu](https://www.temu.com/) is also part of that group.

## What it does and where it stops

Shopping on Pinduoduo revolves around interaction and team purchases: users place team-buy orders with sellers or manufacturers for lower prices, with the mobile app as the primary way to shop. The platform has a long-standing focus on agricultural categories; merchants onboard, open stores, and manage shops through the “拼多多商家入驻” (merchant onboarding) section of the [official site](https://www.pinduoduo.com/).

Platform rules, promotions, and after-sales policies keep changing, so purchase decisions should follow what is published in the app and on the [official site](https://www.pinduoduo.com/). Pinduoduo and Temu are separate platforms for separate markets: Temu launched in September 2022 and serves markets outside mainland China.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is Pinduoduo's official website?

The official site is [pinduoduo.com](https://www.pinduoduo.com/) with company information, the merchant-onboarding entry point, and app download guidance; day-to-day shopping happens mainly in the Pinduoduo app.

### What company is behind Pinduoduo?

The platform is operated by Shanghai Xunmeng Information Technology Co., Ltd., founded in September 2015 by Colin Huang; at group level it belongs to PDD Holdings, listed on Nasdaq (ticker PDD). See the [PDD Holdings site](https://www.pddholdings.com/).

### How do Pinduoduo and Temu relate?

Both belong to PDD Holdings: Pinduoduo mainly serves the China market, while Temu launched in September 2022 for markets outside mainland China. Goods, merchants, and rules are independent across the two platforms.

### How do merchants join Pinduoduo?

Merchant onboarding starts in the “拼多多商家入驻” section of the [official site](https://www.pinduoduo.com/), where qualifications are submitted per the official guide; onboarding conditions and fees follow the official pages.
