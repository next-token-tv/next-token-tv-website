## Definition

Dragon Ball is a TV anime adapted from Akira Toriyama's manga, following Goku's adventures and the search for the Dragon Balls, produced by Toei Animation. The original manga ran in Weekly Shōnen Jump from November 1984 to May 1995, collected in 42 tankōbon volumes. Toei Animation's official page states the TV anime was broadcast on the Fuji TV network from February 1986 to April 1989, Sundays at 19:00–19:30 (see the [official page](https://www.toei-anim.co.jp/tv/dragon/)).

## How it works

As an adaptation, the anime was produced on the basis of Toriyama's manga: the copyright line reads "©バードスタジオ／集英社・フジテレビ・東映アニメーション" (Bird Studio / Shueisha / Fuji TV / Toei Animation). The TV anime was followed by sequel series such as Dragon Ball Z, and together with the manga, films and games forms the Dragon Ball franchise.

## Usage

Viewers watch it through TV reruns, officially released home video and streaming channels; Toei's official page also lists entries for Dragon Ball Z and Dragon Ball GT, which serve as entry points to the franchise's anime history. In Chinese-speaking contexts the series is also known by titles such as "七龙珠".

## Related and confusable terms

"Dragon Ball" means different things by context: Toriyama's original manga, Toei's TV anime, the franchise as a whole, and its many game adaptations. This entry covers the anime; episode 007 only borrows the wish-granting dragon story device as an analogy and discusses no specific game or series entry. Searching "龙珠" also returns same-name items such as a livestreaming platform and a medicinal ointment.

## Frequently asked questions

### When did the Dragon Ball anime first air?

Per Toei Animation's official page, the TV anime was broadcast from February 1986 to April 1989 on the Fuji TV network.

### Who created the Dragon Ball anime?

The original manga is by Akira Toriyama; the TV anime was produced by Toei Animation, with copyright held by Bird Studio / Shueisha / Fuji TV / Toei Animation.

### Which Dragon Ball anime series exist?

The official Toei page lists the TV anime Dragon Ball (1986–1989) and sequels such as Dragon Ball Z and Dragon Ball GT; broadcast details are on the [official page](https://www.toei-anim.co.jp/tv/dragon/) and its series pages.
