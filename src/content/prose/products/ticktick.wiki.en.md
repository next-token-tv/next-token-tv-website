## What is TickTick

TickTick (Chinese name 滴答清单, "Dida365") is a task management app combining to-do lists, calendars, and reminders. The official story says the team started with GTasks, an Android app that synced with Google Tasks, then built and refined TickTick "for more than a decade," with tens of millions of users ([official About page](https://ticktick.com/about)). The version for mainland China is called Dida365 (滴答清单), with its site at [dida365.com](https://dida365.com/) operated by Hangzhou Suibiji Network Technology Co., Ltd.; the international version is TickTick at [ticktick.com](https://ticktick.com/).

## Core features

The official site lists these main capabilities:

- **To-do list**: organizing work projects, personal tasks, and study plans, with reminders and repeats.
- **Calendar views**: yearly, monthly, weekly, daily, and agenda views, plus multi-day and multi-week layouts.
- **Pomodoro timer**: tracking time in 25-minute intervals to stay focused.
- **Habit tracker**: a habit library, flexible tracking options, and statistics.
- **Collaboration and views**: shared lists with assigned tasks, plus Kanban, Timeline, the Eisenhower Matrix, statistics, and 40+ themes.

The site also mentions AI features (Voice Capture, Audio Summary) and entry points for building automated workflows via MCP, CLI, or OpenClaw ([official homepage](https://ticktick.com/)). The exact scope and availability of these capabilities change over time; rely on the official site for current details.

## Platform support

The official story describes the app as covering phones, computers, tablets, watches, and the web, with real-time sync ([official About page](https://ticktick.com/about)).

## Frequently asked questions

### How do Dida365 and TickTick differ?

They are versions of the same product for different markets: Dida365 serves mainland China ([dida365.com](https://dida365.com/)) and TickTick serves the international market ([ticktick.com](https://ticktick.com/)), with the same core features.

### Is TickTick free?

The app offers free sign-up and use alongside paid upgrade plans; the feature split between free and premium is on the official pricing page ([TickTick Pricing](https://ticktick.com/upgrade); for Dida365, see the membership entry on [dida365.com](https://dida365.com/)).

### Which platforms does TickTick support?

Officially, phones, computers, tablets, watches, and the web, with real-time sync ([official About page](https://ticktick.com/about)).

### Does TickTick support MCP, CLI, or an API?

The official homepage mentions automated workflows built via MCP, CLI, or OpenClaw ([official homepage](https://ticktick.com/)). For integration details and current availability, rely on the official documentation.
