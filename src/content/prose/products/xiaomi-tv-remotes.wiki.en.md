## Definition

Xiaomi TV remotes are a family of remotes for operating Xiaomi televisions and related devices, with connectivity and voice features varying by model. Products on official sale include the Xiaomi Bluetooth Remote 2 Pro, which the official product information describes as supporting smart voice, NFC casting, and USB-C charging (see the [official product page](https://www.mi.com/xiaomi-bluetooth-remote-2-pro)).

## How it works

After pairing with the TV, a remote handles navigation, volume, and confirmation; voice-capable models let you hold the voice key and speak commands to the TV, working with Xiaomi's voice-assistant stack for search and control. Connectivity differs by model: the 2 Pro is a Bluetooth voice remote, while more basic models exist; the exact connection and compatibility of each model is governed by its official product page.

## Usage

The everyday use is with a [Xiaomi TV](/en/wiki/products/xiaomi-tv) or TV box: picking content, adjusting volume, and navigating apps, with voice models adding voice search. The community has also extended the family in two directions: using the 2 Pro as a voice input device for a computer (see the [Xiaomi Bluetooth Remote 2 Pro](/en/wiki/products/xiaomi-bluetooth-remote) entry), and — in episode 007 — treating the remote as the natural input device for AI interaction on the TV side.

## Related and confusable terms

- This entry is the product family; the [Xiaomi Bluetooth Remote 2 Pro](/en/wiki/products/xiaomi-bluetooth-remote) is one specific product within it. When an episode or article says just "Xiaomi remote", do not assume a specific model.
- The remote (hardware) is separate from the voice assistant (software capabilities such as [HyperXiaoAI](/en/wiki/products/hyperxiaoai)); the voice key is only one trigger for the assistant on the TV.
- Other entries such as [Xiaomi smartphones](/en/wiki/products/xiaomi-smartphones) and [XiaoAI speakers](/en/wiki/products/xiaoai-speakers) cover different Xiaomi product lines.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where does the Xiaomi Bluetooth Remote 2 Pro fit in this family?

It is one of Xiaomi's currently sold Bluetooth voice remotes, described by official product information as supporting smart voice, NFC casting, and USB-C charging, mainly for Xiaomi TVs, boxes, and related devices. See the [Xiaomi Bluetooth Remote 2 Pro entry](/en/wiki/products/xiaomi-bluetooth-remote) and the [official product page](https://www.mi.com/xiaomi-bluetooth-remote-2-pro).

### How do I pair a Xiaomi remote?

A Bluetooth voice remote needs to be paired with the TV before its buttons and voice features work; pairing steps differ by model, so follow the remote's manual and the [official product page](https://www.mi.com/xiaomi-bluetooth-remote-2-pro). If pairing fails, first check whether your TV model is within the remote's compatibility range.

### Where can I buy a replacement remote?

The official channel is the remote product page on Xiaomi's store (for example, the [Xiaomi Bluetooth Remote 2 Pro](https://www.mi.com/xiaomi-bluetooth-remote-2-pro) page). Before buying, confirm the remote's compatibility with your TV and box so the replacement supports the features you had.
