## Unreal Engine

Unreal Engine is a real-time 3D creation and game development engine developed by [Epic Games](/en/wiki/brands/epic-games), originally built for the 1998 first-person shooter Unreal. It has since been used across game genres and adopted by other industries, most notably film and television (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unreal_Engine)). Unreal Engine 5 (announced May 2020, released April 2022) introduced features such as Nanite and Lumen (per English Wikipedia). The official site is [unrealengine.com](https://www.unrealengine.com).

## Uses and licensing

Unreal Engine targets real-time 3D work — game development, film, and visualization — and can be downloaded from the official site; commercial release terms follow the official licensing documentation. Development is typically done in C++ or with the Blueprint visual scripting system. The engine also underpins Epic's creator-facing ecosystem, including Unreal Editor for Fortnite.

These are participants' personal impressions and retellings, not engine benchmarks.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Unreal Engine?

A real-time 3D game and content creation engine developed by Epic Games, born in 1998 with the game Unreal; see [unrealengine.com](https://www.unrealengine.com).

### When was UE5 released?

Unreal Engine 5 was revealed in May 2020 and released in April 2022, with Nanite mesh geometry and Lumen dynamic global illumination as headline features (per English Wikipedia).

### Is Unreal Engine free?

You can download and use it for free from the official site; commercial releases follow the official license terms — rates and conditions are on the official licensing page.

### Is Unreal Engine only for games?

No. It began with PC first-person shooters, spread across game genres, and has been heavily adopted by the film and television industry (per English Wikipedia).

### Is the "UE 5.6 AI-First" claim in the show official?

In the show it is Guizang relaying Epic Games' CEO's social-media statements — making the engine easier for AI to use, improving WebGL and Three.js adaptation. Treat it as a retelling; scope and progress come from Epic's own announcements.
