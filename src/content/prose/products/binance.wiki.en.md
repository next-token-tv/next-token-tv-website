## What Binance is

Binance is an online platform for digital asset trading and related services, founded in 2017 by Changpeng Zhao (CZ) and launched with roughly 15 million dollars raised through an initial coin offering. By daily trading volume it is the largest cryptocurrency exchange. Its ecosystem also includes the BNB token, launched in July 2017, and the Trust Wallet.

## Boundaries and compliance

Binance was initially based in China; after Chinese regulators began cracking down on cryptocurrency trading in 2017, the company moved its operations out of mainland China, and it has had no official company headquarters since. On November 21, 2023, Binance pleaded guilty to US anti-money-laundering violations and paid a 4.3-billion-dollar fine, and Zhao stepped down as CEO. Service availability differs across jurisdictions; the official terms of service and local regulations govern.

## The October 2026 security breach

In late October 2026, Binance suffered a major security breach involving crypto assets estimated at 6.3 billion dollars — the largest digital-asset theft on record. Binance temporarily suspended deposits and withdrawals while investigating and coordinating with law enforcement and crypto issuers. According to public reporting ([Wikipedia: Binance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binance)), Tether froze about 1.57 billion dollars, Circle froze more than 800 million dollars, and Ripple froze about 200 million dollars of affected assets, roughly 3 billion dollars in total. Binance said it would compensate affected users from its own reserves plus the Secure Asset Fund for Users (SAFU), an emergency fund valued at about 1 billion dollars ([Wikipedia: Binance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binance)).
