## What Profound is

Profound is a service for analyzing brand visibility and citations in AI search and answer engines; its official entry point is [tryprofound.com](https://www.tryprofound.com/). The work it belongs to is usually called GEO (generative engine optimization): as more people get information from AI answers — [Google AI Mode](/en/wiki/products/google-ai-mode), ChatGPT, Copilot — instead of traditional search results, brands need to know whether those answers mention them, cite them, and cite their own information rather than someone else's.

## Features and usage

Per the official documentation, Profound's product covers:

- Analytics and dashboards: Answer Engine Insights, Prompt Volumes, and related reporting entry points;
- Developer interfaces: an API and SDKs for pulling Profound data into your own applications and reports, plus ready-made queries covering visibility, citations, and metrics;
- AI marketing automation: AI Marketer (Aim) and Agents, including Projects, Agents, Sheets, and brand context;
- Integrations: connectors for CMS, analytics, and messaging tools, with SSO, team access, and agency mode;
- MCP support: ChatGPT, Copilot, and other tools can read Profound data directly.

The documentation also includes learning resources such as a blog, community, Profound University courses, and a research hub.

## How it differs from SEO tools

An [SEO](/en/wiki/terms/seo) tool tracks where a page ranks in search results and how much traffic it earns; a [GEO](/en/wiki/terms/geo) tool like Profound tracks how a brand is mentioned, cited, and paraphrased inside AI-generated answers. They address different entry points: the search results page versus the AI answer.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Profound?

Profound is a service that analyzes brand visibility and citations in AI search and answer engines — a tool in the GEO (generative engine optimization) category.

### What can Profound do?

It tracks brand mentions and citations in AI answers (Answer Engine Insights, Prompt Volumes), exposes data through an API/SDK, and automates AI marketing actions with AI Marketer (Aim) and Agents; integrations connect CMS, analytics, and messaging tools.

### How is Profound different from an SEO tool?

SEO tools track rankings and clicks on a search results page; Profound tracks how a brand is cited and paraphrased inside AI-generated answers — a different entry point.

### Does Profound have an API?

Yes. The official documentation lists an API and SDKs with ready-made queries for visibility, citations, and metrics; see the [Profound documentation](https://docs.tryprofound.com/).
