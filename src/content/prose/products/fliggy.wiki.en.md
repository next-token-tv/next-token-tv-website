## Fliggy

Fliggy (飞猪) is an online travel platform owned by [Alibaba Group](/en/wiki/brands/alibaba), offering bookings for flights, trains, hotels, attraction tickets, and other travel products. Its lineage goes back to Taobao Travel, launched in 2010; it was renamed “Alitrip” (阿里旅行·去啊) in October 2014 and adopted the Fliggy brand on October 27, 2016.

## Usage and boundaries

Fliggy serves travel within and from China; the [official website](https://www.fliggy.com/) and the app are the main booking entry points. Flights, hotels, and other products on the platform come from different merchants, with prices, change-and-refund rules, and terms varying per product; the specific product page and platform rules govern before booking.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Fliggy?

An online travel platform under Alibaba Group for booking flights, trains, hotels, attraction tickets, and other travel products.

### Where are Fliggy’s website and app?

The official website is [fliggy.com](https://www.fliggy.com/); the app is available from major app stores.

### What was Fliggy called before?

It operated as “Taobao Travel” and then “Alitrip” (阿里旅行·去啊) before adopting the Fliggy name on October 27, 2016.

### Is Fliggy part of Alibaba?

Yes. Alibaba Group’s [list of global businesses](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US/global-businesses) includes Fliggy among its businesses.
