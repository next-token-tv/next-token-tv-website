## What Siri is

Siri is the intelligent assistant built into [Apple](/en/wiki/brands/apple) devices, spanning the [iPhone](/en/wiki/products/iphone), iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and Apple Vision Pro. The technology originated in an SRI International AI project, was acquired by Apple in 2010, and first shipped as a beta alongside the iPhone 4S on October 14, 2011.

On September 14, 2026, Apple launched [Siri AI](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/) together with iOS 27 and the year's other system releases — officially framed as the next generation of Apple Intelligence: natural multi-turn conversation, answers grounded in personal context (messages, email, photos), onscreen awareness, systemwide app actions, plus a dedicated Siri app whose conversation history syncs across devices via iCloud. Current capabilities and rollout status are on [Apple's Siri page](https://www.apple.com/siri/).

## Boundaries of use

Siri AI's availability depends on device, language, and region: it ships in beta with iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, and visionOS 27, in English at launch, with French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish following from October 2026; it is not available initially in the EU or in mainland China. On hardware, the press release sets the Apple Intelligence floor at iPhone 16 models or later (plus iPhone 15 Pro / Pro Max), M1-or-newer iPads and Macs, and more, with the strongest on-device model requiring newer chips still. Server-dependent features have daily usage limits, expanded access is planned for a fee, and the feature is unavailable to users under 13. The privacy design combines on-device processing with Private Cloud Compute. For the current language, region, and device list, check [Apple's Siri page](https://www.apple.com/siri/).

## Discussion in the show

In Weekly #002's chapter “界面标准与长期一致性为什么重要” (why interface standards and long-term consistency matter), the participants were scathing about Siri. [Juzi called Siri in 2026 "still an idiot," lumping it with Apple Car and Apple Vision to argue that a successful new Apple product is rare](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-5f00244ba4a06fad1124); he relayed an anecdote — [asking Siri to log a workout, where after confirmation Siri merely created a note](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-0cd6e70adb9e9eec37c9) — as evidence that it still lacks memory; and he marveled that [after fifteen years of iteration, from Tim Cook's rise through his departure, Siri remains at this level](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-5eec577ed761728d64bf).

Asked why, Yang Pan attributed it partly to the models, and partly to [Apple's obsession with privacy: AI is deeply entangled with privacy, and Apple will not move until local secure storage and encrypted cloud storage designs are settled](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-498f7c8e15b6988ae8f7). Xiangyang Qiaomu agreed Apple had shown little progress in AI. These are participant opinions in a critical conversation, recorded around the same episode where they praised other [iPhone](/en/wiki/products/iphone) design decisions; Siri's actual capabilities are a matter for official documentation and personal experience.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Siri?

Siri is the assistant built into Apple devices, first shipped with the iPhone 4S in 2011, usable by voice or text across iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and Apple Vision Pro. See [Apple's Siri page](https://www.apple.com/siri/).

### Which languages does Siri AI support? Can I use it in mainland China?

[Siri AI](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/) shipped in beta with the 2026 fall systems in English, adding French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish from October 2026; the press release states it is not available initially in the EU or in mainland China. Check [Apple's Siri page](https://www.apple.com/siri/) for the current rollout.

### Which devices can run Siri AI?

Per the [official press release](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/), Siri AI follows the Apple Intelligence device floor: iPhone 16 models or later (plus iPhone 15 Pro / Pro Max), iPad mini (A17 Pro) and M1-or-newer iPads, M1-or-newer Macs, Apple Vision Pro, and Apple Watch paired with an Apple Intelligence-capable iPhone. The strongest on-device model requires newer hardware.
