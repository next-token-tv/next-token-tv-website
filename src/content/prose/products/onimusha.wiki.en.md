## Onimusha

Onimusha is an action game series developed and published by Capcom. The first title, Onimusha: Warlords, launched on PlayStation 2 in 2001, retelling stories of historical Japanese figures with supernatural elements, with third-person hack-and-slash combat and puzzle elements at its core (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onimusha)).

## The series and the new title

The mainline series went quiet for years after 2006's Onimusha: Dawn of Dreams. The new entry, Onimusha: Way of the Sword, launched on September 4, 2026 for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, Windows, and Nintendo Switch 2 — the first mainline installment since 2006 — and sold one million copies on its launch day (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Onimusha:_Way_of_the_Sword)). Capcom's series introduction is on its [official licensing page](https://www.capcom.sg/licensing/detail_11.html).

## Discussion in the show

Episode 009, chapter 4 ("软件和游戏网文化与实践" — [the web-novelization of software and games](/weekly/009/transcript#chapter-04)), uses Onimusha as a reference point for the scale Chinese teams are now delivering. Guizang says [Phantom Blade Zero](/en/wiki/products/phantom-blade-zero) was built by a team of several hundred people over three or four years, and [names Onimusha as the comparison: it also sold well, and Phantom Blade Zero's scale feels like ten times Onimusha's](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-3a3e3efffc44ae92110b). Given the recording date and the "sold well" wording, he is referring to Onimusha: Way of the Sword, released September 4, 2026. This is a participant's impression about game scale, not an official figure for production size or sales.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Onimusha series?

Capcom's action game series, whose first entry reached PlayStation 2 in 2001, blending stories of historical Japanese figures with supernatural elements and centering on third-person hack-and-slash combat with puzzles (per English Wikipedia).

### What is the newest Onimusha game?

Onimusha: Way of the Sword, released September 4, 2026 — the first mainline installment since 2006's Onimusha: Dawn of Dreams — on PS5, Xbox Series X/S, Windows, and Nintendo Switch 2.

### How well has the series sold?

Per English Wikipedia, the series had sold 10 million copies worldwide as of September 2026, with Onimusha: Way of the Sword selling one million on launch day. For current figures, go by Capcom's financial reports.

### Which company makes Onimusha?

Capcom develops and publishes it; the company's series introduction is on its [official licensing page](https://www.capcom.sg/licensing/detail_11.html).
