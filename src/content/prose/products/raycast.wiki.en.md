## What Raycast is

Raycast is a launcher and productivity application developed by Raycast Technologies for macOS and Windows: a command window summoned by a keyboard shortcut, from which you launch apps, search files, and use built-in features such as clipboard history and text snippets. It is often described as a richer alternative to macOS's built-in Spotlight (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raycast_(software))). The official site is [raycast.com](https://www.raycast.com/) and the extension manual lives at [manual.raycast.com/extensions](https://manual.raycast.com/extensions).

According to the official blog, Raycast announced its public beta and seed round on October 29, 2020, and [Raycast for Windows entered public beta on November 20, 2025](https://www.raycast.com/blog/raycast-for-windows).

## Extension store and Raycast AI

Raycast itself is closed source, but it ships with a built-in extension store where extensions are written against the official API, and some extensions are open source (per English Wikipedia). The Extensions API and the store became generally available on November 30, 2021 (see the [official announcement](https://www.raycast.com/blog/series-a)).

AI capabilities are layered onto the same interface as Raycast AI; the official blog states AI was added to Raycast in 2023 (see the [pricing post](https://www.raycast.com/blog/changing-how-raycast-ai-is-priced)).

## How it differs from Spotlight

Spotlight is the search entry point built into macOS, focused on built-in retrieval; Raycast's distinguishing points are its programmable extension ecosystem and AI integration. Both center on keyboard-driven interaction (per English Wikipedia and the [official page](https://www.raycast.com/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Raycast?

A keyboard-first launcher and productivity app that combines app launching, clipboard history, snippets, extensions, and Raycast AI in one command window, developed by Raycast Technologies. See the [official site](https://www.raycast.com/).

### Which platforms does Raycast support?

The macOS version entered public beta in October 2020, and the Windows version entered public beta on November 20, 2025 (per the official blog); the official site also lists an iOS entry. Downloads are on the [official page](https://www.raycast.com/).

### Is Raycast free?

It is free to download and use, with paid subscriptions that include AI features; plans and prices are on the [official pricing page](https://www.raycast.com/pricing).

### What is Raycast AI?

Raycast's built-in AI layer, added to the product in 2023; its pricing moved to usage-based billing in September 2026 (per the [official blog](https://www.raycast.com/blog/changing-how-raycast-ai-is-priced)). Current plans are on the official pricing page.

### How do I build an extension for Raycast?

Write an extension against the official Extensions API and submit it to the store; the getting-started material and reference are in the [extension manual](https://manual.raycast.com/extensions).
