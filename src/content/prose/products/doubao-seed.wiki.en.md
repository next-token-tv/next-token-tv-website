## What Doubao Seed is

Doubao Seed is a model family developed by ByteDance's Seed team, covering general agentic tasks, coding, multimodal understanding, and reasoning. It is the name of a model family, not a consumer app: the product end users touch is the Doubao assistant (see [Doubao](/en/wiki/products/doubao)), while the [official Seed site](https://seed.bytedance.com/) publishes the team's research and releases. The same team also produces models in other directions, such as Seedance for video generation (see [Seedance](/en/wiki/products/seedance)) and Seedream for image generation.

On June 23, 2026, the Seed team [released the Seed2.1 model family](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/seed2-1-officially-released-advancing-ai-productivity), officially positioned as "a new generation of agent-capable models built for real-world productivity", iterating on Seed2.0.

## Uses and boundaries

Per the official announcement, Seed2.1's main focus areas are:

- **General agent capability**: multi-step tasks across tools and environments, such as project planning, document processing, and computer/GUI use;
- **Coding**: end-to-end development work from requirement analysis through implementation, bug fixing, environment setup, and validation;
- **Multimodal understanding and reasoning**: chart understanding, long-context, and multilingual capabilities.

For availability, the announcement states that Seed2.1 is accessible through Doubao and Volcano Engine ([Volcano Engine](/en/wiki/brands/volcengine)): consumers use these models indirectly inside the Doubao assistant, while developers integrate through Volcano Engine's model services. The announcement does not describe a rolling-update scheme or future version numbering; model naming, versions, and availability are governed by the [official site](https://seed.bytedance.com/).

## FAQ

### What is Doubao Seed?

Doubao Seed is a model family developed by ByteDance's Seed team, focused on general agent tasks, coding, multimodal understanding, and reasoning; Seed2.1 was officially released on June 23, 2026. See the [official announcement](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/seed2-1-officially-released-advancing-ai-productivity).

### What is the relationship between Doubao Seed and Doubao?

Doubao is the consumer assistant app; Doubao Seed is one of the model families behind it. Some of what Doubao users experience comes from Seed-team models, and developers integrate through Volcano Engine. See [Doubao](/en/wiki/products/doubao) and the [Seed site](https://seed.bytedance.com/).

### Where can Seed 2.1 be used?

The official announcement states it is accessible through Doubao and Volcano Engine: individual users get it inside the Doubao assistant, and developers integrate through Volcano Engine's model services.

### Who is the Seed team?

Seed is ByteDance's ([ByteDance](/en/wiki/brands/bytedance)) in-house AI research team; official information is published at [seed.bytedance.com](https://seed.bytedance.com/).
