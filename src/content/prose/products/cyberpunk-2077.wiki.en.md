## Cyberpunk 2077

Cyberpunk 2077 is an open-world action role-playing game developed by CD PROJEKT RED, set in the fictional megalopolis of Night City. Players control the mercenary V, drawn into a job involving an implant said to hold the key to immortality. The game first shipped on December 10, 2020 for Windows, PlayStation 4, and Xbox One, and later reached more platforms: PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on February 15, 2022, Nintendo Switch 2 as a launch title on June 5, 2025, and macOS on July 17, 2025.

## Expansion and spin-offs

The official spy-thriller expansion Phantom Liberty (released September 2023, set in the walled-off district of Dogtown) and the anime Cyberpunk: Edgerunners, based on the same universe, are part of the franchise. Current editions, updates, and platform support belong on the [official website](https://www.cyberpunk.net/).

## Discussion in the show

In Weekly #004’s chapter “Muse Charm, Phones, and the Entry Point for AI,” [Guizang brings up Cyberpunk 2077 while discussing excess phone performance and closed systems in the Chinese transcript](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-5ad32031308990664471): in his view, today’s phones can run games of this kind without trouble, yet as closed systems they cannot even execute a command, and agents cannot access anything on them. The remark is an analogy about the gap between hardware capability and system openness, not a review of the game.

## Frequently asked questions

### What type of game is Cyberpunk 2077?

An open-world action RPG with combat, stealth, hacking, and weapon customization, set in Night City.

### When was Cyberpunk 2077 released?

December 10, 2020 on Windows, PS4, and Xbox One; it later came to PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2, and macOS.

### Can I play Cyberpunk 2077 on Switch 2?

Yes. The game launched alongside the Nintendo Switch 2 on June 5, 2025; check the [official website](https://www.cyberpunk.net/) for editions and storage requirements.

### What is the relation between Edgerunners and the game?

Cyberpunk: Edgerunners is an anime adaptation set in the same universe; the [official site](https://www.cyberpunk.net/) collects the works of the Cyberpunk universe.
