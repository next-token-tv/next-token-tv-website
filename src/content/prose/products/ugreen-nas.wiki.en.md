## What UGREEN NAS is

UGREEN NAS is the network-attached storage product range from UGREEN, officially branded "UGREEN NAS Private Cloud" and positioned as a centralized data center for homes and small teams (see the [official site](https://www.ugnas.com/)). Compared with Xiaomi Smart Storage (see [Xiaomi Smart Storage](/en/wiki/products/xiaomi-smart-storage)), which leans on the smart-home ecosystem, and Synology (see [Synology NAS](/en/wiki/products/synology-nas)), which is known for its enterprise-grade system, UGREEN takes a general consumer-NAS route, covering file, backup, and media scenarios through its self-developed system and app center.

## Product lines and system

The hardware lines listed on the official site include:

- **DXP series**: the main multi-bay models, such as DXP2800 GT, DXP4800 GT, DXP6800 Ultra, and DXP8800 Ultra;
- **DH series**: entry two- and four-bay models, such as DH2300 and DH4300 Plus;
- **iDX series**: such as the iDX6011 Pro;
- Companion power devices such as the **US7000 and US3000 smart UPS**.

On the software side, the company offers UGOS Pro (the newer system) and the older UGOS, with an online demo available. Current models and configurations are governed by the [official site](https://www.ugnas.com/).

## Uses and boundaries

Per the official site, the main features are:

- **File management**: centralized storage, file sync and backup, sharing and collaboration, permission management, and external sharing links;
- **Backup**: one-click automatic backup, lossless photo backup with iPhone Live Photo support;
- **Media and photos**: a cinema app with 4K transcoding, Dolby Vision, and HDMI output; an AI photo album with face recognition, semantic search, and text extraction;
- **Remote access**: officially described as reachable from outside the home without a public IP address;
- **App center**: one-click installation of media, office, development, and other app categories.

Clients cover phones, tablets, computers, smart TVs, browsers, and mini-programs, with one app covering all features. Model prices, per-model specifications, and third-party app ecosystems are governed by official materials; this page does not fix numbers.

## FAQ

### What is UGREEN NAS?

UGREEN NAS Private Cloud is UGREEN's network-attached storage product range for centralized file management, multi-device backup, family sharing, and media storage. See the [official site](https://www.ugnas.com/).

### What is UGOS Pro?

UGOS Pro is UGREEN's newer NAS operating system, offered alongside the older UGOS, covering file management, backup, media, AI photos, and an app center; the official site provides a comparison of the two systems and an online demo.

### Can UGREEN NAS be accessed from outside the home?

Yes. The company describes remote access without a public IP address, letting users view and download files on the home device through the client while away; connection details are in the official documentation.

### How do I choose a UGREEN NAS model?

The official site divides models into the DXP, DH, and iDX series by bay count and positioning, with companion UPS devices. Match bay count, performance, and budget against the current lineup on the official site.
