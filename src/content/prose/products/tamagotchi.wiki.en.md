## What is Tamagotchi

Tamagotchi (Japanese: たまごっち) is Bandai's (a division of Bandai Namco Holdings) family of handheld virtual pet toys. The original was developed by Aki Maita of Bandai and Akihiro Yokoi of WiZ, released in Japan on November 23, 1996, and in the United States on May 1, 1997; the name combines "tamago," meaning egg, with "watch," reflecting its original wristwatch concept ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamagotchi)). Players care for a virtual creature on an egg-shaped device, feeding and cleaning it as it grows.

## Series history

The first Tamagotchi became a fad that faded around 1998; the series returned in March 2004 with "Kaette Kita! Tamagotchi Plus," which added infrared communication, released overseas from August 2004 as Tamagotchi Connection ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamagotchi)). The first color model arrived in November 2008. Later milestones on the official history page include the October 2018 re-release of the original, Tamagotchi Uni in July 2023, and Tamagotchi Paradise with its Zoom Dial feature, released worldwide from July 2025 ([official history page](https://tamagotchi-official.com/us/history/)). The product lines listed on the official site are Paradise, Original, Connection, Uni, and Nano ([official website](https://tamagotchi-official.com/us/)).

## Sales and influence

The official history page records that over 100 million units have been shipped worldwide since 1996 (as of July 2025) ([official history page](https://tamagotchi-official.com/us/history/)). The two developers won the 1997 Ig Nobel Prize in economics ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamagotchi)). As the archetypal handheld digital pet, the Tamagotchi is often used as a reference point for later small pocket-sized smart devices.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is the official Tamagotchi website?

Bandai's official site is [tamagotchi-official.com](https://tamagotchi-official.com/us/), with news, history, characters, and product pages, and regional versions.

### What is a Tamagotchi?

It is Bandai's family of handheld virtual pet toys, first released in Japan on November 23, 1996, in which players raise a virtual creature on an egg-shaped device ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamagotchi)).

### Who invented the Tamagotchi, and what does the name mean?

The original was developed by Aki Maita of Bandai and Akihiro Yokoi of WiZ; the name combines the Japanese "tamago" (egg) with "watch," from the original wristwatch concept ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamagotchi)).

### Are Tamagotchi still being made?

Yes. The official history page records over 100 million units shipped worldwide as of July 2025, and Tamagotchi Paradise with its Zoom Dial feature was released worldwide from July 2025; current lines are on the [official products page](https://tamagotchi-official.com/us/).
