## What Chatterfly is

Chatterfly is an AI voice-input tool from [Tencent](/en/wiki/brands/tencent). The official site describes it as supporting "dual voice and keyboard input modes, intelligently recognizing different scenes and intents, and invoking Skills for deep polishing, turning casually spoken words into well-formed writing," with the tagline “不必想好，开口就好” ("no need to think it through, just start speaking"). The official entry point is [chatterfly.tencent.com](https://chatterfly.tencent.com/).

Two distinctions matter. In the show, participants separated it from an "input method": it is a standalone voice-input app, not a keyboard input method. Also, searching "chatterfly" mixes in an unrelated social-skills training app of the same name, which has nothing to do with Tencent’s product.

## Usage and boundaries

Per the official description, the workflow is to speak a passage, have the model polish it with scene and intent awareness, and then output the text, with the keyboard as a second mode. Skills are its distinguishing feature: a spoken passage can be routed to a specific Skill for deeper processing, which the official copy calls "invoking Skills for deep polishing."

On boundaries, the show flagged its positioning: Guizang pointed out that its Skill is essentially still a prompt and cannot operate the computer or execute tools. Supported platforms, download channels, and pricing are not listed on the official page; the [official site](https://chatterfly.tencent.com/) is the reference. Comparable products include [Typeless](/en/wiki/products/typeless), [Shandianshuo](/en/wiki/products/shandianshuo), and the voice features of WeChat’s input method.

## Discussion in the show

Weekly #003’s chapter “语音输入产品与实时反馈” (“Voice-input products and live feedback”) covers the product:

- [Guizang first called it “another AI input method from Tencent,” then corrected himself: “it is not an input method … it is an app”](/weekly/003/transcript#quote-24183c8fd06402faad2a), and [Xiangyang Qiaomu confirmed the name](/weekly/003/transcript#quote-197fe93e9efc53bd4a0f).
- [Yang Pan placed it in the same category as Typeless and named NetEase Youdao’s Ba-ge as another peer](/weekly/003/transcript#quote-5c87d0e8760938933ef2); [he called the Skill feature — processing a spoken passage with a specific Skill — an innovation](/weekly/003/transcript#quote-c197016df45f87dd74c1).
- In the trade-off discussion that followed, Guizang noted the Skill is essentially a prompt and cannot execute tools, while Xiangyang Qiaomu argued Shandianshuo had done this long ago and that the direction overcomplicates the product. These are participant assessments based on their own experience, not official statements.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Chatterfly?

In this article, Chatterfly refers to Tencent’s AI voice-input tool: dual voice and keyboard input that recognizes scene and intent, polishes the output, and can invoke Skills for deeper processing. Be aware that an unrelated social-skills training app shares the name.

### Is Chatterfly an input method?

No — it is a standalone voice-input app, not a keyboard input method. Guizang made this distinction in the show; in the official description, the keyboard is only the second of its two input modes.

### What are Chatterfly’s Skills?

Skills are its distinguishing feature: after speaking a passage, you can assign a specific Skill to process or polish it further. The official copy calls this "invoking Skills for deep polishing"; show participants caution that it is essentially prompt-based processing and cannot execute tools.

### Where can I get Chatterfly?

The official entry point is [chatterfly.tencent.com](https://chatterfly.tencent.com/). Supported platforms and download channels are not listed on the official page; the site itself is the reference.
