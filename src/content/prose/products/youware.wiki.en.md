## What YouWare is

YouWare is an AI agent workspace for knowledge workers, provided by YouWare. According to the [official website](https://www.youware.com/), users hand tasks to AI agent teams that produce editable deliverables: websites, slide decks (exportable as PowerPoint or PDF), research reports, and spreadsheets. The system accepts PDF, PPTX, Word, Excel, and CSV files as input and works from the user's own materials and data.

The official page also describes a multi-agent workflow with roles such as project manager, researcher, data analyst, writer and designer, and reviewer; outputs show shown calculations and flag low-confidence items. Users can pick different underlying models — the site lists Gemini 3 Pro, Claude Sonnet, and GPT-5-Codex — without applying for API keys. Per the website, uploaded files are used only for the given task and are not used for model training.

## Usage and entries

The main entry is the [official website](https://www.youware.com/), which offers a free sign-up. Accounts work across the desktop web and mobile apps on the App Store and Google Play, with files, context, and progress syncing between devices. Generated websites can be published directly; documents can be exported to PowerPoint or PDF or continued in the user's existing tools. The site also lists Docs, a help center, a blog, and a Discord community.

Plan details and billing structure are on the [official pricing page](https://www.youware.com/pricing/); this page does not restate prices.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is YouWare?

An AI agent workspace for knowledge workers: you assign tasks in natural language, and agent teams deliver editable websites, slide decks, reports, and spreadsheets. See the [official website](https://www.youware.com/).

### Where are the YouWare website and login?

The website is [youware.com](https://www.youware.com/); sign in from the site's navigation. There are also iOS and Android apps.

### Does YouWare have a mobile app?

Yes. The official site links apps on the App Store and Google Play, which sync files and progress with the desktop web.

### Is YouWare free?

The official site offers free sign-up to try it. Full plans and billing are on the [official pricing page](https://www.youware.com/pricing/).
