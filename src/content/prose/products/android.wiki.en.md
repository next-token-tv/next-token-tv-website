## What is Android

Android is an open mobile operating system led by [Google](/en/wiki/brands/google), first released in September 2008 on the T-Mobile G1, the first commercial Android phone. Its core is opened through the Android Open Source Project (AOSP), and device makers build deeply customized versions on top of AOSP. The Android trademark is owned by Google LLC.

Per the official site, Android has expanded from phones to tablets, watches, TVs (Android TV), cars (Android Auto), and XR headsets and glasses (Android XR). For branding, Google [unveiled a refreshed Android visual identity](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/modern-look/) on September 5, 2023: the wordmark capitalized the "A", and the robot got an entirely new 3D look.

## Ecosystem and boundaries

- Apps and distribution: consumer apps are mainly distributed through Google Play; developer resources include [developer.android.com](https://developer.android.com/), Android Studio and the SDK, and the AOSP source.
- AI integration: the official site lists [Gemini](/en/wiki/products/gemini) as the AI assistant on Android, alongside system-level features such as Circle to Search and Scam Detection for calls.
- Versions and upgrades: platform evolution is driven by Google, but when consumers actually receive updates depends on the device maker and model — a main source of Android ecosystem fragmentation. Current version information is on the [official site](https://www.android.com/).
- Boundaries: what is open is the system core; Google Play, Gemini, and other Google Mobile Services are separately licensed commercial components. Devices sold in the Chinese mainland market typically ship without Google services, and Chinese vendors routinely customize the system interface and preinstalled software.

## FAQ

### Who develops Android, and is it open source?

Android is led by Google, and the system core is open through the Android Open Source Project (AOSP), which device makers customize. Google Play, Gemini, and other Google Mobile Services are separately licensed components. The Android trademark belongs to Google LLC.

### What is the latest Android version?

As of September 2026, the Android official site lists Android 17 as its newest version. Release cadence and features are governed by the [official site](https://www.android.com/) and the [developer site](https://developer.android.com/).

### How do I check and update my Android version?

Open Settings and tap About phone to see your Android version; system updates are installed through the Settings app or system notifications, per [Google's official support note](https://support.google.com/android/answer/7680439). Update timing is decided by the device maker and varies widely across brands and models.

### How do Gemini and Android relate?

The official site promotes Gemini as the AI assistant on Android, along with system-level AI features such as Circle to Search. Gemini's features and availability are governed by [Google's official page](https://gemini.google.com/).
