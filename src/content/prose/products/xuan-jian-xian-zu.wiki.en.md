## Definition

Xuan Jian Xian Zu (玄鉴仙族) is a family-cultivation web novel by Ji Yueren, serialized on [Qidian](/en/wiki/products/qidian). It follows a family across generations in a cultivation world, tracing its rise and continuity (see the [work's page on Qidian](https://www.qidian.com/book/1035420986/)).

## Discussed on Next Token

In episode 008, in the chapter "AI 短剧：个人与小团队的创作机会" ([AI short dramas: a creation opportunity for individuals and small teams](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-04); Chinese transcript), Guizang used the web-novel industry as an analogy for the barrier to making AI dramas: according to his account, the novel's author is a Platinum-tier (白金) author on Qidian — a rank usually reached over multiple books — and this book reached it before completion; he also noted that the author was about 21 or 22 years old in college, typed every chapter on a phone, and updated about 3,000 characters a day. The point was that when the tooling barrier is low enough, taste and storytelling decide the work. These details are the participant's relay on the show.
