## Black Myth: Wukong

Black Myth: Wukong is an action role-playing game developed and published by Game Science, inspired by Chinese mythology. It launched on Windows and PlayStation 5 on August 20, 2024, and reached Xbox Series X/S on August 20, 2025 (per [Chinese Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/黑神话：悟空)). Xinhua has called it China's first "AAA" game. The official page is [gamesci.cn/wukong](https://gamesci.cn/wukong).

## Subject matter and genre

The game adapts Journey to the West and Chinese mythology with its own retelling: players fight through a single-player action adventure against mythological figures. Story details are a reimagining rather than a scene-by-scene retelling of the novel; specifics follow the official public introduction.

## Discussion in the show

Episode 009, chapter 4 ("软件和游戏网文化与实践" — [the web-novelization of software and games](/weekly/009/transcript#chapter-04)), discusses [Phantom Blade Zero](/en/wiki/products/phantom-blade-zero) with Black Myth: Wukong as the comparison point. Yang Pan relays online reactions of the time — ["they say Black Myth: Wukong is trash in front of Phantom Blade Zero"](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-42832bbfca48beedf18c) — and Guizang adds that the gap is mainly [in map size and content volume](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-1ea2efe8a666503843a4). These are participants relaying player chatter and personal impressions, not a review verdict on either game.

## Frequently asked questions

### What type of game is Black Myth: Wukong?

A single-player action RPG set in Chinese mythology, developed and published by Game Science; the official introduction is at [gamesci.cn/wukong](https://gamesci.cn/wukong).

### When did Black Myth: Wukong launch, and on which platforms?

August 20, 2024 on Windows and PlayStation 5; August 20, 2025 on Xbox Series X/S (per Chinese Wikipedia).

### Who developed Black Myth: Wukong?

### How does Black Myth: Wukong relate to Journey to the West?

It takes Journey to the West and Chinese mythology as source material and reimagines them: characters and settings reference the novel, but the plot is an original retelling rather than a chapter-by-chapter adaptation.

That was player discourse the participants relayed while recording episode 009: the topic was Phantom Blade Zero's map and content volume, and Black Myth: Wukong came up as the benchmark. It is a personal impression — judge each game on its own official materials and reviews.
