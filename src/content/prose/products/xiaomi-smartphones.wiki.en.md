## Definition

Xiaomi smartphones are [Xiaomi](/en/wiki/brands/xiaomi)'s smartphone family, spanning multiple generations and product lines with different hardware configurations. The official store's "phones" category lists the Xiaomi numeric flagship, MIX, and Civi lines, along with the REDMI K, Turbo, Note, and numeric series (see the [Xiaomi store category page](https://www.mi.com/shop/category/list)); current models and specifications are governed by the store's live pages.

## How it works

Xiaomi phones ship with [Xiaomi HyperOS](/en/wiki/products/hyperos), which provides built-in AI assistant capabilities such as [HyperXiaoAI](/en/wiki/products/hyperxiaoai) and ties into Xiaomi's other hardware through accounts and device-interconnection features. The feature set varies by model and system version; specific capabilities are governed by the official system documentation and each model's product page.

## Usage

Everyday smartphone use: communication, imaging, and mobile apps. In the Next Token context, Xiaomi phones also come up in old-device repurposing topics: the community flashes third-party systems onto older handsets or turns them into single-purpose devices — the use episode 007 touched on.

## Related and confusable terms

- This entry covers only the smartphone line; the [Xiaomi](/en/wiki/brands/xiaomi) brand entry covers the company's whole product matrix (phones, TVs, IoT devices, cars, and more).
- REDMI is a product line listed alongside the Xiaomi series in the store's "phones" category; the two share one category but are positioned differently, and the exact split is governed by the official store.
- Separate entries cover other Xiaomi hardware lines such as [Xiaomi TV](/en/wiki/products/xiaomi-tv) and [XiaoAI speakers](/en/wiki/products/xiaoai-speakers); each corresponds to its own product line.

## Frequently asked questions

### What product lines do Xiaomi smartphones have?

Per the [Xiaomi store category page](https://www.mi.com/shop/category/list), the "phones" category lists the Xiaomi numeric flagship, MIX, and Civi lines, plus the REDMI K, Turbo, Note, and numeric series; available models and naming change over time, so check the store's live pages.

### What system do Xiaomi phones run?

Xiaomi phones ship with Xiaomi HyperOS; see the [HyperOS entry](/en/wiki/products/hyperos) and the [official HyperOS page](https://hyperos.mi.com/).

### Can Xiaomi phones be flashed with other systems?

Flashing means replacing the operating system on the device. It is community practice with prerequisites such as unlocking the bootloader, plus limits and risks. What the official channels support for unlocking and system installation is governed by Xiaomi's own documentation; the flashing mentioned in episode 007 is a participant's passing remark, without a specific model or procedure.
