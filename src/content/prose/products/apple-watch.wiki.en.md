## What Apple Watch is

Apple Watch is [Apple](/en/wiki/brands/apple)'s smartwatch family, running watchOS and designed to pair with the iPhone. The official product page lists the current lines as Apple Watch Series (health-focused, "The ultimate way to watch your health"), Apple Watch Ultra (sports and adventure), and Apple Watch SE (essential health features at a lower price), plus Nike and Hermès editions. Models and configurations follow the [official product page](https://www.apple.com/watch/).

Announced on September 9, 2026, Apple Watch Series 12 introduced the new Health Sensing System: according to the official press release, it uses new optical and electrical heart sensors to measure heart rate every five seconds all day long, measures HRV up to 24 times more often, and adds a 0–10 readiness score. The S11 chip also brings Audio Intelligence features — Sound Recognition, Live Rewind, and Siri Recap — protected through the S11's Secure Exclave. Series 12 became available on September 18 starting at $399 in the US; see the [official press release](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/introducing-apple-watch-series-12-with-the-all-new-health-sensing-system/).

## Usage and boundaries

Apple Watch requires an iPhone: the official product page notes that Series 12, Ultra 4, and SE 3 require iPhone 11 or later running iOS 27 or later. Health and workout data captured by the watch is viewed in the iPhone Health app; watchOS 27 began arriving on September 14, 2026 for Series 9 and later, SE 3, and Ultra 2 or later. Paired with AirPods, the watch can take calls, play music, and handle messages without the iPhone nearby. The basic lineup logic: Series for the fullest health experience, Ultra for durability and outdoor battery life, SE for essential health features at a lower price.
