## What Oura Ring is

Oura Ring is a smart ring from Oura Health for sleep, recovery, and everyday health tracking: the ring collects physiological signals against the skin, and the data is read in the Oura App on iOS and Android. The official site says it tracks more than 50 health metrics spanning sleep, activity, stress, heart health, and women's health. The official site is [ouraring.com](https://ouraring.com/), and the newest generation it currently promotes is the Oura Ring 5. The ring is made of titanium inside and out with skin-contacting sensors; the official product page for Oura Ring 4 describes a 5–8 day battery life depending on size and usage.

## Usage and boundaries

You wear the ring around the clock and interpret the data in the app: the ring collects continuously, while the app provides sleep, readiness, and activity scores plus long-term trends. The official Oura Membership page describes the subscription as part of the complete experience: members get personalized health insights and features such as Oura Advisor, described officially as an AI-powered health companion. New members get a free first month; current pricing and benefits are on the [official Membership page](https://ouraring.com/membership).

Boundaries: it is a health-tracking device, not a medical device — health concerns belong with a doctor. Its readings depend on wear habits and algorithms, and accuracy claims should be checked against the official validation material. Compared with watches, the ring form trades screens and notifications for a discreet, always-on fit.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Oura Ring?

The Oura Ring is a smart ring from Oura Health for sleep, recovery, and everyday health tracking, covering more than 50 health metrics read through the Oura App. See the [official site](https://ouraring.com/).

### Do I need a membership to use an Oura Ring?

A subscription is not required to pair the ring with the Oura App and see basic data, but the official site frames Oura Membership as part of the complete experience: personalized insights and features like Oura Advisor are member benefits. Whether it is worth subscribing depends on the features you want; the benefit list is on the [official Membership page](https://ouraring.com/membership).

### How much does Oura Membership cost?

As of September 2026, the official Membership page lists $5.99 USD per month, with the first month free for new members (conditions apply per the page). Current pricing and benefits are on the [official page](https://ouraring.com/membership).

### What is the difference between Oura Ring 4 and Oura Ring 5?

They are successive hardware generations. The site currently promotes the Oura Ring 5, calling it "the world's smallest smart ring"; the Oura Ring 4 product page describes its all-titanium build, Smart Sensing, and 5–8 day battery. For specification differences, check the [official product pages](https://ouraring.com/product/rings).

### How is an Oura Ring different from an Apple Watch?

The form and focus differ: the Oura Ring is a screen-less ring for health tracking, with data read in a phone app, while the Apple Watch is a fuller smartwatch that also handles notifications and apps. Health tracking is where they overlap, with different metrics on each side. See the [Apple Watch](/en/wiki/products/apple-watch) entry.
