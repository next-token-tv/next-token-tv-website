## What soundcore Work is

soundcore Work is a portable AI voice recorder from soundcore, the audio brand under Anker. Participants in episode 008 of Next Token referred to it as the "Anker recording bean" (安克录音豆). The device is coin-sized and, per the official page, weighs about 10 grams; it can be worn as a button, clipped to a collar or worn on a necklace with a magnetic mount, or placed in its companion dock.

## Usage and features

soundcore Work targets wearable recording for meetings and interviews, with AI-based organization afterwards:

- Recording and battery: the official page claims 8 hours of standalone recording and up to 32 hours of battery life with the dock, plus a microphone pickup radius of 5 meters said to cover meetings of eight to ten people.
- Transcription and summaries: the official page says transcription is powered by ChatGPT, with more than 25 templates and more than 100 languages, plus smart organization and key-point summaries.
- Operation and transfer: the device records on its own as soon as it is powered on, without a phone present; recordings transfer over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Double-tapping marks key moments for later review. The page also lists MFi support for recording-and-transferring with iPhone and Find My support.

These are the official page's claims; available features and subscription plans vary by region and version, and the official documentation is authoritative.

## Using it and how it differs

soundcore Work is a record-first device: no phone is needed while recording, and the companion app retrieves the audio for transcription and summaries afterwards. Compared with recording a meeting on a phone, the difference is wearable sound pickup and longer battery life; compared with other wearable AI recorders, its distinguishing points are the dock-based battery scheme and the transcription template system inside the soundcore app.
