## What Google Antigravity is

Google Antigravity is Google's agentic development platform. The official site presents it as built for the agent-first era, "allowing anyone to build" software. Google splits the product into four surfaces: the Antigravity App (a command center for managing multiple local agents in parallel, with Projects, multiple workspaces, and scheduled messages), the Antigravity IDE (a fully featured agentic IDE with the agent manager, artifacts, and codebase understanding), the Antigravity CLI (a lightweight, terminal-first surface for autonomous coding agents and background subagents), and the Antigravity SDK (prototyping custom agents on the Antigravity agent harness). It is part of [Google](/en/wiki/brands/google)'s developer product line and is closely tied to [Gemini](/en/wiki/products/gemini) on the model side.

## Uses and boundaries

Desktop builds cover macOS, Windows, and Linux, with downloads on the [official download page](https://antigravity.google/). According to the [official pricing page](https://antigravity.google/pricing), individual developers get a free plan (marked Generally Available, with basic rate limits); higher rate limits and credit pools are tied to Google AI Pro and Google AI Ultra subscriptions; organizations access it via Google Cloud, and Gemini Enterprise customers can connect through subscriptions or consumption-based API pricing. Current limits and prices follow the official pricing page.

Model availability is announced through the official blog and site banner: on October 13, 2026, the official blog announced Gemini 4 Argon inside Google Antigravity for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, covering the desktop app and the CLI; businesses can use it via the Gemini platform API or eligible Gemini Enterprise subscriptions. Which models are available on which surface follows the official changelog and blog.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where are Google Antigravity's official site and download?

The official site is [antigravity.google](https://antigravity.google/); the download page offers macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows, and Linux builds.

### Is Google Antigravity free?

The official pricing page includes a free individual plan with basic rate limits; higher limits are tied to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscriptions, and organizations access it via Google Cloud and Gemini Enterprise. Current rates are on the [official pricing page](https://antigravity.google/pricing).

### What is the difference between Antigravity IDE and Antigravity?

Antigravity is the name of the whole agentic development platform; the Antigravity IDE is one surface among several — the same brand also covers the App, CLI, extensions, and the SDK. The official product list is on the [official site](https://antigravity.google/).

### Which models can be used in Google Antigravity?

The model list is set by official announcements — for example, the official blog announced Gemini 4 Argon in the Antigravity desktop app and CLI on October 13, 2026, for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers. Current model support follows the [official blog](https://antigravity.google/blog).
