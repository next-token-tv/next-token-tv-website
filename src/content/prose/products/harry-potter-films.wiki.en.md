## Definition

The Harry Potter film series is a fantasy film series adapted from the novels of British author J. K. Rowling, released by Warner Bros. Pictures in eight installments from 2001 to 2011, from Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone (2001) to Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 2 (2011). The films follow the young wizard Harry Potter through his years at Hogwarts and his confrontation with Lord Voldemort.

## Related and confusable terms

"Harry Potter" can refer to Rowling's original novels, these eight films, the stage play Harry Potter and the Cursed Child, video games, and theme-park attractions. This entry covers the film series only. The "gender-swapped Malfoy" video series mentioned on episode 008 is fan-created AI content (see below), not an official production.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many Harry Potter films are there?

Eight, released by Warner Bros. Pictures between 2001 and 2011. The Deathly Hallows adaptation was split into two parts, released in 2010 and 2011.

### Who wrote the novels the films are based on?

The films are adapted from the Harry Potter novel series by J. K. Rowling.

### Where can I find official information about the films?

The official fan site Wizarding World (harrypotter.com) has a films section covering each installment (see the [official page](https://www.harrypotter.com/discover/films)).
