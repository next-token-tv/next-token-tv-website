## Ready Player One

Ready Player One is a 2018 American science-fiction action film directed by Steven Spielberg, written by Zak Penn and Ernest Cline, based on Cline's 2011 novel of the same name, and released by Warner Bros. Pictures. It premiered at South by Southwest on March 11, 2018 and opened theatrically in the United States on March 29 (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ready_Player_One_%28film%29)). The film page is on [warnerbros.com](https://www.warnerbros.com/movies/ready-player-one).

## Subject matter and premise

The story is set in 2045, where much of humanity escapes a troubled real world through OASIS, a large shared virtual-reality universe. Teenage Wade Watts finds clues to a contest hidden inside OASIS whose winner gains ownership of it; he and his friends race to complete the challenge before a corporation seeking control can get there first. The source novel was published in 2011; the film curates the novel's pop-culture references and plot while keeping its central premise.

## Discussion in the show

Episode 010, chapter 10 ("恐龙、流体与古巴比伦：可复用的虚拟世界" — [dinosaurs, fluids and ancient Babylon: reusable virtual worlds](/weekly/010/transcript#chapter-10) in the Chinese transcript), Guizang, discussing how AI-generated 3D assets can be reused and remixed, says: ["we have all seen Ready Player One — that was our imagined ultimate game back then: a game where every game lives inside one world, and you can drop into any game world at any time"](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-3437031bd4a55a35d133). This is a participant borrowing the film's OASIS vision as an analogy for the topic of reusable virtual worlds — a reference to the film, not an analysis or review of it.

## Frequently asked questions

### When was Ready Player One released?

It premiered at South by Southwest on March 11, 2018 and opened in the United States on March 29, 2018, released by Warner Bros. Pictures (per English Wikipedia).

### Who directed Ready Player One?

Steven Spielberg, with a screenplay by Zak Penn and Ernest Cline.

### What is Ready Player One adapted from?

Ernest Cline's 2011 science-fiction novel of the same name; the film keeps the novel's core premise while curating its plot and pop-culture references.

### What is the OASIS?

The large shared virtual-reality universe imagined in the novel and the film, where people play, work and socialize; the central contest of the story is a fight for its ownership.

### Why did the show mention Ready Player One?

While discussing how AI-generated virtual-world assets can be reused and remixed in episode 010, Guizang invoked the film's "ultimate game" vision as an analogy — a participant's reference, not a film review from the show.
