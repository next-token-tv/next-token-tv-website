## NVIDIA GTC

GTC (GPU Technology Conference) is a technology conference hosted by [NVIDIA](/en/wiki/brands/nvidia) for developers, engineers, and researchers, with topics extending from GPUs and accelerated computing to AI, robotics, and healthcare. The first GTC was held September 30 to October 2, 2009 in San Jose, California (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPU_Technology_Conference)); Wikipedia describes it as a semi-annual conference (March and November), and each edition opens with a keynote from [Jensen Huang](/en/wiki/people/jensen-huang).

## What happens at the conference

GTC is a major launch venue for successive NVIDIA product generations: the A100, H100, and Blackwell GPUs, as well as systems such as the DGX-1, were announced at GTC editions (per the list of announcements on English Wikipedia). The program includes keynotes, technical sessions, and training, and the official page gathers information on the individual editions (see the [official NVIDIA GTC page](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/events/)).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is GTC?

A technology conference hosted by NVIDIA covering GPUs, accelerated computing, AI, robotics, and related fields, opening with a keynote by Jensen Huang; see the [official NVIDIA GTC page](https://www.nvidia.com/gtc/events/).

### When did GTC start?

The first GTC was held September 30 to October 2, 2009 in San Jose, California (per English Wikipedia).

### How often does GTC take place?

Wikipedia describes it as a semi-annual conference (March and November), mainly in San Jose, California, with editions also held in Taipei, Paris, and Washington, D.C.

### What products were announced at GTC?

The A100 (2020), H100/Hopper (2022), and Blackwell (2024), along with the earlier DGX-1 (2016), were announced at GTC editions (per the list on English Wikipedia).
