## What Lingguang is

Lingguang (灵光) is a multimodal general-purpose AI assistant from Ant Group. According to Ant Group’s official press release, the Lingguang app was released on November 18, 2025, launching with three features: Lingguang Chat (灵光对话), Flash Apps (灵光闪应用), and Lingguang Open Eyes (灵光开眼); Ant describes it as the industry’s first AI assistant to generate multimodal content as fully written code. The App Store listing names the developer as Alipay (Hangzhou) Technology Co., Ltd., and the app requires iOS 15.0 or later. The official website [lingguang.com](https://www.lingguang.com/) also serves as the web entry point.

## Features and use

Per the official description, the three launch features cover different ways of using the assistant:

- Lingguang Chat: answers questions with visual formats — spoken readouts, 3D models, generated illustrations, charts, interactive maps — plus source tracing.
- Flash Apps: officially described as generating a small interactive app from one sentence of natural language "in 30 seconds"; the accompanying Lingguang Circle (灵光圈) community is used to publish and share these apps.
- Lingguang Open Eyes: image and video generation plus visual understanding, including text-to-image, text-to-video, live scene understanding, and voice Q&A.

These are the official and App Store descriptions; actual behavior and availability are best judged in the app itself. ## FAQ

### What is Lingguang?

Lingguang is a multimodal general-purpose AI assistant from Ant Group, combining chat Q&A, small-app generation, and image/video generation and understanding. Ant’s press release dates the app’s launch to November 18, 2025.

### Who makes Lingguang?

Lingguang comes from Ant Group. The App Store developer is listed as Alipay (Hangzhou) Technology Co., Ltd. It is often called “蚂蚁灵光” (Ant Lingguang) and is not the same product as Alibaba’s Qianwen Go.

### Where can I download the Lingguang app?

On iPhone, search the App Store for “灵光-全模态AI助手” or enter through the [Lingguang website](https://www.lingguang.com/); download channels for other platforms follow the official site.

### What can Lingguang do?

The three launch features are Lingguang Chat (multimodal visual Q&A), Flash Apps (one-sentence generation of small interactive apps, shared via Lingguang Circle), and Lingguang Open Eyes (image/video generation and live visual understanding). Details are on the [official site](https://www.lingguang.com/).

### Is there a web version of Lingguang?

Yes. The [official site, lingguang.com](https://www.lingguang.com/), itself provides the web entry point.

### Is Lingguang free?

The app can be downloaded for free on the App Store; no official channel publishes a subscription price list, so any actual charges are as shown inside the app and on the official site.
