## What Qzone is

Qzone (QQ空间) is a social platform launched by Tencent in 2005 — a micro-blog-style personal space built on QQ friend relationships. It offers updates (说说), journal posts, sharing, photo albums, video, and a message board, and each space can be public or restricted to friends (per [Chinese Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/QQ%E7%A9%BA%E9%97%B4)). Its predecessor, "空间小窝," began internal testing in April 2005, and Qzone launched on June 6, 2005; in 2008 it fully transformed into a social network (per Chinese Wikipedia). Tencent's product overview is on the [Qzone page at tencent.com](https://www.tencent.com/zh-cn/products/qzone/); it is a [Tencent](/en/wiki/brands/tencent) product.

## Dress-up and Yellow Diamond

Qzone supports space customization — intro animations, skins, navigation bars, floating decorations — and per Chinese Wikipedia most decorations require paid memberships such as Yellow Diamond (黄钻) or Super Yellow Diamond, with only a few free. In its early years, the web platform's app center hosted third-party applications such as QQ Farm, QQ Ranch, and Car Parking that were hugely popular for a time (per Chinese Wikipedia).

## Discussion in the show

Episode 009, in the chapter "Harness 与插件生态" ([the Harness and plugin ecosystem](/weekly/009/transcript#chapter-01)), includes Orange defending skinning and desktop-pet plugins by analogy with Qzone's history: ["How can you say Qzone is useless? How much economic value did Qzone have?"](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-76ebe5300ef417e2ec30) Yang Pan quips that ["the moment you mention QQ Show, people say that's too old"](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-1fc2248acd728bb91240). These are nostalgic analogies that use Qzone's paid-dress-up business model to explain why young users pay for customization and emotional value — not a description of Qzone's current state or an endorsement.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Qzone?

Tencent's social platform launched in 2005, built on QQ friend relationships, where users post updates, journals, photos, and videos and can pay to decorate their space. See [Tencent's product overview](https://www.tencent.com/zh-cn/products/qzone/).

### When did Qzone launch?

June 6, 2005; its predecessor began internal testing inside Tencent in April 2005 (per Chinese Wikipedia).

### Does decorating a Qzone space cost money?

Per Chinese Wikipedia, most decorations require paid memberships such as Yellow Diamond or Super Yellow Diamond, with only a few free; specifics are described within the product.

### How is Qzone related to QQ?

Qzone is built on QQ friend relationships — a personal homepage and community beyond QQ messaging — and both are Tencent products.
