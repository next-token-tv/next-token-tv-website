## YouMind

YouMind is an AI studio for learning and content creation, operated by MIND MOTOR PTE. LTD. and founded by [Yubo](/en/wiki/people/yubo). The official site positions it as “Your AI studio for turning inspiration into your own work”: first collect materials such as articles, videos, and podcasts, then build understanding by questioning and exploring with AI, and finally turn that learning into writing, slides, images, videos, or web pages.

## Features and entry points

The official page lists AI Writing, AI Slides, AI Image, AI Video, and AI Webpage, along with Skills built and shared by creators; capture entry points include iOS and Android apps, macOS (beta), and a Chrome extension. Available models, features, and pricing change with product updates and belong on the [official site](https://youmind.com/).

## Discussion in the show

In Weekly #004’s chapter “Harvey: Open-Source Models and Product Moats,” while discussing whether products should let users choose models, [Yang Pan relays Yubo’s positioning for YouMind: “through me, YouMind, you can use all the frontier models,” in the Chinese transcript](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-47deb387eadb2abc0df0). Orange argues that choosing models is mainly a demand from AI-circle users, and Guizang adds that frontier models are too expensive. This is a participant discussion of the product’s positioning; the models YouMind actually supports belong on the official site.
