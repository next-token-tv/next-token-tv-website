## What CatPaw is

CatPaw is an all-scenario AI agent platform released by Meituan on July 28, 2026. The official announcement describes the launch as “Meituan’s all-scenario AI agent platform CatPaw, powered by LongCat 2.0, officially going live.” The official product site, [catpaw.meituan.com](https://catpaw.meituan.com/), carries the Chinese name Miaoshou (妙手) in its page title — “Miaoshou - All-Scenario AI Agent Platform.” This entry keeps CatPaw as the primary name.

The product has two sides: for individual users it is described as a ready-to-use AI workspace; for enterprise customers it offers agent development and hosting (Managed Agents) for building AI digital employees, with private deployment supported. The site’s own keywords include AI coding, code completion, a coding assistant, and IDE, so coding is one of its scenarios — but the platform does not stop at programming.

## Capabilities and boundaries

The official announcement lists a standalone mobile app and a PC client with two-way sync, cloud execution around the clock, and — on the enterprise side — digital employees that run inside messaging tools such as Feishu and WeCom and can be invoked with an @ mention. Merchant operations are an explicitly emphasized scenario: store operations, review management, marketing conversion, and fulfillment are packaged as installable “experts and skills,” with industry experts covering food delivery, service retail, and health services.

On the boundary side, the merchant-facing capabilities are described in the official article as an invitation for “Meituan partner merchants to get early access” — a priority program for partner merchants rather than general availability. Registration, available features, and quotas on the personal and enterprise sides follow the [official product site](https://catpaw.meituan.com/). No public pricing appears in the official materials, so payment terms should not be inferred from the feature list.

He then [summarized Meituan’s move as using agents to manage local-life merchants](/weekly/006/transcript#quote-fdbe18327f0e482abb7f). The “rename” is the participant’s retelling: the official tech blog launched the product as CatPaw on July 28, 2026, and as of October 2026 the product site carries the Miaoshou title — both are official materials, while the renaming itself has not been confirmed through official channels. These are the participants’ observations at the time; see the [Chinese transcript chapter](/weekly/006/transcript#chapter-15) for the full context.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is the CatPaw official site?

The official product site is [catpaw.meituan.com](https://catpaw.meituan.com/), titled “Miaoshou - All-Scenario AI Agent Platform.” The launch article is on [Meituan’s official tech blog](https://tech.meituan.com/2026/07/28/CatPaw-LongCat.html).

### What is the relationship between CatPaw and Miaoshou?

They are the same product: CatPaw is the English name used at launch, while the official site’s page title uses the Chinese name Miaoshou (妙手). This Wiki entry keeps CatPaw as the primary name.

### Who develops CatPaw?

CatPaw is developed by Meituan and was officially announced on July 28, 2026 through the company’s tech blog, which says the platform runs on Meituan’s open-source LongCat 2.0 model.

### What can CatPaw do?

Per the official materials, the personal side is an AI workspace (including AI coding and code completion), the enterprise side provides agent development and hosting, digital employees, and private deployment, and the merchant side packages store operations, reviews, marketing conversion, and fulfillment into installable experts and skills across food delivery, service retail, and health services.

### How much does CatPaw cost?

No public pricing appears in the official product materials. Registration, available features, and any payment terms follow [catpaw.meituan.com](https://catpaw.meituan.com/).
