## What Qiaomu AI RSS is

Qiaomu AI RSS is an RSS reading tool by [Xiangyang Qiaomu](/en/wiki/people/xiangyang-qiaomu) (GitHub account joeseesun), built as an [Obsidian](/en/wiki/products/obsidian) plugin: it reads the Qiaomu-curated feed selection and your own RSS/Atom subscriptions inside Obsidian, supports Chinese AI rewrites and translations, and saves articles as Markdown notes. The project is open source under the GPL-3.0 license in the [GitHub repository joeseesun/qiaomu-ai-rss](https://github.com/joeseesun/qiaomu-ai-rss).

## Usage and features

Per the repository README:

- Reading and saving: read the Qiaomu-curated feeds and your own subscriptions, save articles as Markdown notes, and add links to daily notes.
- Chinese reading: switch between the original text, a Chinese translation, and the Qiaomu AI rewrite; the README notes the rewrite and translation apply to already-published AI assets and never request new generation.
- Extras: podcast playback, YouTube embeds, OPML import/export, offline caching, read-aloud (TTS), and an opt-in link-capture/transcription lab feature.
- The desktop app supports Markdown and PDF export; mobile supports reading.

The README also states that the plugin was independently developed on top of the author's own QMReader iOS app and its public API, and is not a fork of other Obsidian RSS plugins.

## Using it and how it differs

Installation follows the usual Obsidian paths: the official community plugin library, BRAT, or manually downloading the release files into `.obsidian/plugins/qiaomu-ai-rss/` and enabling the plugin. What sets it apart from generic RSS readers is the Obsidian integration: articles land directly in your own Markdown vault, and the Chinese translation and rewrite options lower the cost of reading English-language sources for Chinese readers.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Qiaomu AI RSS?

It is an Obsidian RSS plugin by Xiangyang Qiaomu: read the Qiaomu-curated feeds and your own subscriptions, with Chinese AI rewrites and translations, and save articles as Markdown notes. See the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/joeseesun/qiaomu-ai-rss).

### How do I install Qiaomu AI RSS?

Install it from the official Obsidian community plugin library, via BRAT, or manually by downloading main.js, manifest.json, and styles.css from the releases into `.obsidian/plugins/qiaomu-ai-rss/` and enabling the plugin. See the [README](https://github.com/joeseesun/qiaomu-ai-rss) for details.

### Is Qiaomu AI RSS free?

Public features require no account or payment, and the project is open source under the GPL-3.0 license; the author offers a separate commercial license, see the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/joeseesun/qiaomu-ai-rss).

### How does it differ from other Obsidian RSS plugins?

Per the README, it was independently developed on the author's QMReader iOS app and its public API rather than forked from other Obsidian RSS plugins; distinguishing points include the Qiaomu-curated feeds, the three-way switching between original text, Chinese translation, and AI rewrite, and the tight integration with the Obsidian note workflow.
