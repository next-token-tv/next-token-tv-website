## What iPad is

iPad is [Apple's](/en/wiki/brands/apple) tablet computer family, running iPadOS. The first iPad was announced on January 27, 2010; the [Apple press release](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2010/01/27Apple-Launches-iPad/) described it as a device for browsing the web, sending email, watching videos, playing games, and reading e-books. Together with the [iPhone](/en/wiki/products/iphone), the Mac, and the [Apple Watch](/en/wiki/products/apple-watch), it belongs to Apple's hardware family.

## The lineup and its boundaries

The [official product page](https://www.apple.com/ipad/) splits the family into four lines: iPad Pro ("the ultimate iPad experience with the most advanced technology"), iPad Air (serious performance in a thin and light design), iPad (for everyday things), and iPad mini (the full experience in an ultraportable design). Apple Pencil and keyboards are the listed accessories; with a Mac, iPad supports Sidecar (using iPad as a second display) and Universal Control (one mouse and trackpad across devices), and it pairs with the iPhone for picking up content in progress. For model-by-model differences, Apple's [compare page](https://www.apple.com/ipad/compare/) is the most reliable reference.

iPad sits between a phone and a laptop: touch-first, well suited to the Pencil and media consumption, with desktop-class windowing provided by iPadOS and varying by system version. Choose based on the current lineup and system features rather than treating one version's features as a permanent fact.

## Discussion in the show

In the chapter “Vibe Coding 把硬盘用满之后” (when vibe coding fills the disk) of Weekly #003, Guizang [describes the disk cost of iOS/iPadOS development simulators](/weekly/003/transcript#quote-d56600f6fa6820c593c2): one system for iOS and one for iPad, times the highest, lowest, and middle OS versions for testing, adding up to 40–50 GB; he [adds that Xcode itself is also large](/weekly/003/transcript#quote-5d702aadbf9a8b7f15d3). This is one developer's personal experience, related to [Xcode](/en/wiki/products/xcode) and disk management on the [Mac](/en/wiki/products/mac-studio), not a review of iPad hardware.

## Frequently asked questions

### When was the iPad released?

The first iPad was announced on January 27, 2010, per the [Apple press release](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2010/01/27Apple-Launches-iPad/), which also records the original specs: 0.5 inches thick and 1.5 pounds.

### What iPad models are there, and how do I choose?

The [official product page](https://www.apple.com/ipad/) lists iPad Pro, iPad Air, iPad, and iPad mini — top performance, thin-and-light performance, everyday use, and portability respectively. Compare screens, chips, and accessory support item by item on Apple's [compare page](https://www.apple.com/ipad/compare/).

### Which Apple Pencils and keyboards work with iPad?

Apple Pencil and keyboards are the officially listed accessories, but which Pencil model and keyboard products a given iPad supports varies by model — check compatibility for the specific device on the [official product page](https://www.apple.com/ipad/) before buying.

### How does iPad work together with a Mac?

The official page describes Sidecar (using iPad as a second display for the Mac) and Universal Control (one mouse and trackpad across Mac and iPad), plus editing iPhone-captured content directly on the larger display. These capabilities depend on system versions and device settings.
