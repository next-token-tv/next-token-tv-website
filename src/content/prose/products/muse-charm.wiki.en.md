## Muse Charm

Muse Charm is a portable hardware device from Meta, described in the official announcement as "a delightful and fun device built for you to talk and interact with your Muse" — a pocket-sized piece of hardware carrying a state-of-the-art real-time voice model for talking to the [Muse personal agent](/en/wiki/products/muse-agent). Meta announced it at Connect on September 24, 2026, saying only that there would be "more to share later this year"; no price or availability details were given, and current information should come from the [official announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/the-biggest-news-from-connect-2026/).

## Position and product boundaries

By the official description, Muse Charm's role is a voice entry point to Muse: its value lies not in the hardware itself but in the Muse platform, connector ecosystem, and cloud execution behind it. This sets it apart from the other hardware at the same event — Connect 2026 also brought Meta VR glasses and several other devices, while Muse Charm is the Muse interaction peripheral Meta announced at Connect 2026.

The official announcement does not list hardware specifications such as camera, screen, or connectivity, nor a price. Such details, the release date, and the launch regions are time-sensitive and should follow Meta's later official announcements.

## Discussion in the show

Episode 004 discusses the device in the chapters “Muse Charm、手机与 AI 的入口” (Muse Charm, phones, and the AI entry point) and “掌上设备与日常生活” (Handheld devices and daily life). [Yang Pan notes that Shenzhen companies have explored similar devices over the past year](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-ae816d82fd86f18443b8), [argues the hardware itself does not matter — what matters is the platform and ecosystem behind it](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-e67f548ca14b85281415), and [describes the ideal interaction: with an agent, you simply tell the AI what to do instead of tapping through a phone](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-27e6e41651b6cf7812bb).

In the chapter “掌上设备与日常生活”, [Xiangyang Qiaomu says it looks like a Tamagotchi handheld pet](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-b170d7fba664999459c2); [Guizang mentions voice and video input](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-e7469888f59e70a90a0d) and says [its eSIM lets it work without a phone](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-c85be463b59d002aafab); [Orange relays the specifications as front and rear cameras, a two-inch screen, and 5G](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-5b481f6195043317a90a). [Xiangyang Qiaomu also mentions plans to ship in bulk as a Christmas gift](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-99e47d80735c8f8e7c7a). These specifications and timing details are participant reports; the official announcement does not list them, and they are not official parameters.

Opinions on the hardware itself differ sharply on the show: [Guizang says there is nothing notable about the hardware](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-2a538e3f69c50950f8d5), and [Xiangyang Qiaomu says Huaqiangbei vendors do better](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-9d094afb79a270c77918), while [Orange sees Muse plus Charm as a ten-year imagination of the future](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-82f12c8d7c282ad46d14). See the [episode 004 chapter](/weekly/004/transcript#chapter-12) in the Chinese transcript; an English transcript is not available.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Muse Charm?

Muse Charm is a portable device announced by Meta for talking to the Muse personal agent by voice. The official announcement describes it as bringing a state-of-the-art real-time voice model into a pocket-sized device; see the [Connect 2026 announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/the-biggest-news-from-connect-2026/).

### When does Muse Charm go on sale, and how much does it cost?

As of September 2026, the official announcement only says there will be more to share later in the year; no price or sale date has been given. Launch details should follow Meta's official channels.

### Does Muse Charm require a phone?

Official materials do not state its dependence on a phone. Show participants inferred from the reported parameters that an eSIM would let it work without one; that is a relayed opinion, not an official statement.
