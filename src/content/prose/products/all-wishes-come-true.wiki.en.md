## All Wishes Come True

All Wishes Come True (《八仙！》) is a 2026 Chinese fantasy-comedy animated film written and directed by Mu Zhengyang, produced by Pearl Studio and CMC Pictures. Taking the folk legend "the Eight Immortals cross the sea" as its basis, the story centers on the mortals who would one day become the Eight Immortals: eight ordinary people disguise themselves as immortals to sneak into a treasure vault that only gods can enter, and the heist spirals beyond their plan (per [Chinese Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/八仙！)). The distributor page is [cmc-pictures.com/all-wishes-come-true](https://cmc-pictures.com/all-wishes-come-true/).

## Release dates

The film opened in mainland China on July 18, 2026 (moved up from an initially announced July 24); CMC Pictures released it overseas in stages from August 13, 2026, covering the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand and the Netherlands, among others. Production — story, art and dubbing — was carried out primarily in Chengdu; the creators describe the art style as folkloric nianhua-painting feel with everyday market-street texture (per Chinese Wikipedia).

## Discussion in the show

Episode 010, chapter 10 ("恐龙、流体与古巴比伦：可复用的虚拟世界" — [dinosaurs, fluids and ancient Babylon: reusable virtual worlds](/weekly/010/transcript#chapter-10) in the Chinese transcript), Guizang lists recent Chinese animated features while discussing the production cost of high-definition CG: ["the costs — for example Ne Zha, All Wishes Come True just wrapped its run, and Three Kingdoms: The Beginning just released"](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-04e07fd56f41855e6671). The episode does not discuss the film itself; it is cited as an example in the participants' comparison of traditional CG costs against AI-generated output, which is their own framing.

## Frequently asked questions

### What kind of film is All Wishes Come True?

A fantasy-comedy animation adapted from the Eight Immortals legend: the protagonists are eight mortals with no magical powers who pose as immortals to raid a treasure vault in the immortal realm. The official English title is All Wishes Come True.

### Who made All Wishes Come True?

Mu Zhengyang wrote and directed it; Pearl Studio and CMC Pictures produced (per Chinese Wikipedia).

### When was All Wishes Come True released?

July 18, 2026 in mainland China; overseas markets followed from August 13, 2026 through CMC Pictures.

### How does the film relate to the Eight Immortals legend?

It uses the legend as source material and tells the mortals' stage before the eight become immortals; the characters derive from the legend, while the plot is an original adaptation.
