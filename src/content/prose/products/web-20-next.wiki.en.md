## What Web 2.0 Next is

Web 2.0 Next is an initiative website started by Next Token (nexttoken.tv), arguing for a return to the Web 2.0 tradition of "personal sites, blogs, and reciprocal links" in the AI era: own your own site, publish first-hand content, and connect through links into a trusted information network that both people and AI agents can understand. The code is open source at the [web20next/web20next GitHub repository](https://github.com/web20next/web20next), the site is bilingual in Chinese and English, and its slogan is "express yourself, connect with others."

## Principles and how to take part

The site summarizes its position in a five-point manifesto: own your website and keep adding projects and writing; share real content and take responsibility for facts, opinions, and sources; make yourself legible to both people and agents — a clear self-introduction with evidence, accurate, up to date, easy to read and easy to cite; express appreciation and trust through links, the way blogrolls once did; and build together — help friends set up sites, respond to each other, and maintain the shared directory.

To take part, submit your site on the [join page](https://web20next.com/join) to enter the shared directory. The site offers tutorials for GitHub Pages and Cloudflare Pages and domain registration, plus an agent-facing guide, [llms.txt](/en/wiki/terms/llms-txt), and RSS subscription.

## Discussed on Next Token

The idea came out of the show's own discussions. In Episode 007's "agent traffic, personal websites, and open interfaces" chapter (see the [Chinese transcript](/weekly/007/transcript#chapter-04)), Xiangyang Qiaomu argued that with platforms blocking crawlers, anyone building a personal presence should give AI something it can crawl — a site that is SEO- and GEO-friendly so that AI understands what you do.

Episode 008 (recorded November 1, 2026) continued the topic in two chapters of the Chinese transcript, ["GEO: where does AI get its information, and who does it trust?"](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-08) and ["personal websites and reciprocal links: building your own information sources"](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-09):

- [Yang Pan described the hosts linking to each other's sites, each defining their own information and creating a citable SSOT (Single Source of Truth) on the web](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-03a142f9fb8ae82d0a63). He wrote step-by-step guides on the site for building a personal site at zero cost — hosted on GitHub or Cloudflare, with only a few dozen yuan a year for a domain — including a command you can copy and hand to your agent.
- [Guizang read the reciprocal links as a form of attestation](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-7c1303c80562a9186c9c): a declaration that this information is real and that this is the image I want to convey. He added that someone active on only a single platform has almost no way to rebut an AI hallucination that defames them, so you should make sure your real content is reachable by AI.
- Orange called this [a Trusted Network](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-1dfe190cd49c9cc0dc76), said it was a concept that [Instinct](/en/wiki/products/instinct) had championed, and argued that ["the essence of human commerce is trust."](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-67beb4a53e921b183b1a) Yang Pan noted that [many people in Silicon Valley have been working on similar ideas recently](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-132600184765a84a9d68).
- [Xiangyang Qiaomu added that some companies would pay 500,000 to 1 million yuan](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-2b28118af4225e695340) just to make sure AI assistants such as Doubao return the correct phone number, address, and legal representative — by splitting public information into minimal fields on a dedicated site for AI crawlers to index.

These are the participants' discussions; they are not commitments of the project or any official endorsement.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Web 2.0 Next?

It is an initiative website started by Next Token: own a personal site, publish real content, and link to each other, so that people and AI agents can understand real people and real relationships through links.

### How do I join Web 2.0 Next?

Build a personal site of your own, then submit it on the [join page](https://web20next.com/join) to enter the shared directory; you can also hand the official guide to an agent with shell access and let it do the work.

### Does building a site cost money?

Per the guides discussed on the show, you can host a site for free on GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages and pay only a few dozen yuan a year for a domain; see the [official site](https://web20next.com/) for tutorials.

### How does Web 2.0 Next relate to SEO and GEO?

Its premise is giving AI crawlable, citable information about yourself: a personal site acts as a [GEO](/en/wiki/terms/geo) information source you control, and the reciprocal-link network supplies the trust structure that corroborates it.
