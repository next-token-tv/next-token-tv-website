## What Heroes of Might and Magic is

Heroes of Might and Magic is a fantasy turn-based strategy game series: players move heroes across an adventure map, capture towns and mines, gather resources and recruit armies, and battles take place on a separate tactical grid screen. Heroes grow through experience, artifacts and spells, borrowing role-playing elements. The series was created by New World Computing, the studio founded by Jon Van Caneghem, and the first title, Heroes of Might and Magic: A Strategic Quest, was released in 1995.

## Series history

According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_of_Might_and_Magic), New World Computing developed the games from 1995 to 2003, with 3DO taking over publishing in 1996; after 3DO shut down, Ubisoft has held and published the series since 2003. Later installments came from different studios: New World Computing closed after Heroes IV (2002), Heroes V (2006) was developed by Nival Interactive, Heroes VI (2011) by Black Hole Entertainment, and Heroes VII (2015) by Limbic Entertainment. A new title, Heroes of Might and Magic: Olden Era, is developed by Unfrozen with Hooded Horse and Ubisoft as co-publishers; its release status is on the [official Ubisoft series page](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-gb/games/heroes-of-might-and-magic), which also collects a recap of the series' 30th-anniversary celebrations from October 2025.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the newest Heroes of Might and Magic game, Olden Era?

Olden Era (Heroes of Might and Magic: Olden Era) is a new installment in the series, in development. According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heroes_of_Might_and_Magic), it is developed by Unfrozen with Hooded Horse and Ubisoft as co-publishers. For its release status, see the [official Ubisoft series page](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-gb/games/heroes-of-might-and-magic).
