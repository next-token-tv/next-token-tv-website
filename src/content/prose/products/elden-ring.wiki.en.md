## Elden Ring

Elden Ring is an action role-playing game developed by FromSoftware and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment, directed by Hidetaka Miyazaki with worldbuilding by George R. R. Martin, the author of A Song of Ice and Fire. It was released on February 25, 2022, for PlayStation 4, PlayStation 5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, and Windows. Set in the Lands Between, the game has players take a customizable Tarnished on a quest to repair the shattered Elden Ring and become the new Elden Lord. It shares a developer with [Bloodborne](/en/wiki/products/bloodborne) but belongs to a different publisher and world.

## Editions and spin-offs

The main releases and spin-offs beyond the base game:

- The Shadow of the Erdtree expansion was released in June 2024, set in the Land of Shadow; it earned similar acclaim, with public sources recording over 10 million copies sold.
- Elden Ring Nightreign, a multiplayer-focused spin-off, was released in 2025.
- Elden Ring Tarnished Edition, the Nintendo Switch 2 version, launched on August 28, 2026, including the base game and Shadow of the Erdtree plus two new classes and cosmetics for Torrent; the new classes and cosmetic content are also available on other platforms as paid DLC.

## Reception

By public accounts, Elden Ring won numerous Game of the Year awards and is cited among the defining open-world designs; in April 2025 its sales were announced at over 30 million copies, placing it among the best-selling games.

### What is Tarnished Edition?

Tarnished Edition is the Switch 2 version, launched August 28, 2026, containing the base game and Shadow of the Erdtree plus two new classes and Torrent cosmetics; those additions are also sold as paid DLC on other platforms.

### What is Nightreign?

Elden Ring Nightreign is a multiplayer cooperative spin-off released in 2025, focused on team-based combat rather than the base game's single-player open world.
