## What Jin Yong Qun Xia Zhuan is

Jin Yong Qun Xia Zhuan (金庸群侠传) is a Chinese-language single-player role-playing game released in 1996 for DOS, developed by Heluo Studio and published by Soft-World (智冠科技). Set in the world of Jin Yong’s fourteen wuxia novels, the game puts the player in the role of a modern person drawn into that world: exploration happens freely across an overworld map, characters from works such as Demi-Gods and Semi-Devils, The Legend of the Condor Heroes, and The Smiling, Proud Wanderer can join the party, and battles are turn-based. The publisher lists the title among its classic single-player game IPs in its retro-gaming material ([publisher information](https://www.soft-world.com/News/NewsDetail?Sn=5)).

The game later spawned works including Wulin Qunxia Zhuan, Sanguo Qunxia Zhuan, and the online game Jin Yong Qun Xia Zhuan Online.

## Use and boundaries

This is a single-player game from 1996, originally released for DOS, with no officially operated online service; running the original on modern systems requires solving compatibility issues yourself. Fan-made remakes exist, but they are not official publisher products; their names and completeness vary, so check the source before using them.
