## MacBook

MacBook is [Apple](/en/wiki/brands/apple)’s notebook computer family. The official Mac page lists the current notebooks as MacBook Air (13- and 15-inch, positioned as thin and portable) and MacBook Pro (14- and 16-inch, for demanding professional work), along with the MacBook Neo. The models run Apple silicon; the lineup and configurations change on Apple’s update cadence and belong on the [official page](https://www.apple.com/mac/).

## Division with other Macs

MacBook covers mobile scenarios; for desktop form factors, Apple also offers lines such as the [Mac mini](/en/wiki/products/mac-mini) and [Mac Studio](/en/wiki/products/mac-studio). The choice between them depends on portability, performance, and external display needs.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the difference between MacBook Air and MacBook Pro?

In Apple’s positioning, the Air is the thin, portable option and the Pro targets demanding workloads such as video and development; screen sizes and chip options differ. See the [official model page](https://www.apple.com/mac/).

### What is the MacBook Neo?

One of the current models listed on Apple’s official Mac page alongside the Air and Pro; its positioning and configurations belong on the [official page](https://www.apple.com/mac/).

### Will MacBook prices go up if I buy now?

Show participants speculated that Apple notebooks might see further price increases; this is a judgment from the discussion, not a fact. Current models and prices belong on [Apple’s official page](https://www.apple.com/mac/).
