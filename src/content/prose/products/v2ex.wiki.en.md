## Definition

V2EX is an online discussion community for developers, designers, and other creative professionals. Its official About page carries the slogan "V2EX = way to explore" and says the community is home to over 750,000 creative workers, mainly from the internet, gaming, and media industries. See the [official About page](https://www.v2ex.com/about).

## How it works

Members post topics and replies, sharing what they are working on and exchanging ideas. The community enforces explicit rules: no piracy discussions, no full-text reposting of other people's articles, no low-information replies such as "first!", no personal attacks or doxxing, and "please do not post AI-generated content here". The site runs on its own Project Babel community software, with help and API links in the page footer.

## Usage

Typical uses include showing personal projects, discussing technology and industry topics with peers, and finding collaborators or new opportunities; the official introduction describes V2EX as part of the working and personal life of creative professionals.

## Related and confusable terms

The name's meaning is documented: the official slogan is "way to explore", and the About page's history section adds that "way too extreme" was also part of it when the community launched in 2006. V2EX posts are often screenshotted and shared elsewhere; a post reflects the individual author's view, not a community position.

## Frequently asked questions

### What kind of site is V2EX?

A discussion community for developers, designers, and other creative professionals, centered on work, technology, and life topics; the official introduction is on the [About page](https://www.v2ex.com/about).

### How do I sign up for V2EX? Is an invite code required?

Registration is through the Sign Up page at [v2ex.com](https://www.v2ex.com/); the sign-up process and any opening conditions are governed by the site's own instructions.

### Does V2EX have an API?

The About page footer links to API and help documentation; see the in-site [Help](https://www.v2ex.com/help) and API docs for details.
