## What Diandian is

Diandian is the AI lifestyle question-answering assistant from Xiaohongshu, built around decisions such as travel, shopping, dining, and interest exploration, answering from community content. Its official site is [dots.ai](https://studio.dots.ai/); the feature description in this entry draws mainly on the actual usage reported by Weekly participants, and the official site remains the reference for features and availability.

## Usage and boundaries

As Weekly participants describe it, Diandian is asked questions directly inside Xiaohongshu and fits everyday-life questions: Guizang says he often uses it to look up game guides, Orange gives the example of summarizing recommendation ratios across community notes (such as what share of notes recommend a given car or pair of shoes), and Xiangyang Qiaomu calls it the best AI feature Xiaohongshu has made. Such answers aggregate community content — useful as a quick reference, but important purchasing or health decisions should still be checked against the original notes and sources.

Diandian belongs to the same product shape as WeChat's Xiaowei and Doubao entry assistants: embedded in a platform users already have, dependent on that platform's content and data. Participants also named its boundaries — the data source is the Xiaohongshu ecosystem, the platform is closed, and automated actions are restricted.

Weekly #002's chapter “平台内 AI 助手：入口简单，体验才完整” (in-platform AI assistants: easy entry, complete experience) discusses Diandian directly: [Orange says Xiaohongshu's Diandian works really well](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-35e5698bdce70446930e), and Guizang adds that he often uses it for game guides; Yang Pan [summarizes that "for everyday-life questions, ask Diandian on Xiaohongshu"](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-69bc3c4a96ec98041ff9) and treats Diandian and WeChat's Xiaowei as counterparts differing in data source — Xiaohongshu's notes are better quality while WeChat's Channels has more volume. In the [“健康数据与日常记录” chapter](/weekly/002/transcript#chapter-21), Guizang uses Diandian as an example of platform closure: why one can only use Diandian inside Xiaohongshu and Xiaowei inside WeChat. These are participant experiences; see the [episode 002 chapter](/weekly/002/transcript#chapter-17).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Xiaohongshu's Diandian?

Diandian is Xiaohongshu's AI lifestyle Q&A assistant that answers travel, shopping, and dining questions from community content; its official site is [dots.ai](https://studio.dots.ai/). Weekly participants describe it as the lifestyle-decision entry point embedded in Xiaohongshu.

### Where do I open Diandian?

Weekly participants ask Diandian questions directly inside Xiaohongshu; the official site [dots.ai](https://studio.dots.ai/) is one of the product's entry points. Exact access and availability follow the Xiaohongshu app and official documentation.

### Are Diandian's answers trustworthy?

Its answers aggregate community notes (participants cite summarizing recommendation ratios), which works as a quick reference. For significant purchasing or health decisions, check the original notes and sources; an AI summary does not replace verification.

### How does Diandian relate to WeChat's Xiaowei or Doubao?

They are similar products on different platforms: each is embedded in its own app and answers from its own content ecosystem. In the Weekly show, Yang Pan saw Diandian and Xiaowei as counterparts, with Xiaohongshu's notes better in quality and WeChat larger in volume; that is participant opinion, not an independent evaluation.
