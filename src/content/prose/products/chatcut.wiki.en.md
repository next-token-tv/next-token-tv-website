## What ChatCut is

ChatCut is a video editor from ChatCut Inc., which its official documentation calls "the AI video editor you talk to": an AI agent is built into the editing workspace, so you can ask the agent in natural language to assemble or revise an edit, or make precise changes yourself on a multi-track timeline. The documentation draws a clear line between editor and generator: ChatCut is an AI video editor, video generation is a single media-generation job, and generated results land in the asset library next to imported footage on the same timeline (see the [official documentation](https://chatcut.io/docs/what-is-chatcut)).

## Three entry points

Per the documentation, the product comes as a desktop app (macOS Apple Silicon, Intel chips, and Windows), a web app, and an Agent Plugin; the difference is where the conversation happens, not the editing steps. On the agent side, ChatCut ships plugins for ChatGPT/Codex, Claude Code, and Grok Bot, letting those agents edit a desktop project directly.

## Key features

- Tightening cuts from speech-led footage — talking heads, interviews, podcasts, tutorials, screen recordings — and producing shorter versions from a longer project.
- Generating video, motion graphics, images, music, captions, and narration; the documentation lists video models including Seedance.
- Editing speech by deleting or rearranging words in the transcript panel.
- Exporting video, audio, subtitles, and XML: Premiere / DaVinci XML, plus drafts that can be reopened in Jianying / CapCut.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is ChatCut?

A video editor with a built-in AI agent: you direct the agent in natural language and can still adjust everything on a multi-track timeline. It is available as a desktop app, a web app, and an agent plugin; see the [official documentation](https://chatcut.io/docs/what-is-chatcut).

### Is ChatCut a video generator?

By the documentation's own distinction, ChatCut is an AI video editor: the agent reads project context and changes the active timeline, while video generation submits a single media-generation job whose output returns to the timeline for editing.

### How is ChatCut related to Jianying or CapCut?

They are independent products from different companies; ChatCut is not a component or plugin of Jianying (known internationally as CapCut). ChatCut can export drafts that reopen in Jianying / CapCut for further editing — a hand-off capability, not the same product (see the [official documentation](https://chatcut.io/docs/what-is-chatcut)).

### What footage is ChatCut suited to?

The documentation names speech-led material as its sweet spot: talking heads, interviews, podcasts, tutorials, webinars, and screen recordings, with the transcript panel for reviewing speech as text and the timeline for manual changes.

### How much does ChatCut cost?

It runs on credits with free and Pro tiers; current plans and allowances are on the [official pricing page](https://chatcut.io/pricing).
