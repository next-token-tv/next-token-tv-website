## What Super XiaoAI is

Super XiaoAI (超级小爱) is [Xiaomi](/en/wiki/brands/xiaomi)’s AI assistant built into [Xiaomi HyperOS](/en/wiki/products/hyperos). "XiaoAI" (小爱同学) is the long-standing name of Xiaomi’s voice assistant, with the official assistant site at [xiaoai.mi.com](https://xiaoai.mi.com/); on the HyperOS 4 official page, the built-in assistant appears as "Super XiaoAI 2.0," which the page says is based on the [Xiaomi MiMo model family](/en/wiki/products/mimo).

## Officially described capabilities

According to the HyperOS 4 official page, Super XiaoAI 2.0 centers on system execution and content generation:

- On-island tasks: long-running tasks are handed to the "Xiaomi Super Island" to run in the background, with results expanding automatically when done.
- Inspiration ball: drag it out from the screen edge and point at on-screen content while speaking, to trigger navigation, finding similar products, adding calendar entries, photo editing, and more.
- Expert mode: official examples include searching the photo album and curating a set with one sentence, trip planning, home control, pre-cooling the car, scheduled recurring tasks, and deep-research-style content generation.
- Invocation: press and hold the bottom pill bar and speak.

The page also notes that some related features (such as AI Call and the Super XiaoAI input method) are limited to mainland China; feature availability follows the [official page](https://hyperos.mi.com/) and on-device descriptions.

## Discussion in the show

In Weekly #005’s chapter "小米蓝牙遥控器做语音输入" (Xiaomi’s Bluetooth remote as a voice input device), Yang Pan asked whether Xiaomi MiMo had an agent, and [Guizang answered that "it has all been merged into Super XiaoAI now" in the Chinese transcript](/weekly/005/transcript#quote-7c7b23a9919e61822061), with Orange adding that Xiaomi has an on-device harness. This is a participant’s paraphrase of Xiaomi’s AI product moves; the official account of the MiMo–Super XiaoAI relationship is on the HyperOS 4 page.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the relationship between Super XiaoAI and XiaoAI (小爱同学)?

Both are Xiaomi assistant names: "XiaoAI" is the long-standing voice-assistant name with the official site at xiaoai.mi.com, while "Super XiaoAI" is the built-in AI assistant described on the HyperOS official pages. The exact version relationship should follow Xiaomi’s official announcements.

### What model does Super XiaoAI use?

The HyperOS 4 page says Super XiaoAI 2.0 is based on the Xiaomi MiMo model family; see the [MiMo entry](/en/wiki/products/mimo).

### Can Super XiaoAI be used outside mainland China?

The official page notes that features such as AI Call and the Super XiaoAI input method are limited to mainland China; availability of specific features elsewhere follows the official page and on-device descriptions.

### How do I invoke Super XiaoAI?

The HyperOS 4 page lists voice interaction and pressing and holding the bottom pill bar to speak.
