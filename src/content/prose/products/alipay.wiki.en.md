## What Alipay is

Alipay is a digital payments and everyday-services platform under Ant Group. It launched on October 15, 2003, originally as the payment service of [Taobao](/en/wiki/products/taobao) under Alibaba, created to solve the trust problem in online shopping; it began operating independently on December 8, 2004. Alipay has since grown from a single payment tool into an app covering payments, everyday services, government services, wealth management, insurance, and other scenarios. The official site is [alipay.com](https://www.alipay.com/).

## Use and boundaries

Through the Alipay app and web, users make and receive payments, transfer money, and pay bills, and use in-app everyday services and mini programs. As a payment platform, it connects consumers, merchants, and financial institutions; funds, accounts, and risk-control rules are governed by the platform and regulatory requirements, and the exact service scope is defined by official documentation. Alipay belongs to the Alibaba ecosystem alongside [Taobao](/en/wiki/products/taobao) and [Alibaba](/en/wiki/brands/alibaba).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Alipay?

Alipay is a digital platform for payments and everyday services: users make and receive payments, transfer money, and pay bills, and use in-app services and mini programs.

### Which company owns Alipay?

Alipay launched in 2003 as part of Taobao, began independent operation in December 2004, and is a platform under Ant Group.

### What is the relationship between Alipay and Taobao?

Alipay was originally created to solve the trust problem in Taobao purchases, serving as an escrow-style payment tool; the two belong to the same Alibaba ecosystem, but Alipay has since become a standalone payments and everyday-services platform.

### Where is the Alipay official website?

The official site is [www.alipay.com](https://www.alipay.com/); everyday use is primarily through the Alipay app.
