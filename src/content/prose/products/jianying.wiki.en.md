## What Jianying is

Jianying is the video editing, captioning, and content-creation application under ByteDance, with its mainland-China site running on the [capcut.cn](https://www.capcut.cn/) domain; the page lists Shenzhen Lianmeng Technology Co., Ltd. as the developer. The product comes in a mobile edition and a desktop Pro edition, and the site positions it as an all-in-one AI creation companion: AI tools such as smart talking-head editing, smart commentary rough cuts, digital humans, background removal, text-to-speech with voice cloning, alongside professional editing features like masks, keyframes, color wheels, multi-camera, and multiple timelines. Features and editions follow the [official site](https://www.capcut.cn/).

## Usage and boundaries

Jianying targets self-media talking-head videos, re-edits of film and TV content, and marketing clips; rough cuts, captions, and final renders all happen in the app. The mobile and desktop Pro editions differ in emphasis: Pro stresses multi-timeline, multi-camera, and advanced color work, while mobile favors templates and quick output; details belong to the official download page and in-app documentation. The homepage does not list prices; memberships and paid assets follow in-app and official channel terms.

For AI automation, one boundary matters: Jianying currently exposes no public CLI or scripting interface, so agents cannot drive it the way they drive FFmpeg — the essential difference from command-line tools in automated workflows.

## Discussion in the show

In Weekly #002's chapter “我们如何用 Agent 制作和发布播客” (how we use agents to produce and publish a podcast), Orange [says Jianying never adapted well to agents, so he went back to FFmpeg](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-555026c6546093f334d0); [Yang Pan says he bought a Jianying annual membership for editing but gave up after a very short time](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-44ebeac53c74a2109226) and cut the first episode with Codex plus FFmpeg. Orange argues Jianying "clearly could adapt but refuses to ship its CLI," leaving it open to disruption; Guizang cautions that Jianying is the youngest of these editing apps, so the verdict may not come quickly. These are the participants' judgments from their own workflows; see the [episode 002 chapter](/weekly/002/transcript#chapter-05).

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is the official Jianying website?

The mainland-China site is [capcut.cn](https://www.capcut.cn/), offering desktop Pro and mobile downloads plus official learning resources; download only from the official site or app stores.

### What is the difference between Jianying Pro and the mobile app?

The site lists them as the desktop (Pro) edition and the mobile edition: Pro provides multi-timeline, multi-camera, and color-wheel workflows, while mobile leans on templates and quick creation. The detailed feature lists are on the [official site](https://www.capcut.cn/).

### Is Jianying free?

The official site offers free downloads and does not list prices on its homepage; the product has a membership system (a show participant mentions an annual membership), and some AI tools, templates, and assets may require a membership or separate payment. Current terms follow the app and official channels.

### What is the relationship between Jianying and CapCut?

Their sites are [capcut.cn](https://www.capcut.cn/) and [capcut.com](https://www.capcut.com/), serving the mainland-China and overseas markets respectively. The official pages do not spell out how the two products correspond; use each site's own feature and availability documentation.

### Can AI agents use Jianying?

There is no public CLI or scripting interface. In the Weekly show, two participants switched to a Codex-plus-FFmpeg command-line workflow precisely because agents could not adapt to Jianying; for AI-automated editing, command-line tools remain the more direct path, and Jianying's boundaries should follow official announcements.
