## What Dots is

Dots are always-on personal AI agents from [OpenAI](/en/wiki/brands/openai), announced at [DevDay](/en/wiki/products/openai-devday) on September 29, 2026. The official page describes them as "always-on agents built to handle everything": you give a dot a project or a responsibility, it figures out the next steps on its own, keeps working between conversations, and brings items back for your review. Dots live inside [ChatGPT](/en/wiki/products/chatgpt), and the official page says they run on the GPT-6 Astra model.

## Usage and boundaries

- **Starting point and division of work**: each dot starts with context from your ChatGPT memory; coding-related work is handed to [Codex](/en/wiki/products/codex), with the official example of preparing code and tests for engineers to review.
- **Cloud computer**: a dot works on what the official page calls its own cloud computer, and can also connect to your own computer.
- **Permissions and control**: Custom Rules decide which actions are allowed, which require approval, and which are blocked. Sensitive actions pass through Auto-review, and things like password changes stay with the user.
- **Interaction and entry points**: you message or call your dot in ChatGPT on web, mobile, and desktop. The page also lists Slack and Microsoft Teams integrations and previews texting as a coming interaction. Dots are created in the ChatGPT desktop app.
- **Availability**: Dots ship with ChatGPT plans; the current plans and markets are listed on the official entry page, [chatgpt.com/features/dots](https://chatgpt.com/features/dots/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What are Dots?

Always-on personal AI agents from OpenAI that run inside ChatGPT, powered by the GPT-6 Astra model, working on their own cloud computers. Entry point: [chatgpt.com/features/dots](https://chatgpt.com/features/dots/).

### How do Dots differ from ChatGPT and Codex?

ChatGPT is a conversational assistant and Codex is centered on coding workflows; a dot is an agent you give a responsibility, and it keeps working between conversations: it starts from your ChatGPT memory and hands coding tasks to Codex. All three live in the OpenAI ecosystem, and [with Dots placed in the Codex sidebar](/weekly/005/transcript#quote-e9ddc3dde2d66ccb6c70) they are easy to confuse.

### Which plans include Dots?

As of October 2026, the official page says Dots are rolling out to Pro, Business Premium, and Enterprise plans in eligible markets, with Enterprise requiring a workspace admin to enable them; check the [official page](https://chatgpt.com/features/dots/) for the current scope.

### Can a dot connect to my own computer?

Yes, per the official page: a dot has its own cloud computer and can also connect to your computer. In episode 005, Yang Pan's dot had both the cloud computer and a Mac Studio registered, while Guizang reported a tightly closed cloud computer, [with no SSH access and no installing software](/weekly/005/transcript#quote-3ae52366e525c64e26aa).

### Do Dots cost extra?

The official page lists no standalone price for Dots; they ship with ChatGPT plans. Plans and pricing belong to the [official pricing page](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/).
