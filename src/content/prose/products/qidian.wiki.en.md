## What Qidian is

Qidian (起点中文网, Qidian Chinese Website) is a Chinese original web-fiction platform founded in May 2002, with roots in the Xuanhuan Literature Association, and operated by Shanghai Xuanting Entertainment Information Technology Co., Ltd. The site is known for serialized novels in genres such as xuanhuan fantasy, wuxia, and military fiction; completed works may also be published as print or e-books. Qidian began offering paid online reading in October 2003.

## Ownership history

Shanda acquired Qidian in October 2004, making it a wholly owned subsidiary. When Shanda Literature was formed in July 2008, Qidian became part of it. After Tencent Literature acquired Shanda Literature, Qidian became part of China Literature (Yuewen Group) in 2015.
