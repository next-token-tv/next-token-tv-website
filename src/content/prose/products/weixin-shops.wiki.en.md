## Definition

Weixin Mini Shops is a merchant storefront and commerce service within the Weixin ecosystem.

## Usage and related concepts

Merchants organize products in storefronts and use Weixin’s social, content, and payment capabilities to reach customers. Mini Shops is a specific commerce service, distinct from the messaging application and from an ordinary personal chat.
