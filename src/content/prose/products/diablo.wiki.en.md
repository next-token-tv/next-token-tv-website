## Definition

Diablo is an action role-playing game series by Blizzard centered on combat, character progression and loot: players pick a class, fight through randomized dungeons in real time, and keep collecting better weapons and armor. The series is set in the dark fantasy world of Sanctuary; the first game was developed by Blizzard North and released on January 3, 1997. Entry points to each title are listed on the [official Blizzard games page](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/games).

## Series and releases

The main entries are Diablo (January 1997), Diablo II (June 2000), Diablo III (May 15, 2012) and Diablo IV (June 5, 2023). The free-to-play Diablo Immortal, developed with NetEase, launched on June 2, 2022. Lord of Hatred, the second expansion for Diablo IV, was released on April 28, 2026.

## Gameplay

The core loop combines real-time combat, randomized maps, class skill builds and loot drops: defeat enemies, pick up equipment with varied attributes, refine a build and take on stronger foes. Specific mechanics, seasons and modes for each title are documented on the official pages.

## Related and confusable terms

"Diablo" refers to the whole series, of which Diablo IV is one entry. "Diablo-like" describes a gameplay genre borrowed from this kill-and-loot progression loop; it does not name a specific title in the series.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which Diablo game does this entry cover?

This entry covers the series as a whole. Episode 007 also uses Diablo as a gameplay comparison without limiting the reference to one title.

### Is Diablo free to play?

The mainline titles are premium purchases; Diablo Immortal, developed with NetEase, is free to download with in-app purchases. Editions and pricing for each title are documented on the [official Blizzard pages](https://www.blizzard.com/en-us/games).
