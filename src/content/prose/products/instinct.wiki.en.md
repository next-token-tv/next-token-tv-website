## What Instinct is

Instinct is a personal AI assistant that, per its official page, "understands what you're working on and what's important to you." It works by connecting to your applications and devices — the official page lists email, messaging, screen, audio, and location, among others.

## Usage and interaction

The official description stresses two points. First, it is trained to use a phone and a computer: "You can text or call it. It uses its devices in the same way that humans do." Second, it ships no dedicated app interface — the page calls this "there are no new interfaces": the interaction is messaging and phone calls. Official examples of everyday tasks include following up on threads you've dropped, proactively calling or texting you, arranging a ride to the airport, and booking a handyman.

The page does not disclose the company behind the product, supported platforms, pricing, or availability. The sign-up entry is "Text Instinct to get started" on [instinct.com](https://instinct.com/), and the page carries a "© 2026 Instinct" notice.
