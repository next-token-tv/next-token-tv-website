## What ColorOS is

ColorOS is the operating system for smart devices developed by [OPPO](/en/wiki/brands/oppo) on top of Android, first released in 2013 and shipped mainly on OPPO and OnePlus phones and tablets. It redesigns the interface, animations, and system capabilities over Android, includes the Xiaobu voice assistant (descended from the earlier Breeno assistant), iterates major versions regularly, and has been gaining AI features such as AI photo erasing and call summaries.

OPPO and OnePlus merged their software development, announced in December 2021; since then OnePlus phones sold in mainland China ship with ColorOS, while the OxygenOS on global OnePlus devices shares the same code base with ColorOS. So the claim "OnePlus runs OPPO's system" holds for mainland China.

## Uses and differences

System updates run from Settings > Software update; the cadence of major releases and the list of supported models are on the [ColorOS site](https://www.coloros.com/). Which major version is current and which devices can upgrade are time-sensitive details — go by the official site.

Three things are easy to confuse: ColorOS is the operating system, while OPPO, OnePlus, and Realme are phone brands (Realme's overseas market uses the related but separate Realme UI); OxygenOS is the name of the system on global OnePlus devices, sharing the same base as ColorOS; and stock Android is Google's open-source foundation on which both ColorOS and OxygenOS sit as customization layers.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is ColorOS Android?

Yes. ColorOS is built on Android — OPPO's customization layer over the Android Open Source Project — with a compatible app ecosystem and APK installation. Debates about "whether it counts as Android" concern the depth of customization, not app compatibility.

### Do OnePlus phones run ColorOS?

In mainland China, yes: after OPPO and OnePlus announced their merged software development in December 2021, OnePlus phones for the Chinese market ship with ColorOS. Global OnePlus devices use OxygenOS, which shares the same code base. See the [ColorOS site](https://www.coloros.com/) and the OnePlus site for details.

### How do I update ColorOS?

Check for updates under Settings > Software update; major-version rollouts come in waves, and per-model plans are published on the [ColorOS site](https://www.coloros.com/). The current major version is whatever the official site shows.

### What is the Xiaobu assistant?

Xiaobu is the voice assistant built into ColorOS, developed from OPPO's earlier Breeno assistant. It handles voice commands, on-screen recognition, and daily reminders; recent OPPO AI features such as call summaries also reach users through it and the system apps.

### How do ColorOS and OxygenOS differ?

Both are OPPO-family Android systems: ColorOS ships on OPPO devices and on OnePlus devices in mainland China, OxygenOS on global OnePlus devices. Since the merged development announced in late 2021, they share a code base, differing mainly in preinstalled services, interface details, and regional features.
