## Three Kingdoms: The Beginning

Three Kingdoms: The Beginning (《三国第一部：争洛阳》) is a 2026 historical animated film from Light Chaser Animation, directed by Xie Junwei and written by Wang Wei. It opens Light Chaser's Three Kingdoms series and focuses on the struggle for Luoyang at the end of the Eastern Han dynasty: the trajectories of the young Cao Cao and Yuan Shao form the main storyline, with events from the power play of the Ten Regular Attendants and the Yellow Turban rebellion through the campaign against Dong Zhuo — stopping short of the three-way division of the realm (per [Chinese Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/三国第一部：争洛阳)).

## Release and withdrawal

The film opened in mainland China on July 10, 2026; on July 25, 2026, the studio announced it was pulled from theaters and would return at a later date (per Chinese Wikipedia). It was made by the team behind Light Chaser's "new culture" line of historical features, whose first entry was Chang'an (《长安三万里》, 2023; international title "30,000 Miles from Chang'an").

## Discussion in the show

Episode 010, chapter 10 ("恐龙、流体与古巴比伦：可复用的虚拟世界" — [dinosaurs, fluids and ancient Babylon: reusable virtual worlds](/weekly/010/transcript#chapter-10) in the Chinese transcript), Guizang frames high-definition CG as the most expensive part of the content production chain and points to recent Chinese animated features: ["the costs — for example Ne Zha, All Wishes Come True! just wrapped its run, and Three Kingdoms: The Beginning just released"](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-04e07fd56f41855e6671). The episode does not discuss the film itself; it is cited as an example in the participants' comparison of traditional CG costs against AI-generated output, which is their own framing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who made Three Kingdoms: The Beginning?

Light Chaser Animation produced it, directed by Xie Junwei and written by Wang Wei, with the studio's "new culture" historical-feature team (per Chinese Wikipedia).

### When was Three Kingdoms: The Beginning released?

July 10, 2026 in mainland China; on July 25, 2026 the studio announced it was pulled from theaters and would return at a later date.

### What is Three Kingdoms: The Beginning about?

Set at the end of the Eastern Han dynasty, it follows the young Cao Cao and Yuan Shao, covering the Ten Regular Attendants' hold on power, the Yellow Turban rebellion and the campaign against Dong Zhuo — the struggle for Luoyang that precedes the three-way division of the realm.

### How is it related to Chang'an (30,000 Miles from Chang'an)?

Both belong to Light Chaser Animation's "new culture" line of historical features: Chang'an was the line's first film, and Three Kingdoms: The Beginning was made with that same team as the opening of its Three Kingdoms series.
