## What NetEase UU Remote is

NetEase UU Remote is a remote-control and remote-desktop application from [NetEase](/en/wiki/brands/netease), with its official entry point at [uuyc.163.com](https://uuyc.163.com/). The official site describes it as a free remote-control tool promoted as "true 4K, truly free", produced by what the company calls a senior NetEase team. It belongs to the remote-access tool category: once the controlled and controlling devices are signed into the same account, you can see and operate a remote computer's desktop from another device.

Do not confuse it with NetEase's UU Accelerator: the UU Accelerator ([uu.163.com](https://uu.163.com/)) is a game-network boosting service, while UU Remote is a remote-desktop tool. They are different products.

## Uses and boundaries

The official site lists remote work, remote gaming, and remote assistance as its main scenarios, with these capabilities:

- Remote desktop and control, advertised at up to 4K 144 fps;
- File transfer advertised with no limits on count, format, or size;
- One-click remote wake-up based on WOL (Wake-on-LAN);
- Privacy protections such as an anti-peeping mode on the controlled device;
- Multi-screen collaboration, a wireless second display, key mapping (the site says 300+ presets), and Mac-as-controlled-device support.

For platforms, the site lists Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, and TV clients. The site advertises the features as free; exact terms and limits are governed by the [official site](https://uuyc.163.com/) and in-app notices.

Search results for "UU Remote official site" or "UU Remote download" include many imitation domains that are not 163.com; downloads should go through the official domain uuyc.163.com.

## FAQ

### What is the official NetEase UU Remote website?

The official site is [uuyc.163.com](https://uuyc.163.com/); downloads and feature introductions live there. Domains such as uuycpro.com.cn and uu-gw.com.cn that appear in search results are not NetEase's official site — be cautious.

### Is UU Remote free?

The official site advertises UU Remote as "truly free", with remote control and file transfer not billed by duration. Terms and limits may change; check the official site before relying on them.

### Which platforms does UU Remote support?

The official site lists Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, and TV clients. Windows and macOS computers can be controlled, and Mac-as-controlled-device support is listed as a dedicated feature.

### Are UU Remote and NetEase UU Accelerator the same thing?

No. UU Remote (uuyc.163.com) is a remote-control application; UU Accelerator (uu.163.com) is a game-network boosting service. Both belong to NetEase but serve different purposes.
