## What Doubao is

Doubao is [ByteDance](/en/wiki/brands/bytedance)’s AI assistant application. The official feature introduction groups its capabilities into chat, voice input with near-human voice replies, creating and customizing your own agents, and writing assistance for creative work. It is an end-user product, distinct from other ByteDance entries: the underlying model family is [Doubao Seed](/en/wiki/products/doubao-seed), developed by ByteDance’s Seed team; the office-focused product is [Doubao Work](/en/wiki/products/doubao-work); the input tool is [Doubao Input Method](/en/wiki/products/doubao-input-method).

## Usage and entry points

The web version is at [doubao.com](https://www.doubao.com/); the official [download page](https://www.doubao.com/download/) covers desktop clients, and the mobile app is distributed through app stores. Doubao is a consumer application whose capabilities evolve with releases; the exact shape of agent and connector features should be checked against the official feature introduction rather than show remarks or community claims.

## Discussion in the show

In Weekly #001’s chapter “语音与多模态模型为什么仍然难用” (Why voice and multimodal models remain hard to use), Orange [said that for Chinese transcription “Doubao’s transcription is already the best, with no real competition”](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-ddfed4fc2efd477cf98d) — a participant’s judgment. In the chapter “金融数据、算命与真正有收益的 Agent 场景” (Finance data, fortune-telling, and agents with real returns), Guizang [described how Doubao’s connectors pulling in finance and stock data made those categories noticeably richer](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-4ca0a8e2bfe5e15ccb02). In the chapter “为什么 AI 应用团队仍然跑不过模型厂商” (Why AI app teams still can’t outpace model vendors), guests criticized Doubao’s Skill and scheduled-task experience: Orange [noted that a newly installed Skill requires restarting Doubao before it works](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-a44f7bbc722ba0500add) and [that scheduled tasks exist only inside the session that created them](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-b4d3a042ab5a16ea212f) — experience snapshots from the recording. Earlier in the same episode, Yang Pan [described how “Doubao Work” keeps being miswritten as the similar-sounding “Doubao Office”](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-0dbb1daa341ec98b63dc).

In Weekly #002’s chapter “Grok Bot / Muse：为什么 Agent 需要云电脑” (Why agents need a cloud computer), Orange [likened Meta’s Muse to “the American Doubao” in positioning](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-e05b4279cf60687bde0c) and [argued that half of Doubao’s features are incomprehensible to ordinary users, so agent products must balance simplicity against complexity](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-40021a3531cadcb2de65); Yang Pan placed Doubao Work among agents that come with a cloud computer. In the chapter “语音输入：准确率之外的使用摩擦” (Voice input: friction beyond accuracy), Guizang [said he uses Doubao Input Method on his phone but wanted to uninstall the desktop version because it forces its own typing experience](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-fcd8072ea8d43c45fbe5), and [judged that Doubao does only the most basic cleanup of long voice-to-text output](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-8988b6ddafa8517d6e43). These are participants’ experiences and opinions, not an independent review. All transcripts are in Chinese; no English transcript is available.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Doubao?

Doubao is ByteDance’s AI assistant application, offering chat, voice input and voice replies, agent customization, and writing assistance. The official entry point is [doubao.com](https://www.doubao.com/).

### Where are Doubao’s web and desktop versions?

Use the web version at [doubao.com](https://www.doubao.com/) directly; install the desktop client from the official [download page](https://www.doubao.com/download/); the mobile app is available in app stores under the name Doubao.

### Which model does Doubao use?

Doubao runs on the [Doubao Seed](/en/wiki/products/doubao-seed) model family developed by ByteDance’s Seed team. The models and the Doubao app are separate layers; which models are available inside the app depends on the official pages.

### What is the difference between Doubao and Doubao Work?

Doubao is the general AI assistant; [Doubao Work](/en/wiki/products/doubao-work) is the office product for individuals and teams, covering research, documents and presentations, spreadsheets, scheduled tasks, and computer use. They have different entry points and positioning.

### Why do searches for “Doubao” return food results?

“Doubao” is also the name of a Chinese bean-paste bun, so same-name content mixes into results. To find the AI assistant, add “Doubao AI” or “ByteDance” to the query, or go straight to [doubao.com](https://www.doubao.com/).
