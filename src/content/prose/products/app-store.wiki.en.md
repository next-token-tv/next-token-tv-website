## What the App Store is

The App Store is [Apple](/en/wiki/brands/apple)’s app distribution store. It opened on July 10, 2008 with roughly 500 apps. Built into [iPhone](/en/wiki/products/iphone), iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV, and Apple Vision Pro, it handles downloading and updating applications and games. The App Store exists only on Apple platforms; there is no official client for Android or other systems.

## Discovery and safety mechanisms

Apple’s page describes the store as "the apps you love. From a place you can trust." Its main mechanisms include:

- Discovery: the Today tab carries editorial stories and collections, curators offer personalized recommendations, and app product pages provide videos, ratings, and rankings.
- Search: the official page says search supports natural language, with results including inline video previews, editorial stories, and tips.
- Safety: the official page says 100% of apps are automatically screened for known malware and go through review before listing.

Figures on the official page include nearly 2 million apps, 175 storefronts in over 40 languages, and more than 120 expert editors; such numbers change over time, so treat the [official page](https://www.apple.com/app-store/) as the reference.

## Developers and boundaries

Developers join the Apple Developer Program and submit apps through [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/) for review before release; distribution rules, commission, and regional policies change over time, so the [official Apple developer documentation](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/) is the reference. Issues with purchases, such as refund requests, go through Apple’s official [Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) page and are reviewed by Apple.

## Frequently asked questions

### When did the App Store launch?

It opened on July 10, 2008 with roughly 500 apps, as recorded in the [Wikipedia article on the App Store](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/App_Store_(Apple)) and its cited sources.

### Can I install the App Store on an Android phone?

No. The App Store is only available on Apple devices; there is no official App Store app for Android.

### How do I request a refund from the App Store?

Sign in with your Apple Account on Apple’s official [Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) page and submit the request; refunds are reviewed by Apple.

### How do developers publish an app on the App Store?

Join the Apple Developer Program, submit the app in [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/), and pass [App Review](https://developer.apple.com/app-review/). Rules and commission follow Apple’s developer documentation.
