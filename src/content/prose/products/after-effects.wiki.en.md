## Adobe After Effects

After Effects is [Adobe](/en/wiki/brands/adobe)’s software for motion graphics and visual effects, part of the Creative Cloud subscription family. Adobe positions it as motion graphics software, commonly used for title animation, compositing, and effects work. Features and plans change across releases; see the [official product page](https://www.adobe.com/products/aftereffects.html).

## Usage and boundaries

After Effects covers animation, compositing, and effects: title sequences, visual effects, and motion design for video, with Adobe’s page also noting connections to tools such as Adobe Express. Timeline editing and final assembly are usually done in [Premiere Pro](/en/wiki/products/adobe-premiere). Plans and system requirements belong on the [official page](https://www.adobe.com/products/aftereffects.html).

## Frequently asked questions

### What does “AE” stand for?

“AE” commonly refers to Adobe After Effects, a motion graphics and visual effects application.

### Where can I download After Effects?

Install it from the [official product page](https://www.adobe.com/products/aftereffects.html) or through the Creative Cloud desktop app; follow the official installation steps.

### Is After Effects free, and how is it priced?

It is sold through Creative Cloud subscriptions with individual, business, and students-and-teachers plans. Plan contents and prices are listed on the [official page](https://www.adobe.com/products/aftereffects.html).

### What is the difference between AE and PR?

AE focuses on motion graphics and visual effects; PR (Premiere Pro) focuses on editing and finishing. They are commonly used together — see the [After Effects](/en/wiki/products/after-effects) and [Premiere Pro](/en/wiki/products/adobe-premiere) entries.
