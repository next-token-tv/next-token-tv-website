## Definition

OPPO Find N is the foldable smartphone series of [OPPO](/en/wiki/brands/oppo), built around the book-style horizontal fold. The original Find N launched in 2021, followed by the Find N2 (2022), Find N3 (2023), and Find N5 (2025), along with vertical-fold Flip models such as the Find N2 Flip and Find N3 Flip. Current models and official information are on the [OPPO website](https://www.oppo.com/cn/smartphones/).

## Design traits

The defining design of the Find N series is the "narrow fold": when closed, the body's proportions are close to a regular slab phone, with a folded length similar to an ordinary handset, and it opens into a larger, nearly square inner screen. This form has continued from the first Find N onward and is the series' main contrast with book-style foldables that keep a taller, narrower unfolded ratio. The Flip models in the series fold vertically instead, complementing the book-style form.

## Related concepts

Find N is the book-style foldable series, while the Find N Flip models are vertical folds; both belong to the Find series with different form factors. They compete in the same market as Apple's [iPhone](/en/wiki/products/iphone) foldable and Xiaomi's foldables. For OPPO's business as a whole, see the [OPPO](/en/wiki/brands/oppo) entry.

## Frequently asked questions

### What models are in the OPPO Find N series?

After the original Find N (2021), the book-style line continued with the Find N2 (2022), Find N3 (2023), and Find N5 (2025), alongside vertical-fold Flip models such as the Find N2 Flip and Find N3 Flip. Official information on each model is on the [OPPO website](https://www.oppo.com/cn/smartphones/).

### How do Find N and Find Flip differ?

Find N models use the book-style horizontal fold: closed, they approach the size of a regular phone; open, they reveal a larger inner screen. Flip models fold vertically, becoming shorter when closed and opening to near a regular phone's shape. Both belong to the OPPO Find series and serve different preferences.

### What does the "narrow fold" of the Find N series mean?

The narrow fold means the closed body is relatively narrow, with proportions close to a regular slab phone and a similar in-hand feel — a design trait of the Find N series since the first model. In Episode 008, Orange also notes that "the folded length is about the same as a phone, which makes it easy to use."

### How is the crease on OPPO foldables?

In Episode 008, Orange says a Find N6 used for months showed no visible crease, and Guizang says OPPO's crease control beats Apple's — both are subjective participant opinions. Crease visibility varies by model, screen protector, and usage habits; a hands-on look or a review of the specific model is the better guide before buying.
