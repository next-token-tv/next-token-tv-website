## What Youzhiyouxing is

Youzhiyouxing (有知有行) is an investment education and service app for Chinese individual investors, built around the brand idea "investing for a better life," founded by Meng Yan and his team. The app's core methodology is "mental accounts" and the "four pots of money" asset-allocation approach: split household assets by purpose, then choose an investment method for each layer.

## Features and usage

The official site lists this content:

- Courses and articles: "Investment Lesson One" starting from zero, plus investment education articles;
- Tools: the ZhiXing Thermometer, a valuation tool for reading market temperature, plus household asset records and investment return bookkeeping;
- Advisory services: the "Long Money Account" for long-term investing and the "Stable Money Account" for conservative investing;
- Learning guides: topics such as how to invest, what to buy, interesting investments, and bookkeeping-based investing.

## Boundaries and compliance

Fund sales are provided by Hainan Youzhiyouxing Fund Sales Co., Ltd., which holds the securities and futures business license issued by the China Securities Regulatory Commission for public fund distribution. The official site states that its investment content is for learning and reference only and should not be presented as personalized investment advice.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Youzhiyouxing?

Youzhiyouxing is an investment education and service app for individual investors: investment courses and articles, valuation and bookkeeping tools, and two advisory account services for long-term and conservative investing.

### Who founded Youzhiyouxing?

[Meng Yan](/en/wiki/people/meng-yan) and his team. Meng Yan focuses on investment education and long-term investing.

### What does Youzhiyouxing offer?

The "Investment Lesson One" course, investment education articles, the ZhiXing Thermometer valuation tool, household asset records and investment return bookkeeping, and the "Long Money Account" and "Stable Money Account" advisory services; see the [official introduction](https://youzhiyouxing.cn/materials/975).

### Can I buy funds through Youzhiyouxing?

Fund sales are provided by Hainan Youzhiyouxing Fund Sales Co., Ltd., which holds a CSRC securities and futures business license for public fund distribution; the products actually offered are as disclosed inside the app.
