## What X is

X is a social platform for public posting, real-time conversation, and creator content, formerly known as Twitter, which launched in 2006. Its ownership history consists of completed events: in October 2022, Elon Musk completed his $44 billion acquisition of Twitter; in July 2023 the platform was renamed X, and the domain moved to x.com in May 2024. On March 28, 2025, Musk’s xAI acquired X Corp in an all-stock deal — announced valuations were $33 billion for X ($45 billion including $12 billion in debt) and $80 billion for xAI, combining into X.AI Holdings. After SpaceX acquired xAI in February 2026, X and Grok sit within the SpaceX corporate family (later renamed SpaceXAI). The official introduction is at [about.x.com](https://about.x.com/en).

## How X differs from Grok and Grok Bot

The three are easy to conflate: X is the social platform that carries public posts and real-time discussion; [Grok](/en/wiki/products/grok) is the model family and assistant from xAI; and [Grok Bot](/en/wiki/products/grok-bot) is an agent product built on a persistent cloud computer. After the acquisitions they belong to one corporate family but occupy different layers — platform, model, and agent. X is also one of the primary channels where AI labs announce model releases.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is X, and what is its relationship to Twitter?

X is Twitter: the platform launched in 2006, was renamed X in July 2023, and moved to the x.com domain in May 2024. The old name Twitter and the Chinese nickname “推特” remain widely used.

### Where is X’s official website?

The platform’s web entry is [x.com](https://x.com/); the corporate introduction is at [about.x.com](https://about.x.com/en).

### Who owns X now?

The timeline: Elon Musk completed the acquisition in October 2022; on March 28, 2025, xAI acquired X Corp and the two combined into X.AI Holdings; after SpaceX acquired xAI in February 2026, X and Grok belong to the SpaceX corporate family (later renamed SpaceXAI).

### What is the relationship between X and Grok?

Grok is the model family and assistant from xAI, which has shared a parent company with X since 2025, but they are different layers of product: X is the social platform, Grok is the model and assistant, and [Grok Bot](/en/wiki/products/grok-bot) is the cloud-based agent product.
