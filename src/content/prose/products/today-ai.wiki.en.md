## What Today AI is

Today AI is a personal AI agent product. The official blog describes it as "an AI personal agent with living memory—it remembers your life, notices changes, and acts before you ask", available free on Mac, iOS, and Android. The signed post "Meet Today" was written by founder Junyuan and published, together with the product post "What Is Today?", on September 15, 2026.

## Uses and boundaries

The official blog builds the product around three mechanisms:

- Living memory: memory is stored as structured, inspectable rows rather than chat logs; users can read, edit, or delete any fact, which the blog says makes the agent's behavior traceable.
- Proactive by default: the agent speaks first at two daily anchors, a morning brief and an evening close-out; the stated bar for a proactive message is "only what you would have wanted a person to tell you".
- Connectors: scoped connections to calendar, mail, notes, files, and health data (Apple HealthKit, Android Health Connect), optional and revocable; anything external — sending mail, moving events, messaging others — requires the user's explicit confirmation before it sends. Today Recording produces meeting notes grounded in your projects and contacts, and Community Skills extend the agent's repeatable workflows.

Under the hood, the blog says the agent loop combines frontier models, task execution, and always-on cloud computers so multi-step work can continue while the user is offline. These capability descriptions come from the official blog; actual availability follows the download page and the product itself.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Today AI?

By the official blog's definition, Today is an AI personal agent: it combines durable, inspectable memory with proactive suggestions tied to your day and cross-app execution after your confirmation, as distinct from a chatbot that answers one prompt at a time. See the [official explainer post](https://today.ai/articles/blog/what-is-today).

### Which platforms does Today AI support, and is it free?

The official blog says Today is free to use on Mac, iPhone, and Android; download entry points are on the [official site](https://today.ai/). Any future paid plans would appear on official pages.

### How does Today AI handle memory and privacy?

The official blog states that memory consists of structured rows users can read, edit, and delete; that connector permissions are revocable; and that external actions require confirmation before sending. Note that these are the company's design commitments — no independent security audit or third-party assessment was found.

### Who makes Today AI?

The official "Meet Today" post is signed by founder Junyuan, laying out the vision and the three commitments: knows you, acts before you ask, walks with you.
