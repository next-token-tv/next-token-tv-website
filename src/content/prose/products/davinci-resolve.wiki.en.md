## What DaVinci Resolve is

DaVinci Resolve is post-production software from Blackmagic Design that combines editing, color grading, visual effects, motion graphics, and audio post in a single application, running on macOS, Windows, and Linux. It began as a professional color-grading system and expanded into a full post pipeline; the current major version shown on the official product page is DaVinci Resolve 21. Features and versions follow the [official product page](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve).

## Features and edition boundaries

The official product page organizes the software into “pages”: Media and Deliver handle media management and final output, Cut and Edit provide fast-turnaround and full-fidelity editing, Color is the grading page, Fusion offers node-based compositing, Fairlight is a built-in audio-post DAW, and version 21 adds a photo editing page. The software comes in a free edition and the paid DaVinci Resolve Studio: the free edition includes multi-user collaboration and HDR grading with output limits, while Studio adds the AI Neural Engine, extra Resolve FX, AI noise reduction, text-based editing, Magic Mask, and support for higher bit depths and resolutions. AI capabilities include facial recognition, smart reframing, Super Scale upscaling, auto color, and color matching. Full edition comparisons and pricing are on the [official product page](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve); hardware control surfaces and editor keyboards are sold by Blackmagic Design.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is DaVinci Resolve?

Blackmagic Design’s all-in-one post-production software covering editing, color grading, visual effects, motion graphics, and audio post, with a photo editing page added in version 21, on macOS, Windows, and Linux.

### How do the free and Studio editions differ?

The free edition includes the core feature set, including multi-user collaboration and HDR grading, with output limits; Studio adds the AI Neural Engine, extra Resolve FX, AI noise reduction, text-based editing, and Magic Mask, and lifts bit-depth and resolution limits. The full comparison is on the [official product page](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve).

### Where do I download DaVinci Resolve?

Download links for each operating system are on the [official product page](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve); Studio licenses are sold through official channels as well.

### What AI features does DaVinci Resolve have?

The official page groups AI features under the Neural Engine: facial recognition, object detection, smart reframing, Speed Warp retiming, Super Scale upscaling, auto color and color matching, plus Voice Isolation in Fairlight — some of them Studio-only.

### Does DaVinci Resolve support MCP or AI agent control?

In Weekly #002, Guizang said DaVinci had shipped an MCP for AI use, paid edition only — a participant’s retelling; the official product page does not detail such an interface. For availability and integration, rely on Blackmagic Design’s official documentation.
