## What AI Passport is

AI Passport is an open wearable AI agent device from [FoloToy](/en/wiki/brands/folotoy), presented by its maker as “a passport to build the world together with an AI Agent.” The device uses a transparent shell that makes the screen, mainboard, NFC, microphone, speaker, and battery part of the product’s look. The official site is [ai-passport.folotoy.cn](https://ai-passport.folotoy.cn/).

## Usage and play modes

Out of the box, the device’s default form is an identity card: a WeChat mini-program syncs your name, avatar, self-introduction, and a full-screen image over Bluetooth, and a built-in mini game is included. Users can also install community-shared firmware from the official “plays community,” covering games, tools, and AI interactions. Installation runs through a web flasher, and the official notes warn that installing community firmware overwrites everything currently on the device; restoring factory firmware requires a Chrome or Edge browser and a Type-C cable.

## Development

AI Passport emphasizes open firmware: users can build their own “plays” and share them with the community. Official tutorials cover workflows that use AI coding agents — including [Codex](/en/wiki/products/codex), Claude Code, and TRAE — so wearable firmware development can be handed directly to a coding agent.

## Frequently asked questions

### What kind of device is the AI Passport?

It is FoloToy’s open wearable AI hardware: by default an identity card that syncs personal information, with support for installing community firmware for games and tools, and open firmware for custom development. See the [official site](https://ai-passport.folotoy.cn/).

### How do I develop my own play for the AI Passport?

Official tutorials describe building plays with AI coding agents such as Codex, Claude Code, and TRAE, then publishing the firmware to the plays community. The entry point is the [official guide](https://ai-passport.folotoy.cn/).

### What happens if I install community firmware?

The official notes state that installing community firmware through the web flasher overwrites everything currently on the device. To restore factory firmware, follow the official guide using a Chrome or Edge browser and a Type-C cable.

Current pricing and editions are on the [official site](https://ai-passport.folotoy.cn/).
