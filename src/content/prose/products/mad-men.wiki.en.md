## What Mad Men is

Mad Men is an American drama series created by Matthew Weiner for AMC. It premiered on July 19, 2007 and ended on May 17, 2015, spanning seven seasons and 92 episodes. Set in 1960s New York, it follows the fictional Madison Avenue agency Sterling Cooper, with Jon Hamm starring as advertising executive Don Draper. The full series streams on AMC's streaming service AMC+.

## Series facts

AMC's official page calls it the network's critically acclaimed original series that pulls viewers into the high-powered "Golden Age" of advertising. On awards: the series won the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Drama Series for each of its first four seasons (2008–2011) — the first basic cable series to win it — and received 16 Emmys and 5 Golden Globes in total, along with honors such as a Peabody Award. ## FAQ

### Where can I watch Mad Men?

Producer AMC streams the full series on AMC+; the platforms available differ by region. See the [official AMC page](https://www.amc.com/shows/mad-men/explore--1002238) and licensed platforms where you are.

### How many seasons and episodes does Mad Men have?

Seven seasons and 92 episodes, premiering July 19, 2007 and concluding May 17, 2015.

### What is Mad Men about?

Advertising on 1960s Madison Avenue in New York, centered on the agency Sterling Cooper and ad man Don Draper's professional and personal life; AMC frames it as a portrait of the industry's "Golden Age".

### What awards did Mad Men win?

It won the Primetime Emmy Award for Outstanding Drama Series four years in a row for its first four seasons (2008–2011), with 16 Emmys and 5 Golden Globes in total. Full lists are on the [Wikipedia awards summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_awards_and_nominations_received_by_Mad_Men).
