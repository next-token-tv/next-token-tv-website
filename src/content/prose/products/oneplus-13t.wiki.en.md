## Definition

The OnePlus 13T is a compact Android smartphone from OnePlus, released in China in April 2025. Its international counterpart is the OnePlus 13s, announced in India in June 2025; the two names refer to the same generation of product in different markets. Product information is on the [official page](https://www.oneplus.com/cn/13t).

## Positioning and software

The OnePlus 13T belongs to the OnePlus 13 series, which ships with Android; devices for the Chinese market use OPPO's ColorOS (international models use OxygenOS, and [ColorOS](/en/wiki/products/coloros) is OPPO's phone system brand). A compact flagship is the model's defining positioning within the series: it targets users who prefer a smaller body without giving up flagship specifications. Specific specifications are on the [official specs page](https://www.oneplus.com/cn/13t).

## Related concepts

The OnePlus 13T belongs to the 13 series alongside the OnePlus 13 and 13R, but it is the smallest model among them. The OnePlus 13T (China) and the OnePlus 13s (international) are two market names for the same generation, not two separate models. OnePlus is a phone brand under OPPO, but brand and company entries remain distinct: see the [OnePlus](/en/wiki/brands/oneplus) and [OPPO](/en/wiki/brands/oppo) entries on this site.

## Frequently asked questions

### When was the OnePlus 13T released?

The OnePlus 13T was released in China in April 2025; its international counterpart, the OnePlus 13s, was announced in India in June 2025. See the [official page](https://www.oneplus.com/cn/13t) for sales channels.

### Are the OnePlus 13T and OnePlus 13s the same phone?

They are two market names for the same generation: the 13T is sold in China, and the 13s is the international counterpart announced in India in June 2025. Configuration and software may vary by market, so check each market's official page.

### What system does the OnePlus 13T run?

The OnePlus 13 series ships with Android; Chinese-market devices use OPPO's ColorOS, and international models use OxygenOS. System versions and update policies are governed by the [official page](https://www.oneplus.com/cn/13t).

### Is the OnePlus 13T a compact phone?

Yes — a compact flagship is the 13T's positioning, and it is the smallest-bodied model in the 13 series, aimed at users who prefer a smaller handset. Yang Pan, a participant on the show, also calls it a compact flagship. Exact dimensions are on the [official specs page](https://www.oneplus.com/cn/13t).
