## What Cue is

Cue is a personal agent app from Manus. The official site, [cue.im](https://cue.im/), is credited “Cue by Manus” and positions it as “Your Personal Agents, With Their Own Identity.” According to the official description, each agent on Cue has its own email, phone number, wallet, and computer to get real work done — consistent with Manus’s broader idea of giving agents a complete execution environment.

Its relationship to the main Manus product is cross-device continuity: [Manus’s official page](https://manus.im/desktop) says “Take your agents with you on Cue, then pick up right where you left off on desktop.” Cue serves as the mobile entry point, and continuing on the desktop happens through the Manus desktop app. For the Manus general agent itself, the desktop workspace, and the API, see the [Manus](/en/wiki/products/manus) and [Manus Studio](/en/wiki/products/manus-studio) entries.

## Usage and boundaries

The official entry point for Cue is [cue.im](https://cue.im/); the mobile app’s download entry sits in the download area of the Manus site. Registration, available features, and quotas follow the official channels.

One distinction matters: some capabilities in the Manus ecosystem belong to Manus itself or to Manus Studio, not to Cue — the show’s participants also noted that the two products’ plans and billing are separate (see below). When delegating tasks on Cue, the usual agent boundaries apply: authorize external services step by step through the official flow, and review the output yourself.

Orange mentioned [the cloud computer was billed separately, “a dozen-odd dollars a month”](/weekly/006/transcript#quote-608736df6458e977a6af) — for billing details, rely on the [official pricing page](https://manus.im/pricing). All of these are the participants’ experiences at the time; see the [Chinese transcript chapter](/weekly/006/transcript#chapter-15) for the full discussion.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Cue?

Cue is a personal agent app from Manus: each agent has its own email, phone number, wallet, and computer, so you can hand over and follow up on tasks from a mobile device and continue on the desktop. The official site is [cue.im](https://cue.im/).

### How do I download Cue?

The mobile app’s download entry is in the download area of the [Manus site](https://manus.im/desktop); continuing on the desktop uses the Manus desktop app (macOS/Windows).

### Does Cue require an invite code?

Registration and access follow the official channels. In the show, Guizang mentioned a friend signing up with an invite code and being asked for a non-+86 phone number — that was the participants’ experience when the episode was recorded in October 2026 and has not been confirmed through official channels; current registration requirements follow [cue.im](https://cue.im/) and the official sign-up flow.

### What is the relationship between Cue and Manus?

Cue is Manus’s mobile personal-agent app (officially credited “Cue by Manus”): you use your agents on the phone through Cue and continue with the Manus desktop app. The Manus general agent and the desktop workspace are covered in the [Manus](/en/wiki/products/manus) and [Manus Studio](/en/wiki/products/manus-studio) entries.

### How much does Cue cost?

Show participants mentioned that Manus’s cloud computer was billed separately; the official page does not list plan or pricing details, and the fee structure follows the [official pricing page](https://manus.im/pricing).
