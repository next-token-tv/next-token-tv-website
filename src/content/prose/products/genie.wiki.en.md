## What is Genie?

Genie is a world model family from [Google DeepMind](/en/wiki/brands/google-deepmind) that generates interactive virtual environments from prompts such as text or images. Unlike a video model that produces a fixed clip, a world model lets the user steer a character or a viewpoint and keep moving and acting in the generated environment. Users can start with text or an image. Episode 011 discusses Genie 4 around its interactive world-model experience.

## Version history

- **Genie**: announced in February 2024, generating basic playable 2D environments from images, initially trained on roughly 30,000 hours of 2D platformer gameplay.
- **Genie 2**: announced in December 2024, generating 3D environments from isometric and first-person perspectives for short interactions.
- **Genie 3**: announced in August 2025, generating and modifying interactive 720p worlds at 24 fps in real time while retaining about one minute of situational memory; it also underpins driving-simulation uses such as world models for autonomous vehicles.
- **Genie 4**: announced on October 27, 2026, adding synchronized audio, photorealistic rendering, an egocentric perspective, and runtime prompting.

## Access

Project Genie, the official research preview, opened through Google Labs to Google AI Ultra subscribers on January 29, 2026, with exploration capped at roughly 60 seconds per session. Each version's description and availability is listed on the [official product page](https://deepmind.google/models/genie/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Genie and who develops it?

Genie is a world model family developed by Google DeepMind that generates interactive virtual environments from text or image prompts. The first version was announced in February 2024.

### How do Genie 3 and Genie 4 differ?

Genie 3, announced in August 2025, generates interactive 720p worlds at 24 fps in real time and retains about one minute of situational memory. Genie 4, announced on October 27, 2026, adds synchronized audio, photorealistic rendering, an egocentric perspective, and runtime prompting.

### How can I try Genie?

Since January 29, 2026, the official research preview Project Genie has been available through Google Labs to Google AI Ultra subscribers, with sessions capped at about 60 seconds. Availability of each version is described on the [official product page](https://deepmind.google/models/genie/).

### How is Genie different from a video generation model?

A video model outputs a fixed clip. Genie generates an interactive environment: the user controls a character that moves and acts, and the world responds to those actions in real time.
