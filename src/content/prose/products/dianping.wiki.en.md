## Definition

Dianping is a platform for business listings, consumer reviews and local-life discovery. Meituan's rules center describes its "大众点评排行榜" (Dianping rankings) as "基于大众点评APP、小程序等终端内的用户大数据，经算法自动计算，每日更新" — computed daily by an algorithm from user data — and states the rankings are "公正不收费……不接受任何形式购买排名" (fair and unpaid; buying ranks is not accepted).

## How it works

Users look up businesses and read or post reviews; the platform turns this data into star ratings, rankings and recommendations. Rankings come in types such as category lists, city guides and specialty-dish lists, and businesses must clear thresholds on rating and review counts to qualify; the official rules say review manipulation and fake traffic lead to immediate delisting.

## Usage

The typical use is checking a restaurant's or venue's reputation, average spend and recommended dishes before going out, and discovering local services. On October 8, 2015, Meituan and Dianping merged into one company (renamed "Meituan" in September 2020); Dianping is now a Meituan brand, and Meituan's official site lists the Dianping app among its applications.

## Related and confusable terms

Dianping and [Meituan](/en/wiki/products/meituan) are two consumer brands under the same company: Dianping centers on in-store business listings and user reviews, while Meituan spans a wider set of local-life services such as delivery. "Dianping" names the platform, while "大众点评排行榜" is only its in-app ranking feature; the generic word for reviewing does not refer to this platform either.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the relationship between Dianping and Meituan?

The two companies merged on October 8, 2015 (renamed "Meituan" from September 2020), and Dianping is now a consumer brand under Meituan; Meituan's official site lists the Dianping app among its applications.

### Where can I download the Dianping app?

Meituan's official download area links the Dianping app (w.dianping.com); use the entry currently provided on [Meituan's official site](https://www.meituan.com/).

### How are Dianping rankings computed?

The official rules say rankings are computed daily by an algorithm from user data, are independent of whether a business cooperates with the platform, and cannot be bought; eligibility and delisting rules are in the [Meituan Rules Center](https://rules-center.meituan.com/m/detail/guize/1567?activeRule=1).
