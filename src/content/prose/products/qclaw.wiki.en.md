## What QClaw is

QClaw is a personal AI assistant from Tencent, positioned on its official site as a "WeChat remote-work AI assistant" (微信远程办公 AI 助手): users assign tasks, organize files, and run complex work remotely through WeChat, and can extend its office, creation, and development abilities with skills. The official site is [qclaw.qq.com](https://qclaw.qq.com/), which also links product pricing, documentation, news, and an open platform. Tencent's WorkBuddy is a separate desktop-office agent product with a different focus — see the [WorkBuddy](/en/wiki/products/workbuddy) entry.

## Usage and boundaries

- Clients: the site offers downloads for macOS (Apple silicon and Intel), Windows, iOS, and Android, plus a mini-program entry; sign-in is required.
- Remote collaboration: per the official positioning, users interact with QClaw through WeChat to assign file and task work remotely; the supported connection methods and operation scope are described in the [official documentation](https://qclaw.qq.com/docs).
- Open platform: the [QClaw open platform](https://qclaw.qq.com/open-platform) gives developers two ways to publish — submitting a finished agent configuration (AGENT.md, IDENTITY.md, SOUL.md, and a knowledge base) to the expert gallery, or connecting third-party apps as connectors (OAuth 2.0, API keys, and skill packages). The platform states that core configuration files and knowledge bases are delivered with cloud-side encryption and that developers can price their work as free, one-time purchase, subscription, or a combination. Listed ecosystem partners include Tencent Docs, ima, QQ Mail, Kingsoft Docs, Baidu Netdisk, and WeCom.
- Boundaries: what QClaw can actually do depends on the model, skills, and permissions in use. The site's subpages (pricing, docs, news) are dynamically loaded, so current billing and feature details belong to the official pages.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is QClaw, and is it from Tencent?

Yes. The official site describes QClaw as Tencent's WeChat remote-work AI assistant for assigning tasks, organizing files, and executing complex work remotely, with skill-based extensions. See the [official site](https://qclaw.qq.com/).

### Where can I download QClaw, and which platforms are supported?

Download from the [official site](https://qclaw.qq.com/): installers are offered for macOS (Apple silicon), macOS (Intel), Windows, and iOS, plus an Android package and a mini-program entry. The site's download section is authoritative.

### How is QClaw different from WorkBuddy?

Both are Tencent products with different focuses: QClaw targets individuals and emphasizes assigning and following up on tasks remotely through WeChat, while WorkBuddy is a full-scenario AI office workstation for desktop work spanning office, coding, and design. See the [WorkBuddy](/en/wiki/products/workbuddy) entry and both official sites for the comparison.

### How much does QClaw cost?

The official site has a [pricing page](https://qclaw.qq.com/pricing); current tiers belong to that page. For developers, the open platform supports free, one-time purchase, subscription, and combined pricing, with revenue rules described on the [open platform](https://qclaw.qq.com/open-platform).

### What is the QClaw open platform for?

It is the developer ecosystem entry point: submit a configured agent (configuration files plus a knowledge base) to the expert gallery, or connect third-party apps as connectors that users operate through natural language. The platform handles review, cloud-side protection, and commercial revenue sharing. See the [open platform](https://qclaw.qq.com/open-platform).
