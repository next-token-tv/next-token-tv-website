## What Dia is

Dia is an AI browser developed by The Browser Company. The company announced the product in December 2024, and in May 2025 it stopped active development of Arc to focus on Dia; in September 2025 The Browser Company was acquired by Atlassian. The official tagline is "Dia reads between the tabs." This history follows the [Wikipedia entry on The Browser Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Browser_Company).

## Features and boundaries

Per the [official site](https://www.diabrowser.com/), Dia's AI features build on the user's browsing and work context: Morning Brief summarizes calendar, inbox, and key links; Reports gather information scattered across tools such as Slack, Notion, and Calendar into shareable reports; cross-context questions can reach GSuite, Slack, and open tabs; and Decks generate presentation slides from that context. The browser also ships Live Work, Profiles, Splits, and organized tabs.

For downloads, the official download page notes it is "currently available on Apple macOS 14+ with M1 chips or later"; check the [download page](https://www.diabrowser.com/) for current system requirements. Commercially, the download includes a 14-day free trial of the Better Days subscription, and Dia for Work adds team guardrails such as SSO and admin tools. On privacy, the site states data is "never sold or used to build ad profiles," with toggles for trackers, ads, memory, and data sharing.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Dia browser?

An AI browser from The Browser Company that uses open tabs, Slack, GSuite, and other context for questions, summaries, and writing. See [diabrowser.com](https://www.diabrowser.com/).

### Does Dia support Windows or Linux?

The official download page notes it is currently available on Apple macOS 14+ with M1 chips or later; other platforms follow whatever the download page currently states.

### Is Dia paid?

The download includes a 14-day free trial of the Better Days subscription, and the browser itself is downloadable; Dia for Work adds SSO and admin tools for teams. Subscription pricing is on the official site.

### How do Dia and Arc relate?

Both are products of The Browser Company. In May 2025 the company announced it would stop active development of Arc and focus on Dia; see the [Wikipedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Browser_Company).
