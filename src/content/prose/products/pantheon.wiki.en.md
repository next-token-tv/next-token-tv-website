## Definition

Pantheon is an adult animated science-fiction series about mind uploading and digital life, created by Craig Silverstein and adapted from several short stories by Ken Liu collected in The Hidden Girl and Other Stories. Produced by AMC Studios and Titmouse among others, it runs two seasons of eight episodes each. Season 1 premiered on AMC+ in September 2022; after AMC canceled the show in January 2023, season 2 was released in October 2023. Netflix acquired international distribution rights, streaming season 1 from November 2024 and season 2 from February 2025 (see the [Netflix title page](https://www.netflix.com/title/81937398)).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Pantheon based on?

It is adapted from several of Ken Liu's short stories, including "The Gods Will Not Be Chained", collected in The Hidden Girl and Other Stories.

### How many seasons of Pantheon are there?

Two seasons of eight episodes each, 16 in total: season 1 premiered in September 2022 and season 2 was released in October 2023.

### Who created Pantheon?

The series was created by Craig Silverstein, with production by AMC Studios, Titmouse and others.
