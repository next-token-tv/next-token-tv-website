## Renoise

Renoise (site: [renoise.ai](https://renoise.ai)) is an AI video and image creation suite aimed at marketers and creators. It aggregates generative models from multiple providers behind one interface, so users can produce video and images without configuring each model themselves, and it adds image- and video-editing tools for post-processing (see the [official site](https://renoise.ai); the list of supported models and features is whatever the site currently shows).

## Agent and MCP access

Beyond the web app, Renoise exposes an agent capability and an MCP interface: AI coding tools and agents can call it directly to generate video and images. The official site also offers an app download and documentation; see the [site](https://renoise.ai) for integration details.

## Templates and remaking

The site organizes its entry points by use case — marketing creatives, UGC social content, story shorts, anime and illustration — and includes a trending-templates section: users start from a template and modify it into their own version instead of beginning from a blank canvas. That is one of its main differences from raw model tools.

## Discussion in the show

Episode 009, chapter 1 ("Harness 与插件生态" — [Harness and the plugin ecosystem](/weekly/009/transcript#chapter-01)), names Renoise as a positive example of "growing toward agents." Xiangyang Qiaomu says "Xiaoming's team's Renoise" also builds on [Hyperframes](/en/wiki/products/hyperframes) but is smart to aggregate all the models people are too lazy to configure and ship it as an MCP (see [the passage](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-2f16bb6bca60bb7c0c9f)); Orange adds that letting users "make the same thing" from templates — giving them inspiration — is more essential than handing them a bare tool (see [the passage](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-adcafb397075c8d00fed)). These are the participants' observations and opinions about the product strategy; team and product details are authoritative on the official site.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Renoise?

An AI video and image creation suite that aggregates multiple generative models, aimed at marketing and creator use cases; the official site is [renoise.ai](https://renoise.ai).

### Is it the same product as the music software Renoise?

No. renoise.ai is the AI video and image suite; the identically named Renoise ([renoise.com](https://renoise.com)) is a tracker-based digital audio workstation. The two are unrelated.

### Which models does Renoise support?

It aggregates generative models from several providers, covering video and image generation; the supported list changes with updates and is shown on the [official site](https://renoise.ai).

### How do I connect Renoise to an AI coding tool?

Through the MCP interface: agents call Renoise to generate video or images. Integration steps are in the documentation on the official site.

### Who develops Renoise?

The site operates under the Renoise AI name without disclosing a specific team or members; in the show, Xiangyang Qiaomu refers to "Xiaoming's team's Renoise," which is a participant's account.
