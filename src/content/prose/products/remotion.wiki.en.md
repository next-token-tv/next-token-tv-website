## Remotion

Remotion is a set of tools for creating and rendering videos programmatically with [React](/en/wiki/products/react): the video is described as React components, and the tooling renders it into video files. The project was created by Jonny Burger, with the GitHub repository dating to 2020 and version 1.0 released on February 6, 2021 (per the [GitHub release record](https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion/releases/tag/v1.0.0)). The official site is [remotion.dev](https://www.remotion.dev).

## Usage and distinctions

Remotion treats video as a code asset: captions, motion graphics and data-driven batch videos can all be generated from components and parameters, with companion products such as cloud rendering (Remotion Lambda) and an embeddable web player (Remotion Player).

It does not use a conventional open-source license: the official [license and pricing page](https://www.remotion.dev/license) states that individuals and companies of up to 3 people can use it for free, while larger companies need a paid Company License. Compared with [HyperFrames](/en/wiki/products/hyperframes), both bring web technology to video rendering; in episode 009 participants said HeyGen's HyperFrames integrated parts of Remotion's underlying work — a claim from the participants, not from either company.

## Discussion in the show

Episode 009 discusses Remotion in the chapter on the harness and plugin ecosystem ("Harness 与插件生态", [English gloss](/weekly/009/transcript#chapter-01) in the Chinese transcript). Xiangyang Qiaomu says HyperFrames "itself bundles several of Remotion's underlying pieces" and that HeyGen packaged it into a framework, becoming the consensus first choice for HTML video rendering within about half a year (see [the passage](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-d54f0ea51c642151a619)). Guizang calls Remotion "really a half-finished product" with various problems in use, and argues HeyGen won by spotting the opportunity earlier and shipping a more complete tool (see [the passage](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-8d8d1aef3dbc66335abc)). These are the participants' personal assessments, not a survey of Remotion's developers or users.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does Remotion do?

It describes videos as React components and renders them from code, suited to programmatic video such as captions, motion graphics and data-driven batch generation; see the [official documentation](https://www.remotion.dev/docs).

### Is Remotion free?

Per the official [license and pricing page](https://www.remotion.dev/license), individuals and companies of up to 3 people can use it for free; larger companies need a paid Company License.

### Who created Remotion?

Jonny Burger created it, with the GitHub repository dating to 2020 and version 1.0 released in February 2021.

### How do Remotion and HyperFrames relate?

Both render video with web technology. [HyperFrames](/en/wiki/products/hyperframes) is open-sourced by [HeyGen](/en/wiki/brands/heygen); in episode 009 participants said HyperFrames integrated parts of Remotion's underlying work — a participant claim, with no official statement from either side.
