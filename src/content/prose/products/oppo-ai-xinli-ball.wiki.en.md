## What the OPPO AI Xinli Ball is

The OPPO AI Xinli Ball is a wearable AI recording device from [OPPO](/en/wiki/brands/oppo). It works independently of a phone: the ball records in-person conversations as they happen, and a companion application turns the recordings into organized, reviewable notes. The product page on the OPPO official store notes that the device began shipping in batches from November 5, 2026.

## Usage and how it works

The Xinli Ball is built to turn everyday conversations into structured records: wear it or place it near the conversation to record, then use the companion app for organization and summaries. In episode 008, Xiangyang Qiaomu described using one for a full day: once the day's recordings are complete, the device exposes an MCP interface, so the records can be fed into a processing workflow of your own. Orange added that it also automatically summarizes what was discussed.

## Using it and how it differs

Compared with recording on a phone or using a meeting-transcription app, the Xinli Ball is standalone hardware that does not need the phone present while recording. Participants in the show mentioned that the device initially worked only with OPPO phones, and that users later extracted its installation package so it can be installed on other Android phones as well, with the ball itself purchased separately; Guizang thought phones running OPPO's ColorOS could use it directly. Actual package compatibility and supported devices follow the official documentation.
