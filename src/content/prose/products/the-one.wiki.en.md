## What The One is

The One (theOne companion) is an AI companion product that runs inside WeChat: users chat with AI characters in their WeChat contact list, with no separate app to install. It was created by [Wang Dengke (DK)](/en/wiki/people/wang-dengke) and his team behind Duxiang; the third-party AI job-mapping site bonjour.bio lists Wang Dengke as the founder of the Duxiang/TheOne team. The positioning is emotional companionship rather than productivity — in his blog, Wang Dengke describes the goal as letting ordinary people use AI in WeChat compliantly and simply, aiming for "companionship closer to real-life scenarios."

## Connection and product form

The One runs on the WeChat plugin entry Tencent opened to OpenClaw. According to Wang Dengke's blog, Tencent opened a plugin entry in March 2026 letting [OpenClaw](/en/wiki/products/openclaw) connect to WeChat through the open-source openclaw-weixin repository; he then built theOne in a few days, launching in early April 2026, where users scan a QR code to add the product as a WeChat contact and chat. This differs from standalone agent clients; as the show's participant Orange put it, "The One，它都不是 Agent，它就是一个 Chat" — it is not an agent, just a chat. For WeChat the platform more broadly, see [WeChat](/en/wiki/products/wechat).

## Growth and discussion in the show

Wang Dengke's blog records the early growth: starting April 24, 2026, online users jumped from roughly one to two hundred to 1,600, then 15,000 the next day, peaking above 60,000 on the third night; during the May Day holiday the product reached "one million registered users in three days," consuming on the order of a hundred billion tokens in a single day; at the time of writing, the blog says it handled over 100 million messages daily.

In Weekly #002's chapter “平台内 AI 助手：入口简单，体验才完整” (In-platform AI assistants: simple entry, complete experience), [Orange said Wang Dengke's team "blew up" with The One, relaying their feedback that the OpenClaw QR codes provided by WeChat were unstable and failed to receive messages about 10% of the time](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-5d6a2818237d83fa41bb) in the Chinese transcript; when he asked why they did not build an app or website, the answer was that "users just love the contact list." [Orange also judged that scan-to-chat raised conversion to nearly 100%, about six times better than the "exposure-to-download" path](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-a7d67da6c371a0ecb83a). These are Orange's retelling of that team's comments and his personal evaluation, not official figures.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who built The One?

Wang Dengke (DK) and his team behind Duxiang; see his [blog post](https://greatdk.com/2149.html) and the [team page](https://bonjour.bio/jobs-mapping/team/theone) on bonjour.bio.

### How do you use The One?

Scan the QR code in WeChat to add The One as a contact, then chat with it directly in the contact list — no separate app install. The connection depends on WeChat's OpenClaw plugin entry.

### Is The One an agent?

Per participant Orange on the show, "it is not an agent, it is just a chat" — the form is a companion character you chat with inside WeChat, not an agent that executes tasks.

### What is the relationship between The One and Duxiang?

Both come from the same founder, Wang Dengke, and his team: Duxiang is a standalone "AI friend circle" app, while The One is the team's AI companion product inside WeChat.
