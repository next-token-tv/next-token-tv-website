## What Airbnb is

Airbnb is an online booking platform connecting hosts and guests across stays, experiences, and services. It was founded in 2008 in San Francisco by Brian Chesky, Joe Gebbia, and Nathan Blecharczyk, is still headquartered there, and went public in December 2020. The official help page puts it this way: "Airbnb was born in 2007 when 2 hosts welcomed 3 guests to their San Francisco home." That page lists more than 5 million hosts and over 2 billion cumulative guest arrivals; the numbers belong to the [official page](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/2503).

## Usage and boundaries

- **Guest side**: on [airbnb.com](https://www.airbnb.com/) you can book stays ranging from whole homes to long-term lodging, join Experiences hosted by local experts (cooking, hiking, art, music, and more), or book Services such as a private chef or an in-home massage. The platform offers identity verification and secure payments, and the official page says the Community Support team assists with rebooking, refunds, and reimbursements around the clock in 11 languages.
- **Host side**: the entry point is [airbnb.com/host/homes](https://www.airbnb.com/host/homes). The official page says listing is free, hosts set their own price, and Airbnb collects a service fee after a reservation completes — stays use split-fee or single-fee structures, with a transition toward single fees underway; 3% is not a universal host rate. See the [service-fee policy](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1857). AirCover for hosts includes guest identity verification, reservation screening, up to $3M in damage protection, and $1M in liability insurance, among other items.
- **Boundaries**: availability, payment methods, taxes, and legal requirements vary by region and listing; the charge summary at checkout and the official Help Center are the deciding references before booking.

## Discussion in the show

The Weekly #005 chapter “Instinct：订房、商旅与信任” (Instinct: booking, business travel, and trust) places Airbnb in the context of AI-agent booking. In the Chinese transcript, Orange [relays a widely circulated article](/weekly/005/transcript#quote-41a0f47665e942511b60): the author uninstalled Airbnb and sold the stock after booking with [Instinct](/en/wiki/products/instinct) or [Muse](/en/wiki/products/muse-agent), because the agents' search showed booking outside Airbnb could save 70%; he also mentions the trust dispute around Airbnb ratings at the time. These are the participants' retellings of an online article and events; the show did not verify them independently. Yang Pan follows up that [against traditional businesses like Airbnb and Ctrip, as long as a new channel is cheaper and exists on the internet, a little GEO or SEO work means agents will in theory pull users over](/weekly/005/transcript#quote-0f281213647f94aa759d); Guizang counters with a constraint: [agents still answer to people, and a channel that books bad stays gets ruled out in user feedback](/weekly/005/transcript#quote-32c09a350ffea55af0b7).

In the chapter “邮件和微信：国内外的信息流差异” (Email and WeChat: information-flow differences), Yang Pan [uses Airbnb as an example of the difference between markets: overseas users' daily bills and orders live in their inboxes, while Chinese users' daily affairs run inside mini-programs and apps](/weekly/005/transcript#quote-3376c105562013f6957d) — which changes the context a personal agent can reach in each market.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Airbnb?

An online booking platform connecting hosts and guests for stays, experiences, and services, founded in 2008 in San Francisco by Brian Chesky, Joe Gebbia, and Nathan Blecharczyk. Entry point: [airbnb.com](https://www.airbnb.com/).

### How is booking on Airbnb charged?

Stay and experience prices are set by the listing's host, and the checkout page shows the full charge breakdown; stay fees depend on the split-fee or single-fee structure, region, and listing, with a transition to single fees underway. See the [official fee policy](https://www.airbnb.com/help/article/1857).

### How do I become an Airbnb host?

Start at [airbnb.com/host/homes](https://www.airbnb.com/host/homes): create a listing in a few steps, set your own price, and Airbnb collects a service fee after you get paid. You can also host Experiences and Services, or hire a local co-host to manage things for you.

### Where do I find Airbnb's refund and protection policies?

Refunds, rebooking, and reimbursements can be looked up and requested in the [official Help Center](https://www.airbnb.com/help/); the official page says the support team handles rebooking, refunds, and reimbursements around the clock, and hosts additionally have the AirCover protection program.

### What does Airbnb offer besides stays?

Experiences — activities hosted by local experts — and Services such as a private chef or an in-home massage; together with stays, these are the three supply categories on the official page.
