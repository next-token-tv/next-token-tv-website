## What Chi Xie Ru Song is

Chi Xie Ru Song (持械入宋, roughly "Entering the Song Dynasty Armed") is an AI-produced short drama about modern armed characters entering the Song dynasty: it uses AI-generated imagery to tell a story of people carrying modern weapons into the Song era. The work is published on [Bilibili](/en/wiki/products/bilibili), where it took off around November 2026.

## Discussed on Next Token

Episode 008 (recorded November 1, 2026) discusses the series at length in the chapter on [AI short dramas as a creative opportunity for individuals and small teams](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-04), in the Chinese transcript:

- Orange said the recently viral Chi Xie Ru Song ["took off on Bilibili first"](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-b881be5c85c48f9ba456), holding it up as an example of an AI short drama rising through a platform's organic traffic.
- Xiangyang Qiaomu said [the further he watched, the more visibly the AI technology was improving](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-ab199c26df599b9b0f5b): the camera work kept getting better, with shots from the enemy's perspective — an arrow strike and blood on screen — and framing from different viewpoints. He argued that large-scale battle scenes are a natural fit for AI production, while a traditional production would spend enormous sums on the same scenes.
- Xiangyang Qiaomu also relayed the impression that the work was started by ["two girls in Zhengzhou, it seems"](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-55f50011bde4f0dde04f). This is secondhand hearsay from a participant, not confirmed information about the production team.

The context of the discussion: once AI lowered the production threshold, individuals and small teams could break out on platforms through quality and creativity.
