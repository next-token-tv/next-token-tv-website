## Definition

Phantom Blade Zero (stylized Phantom Blade Ø, 影之刃零) is a wuxia action role-playing game developed and published by S-GAME, built in Unreal Engine 5 and released for PlayStation 5 and Windows (PC). With a "kungfupunk" art direction that mixes kung fu with steampunk and cyberpunk elements, it puts the player in the role of an elite assassin. The game was first announced with a trailer at the PlayStation Showcase on 24 May 2023; a 9 September 2026 release date was announced at The Game Awards in December 2025, the launch was delayed in June 2026 for further polishing, and the game was released on 29 October 2026 (see the [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4115450/Phantom-Blade-Zero/)).
