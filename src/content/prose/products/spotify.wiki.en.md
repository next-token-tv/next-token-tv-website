## What Spotify is

Spotify is an audio streaming subscription service for music, podcasts, and audiobooks, launched in 2008 and founded by Daniel Ek (see the [Spotify Newsroom](https://newsroom.spotify.com/company-info/)). By its own newsroom's description, Spotify is the world's most popular audio streaming subscription service, present in 184 markets, with a catalog of more than 100 million tracks, 7 million podcast titles, and 700,000 audiobooks; user and catalog figures change over time, and official disclosures govern current numbers.

## Usage and plans

Spotify offers a free, ad-supported tier alongside paid Premium subscriptions. Per the [US Premium page](https://www.spotify.com/us/premium/), Premium comes in Individual (1 account), Duo (2 accounts), Family (up to 6 accounts), and Student (with verification) plans, plus a separate Audiobooks Access plan; prices vary by country and are governed by the [official site](https://www.spotify.com/). Listening options include mobile apps, desktop applications, and the web player in a browser.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Spotify?

An audio streaming subscription service for music, podcasts, and audiobooks, launched in 2008 and, by its official description, available in 184 markets. See the [Spotify Newsroom](https://newsroom.spotify.com/company-info/).

### Does Spotify have a free version?

Yes. The free tier is ad-supported and can be used instead of, or alongside, a paid Premium subscription — see the [US Premium page](https://www.spotify.com/us/premium/).

### Which Spotify Premium plans are there?

Per the US page: Individual, Duo, Family (up to 6 accounts), and Student, plus a separate Audiobooks Access plan. Plan availability and prices vary by country — see the [official site](https://www.spotify.com/).

### Can I listen to Spotify in a browser?

Yes. The official web player runs in the browser, alongside mobile and desktop apps — see [spotify.com](https://www.spotify.com/).
