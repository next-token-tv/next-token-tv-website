## Grand Theft Auto V

Grand Theft Auto V (GTA V or GTA 5) is an open-world action-adventure game developed by Rockstar North and published by Rockstar Games, first released on September 17, 2013, for PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_V)). The story is set in the fictional state of San Andreas, based on Southern California, with the Los Angeles-inspired city of Los Santos as the main stage. Official information is on the [Rockstar page](https://www.rockstargames.com/gta-v).

## Release timeline by platform

- September 17, 2013: PlayStation 3, Xbox 360
- November 18, 2014: PlayStation 4, Xbox One
- April 14, 2015: Windows (PC)
- March 15, 2022: PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S ("Expanded & Enhanced" edition)

## Gameplay and online mode

The game follows three protagonists — Michael De Santa, Franklin Clinton and Trevor Philips. Players roam a large open world, switch between the three characters and advance a heist-centered story; the online mode, Grand Theft Auto Online, supports multiplayer co-op and competition and has received free content updates since launch.

## Sales and place in the series

Per English Wikipedia, the game earned one billion US dollars within three days of launch, then the fastest-selling entertainment product ever; it has since shipped more than 200 million copies, making it the second best-selling video game of all time. Its successor, [Grand Theft Auto VI](/en/wiki/products/grand-theft-auto-vi), has an officially announced release date of November 19, 2026.

## Frequently asked questions

### What year did GTA 5 come out?

It first released on September 17, 2013, for PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360; it later arrived on PS4 and Xbox One in 2014, PC in 2015, and PS5 and Xbox Series X|S in 2022 (per English Wikipedia).

### Where is GTA 5 set?

In the fictional state of San Andreas, based on Southern California; the main city, Los Santos, is modeled on Los Angeles, with rural Blaine County nearby.

### How are GTA 5 and GTA 6 related?

GTA 6 (Grand Theft Auto VI) is the next mainline entry in the series, with an officially announced release date of November 19, 2026; GTA V is its 2013 predecessor. They are different games; see the [GTA VI entry](/en/wiki/products/grand-theft-auto-vi).

### How many copies has GTA 5 sold?

Per English Wikipedia, it has shipped more than 200 million copies, the second best-selling video game of all time; its online mode, GTA Online, kept receiving updates for years after launch.
