## What Bug Say is

Bug Say (叭哥说) is an AI voice agent from [NetEase Youdao](/en/wiki/brands/netease-youdao). The official page positions it in one line: "AI语音Agent，比打字快5倍" (an AI voice agent, five times faster than typing). It is a voice-centered intelligent tool under the Youdao brand, separate from the company’s dictionary and translation products.

## Usage and boundaries

The official site's own description calls it "a desktop AI voice-input tool" and lists speech-to-text, AI-powered polishing, automatic filler-word removal, voice translation, and cross-app input as its capabilities; the page lists no pricing. The product entry point is [bug.youdao.com](https://bug.youdao.com/); specifics belong to the official page and in-product documentation.

Where third parties place it also describes its niche: the Weekly #003 discussion puts it alongside Typeless and the voice input of WeChat’s keyboard — products that share the scenario of speaking instead of typing and letting AI clean up the text.

## Discussion in the show

In Weekly #003’s chapter “语音输入产品与实时反馈” (voice-input products and real-time feedback), [Yang Pan places Bug Say in the same category as Typeless and Tencent’s Chatterfly in the Chinese transcript](/weekly/003/transcript#quote-5c87d0e8760938933ef2), then [describes his own experience: he installed it and uninstalled it, reasoning that it is not much simpler than Typeless, not as good as WeChat’s voice input, and that transcription is not shown in real time — after two or three minutes of speaking he saw nothing until the result appeared all at once](/weekly/003/transcript#quote-f627091715d2a8075cfa). This is one participant’s experience and choice, not a verdict on the product overall. The [episode 003 chapter](/weekly/003/transcript#chapter-17) has the full context; an English transcript is not available.

### Are “叭哥” and “叭哥说” the same product?

Yes. The official domain is bug.youdao.com, and 叭哥 (Bug Ge) is the common short name for the same product.

### Is Bug Say the same kind of product as Typeless or WeChat input?

They are often compared but differ in form: WeChat input is a [keyboard app](/en/wiki/products/wechat-input-method), Typeless is a [voice-input tool](/en/wiki/products/typeless), and Bug Say’s official positioning is an AI voice agent. In Weekly #003, Yang Pan compared them from the angle of keeping one keyboard and one Typeless-like tool.
