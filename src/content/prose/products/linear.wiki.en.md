## What Linear is

Linear is a work management app for product and engineering teams, positioned by its official site as "The system for product development." It covers issue tracking (Issues), Projects, Cycles, Initiatives, and Documents across the product development process, and is described as purpose-built for an AI era where humans and agents share workflows. The official site is [linear.app](https://linear.app/), and product updates are published on the [Changelog](https://linear.app/changelog).

A note for searchers: linear as an English adjective and mathematical concepts such as linear algebra have nothing to do with this product; searches for the app usually need a qualifier like "app," "project management," or the domain.

## Usage and boundaries

- Core workflow: track work as Issues, plan iterations in Cycles, and organize cross-team goals with Projects and Initiatives. The official site also describes Intake — automatically turning conversations and customer feedback into issues that are routed, labeled, and prioritized.
- AI and agents: Linear Agent can be assigned issues and produce changes such as draft PRs; the official pricing page lists Triage Intelligence, Linear Asks, Code Insights, and MCP access. Loops is the officially described automation loop: triggered by workspace events, able to edit documents and post Slack updates, with each run being a resumable agent conversation.
- Integrations: the official site lists Slack, GitHub, Zendesk, and Intercom, plus an API, webhooks, and MCP support.
- Pricing: a free plan and per-seat paid tiers exist; details are in the FAQ below and on the [official pricing page](https://linear.app/pricing).

Boundaries: Linear targets product development collaboration, not general project management or help desks. Features and tier placement change, so the official pages are authoritative.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Linear?

Linear is a work management app for product and engineering teams to track issues, plan cycles and projects, and coordinate product development, officially positioned as "the system for product development." See the [official site](https://linear.app/).

### How much does Linear cost, and is there a free plan?

Yes. As of September 2026, the official pricing page lists Free (unlimited members, 2 teams, 250 issues), Basic ($10 per user per month, billed yearly), Business ($16 per user per month, including Loops and Triage Intelligence), and Enterprise (custom, annual billing only). Current tiers and prices are on the [official pricing page](https://linear.app/pricing).

### How does Linear compare to Jira?

Both are work management tools for engineering teams. Linear's official positioning emphasizes speed, reduced noise, and AI workflows shared by humans and agents; Jira's positioning and ecosystem are described in its own official material. The right choice depends on team size and process preferences.

### What AI features does Linear have?

Per official material: Linear Agent can take on issues and produce changes such as draft PRs; Loops are triggered by workspace events and can edit documents and post Slack updates; the pricing page also lists Triage Intelligence, Linear Asks, and MCP access. Which tier includes which feature changes over time — see the [official pricing page](https://linear.app/pricing) and the [Changelog](https://linear.app/changelog).

### Which integrations does Linear support?

The official site lists Slack, GitHub, Zendesk, and Intercom, plus an API, webhooks, and MCP support for turning external conversations and events into issues or driving agents. See the [official site](https://linear.app/).
