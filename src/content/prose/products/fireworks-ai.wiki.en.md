## Fireworks AI

Fireworks AI is an AI infrastructure platform for inference, training, and deployment of open models; its site describes the team as “From the Creators of PyTorch.” Developers can call hosted open models through the API or deploy their own fine-tuned models. The list of hosted models changes over time and should be checked on the [official site](https://fireworks.ai/).

## Service tiers and boundaries

The official page describes three ways to serve models: Serverless (shared hosting billed per token), On-Demand (dedicated deployments), and Reserved (guaranteed capacity); the APIs are compatible with OpenAI’s and Anthropic’s interfaces. The platform also offers training and fine-tuning capabilities (including reinforcement-learning training flows) and Fireworks Nexus, a product for routing across models. Capabilities and rates belong on the [official pricing page](https://fireworks.ai/pricing) and [documentation](https://docs.fireworks.ai/). Whether a hosted inference service fits a workload depends on model support, latency, data compliance, and cost, which each team should evaluate.

## Discussion in the show

In Weekly #004’s chapter “Harvey: Open-Source Models and Product Moats,” [Yang Pan notes that many Silicon Valley companies are moving to their own models, “including Fireworks pushing these efforts,” in the Chinese transcript](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-be20aa68704a5ecebd0b). His argument: swapping frontier models for open ones to cut costs suits top companies like Harvey that have already validated their business loop and market position, not every startup; Guizang adds that closed verticals such as law carry stronger barriers. This is participant judgment about industry practice, not an assessment of Fireworks’ products.

### How is Fireworks AI priced?

Official options include per-token Serverless, dedicated On-Demand deployments, and Reserved capacity; current rates belong on the [official pricing page](https://fireworks.ai/pricing).

### Which APIs is Fireworks AI compatible with?

The official page states that its Serverless APIs are compatible with OpenAI and Anthropic interfaces; verify against the [official documentation](https://docs.fireworks.ai/) before integrating.
