## What Claude Cowork is

Claude Cowork is Anthropic's agent product for office and file-based work, part of the Claude product family. The official description of how it works: you give a goal, and Claude works across folders and tools you choose to complete a multi-step task and deliver work for your review. It ships as desktop apps for macOS, Windows, ChromeOS and Linux, plus web and mobile entries in beta; the official page notes it works with a paid plan (see the [Claude Cowork product page](https://claude.com/product/cowork)).

On September 16, 2026, Anthropic announced that Claude Cowork and chat are merging into one Claude, rolling out to Pro and Max plans first, with Team and Free plans to follow and Enterprise customers receiving at least 30 days' notice (see the [official blog post](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude/)). Product entries and availability are changing with this merger; the official pages carry the current state.

## Uses and boundaries

Typical Cowork tasks on the official page are multi-step knowledge work — organizing folders, reviewing in bulk, scheduled reports, and building spreadsheets: you say what you need rather than how, Claude opens the files and tools required, shows each step, and can run tasks on a schedule or several chunks in parallel, continuing in the cloud after you close your laptop. It includes a built-in browser separate from your own for opening sites, reading pages and filling forms; the page marks this as available in the Cowork desktop app on Pro, Max, and Team plans.

Permissions are the core boundary: Claude can only reach the folders and tools you choose, deleting anything needs your approval, and significant actions ask first by default (see the official [Use Cowork safely](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13364135-use-cowork-safely) article). The official FAQ adds that computer use — direct screen interaction — is in research preview for both Cowork and [Claude Code](/en/wiki/products/claude-code), and that agentic tasks consume your usage limits faster than chat.

## How it relates to Claude and Claude Code

[Claude](/en/wiki/products/claude) is Anthropic's general model and assistant family. The official FAQ positions [Claude Code](/en/wiki/products/claude-code) for software engineering — writing, debugging and shipping code — while Cowork is built for non-coding knowledge work such as research, analysis and document creation; both use the same agentic approach. The difference from regular chat is access: chat cannot touch your files directly, while Cowork reads, edits and creates files in folders you specify, so it completes tasks rather than describing them. After the September 2026 merger announcement these capabilities are being folded into a single conversation entry; the current interface follows the official pages.

## FAQ

### What is Claude Cowork?

An Anthropic agent product for office and file-based work: you set a goal, and Claude completes a multi-step task in the folders and tools you chose and hands back work for review. See the [official product page](https://claude.com/product/cowork).

### What is the difference between Claude Cowork and Claude Code?

Per the official FAQ: Claude Code is built for software engineering — writing, debugging, and shipping code — while Cowork is built for non-coding knowledge work such as research, analysis, and document creation. Both use the same agentic approach. See the [product page FAQ](https://claude.com/product/cowork).

### How do I use Claude Cowork? Do I need the desktop app?

Not necessarily. The official FAQ states that Cowork also runs on web and mobile in beta; the desktop app adds what those cannot reach — the folders and applications on your own computer. If your work lives in local files, start from the [desktop app](https://claude.com/product/cowork) (macOS, Windows, ChromeOS, Linux). Getting-started steps are in the official [Getting started with Cowork](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/13345190-getting-started-with-cowork) article.

### Does Claude Cowork cost extra?

The official page notes Cowork works with a paid plan; the official FAQ and pricing page list it as included in Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise plans and caution that agentic tasks consume limits faster than Chat. As of September 2026, rates and limits are on the [official pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing).

### What is the Cowork-and-chat merger about?

On September 16, 2026, Anthropic announced in an [official blog post](https://claude.com/blog/cowork-is-now-claude/) that Cowork and chat are merging into one Claude, rolling out to Pro and Max plans over the coming weeks; the announcement says existing chats, projects, artifacts, connectors, and skills are preserved. The current entry points and feature scope follow the official pages.
