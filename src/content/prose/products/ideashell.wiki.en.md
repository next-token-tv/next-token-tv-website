## What IdeaShell is

IdeaShell (Chinese name 闪念贝壳) is an AI note-taking app developed by RoundRedDot Tech, a company based in Guangzhou, China. The official positioning is "your AI thinking companion", with the tagline "remember for you, act for you". The product captures ideas as voice, text, and to-dos, syncs notes across devices, and — according to the official site — links related notes automatically.

## Uses and boundaries

The official site describes three core uses:

- Capture and organize: record ideas quickly as voice, text, or to-dos, with automatic cross-device sync and linking.
- AI collaborative thinking: discuss plans, deepen ideas, or analyze questions by combining past notes with public research.
- AI action items: turn notes into to-dos, web pages, slides, and research reports, with scheduled tasks and proactive follow-ups on commitments.

The site also advertises personalized memory (remembering meetings, contacts, recordings, and stray thoughts) with app integrations and custom MCP connections, and claims more than one million users — an official claim with no independent corroboration found. The entry points listed on the official page are iPhone, Android, Apple Watch, and OPPO Watch apps plus Mac and Windows desktop apps; the page shows no pricing information.
