## Nintendo Switch

Nintendo Switch is Nintendo’s home video game console, released worldwide on March 3, 2017. Its defining “hybrid” design lets it plug into a TV or travel as a handheld, with detachable Joy-Con controllers — a home console you can take with you. The family later grew with the handheld-only Nintendo Switch Lite (released September 20, 2019) and the OLED-model with a larger OLED screen (released October 8, 2021).

## Switch 2

Nintendo released its successor, the Nintendo Switch 2, on June 5, 2025, keeping the hybrid form factor and supporting compatibility with original Switch games; official pages cover transferring data from the original model and game compatibility. Current lineup and edition information belongs on the [official Switch family page](https://www.nintendo.com/us/gaming-systems/switch/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Nintendo Switch a handheld or a home console?

Both: it supports handheld, tabletop, and docked TV play modes.

### When did the Switch 2 come out?

June 5, 2025, worldwide; it supports original Switch games, and Nintendo provides data-transfer and compatibility guides.

### Which models make up the Switch family?

The original Switch, the handheld-only Switch Lite, the OLED model, and the Switch 2 released in 2025; current models belong on the [official page](https://www.nintendo.com/us/gaming-systems/switch/).

### Can Switch games run on Switch 2?

Nintendo offers a game-compatibility page and data-transfer guides; compatibility for a specific game should be checked against the [official listing](https://www.nintendo.com/us/gaming-systems/switch/).
