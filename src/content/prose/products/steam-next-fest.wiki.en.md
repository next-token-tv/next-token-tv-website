## Definition

Steam Next Fest is a limited-time event run by Steam itself. Valve’s Steamworks documentation defines it as "a weeklong event focused on promoting upcoming games with playable demos" — one week dedicated to demos of unreleased games, held three times a year in February, June, and October. See the [official documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest).

## How it works

Per the official documentation, participating games must meet a set of conditions: they have not previously joined a Next Fest (each title may participate only once), the associated Steamworks account is in good standing, the store page is public, a publicly playable demo is live when the event begins, and the game will not be released before the edition concludes; prologues or short-form versions of already released games do not qualify. Placement is completely randomized during the first days, with an optional livestream section, a Press Preview for selected outlets, and a post-event wrap-up page featuring the most-played demos; demos may stay up after the event ends.

## Usage

For players, it is a chance to try a concentrated batch of upcoming games and decide what to wishlist; for developers, Valve frames the event’s purpose as exposing future releases to potential fans and gathering real-time feedback.

## Related and confusable terms

Next Fest is an event, whereas [Steam](/en/wiki/products/steam) is the platform that carries game distribution, the store, and community features — the two are catalogued separately. A festival demo is also a different mechanism from Early Access: the documentation notes that unreleased Early Access games may participate, but the game must remain unlaunched through the event.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often is Steam Next Fest, and when?

Three times a year, in February, June, and October; the dates of each edition are published in the official documentation at the [Steamworks Next Fest page](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest).

### Which games can join Steam Next Fest?

The official conditions include: the title has never joined a Next Fest (one participation per game), the Steamworks account is in good standing, the store page is public, a publicly playable demo is available when the event begins, the game will not launch before the edition ends, and it is not a prologue or short-form version of an already released game. See the [official documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/upcoming_events/nextfest).

### Can you still play the demos after the festival?

Yes. The official documentation states that participating demos can stay up on Steam after the event concludes.
