## What File Zero is

File Zero (零号档案) is an AI-produced science-fiction mystery series about anomalous entities and a secret containment organization: the story follows the recording, containment, and investigation of anomalous entities, a premise close to the SCP-Foundation-style fiction circulating online — in episode 008, Guizang described it as "something like the SCP Foundation." The series is produced by its creator with AIGC tools and published on platforms including Zcool and Douyin.

## The work and where it is published

The Zcool creator Baidaizi (白袋子) publishes the series under the title "【AIGC】《零号档案》File Zero", categorized as AI creation work; series videos are also published on Douyin. Episode lists and update channels follow the creator's own publishing pages.

## Discussed on Next Token

Episode 008 (recorded November 1, 2026), in the chapter on [AI short dramas as a creative opportunity for individuals and small teams](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-04), discusses why breakout AI dramas tend to come from individuals or small teams rather than companies. Guizang cites File Zero as an example: [the very popular series is SCP-Foundation-like in theme and, as he recalls, made by one person](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-7bf054ec3b8d5ce64a71), alongside works such as Gui Xu (《归墟》). The "very popular" and "made by one person" statements are the participants' impressions at the time, rather than confirmed information about the production team.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is File Zero?

It is an AI-produced science-fiction mystery series about anomalous entities and a secret containment organization, with a premise close to SCP-Foundation-style fiction. See the [Zcool work page](https://www.zcool.com.cn/u/ZNzYxODUwNDA%3D).

### Who made File Zero?

The Zcool creator Baidaizi publishes the File Zero series on their profile; in episode 008, Guizang recalled it being made by one person. Complete information about the production team follows the creator's official releases.

### Where can I watch File Zero?

Series videos are published on Douyin (see the [Douyin video link](https://www.douyin.com/video/7660043887762853158)), and production works are published on [Zcool](https://www.zcool.com.cn/u/ZNzYxODUwNDA%3D). Availability on each platform follows the creator's publishing pages.

### What is the relationship between File Zero and the SCP Foundation?

It borrows the SCP-Foundation-style premise of anomalous entities plus a secret containment organization, but the story and visuals are original AIGC productions, not an official work of the SCP Foundation community. The comparison in episode 008 comes from Guizang's description.
