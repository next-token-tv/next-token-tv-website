## Definition

The SCP Foundation is a collaborative writing project built around a fictional secret organization of the same name, hosted primarily on the SCP Wiki. Works take the form of mock scientific reports: each document describes a numbered anomaly (such as SCP-173) with its object class, containment procedures and description, portraying a foundation that captures, contains and studies paranormal phenomena while hiding them from the public. The project began in June 2007 with the SCP-173 post on 4chan's paranormal board (/x/) and moved to Wikidot in July 2008. Content is licensed under CC BY-SA, the community filters entries by voting, and there is no single central canon.

## Related and confusable terms

"SCP Foundation" refers both to the fictional organization inside the fiction and, in casual usage, to the SCP Wiki community and its setting as a whole. The setting has spun off games (such as SCP – Containment Breach) and film adaptation projects. This entry covers the collaborative writing website itself.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the SCP Foundation?

A collaborative fiction project and shared setting: community members write horror, science-fiction and urban-fantasy stories in the format of containment files for anomalous objects, hosted on the SCP Wiki.

### How did the SCP Foundation start?

In June 2007 a user posted the first file, SCP-173, on 4chan's /x/ board, and others imitated the format; the project moved to Wikidot in July 2008.

### Can SCP content be reused or commercialized?

SCP Wiki content is licensed under CC BY-SA, so derivative works must follow the same license's attribution and share-alike terms.
