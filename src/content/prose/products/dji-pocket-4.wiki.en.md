## What the Osmo Pocket 4 is

The Osmo Pocket 4 (often shortened to Pocket 4) is a handheld gimbal camera in the Pocket series from [DJI](/en/wiki/brands/dji), officially positioned as a one-inch pocket gimbal camera. Its [official product page](https://www.dji.com/osmo-pocket-4) carries the tagline “一寸万象，光影随行” (“an inch of sensor, light travels with you”). It targets vloggers and content creators, combining a camera with a three-axis mechanical gimbal in a palm-sized body; product information and support live on the official product page and the [DJI product support page](https://www.dji.com/support/product/osmo-pocket-4).

## Capabilities and boundaries

Capabilities listed on the official page include: a 1-inch CMOS sensor with an f/2.0 lens and 4K/240fps recording; 14 stops of dynamic range and 10-bit D-Log color; 2x lossless zoom; three-axis mechanical stabilization with a 2-inch rotating touchscreen at 1,000 nits, where rotating the screen starts recording; ActiveTrack 7.0 subject tracking, gesture control, and focus modes such as locking onto a registered subject; 107 GB of built-in storage with microSD expansion; and four-channel OsmoAudio microphone input. The companion mobile app is DJI Mimo. Specifications follow the official product page; announcement dates, available colors, and bundle combinations are not fixed on the page — check the [official store](https://store.dji.com/) before buying.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Osmo Pocket 4?

A handheld gimbal camera in DJI’s Pocket series, built around a one-inch sensor and three-axis mechanical stabilization in a pocketable body, aimed at vlogging and mobile creation. The official entry point is [dji.com/osmo-pocket-4](https://www.dji.com/osmo-pocket-4).

### What are the Osmo Pocket 4’s main specifications?

The official page lists a 1-inch CMOS sensor, f/2.0 lens, 4K/240fps, 14 stops of dynamic range, 10-bit D-Log, 2x lossless zoom, a 2-inch 1,000-nit rotating touchscreen, ActiveTrack 7.0 tracking, 107 GB of built-in storage, and more; full specifications are on the [official product page](https://www.dji.com/osmo-pocket-4).

### Which app does the Osmo Pocket 4 use?

The companion mobile app is DJI Mimo, used for transferring footage, editing, and device management; firmware and documentation come from the [DJI product support page](https://www.dji.com/support/product/osmo-pocket-4).

### Where can I buy the Osmo Pocket 4, and how much does it cost?

Official channels are the [DJI store](https://store.dji.com/) and authorized retailers; prices and bundles vary by region and promotion, so rely on the store’s live pages.
