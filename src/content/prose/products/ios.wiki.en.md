## What iOS is

iOS is the mobile operating system [Apple](/en/wiki/brands/apple) develops for the [iPhone](/en/wiki/products/iphone). It shipped with the first iPhone in 2007 under the name iPhone OS, and Apple rebranded it as iOS in June 2010. Every iPhone ships with iOS preinstalled, and system updates are pushed free of charge through the Settings app. The current version name, feature list, and supported devices are on [Apple's official iOS page](https://www.apple.com/os/ios/).

iOS sits alongside macOS, iPadOS, watchOS, and visionOS in Apple's operating-system family, with iCloud and Handoff tying the platforms together. This article covers the operating system only; the hardware lineup is described in the [iPhone](/en/wiki/products/iphone) article and the assistant in the [Siri](/en/wiki/products/siri) article.

## iOS 27 and core capabilities

On September 14, 2026, Apple released iOS 27 and the rest of the year's [software updates](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/major-updates-for-apples-software-platforms-are-now-available/) as free upgrades. According to official material, the headline items are: the [Siri AI](/en/wiki/products/siri) beta built on Apple Intelligence (English at launch); a dedicated Siri app whose conversation history syncs through iCloud; Write with Siri available wherever you type; photo-editing tools such as Spatial Reframing, Extend, and an improved Clean Up; and automatic Safari tab organization.

Boundaries to know: Apple Intelligence features require newer hardware (officially iPhone 15 Pro or later); server-dependent features carry daily usage limits, with expanded access planned for a fee; and Siri AI is not available initially in the EU or in mainland China. Day-to-day friction such as third-party app permissions or input-method switching depends on the specific app and system version rather than being a uniform OS limitation. For current device support and per-feature availability, check [Apple's official iOS page](https://www.apple.com/os/ios/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which devices does iOS 27 support?

Per the compatibility notes on [Apple's official iOS page](https://www.apple.com/os/ios/), iOS 27 runs from iPhone 11 and the second-generation iPhone SE up through the iPhone 17 series, iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro, and iPhone Duo; Apple Intelligence features require iPhone 15 Pro or later. Check “Settings > General > Software Update” on the device.

### How do I update iOS, and does it cost anything?

System updates are free. On an iPhone, open “Settings > General > Software Update” to check and install. Major versions typically ship alongside the autumn hardware announcement; iOS 27 arrived on September 14, 2026, per the [official press release](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/major-updates-for-apples-software-platforms-are-now-available/).

### Does Siri AI on iOS 27 support Chinese?

Language and region availability is defined by Apple's announcements: Siri AI shipped as a beta with iOS 27 in English, with French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish following in October 2026, and no initial availability in the EU or mainland China, per the [official press release](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/). Check [Apple's Siri page](https://www.apple.com/siri/) for the current rollout.
