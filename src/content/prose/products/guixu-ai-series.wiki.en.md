## Definition

Guixu (归墟) is an AI-produced apocalyptic series by Xuanjiu about survivors facing a transformed world, released on [Douyin](/en/wiki/products/douyin) (see the [Douyin Jingxuan title page](https://jingxuan.douyin.com/m/video/7639009224806157611)).

## Discussed on Next Token

In episode 008, in the chapter "AI 短剧：个人与小团队的创作机会" ([AI short dramas: a creation opportunity for individuals and small teams](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-04); Chinese transcript), Guizang explained why hit AI dramas tend to come from individuals or small teams and cited Guixu as an example: ["《归墟》其实是一个人"](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-7bf054ec3b8d5ce64a71) (Guixu is actually made by one person), alongside 《持械入宋》 and 《零号档案》, to show that solo creators can break out on organic traffic with high-quality work. The remark reflects the participant's observations on the AI-drama scene.
