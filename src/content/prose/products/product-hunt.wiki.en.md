## What Product Hunt is

Product Hunt is a community platform for launching, discovering and discussing new products, where makers submit their products and early adopters vote and comment. Ryan Hoover founded it in November 2013, starting as an email list, and it has been owned by AngelList since November 2016.

## Uses and boundaries

A submission needs only a product title, URL and tagline; voting decides where a product lands on the daily list, and the products with the most votes rise to the top of the day. Beyond tech products, the site covers games, books and podcasts, and sends a daily email digest of the previous day's top products.

As a boundary, ranking is decided by the voting mechanics and launch-day community dynamics; a "number one of the day" is not a verdict on product quality. For discoverers it is one lead source among others, and actual fit still needs hands-on verification.

## FAQ

### What is Product Hunt?

A community for launching and discovering new products: makers submit, members upvote and comment, and daily lists span apps, hardware, games, books and podcasts. See the [official website](https://www.producthunt.com/).

### How do I discover new products on Product Hunt?

Browse the daily list on the site or subscribe to the email digest of the previous day's top products; lists are ranked by votes and browsable by category.

### How do I launch a product on Product Hunt?

Submit a product title, URL and tagline on the site to join a daily list; launch-day preparation and community replies are the common practice, and the exact rules are in the official launch guides.

### Who owns Product Hunt?

Ryan Hoover founded it in November 2013, and AngelList acquired it in 2016; current operations follow announcements on the official site.
