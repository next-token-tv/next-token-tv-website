## What Apple Car is

Apple Car refers to the widely reported vehicle-development project at [Apple](/en/wiki/brands/apple), known in reports by the internal codename "Project Titan": according to years of reporting, the project began around 2014 with the goal of building an electric, self-driving car. Apple never formally confirmed or publicly discussed the project, and no Apple-branded car was ever released. According to a Reuters report on February 27, 2024, Apple canceled the project.

## Status and reported history

- All information about the project comes from media reporting and sourced accounts; Apple never confirmed its existence, and reported goals, timelines and team sizes shifted repeatedly over the years.
- In February 2024, Reuters and other outlets reported that the project was canceled, with some resources shifting to generative AI work.
- There is therefore no official entry point for buying or using an Apple Car; Apple's current car-related product is CarPlay, see the [official CarPlay page](https://www.apple.com/ios/carplay/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Was the Apple Car canceled?

According to Reuters on February 27, 2024, Apple canceled its car project after roughly a decade of development. Apple has issued no official statement.

### Why was the Apple Car canceled?

Reporting says Apple shifted the project's resources toward generative AI work; Apple has given no official reason, so details rest on media accounts.

### What is Project Titan?

The internal codename for Apple's car-development project used in media reports — the project commonly known as Apple Car. The codename likewise comes from reporting, not from any Apple confirmation.

### Does Apple have any car-related products now?

Apple sells no car; its current car-related product is CarPlay, the feature that projects iPhone maps, audio and messaging onto a car's screen. See the [official CarPlay page](https://www.apple.com/ios/carplay/).
