## What Steam is

Steam is a PC gaming platform operated by Valve Corporation, described by the company as "the ultimate destination for playing, discussing, and creating games". The platform combines a store (the official page counts tens of thousands of titles from AAA to indie, with sales and automatic updates), player communities (forums, groups, and in-game chat), Steam Workshop for player-created mods and content, and Steamworks, the developer-side publishing toolkit. Valve also makes hardware for the ecosystem, including the Steam Deck handheld and the Valve Index headset. The Steam client runs on Windows, macOS, Linux, and Chromebook, with iOS and Android mobile apps alongside.

## Usage and boundaries

For players, Steam is the main entry point for buying, downloading, and managing PC games: the client installs for free, purchases are tied to the account, and the platform supplies achievements, Workshop integration, and cloud saves. For developers, publishing goes through Steamworks: register a developer account, pay the publishing-related fee, and ship the game — the exact process, revenue share, and review requirements are documented at the [Steamworks documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/home) (parts require a developer login).

Unlike consoles such as the [Nintendo Switch](/en/wiki/products/nintendo-switch) or [PlayStation 5](/en/wiki/products/playstation-5), Steam is not tied to specific hardware — a PC game runs on computers from any manufacturer, and Valve's own Steam Deck is simply one portable way to run the Steam ecosystem. Searchers should also watch for ambiguity: steam literally means vapor, and results about steampunk or unrelated services should be filtered out.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Steam?

A PC gaming platform run by Valve: a store, download management, player communities, the Workshop, and the Steamworks publishing toolkit, with clients for Windows, macOS, Linux, and Chromebook.

### Where is the Steam website, and how do I download it?

The store and client download live at [store.steampowered.com](https://store.steampowered.com/), with the platform overview on the [official About page](https://store.steampowered.com/about/). The client installs for free; games are purchased separately.

### What cut does Steam take from game sales?

Steam charges a revenue share on store sales, tied to sales tiers and publishing terms; the public explanation is in the [Steamworks documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/home), with some details behind a developer login.

### What is the Steam Deck?

A handheld from Valve that runs the Steam ecosystem, described on the [official Steam Deck page](https://store.steampowered.com/steamdeck); models and configurations update over time, so the official page is the reference.
