## Definition

The Pokémon games are a series centered on collecting, training and battling Pokémon creatures, spanning many standalone titles. The core series is developed by Game Freak and published by Nintendo and The Pokémon Company; official listings are on the [Nintendo Store Pokémon page](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/games/pokemon-games/).

## Series and releases

The series began with Pocket Monsters Red and Green, released in 1996 in Japan for the Game Boy. The core series consists of role-playing games released in generations, each introducing a new region, new Pokémon and versions; the most recent core title, Pokémon Legends: Z-A, was released on October 16, 2025. Beyond the core series, other companies have developed spin-offs across genres such as action and puzzle games, including Pokémon GO.

## Usage

A typical core-series game has the player travel through a region, catch wild Pokémon, train them for battle and trade with others. Platforms and release arrangements differ per title; purchase entry points are on the [Nintendo Store](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/games/pokemon-games/).

## Related and confusable terms

This entry covers the game series, not the whole Pokémon franchise (animation, trading card game and more). [PokéBonk](/en/wiki/products/pokebonk) is an unofficial fan game that uses Pokémon characters and is catalogued separately from official titles; licensing questions are settled by The Pokémon Company's announcements.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does this entry describe one game?

It covers the game series. Individual titles have their own platforms and mechanics; PokéBonk is listed separately as an unofficial fan game.

### In what order should I play Pokémon games?

The core series is released in generations, and each generation is a largely standalone story, so you can start with any of them. Official listings cover the titles and their platforms.

### Can I play Pokémon games on mobile?

Core-series titles are released on Nintendo platforms; on mobile, official spin-offs such as Pokémon GO are available. Pokémon-themed browser games like PokéBonk are unofficial fan works.
