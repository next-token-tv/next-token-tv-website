## What Liblib is

Liblib — also known as LiblibAI or 哩布哩布AI — is a platform for AI image creation, model sharing, and creator communities. Its official site is [liblib.art](https://www.liblib.art/). The site's own page description sums it up as an "AI painting original model sharing community" (AI绘画原创模型分享社区), listing online AI drawing tools (webUI and comfyUI), original-model downloads, and online model training; the page also states "100,000+ models available for free download." It was the first product of Evoken (演语科技), the company founded by [Chen Mian](/en/wiki/people/chen-mian).

According to a June 2026 LatePost funding report (republished by NetEase), Liblib had accumulated more than 30 million users and over 500,000 original models. Those figures come from that report.

## Model sharing and community

Liblib's community element is model sharing: creators publish original models for others to use, with online drawing tools and a model-training entry alongside. In the LateTalk episode 175 interview (July 2026, republished by Chwang), Chen Mian says the company "decided to focus on design, not anime-style art, from day one," describing Liblib as a model-asset sharing community for designers. Current features and rules are on the [official site](https://www.liblib.art/), which is primarily in Chinese.

## How it differs from Lovart and LibTV

Liblib, [Lovart](/en/wiki/products/lovart), and [LibTV](/en/wiki/products/libtv) all belong to Evoken, but their shapes differ: Liblib (liblib.art) is an image-creation and model-sharing community, Lovart is an AI design agent, and LibTV (liblib.tv) is an AI video creation tool. The similar domains — liblib.art versus liblib.tv — are easy to mix up in search.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Liblib?

Liblib (哩布哩布AI) is an AI image-creation and model-sharing community offering online drawing tools, original-model sharing, and model training. See the [official site](https://www.liblib.art/).

### Where is Liblib's official website?

The official site is [liblib.art](https://www.liblib.art/). Note that the video tool LibTV lives at a different domain, liblib.tv.

### Which company makes Liblib?

Evoken (演语科技), founded by Chen Mian; the same company also makes Lovart and LibTV.

The site's own description lists free model downloads and free online drawing tools; current features and usage limits follow the [official site](https://www.liblib.art/).

The June 2026 LatePost funding report credits it with more than 30 million cumulative users and over 500,000 original models; current figures follow official statements.
