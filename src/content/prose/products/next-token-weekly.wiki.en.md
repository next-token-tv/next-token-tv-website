## What Next Token Weekly is

Next Token Weekly is the flagship weekly show of the video-podcast brand [Next Token｜词元之外](/en/wiki/brands/next-token). Four co-hosts — Pan YANG, Guizang, Orange, and Xiangyang Qiaomu — sit down each week for a roundtable review of the most consequential AI stories. The show publishes both a video and an audio edition, with its show page at [nexttoken.tv/weekly](https://nexttoken.tv/weekly).

The intro of episode #001 introduces the show this way: "the flagship show Next Token Weekly, where Pan YANG, Guizang, Orange, and Xiangyang Qiaomu hold a weekly roundtable, sharing observations, practice, and differing judgments" ([show intro in the Chinese transcript](/weekly/001/transcript#chapter-01)). At the opening, duty host Pan YANG describes the original idea: "at a fixed time every week, review the events and news of the past week across the AI world, and offer our own reading of them" ([the line in the Chinese transcript](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-bdac18a6b0ac65135c7c)).

## Format

Episodes are not headline read-throughs. The discussion is organized around the week's events — models and agents, AI hardware, business and organizations, creative tools — and the co-hosts' disagreements stay in the final cut. There is no permanent host; a rotating duty host threads each episode, and Pan YANG held that role for episode #001 ([opening line in the Chinese transcript](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-69834eeaff7a45b0175d)).

Published and announced episodes so far:

- [Episode #001](https://nexttoken.tv/weekly/001), "The LLM 'Kill Line' Wars: Tokens Are the New Money" — unpacks 24 AI stories from August 27 to September 3, 2026, covering models and agents, AI hardware, and business and organizations.
- [Episode #002](https://nexttoken.tv/weekly/002), "iPhone Duo launches. Does Astra using a computer count as AGI?" — recorded at AGI Bar in Shanghai, discussing new hardware, model capabilities, and creative tools.
- Episode #003 is officially announced for an online recording on September 19, 2026; timing and topics are updated on the [show page](https://nexttoken.tv/weekly).

Each episode ships with a full Chinese transcript organized by chapters — see [the episode #001 transcript](/weekly/001/transcript) and [the episode #002 transcript](/weekly/002/transcript) (both in Chinese).

## Where to listen and watch

The audio edition is published on Xiaoyuzhou, Apple Podcasts, and Spotify; the video edition on Bilibili and YouTube. Per-episode links are collected on the [show page](https://nexttoken.tv/weekly) and on each episode page, which also carry show notes and transcripts.
