## What Xiaowei is

Xiaowei is a native AI assistant built by the WeChat team, provided by Tencent as a feature inside the WeChat app. In June 2026, the WeChat team confirmed its existence to media and described its positioning: “Xiaowei is a native AI assistant in small-scale internal testing by the WeChat team. Users can talk to Xiaowei by text or voice and, with AI technology, use WeChat better and communicate with friends; it can also bring up Mini Programs to provide better services.”

Per that official description, Xiaowei is conversation-driven. Its stated capabilities cover operating WeChat's own features, invoking Mini Programs for services, searching content, and generating images. According to the June 2026 reporting, rollout is a gray-scale test: only users who receive the test eligibility see the entry point. For the current form and availability, rely on the [official WeChat website](https://weixin.qq.com/) and official announcements inside the WeChat client.

## How it is used and where the boundaries are

According to public reporting, eligible users see a Xiaowei marker in the WeChat main interface and start conversations by text or voice. Two kinds of tasks are described: operating things inside WeChat, such as adjusting settings or sending messages, and completing service tasks through the Mini Program ecosystem, such as booking a hospital appointment or ordering coffee. On the developer side, WeChat published guidance for its AI ecosystem in June 2026, with Meituan, JD.com, and Ctrip announcing integrations, and WeChat Pay introduced a product for agent payment scenarios the same month.

Two boundaries matter. Xiaowei's reach depends on WeChat's own ecosystem — whether a task can be completed depends on the features or Mini Programs that expose capabilities to it. And per the June 2026 reporting it is in limited, gray-scale testing, so the entry point and available functions can change between versions. It is a separate surface from WeChat's in-app search and official accounts. The same pattern of an assistant inside a content platform appears in Xiaohongshu's AI assistant [Diandian](/en/wiki/products/diandian).

## Discussion in the show

Weekly #002 discusses Xiaowei at length in the chapters on the value of vertical applications and on in-app AI assistants. Yang Pan argued that if WeChat turns every Mini Program into a capability interface for Xiaowei, [“Xiaowei actually has the biggest opportunity going forward”](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-7156da18d169099deca9) — a user could just ask Xiaowei to place an order inside a Ctrip Mini Program. Xiangyang Qiaomu mentioned that someone in their circle already had Xiaowei [send a one-message notification to a group](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-68208ed35ccde0e32e64), and expected WeChat to keep moving in this direction.

Yang Pan also gave a personal example: while trying to lock the lens on a Dji Pocket 4, he skipped general-purpose assistants and [asked Xiaowei directly inside WeChat](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-dc7d0bbb900981555418). In the chapter on health data and daily logging, Guizang pointed out that Chinese platforms keep their data and APIs closed, so users [can only use Diandian inside Xiaohongshu and Xiaowei inside WeChat](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-3ca6b332124bc288572f), with little context sharing across apps. These are the participants' experiences and judgments, not an official feature list; see the [Chinese transcript chapter](/weekly/002/transcript#chapter-17).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Xiaowei?

Xiaowei is a native AI assistant in small-scale internal testing by the WeChat team. It supports text or voice conversations to operate WeChat features, invoke Mini Programs, search content, and generate images. This matches the description the WeChat team gave to media in June 2026.

### Where is the Xiaowei entry point, and how do I get access?

Access is a gray-scale test: users who receive test eligibility see the Xiaowei entry in the WeChat main interface, with no separate sign-up. Whether the entry appears depends on WeChat's rollout; watch the [official WeChat website](https://weixin.qq.com/) and official announcements in the client.

### What can Xiaowei do?

Per the official description, it handles text or voice conversations, operates native WeChat features such as settings and messaging, invokes Mini Programs for services like appointments and coffee orders, and supports content search and image generation. Actual availability depends on the test build.

### How does Xiaowei relate to other AI features in WeChat?

Xiaowei is an app-level assistant surface, parallel to in-app search and official accounts, positioned to unify access to WeChat features and Mini Program services through conversation. In the show, participants treat it as the representative “in-platform agent”: a simple entry point whose capabilities come from the platform's own data and services.
