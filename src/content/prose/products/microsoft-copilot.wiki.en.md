## What Microsoft Copilot is

Microsoft Copilot is the [Microsoft](/en/wiki/brands/microsoft) AI assistant for individual users. One of its predecessors was Bing Chat, launched on February 7, 2023 with "the new Bing"; on September 21, 2023, Microsoft announced it was unifying Bing Chat, Microsoft 365 Copilot, and Windows Copilot under the "Microsoft Copilot" brand; on November 15, 2023, it announced that Bing Chat itself was being renamed Copilot. From December 2023, Copilot shipped inside Windows 11, and standalone Copilot apps followed on Android and iOS.

It runs on a freemium model: the free tier covers most features, and the Copilot Pro subscription, announced on January 15, 2024, adds priority access to newer features. The main entry points are [copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com/), the Copilot built into Windows, the Edge browser, and the mobile apps.

## Uses and differences

Microsoft Copilot covers general tasks — questions, search, image generation, voice conversations. It is not a developer tool: the coding assistant for programmers is [GitHub Copilot](/en/wiki/products/github-copilot).

"Copilot" is also an umbrella for a family of Microsoft products, and two confusions are most common: Microsoft 365 Copilot, the work-focused version, became generally available for enterprises on November 1, 2023 and is deeply integrated into Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams under a subscription; the Microsoft Copilot covered on this page is the assistant for consumers. They differ in entry points, billing, and audience.

## Frequently asked questions

### How is Microsoft Copilot different from GitHub Copilot?

They are different products: Microsoft Copilot is the general-purpose assistant for individuals, entered at [copilot.microsoft.com](https://copilot.microsoft.com/); [GitHub Copilot](/en/wiki/products/github-copilot) is the developer-facing coding assistant and coding agent built around repositories, issues, and pull requests. Similar names, different subscriptions and uses.

### Is Microsoft Copilot free?

Yes, on a freemium model: the free tier includes most features, and the Copilot Pro subscription announced in January 2024 adds priority access to newer features. Current tier benefits are on the [official page](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/copilot).

### How is Microsoft 365 Copilot different from Microsoft Copilot?

Microsoft 365 Copilot is the work assistant embedded in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams, generally available for enterprises from November 1, 2023 and billed per user by subscription; Microsoft Copilot is the consumer assistant, free with an optional subscription. One lives inside the office apps; the other is a standalone assistant entry point.

### Are Bing Chat and Microsoft Copilot the same thing?

They are successive names for the same service: launched as Bing Chat in February 2023, folded into the Copilot brand in September 2023, and officially renamed on November 15, 2023. Today's Microsoft Copilot is the continuation of Bing Chat.

### Can you trust citations from Copilot?

Copilot's answers include cited sources, but generative assistants can cite low-quality or auto-generated content — in the case relayed in episode 008, most of Copilot's citations pointed to an AI-generated website. For important decisions, check the original sources rather than relying on the assistant's summary.
