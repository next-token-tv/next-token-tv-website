## What is Ant Afu?

Ant Afu is an AI health app from [Ant Group](/en/wiki/brands/ant-group). In December 2025, Ant Group renamed AQ to Ant Afu and updated the app around everyday health questions, health report explanations, and access to health services. The rename was announced on [Ant Group's website](https://www.antgroup.com/press-releases/1765785600000).

## How it relates to Lingguang

Ant Group runs two AI apps in this space: [Lingguang](/en/wiki/products/lingguang) targets general everyday assistance, while Afu focuses on health. Both come from the same company but cover different daily tasks.
