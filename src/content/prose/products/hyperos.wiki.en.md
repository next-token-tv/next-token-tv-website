## What Xiaomi HyperOS is

Xiaomi HyperOS is [Xiaomi](/en/wiki/brands/xiaomi)’s operating system for phones, tablets, and connected devices. Officially it sits inside the "Human x Car x Home" (人车家) ecosystem: system-level integration across phones, cars, and smart-home devices, with device control and cross-device handoff. It is not a desktop operating system like macOS or Windows: HyperOS runs on mobile and IoT devices and works with Windows and macOS computers through cross-device connectivity — the official page marks several connected features as supporting Windows and macOS.

## HyperOS 4

The version featured on the official product page is Xiaomi HyperOS 4. The official page lists these main directions:

- Visual refresh: "soft-light glass" (柔光玻璃) as the core material, plus lock-screen clock customization, folders, and widget stacking; the page notes these effects are limited to Snapdragon 8 Elite, Dimensity 9500, and XRING O1 platforms or above.
- Performance: official comparisons with the previous generation, such as total launch time across 30 apps and app cold-start times.
- AI: Super Xiao Ai 2.0, described as built on Xiaomi’s MiMo large model, with Task Island (Xiaomi Super Island), Inspiration Sphere, and Expert Mode (deep research, slide and webpage creation); the page also notes that AI Call and the Super Xiao Ai keyboard are limited to mainland China.
- Human x Car x Home connectivity: a merged device center, synced SMS verification codes across two phones, picking up files from a distance, and call handoff.

The rollout, per the official page: the first beta batch pushed on the afternoon of August 14, 2026, covering the Xiaomi 17 Ultra, Xiaomi 17 Pro Max, REDMI K90 Pro Max, and the Xiaomi Pad 8 series; batches on August 27 and September 17 expanded it to the Xiaomi 15 series and more phones and tablets. Xiaomi notes that eligible devices and timing may be adjusted; see the [official page](https://hyperos.mi.com/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which devices does HyperOS 4 support?

The first beta batch on the official page covers the Xiaomi 17 Ultra (including the Leica edition), Xiaomi 17 Pro Max, REDMI K90 Pro Max, and the Xiaomi Pad 8 series, later expanded to the Xiaomi 15 series and more devices; Xiaomi notes eligibility and timing may change. See the [official page](https://hyperos.mi.com/).

### When was HyperOS 4 rolled out?

Per the official page: first beta on the afternoon of August 14, 2026, with expansion batches on August 27 and September 17. The full schedule belongs to the official page.

### How does HyperOS relate to Super Xiao Ai and MiMo?

Super Xiao Ai is the AI assistant built into HyperOS; the official page describes the HyperOS 4 version, Super Xiao Ai 2.0, as based on Xiaomi’s MiMo large model, with Task Island, Inspiration Sphere, and Expert Mode.

### Why are some HyperOS 4 features missing on my phone?

The official page notes two limits: soft-light glass and related visual effects require Snapdragon 8 Elite, Dimensity 9500, XRING O1, or above; AI Call and the Super Xiao Ai keyboard are limited to mainland China. Features such as Inspiration Sphere arrive in batches over OTA. Check the official page and in-system documentation for availability.

### Is HyperOS the same kind of system as macOS?

No. HyperOS is Xiaomi’s operating system for its mobile and connected devices; macOS is Apple’s desktop operating system. They meet only in cross-device connectivity — several HyperOS connected features officially support Windows and macOS computers.
