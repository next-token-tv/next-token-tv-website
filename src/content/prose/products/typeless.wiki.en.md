## What Typeless is

Typeless is an AI voice dictation tool: you speak naturally, and the app turns your speech in real time into polished written text — messages, emails, and documents ready to send. Downloads are available from [typeless.com](https://www.typeless.com/), with apps for macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android that work system-wide across your apps rather than being tied to a particular keyboard or input method.

## Features and boundaries

The official site lists core capabilities including removing filler words and repetition, handling self-corrections as you speak, auto-formatting lists and steps, personalized writing style and tone, a personal dictionary, automatic detection and translation across 100+ languages, and speak-to-edit plus Q&A on selected text. On privacy, the official position is that no audio is retained in the cloud and history is stored on the device.

Note the product category: Typeless is a standalone dictation app, not an input method. It does not take over your keyboard’s dictionary or autocomplete; typing stays with your regular input method. Compared with voice features built into keyboards (such as Doubao Input Method or WeChat input), its focus is long-form spoken output rather than everyday short phrases.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Typeless?

Typeless is an AI voice dictation tool: you speak, and the app turns your words in real time into polished messages, emails, and documents, cleaning up filler words and repetition. The official entry point is [typeless.com](https://www.typeless.com/).

### Which platforms does Typeless support, and where do I download it?

Official apps are available for macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android, working system-wide across your apps. Downloads are on the [official website](https://www.typeless.com/).

### How much does Typeless cost?

As of September 2026, the official pricing page lists three tiers: a Free plan with 8,000 words per week; Pro at $12 per member per month billed yearly ($30 billed monthly) with unlimited words, higher accuracy, and team features; and Enterprise with custom pricing. Check the [official pricing page](https://www.typeless.com/pricing) for current rates.

### Is Typeless an input method?

No. Typeless is a standalone dictation app that does not take over your keyboard’s dictionary or autocomplete; typing remains with your regular input method. The show’s hosts make the same distinction.
