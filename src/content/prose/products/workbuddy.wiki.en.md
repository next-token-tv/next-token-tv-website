## What is WorkBuddy

WorkBuddy is an AI office agent from Tencent: Tencent Cloud's product page calls it a "full-scenario AI office workbench" covering office, coding, and design work, where users describe needs in natural language and WorkBuddy decomposes tasks, plans execution steps, and checks its own results. The product site is [workbuddy.cn](https://www.workbuddy.cn/) (tagline: “AI Agent 办公新范式” — a new paradigm for office work with AI agents) and the open ecosystem site is [open.workbuddy.cn](https://open.workbuddy.cn/), whose footer credits Tencent Cloud Computing (Beijing) Co., Ltd. and Tencent Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd.

It belongs to the mass-market desktop office agent layer — a different shape from the developer-facing [DeepSeek Harness](/en/wiki/products/deepseek-harness) (an open framework), the self-hosted [OpenClaw](/en/wiki/products/openclaw) (a personal assistant), and [Grok Bot](/en/wiki/products/grok-bot) (a persistent cloud computer). Tencent also runs CodeBuddy, a separate product line aimed at software development.

## Usage and boundaries

Per the official pages, the core use is everyday office work: decomposing complex tasks into steps, calling tools, self-checking, handling multimodal input such as text, tables, images, and code, and reading or writing local files inside authorized directories. There are three entry points: download the desktop client from the [official site](https://www.workbuddy.cn/), use the web version, or the enterprise edition; Tencent Cloud's page also describes a WeChat mini-program and remote control from phones via WeChat/WeCom, plus cloud task hosting that continues after the client is closed.

For developers and enterprises, the [open platform](https://open.workbuddy.cn/) opened five ecosystem capabilities in its first phase: Buddy apps (complete scenarios packaged with industry know-how and professional data, ready to use), Experts (preset domain experts, with custom experts built from SOPs and knowledge bases), Skills, Connectors, and hardware. Billing goes through Tencent Cloud using a Credits system; current plans and promotions are on the [Tencent Cloud product page](https://cloud.tencent.com/product/workbuddy).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who makes WorkBuddy?

WorkBuddy is a Tencent product: Tencent Cloud's page calls it "Tencent's full-scenario AI office workbench", and the open platform site is operated under Tencent Cloud Computing (Beijing) Co., Ltd. and Tencent Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd.

### How do I download WorkBuddy, and which platforms does it support?

The download entry is on the [official site workbuddy.cn](https://www.workbuddy.cn/), with web and enterprise editions also available; Tencent Cloud's page additionally mentions the WeChat mini-program and phone-based remote access via WeChat/WeCom. Supported system versions are those currently listed on the official download page.

### What is the difference between WorkBuddy and CodeBuddy?

Both are Tencent agent lines with different aims: WorkBuddy targets office work (documents, data, design, and daily tasks), while CodeBuddy targets software development. Yang Pan also discussed the two side by side as one product line in Weekly #002 ([relevant chapter](/weekly/002/transcript#chapter-21) in the Chinese transcript).

### What are WorkBuddy Experts, Skills, Connectors, and Buddy apps?

Per the open platform: Buddy apps package complete scenarios with industry know-how and professional data for out-of-the-box use; Experts are preset or customized domain-expert configurations; Skills are reusable capabilities; Connectors bring external services into workflows; and hardware is the fifth capability opened in the first phase. See the [WorkBuddy open platform](https://open.workbuddy.cn/).

### Is WorkBuddy free, and how is it priced?

It is sold through Tencent Cloud with a Credits-based billing system; the client download and web trial are publicly accessible, and the enterprise edition has its own procurement flow. Current plans, quotas, and promotions are on the [Tencent Cloud product page](https://cloud.tencent.com/product/workbuddy) and its purchase page.

### Does WorkBuddy have a mobile version?

Tencent Cloud's page describes the WeChat mini-program and remote access to desktop tasks from phones via WeChat/WeCom; on Weekly #001, Orange also mentioned a mobile release (with an early complaint that Skills were missing). The current mobile experience is whatever the official versions ship today.
