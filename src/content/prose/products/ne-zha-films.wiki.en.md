## Ne Zha film series

The Ne Zha film series is a Chinese mythology animation franchise directed by Jiaozi (Yang Yu), comprising Ne Zha (2019) and its sequel Ne Zha II (2025). Both films are based on the Ming-dynasty novel Fengshen Yanyi (Investiture of the Gods) and reimagine the mythological figure Ne Zha: the first film follows the mischievous "demon pill" reincarnation of Ne Zha as he defies prejudice and fate, and the sequel picks up after Ne Zha and Ao Bing survive the "heavenly tribulation" (per [Chinese Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/哪吒之魔童降世)).

## Release dates

- Ne Zha: released in mainland China on July 26, 2019, and in overseas markets from late August 2019 (North America handled by Well Go USA)
- Ne Zha II: released in mainland China on January 29, 2025 (Lunar New Year's Day), with overseas releases from mid-February 2025 (A24 handled North America)

Per Chinese Wikipedia, the sequel surpassed Changjin Lake on its ninth day of release to become the highest-grossing film in Chinese mainland box-office history; the first film set several mainland animated-film box office records at the time.

Episode 010, chapter 10 ("恐龙、流体与古巴比伦：可复用的虚拟世界" — [dinosaurs, fluids and ancient Babylon: reusable virtual worlds](/weekly/010/transcript#chapter-10) in the Chinese transcript), Guizang frames high-definition CG as the most expensive part of the content production chain and points to recent Chinese animated features: ["look at those domestic animated films in recent years, right? The costs — for example Ne Zha, All Wishes Come True! just wrapped its run, and Three Kingdoms: The Beginning just released"](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-04e07fd56f41855e6671). The episode does not discuss the film itself; Ne Zha is mentioned as an example in the participants' comparison between AI-generated CG and traditional production costs, which is their own framing.

## Frequently asked questions

### When was Ne Zha released?

July 26, 2019 in mainland China, with overseas markets from late August 2019 (per Chinese Wikipedia).

### When was Ne Zha II released?

January 29, 2025 (Lunar New Year's Day) in mainland China, as a direct sequel to the 2019 film; overseas releases followed from February 2025.

### Who directed the Ne Zha films?

Both films are directed by Jiaozi (Yang Yu). The 2019 film is his feature-directing debut, and he also wrote and directed the sequel (per Chinese Wikipedia).

### How is Ne Zha II related to the first film?

It is a direct sequel: the story continues from where Ne Zha and Ao Bing survived the "heavenly tribulation," with characters carried over from the first film, so the two form one adapted worldview.

### What are the Ne Zha films adapted from?

They are original adaptations built on the Ming-dynasty novel Fengshen Yanyi and the traditional figure of Ne Zha; characters and plot are reimagined rather than a scene-by-scene retelling of the novel.
