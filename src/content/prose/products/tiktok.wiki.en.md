## What TikTok is

TikTok is a short-video platform owned by ByteDance for watching, creating, and sharing short videos. ByteDance's website describes it as "the leading destination for short-form mobile video," with the mission "to inspire creativity and bring joy" — see the [ByteDance products page](https://www.bytedance.com/en/products) and [TikTok's official about page](https://www.tiktok.com/about?lang=en). The service recommends content by interest and hosts communities such as #BookTok, #LearnOnTikTok, and #GamingOnTikTok; the official about page lists dual headquarters in Los Angeles and Singapore.

## Milestones

According to [Wikipedia's TikTok article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok): TikTok's domestic counterpart Douyin launched on September 20, 2016; ByteDance launched TikTok internationally in September 2017; it bought Musical.ly for nearly $1 billion in November 2017 and merged the two apps into TikTok on August 2, 2018. In September 2021, TikTok announced one billion monthly active users worldwide. In 2026, TikTok's US operations were divested to a consortium including Oracle, Silver Lake, and MGX holding more than 80%, with ByteDance retaining 19.9%. Official disclosures govern current user and corporate figures.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the relationship between TikTok and Douyin?

Both belong to ByteDance. Douyin serves mainland China and launched in 2016; TikTok is the international version introduced in 2017, merged in 2018 with Musical.ly, which ByteDance had acquired.

### Where are the TikTok website and web player?

The website is [tiktok.com](https://www.tiktok.com/), which supports browsing and signing in directly in a browser; the mobile app is TikTok.

### Who owns TikTok?

TikTok was created by ByteDance. As of 2026, its US operations are majority-owned by a consortium including Oracle, Silver Lake, and MGX, with ByteDance holding 19.9%, per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok); official announcements govern the current structure.
