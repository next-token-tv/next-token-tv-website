## Dynasty Warriors

Dynasty Warriors (Japanese title Shin Sangoku Musou, 真・三國無双) is a Three Kingdoms-era action game series developed by Omega Force (ω-Force) within KOEI TECMO, known for one warrior cutting through large battlefield armies. The official 25th-anniversary site counts the series from DYNASTY WARRIORS 2, released in 2000 (the Shin Sangoku Musou title in Japan), and records worldwide sales of more than 24 million copies; the site’s latest mainline entry, DYNASTY WARRIORS: ORIGINS, surpassed 1 million copies within one month of release.

## Series structure

Beyond the mainline games, the series includes “Xtreme Legends” and “Empires” branches, and the IP has extended into stage productions and live-action films. Release status and platforms for individual titles belong on the [official 25th-anniversary page](https://www.koeitecmoamerica.com/smusou25th/us/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What type of game is Dynasty Warriors?

A Three Kingdoms action series where a single officer fights through large-scale battlefields against hundreds of enemies.

### What is the relation between Dynasty Warriors and Shin Sangoku Musou?

They are the same series: Shin Sangoku Musou in Japan, released abroad as Dynasty Warriors. The official 25th-anniversary site counts from DYNASTY WARRIORS 2 in 2000.

### What is the latest entry?

The latest mainline entry named on the official 25th-anniversary page is DYNASTY WARRIORS: ORIGINS; newer announcements belong on the [official page](https://www.koeitecmoamerica.com/smusou25th/us/).
