## Definition

Dimensity is MediaTek's family of 5G mobile chips for smartphones, introduced in late 2019 and in volume use from 2020. The official page groups it into the Flagship family, the Premium 8000 family, the 7000 family, and the 6000 family, covering smartphones from entry level to flagship. See the [official page](https://www.mediatek.com/products/smartphones/dimensity-5g).

## Product lines

Dimensity chips are sold to device makers as 5G SoCs integrating CPU, GPU, ISP, and modem components. Technology themes highlighted on the official page include all-big-core CPU design, on-device generative AI (including large language model support), the HyperEngine gaming feature set, MiraVision display technologies, the Imagiq imaging stack, and DORA (Dimensity Open Resource Architecture) customization for device makers. The flagship family starts at the 9000 series, the 8000 family brings flagship technology to the premium tier, and the 7000 and 6000 families serve the mid-range and entry segments. Specific models and specifications within each family are governed by the [official materials](https://www.mediatek.com/products/smartphones/dimensity-5g).

## Related concepts

Dimensity is the chip family name, not the company itself; MediaTek also makes chips beyond smartphones. Dimensity and Qualcomm's Snapdragon ([Snapdragon](/en/wiki/products/snapdragon)) are the two corresponding smartphone SoC lines and are frequently compared in phone reviews.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do the Dimensity 9000, 8000, 7000, and 6000 series differ?

They are the official market tiers: the flagship family starts at the 9000 series, the 8000 (Premium) family serves the high-end tier using flagship technology, the 7000 family covers the mid-range, and the 6000 family targets entry devices. The specific models in each family are listed on the [official page](https://www.mediatek.com/products/smartphones/dimensity-5g).

### Do Dimensity chips support AI?

Yes — the official page lists on-device generative AI among Dimensity's main technology directions, including device-side support for large language models (LLMs). The model sizes and features supported by a specific chip are governed by the [official materials](https://www.mediatek.com/products/smartphones/dimensity-5g) and the phone's documentation.

### How do I choose between Dimensity and Snapdragon?

They are the smartphone chip families of MediaTek and Qualcomm respectively, and no answer fits every phone. Results within a generation depend on the specific model, device tuning, and thermal design, so third-party reviews of the specific phone are the best reference.
