## What WeChat is

WeChat (Chinese name Weixin) is Tencent’s messaging and social platform, launched in 2011. Weixin, serving mainland China, and WeChat, serving other regions, are separate but interoperable sister services; per Tencent’s official site, their combined monthly active accounts exceeded 1.4 billion as of the first quarter of 2026. Coverage spans messaging (chat, voice and video calls, Moments), WeChat Pay, Mini Programs, Official Accounts, Channels, Search, and Mini Games; Tencent’s site also lists operational figures such as more than 1 billion daily commercial transactions via WeChat Pay since Q4 2019 and RMB 8 trillion in Mini Program GMV for 2024.

WeChat is the platform itself, not a device or a single AI assistant: this entry covers the platform and its main official entry points. Tencent’s separate input tool is covered in the [WeChat Input](/en/wiki/products/wechat-input-method) entry.

## Usage and entry points

Individual users rely primarily on the mobile app, with PC/Mac desktop versions alongside; [WeChat Web](https://wx.qq.com/) logs in by scanning a QR code with the phone and covers basic chat. Content creators and developers use a different entry point: the [WeChat Official Accounts Platform](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/) registers and manages service accounts, official accounts (subscription accounts), Mini Programs, and WeCom (WeChat Work) accounts. Mini Programs are lightweight apps that run inside WeChat, and official accounts are used for publishing and reader communication. Platform capabilities, open interfaces, and rules change with releases; operational and development decisions should follow the official platform pages.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I log in to WeChat Web?

Open [wx.qq.com](https://wx.qq.com/), scan the QR code with the mobile app, and confirm on the phone. WeChat Web covers fewer features than the mobile or desktop apps; for the full experience use the mobile app or the PC/Mac clients linked from the WeChat Web page.

### Where do I register and manage WeChat official accounts?

On the [WeChat Official Accounts Platform](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/). It centrally manages four account types — service accounts, official accounts, Mini Programs, and WeCom — with publishing, follower interaction, and account administration all handled there, plus a companion “公众号助手” app for mobile use.

### What is the difference between WeChat and Weixin?

Weixin serves mainland China users and WeChat serves users in other regions; the two are Tencent’s separate but interoperable sister services. Accounts, features, and compliance requirements differ by region, per the official documentation.

### What are WeChat Mini Programs?

Mini Programs are lightweight apps that run inside WeChat without a separate install, covering shopping, travel, government services, and more. Operators register Mini Program accounts on the [Official Accounts Platform](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/); Tencent’s site publishes ecosystem-scale figures.

### What is XiaoWei, WeChat’s in-app AI assistant?

XiaoWei is the AI assistant entry point inside WeChat. On the show, Yang Pan mentioned [a XiaoWei button appearing in WeChat’s search box](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-dc7d0bbb900981555418), with participants using it for everyday questions and sending group notifications (see the discussion above). XiaoWei’s exact capabilities change with WeChat releases; what the app currently offers is authoritative.
