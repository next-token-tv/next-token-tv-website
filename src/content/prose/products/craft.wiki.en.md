## Craft

Craft is a document app by Craft Docs covering writing, note organization and task management (per the [official site](https://www.craft.do/)). It uses a block-based editor, embeds to-dos inside documents, and ships with a calendar, reminders and daily notes; documents can be published to the web, with templates, whiteboards and AI writing assistance also available.

## Features and platforms

- Writing and docs: from first capture to shareable documents, with templates, whiteboards and web publishing.
- Tasks and planning: to-dos embedded in documents alongside a calendar, reminders and daily notes.
- Organization: spaces, folders, tags and collections.
- Platforms: macOS, iOS, iPadOS, Windows, Android and the web; the company emphasizes native, non-Electron apps with offline support.
- Collaboration and extension: real-time collaboration, end-to-end encryption, an API and MCP connections for tools such as Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor.

## Discussion in the show

In episode 010, in the chapter "Carbon 体验：写作的人味与设计、编码能力" ([the Carbon experience: human-sounding writing, design and coding](/weekly/010/transcript#chapter-17); Chinese transcript), Orange shares a usage detail: he used Carbon (a Google model) to redesign the interface of his own product Cola and told it to ["reference well-received products overseas"](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-05835dce3fabaabe7b0a). The model ran for half an hour with eight browsers open, scraping how various sites look (his retelling of the model's own account), and [the references it settled on](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-7448e81e9771a03a8b83) were "like Craft. Linear. And two others — all products with a great reputation." Orange judged the resulting interface ["actually better than the previous version from our designer"](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-5ff8cce6702fda2f7d46). In this passage Craft appears as a well-regarded design reference picked by the model; it is a participant's retelling of his experience, and the episode does not review Craft's features itself.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Craft?

A document app covering writing, notes and task management: a block-based editor, in-document to-dos, a calendar and daily notes, and the option to publish documents as web pages; see the [official overview](https://www.craft.do/).

### Which platforms does Craft support?

macOS, iOS, iPadOS, Windows, Android and the web; the company describes its apps as native with offline support.

### Is Craft free?

The company offers a free tier plus paid plans for individuals and teams; see the [official site](https://www.craft.do/) for current pricing.

### How does Craft differ from Notion?

Both are document and knowledge-management tools. Craft emphasizes native apps, offline use and the writing and typesetting experience, while [Notion](/en/wiki/products/notion) is known for databases and multi-view organization. The choice depends on your workflow, and both offer free tiers.
