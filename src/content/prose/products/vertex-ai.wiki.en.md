## Vertex AI

Vertex AI is the machine learning and generative AI platform of [Google](/en/wiki/brands/google) Cloud. Official documentation describes it as a comprehensive suite of tools for building, training, and managing machine learning models at scale, covering data preparation, model training (AutoML plus custom training with TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn, and XGBoost), and model registry and deployment, bringing the full ML lifecycle into one platform (see the [official introduction](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning)).

## Naming and product tiers

As Google introduced its new agent platform, several Vertex AI product names changed in official documentation: the renaming page states that "Vertex AI Platform" is now the "Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform" and that "Vertex AI" is renamed to "Agent Platform", with entries such as Vertex AI Studio, Vertex AI Search, and Vertex AI Agent Engine renamed accordingly (see the [official renaming page](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/vertex-ai-name-changes)).

## How it differs from Google AI Studio

Vertex AI and [Google AI Studio](/en/wiki/products/google-ai-studio) are two separate entry points from Google: Vertex AI targets engineering workloads for training, deploying, and managing models on Google Cloud, while AI Studio is the lightweight entry point for trying out and wiring in Gemini models. Each entry records its own access points and positioning.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Vertex AI?

The machine learning and generative AI platform of Google Cloud, providing tools across the ML lifecycle including data preparation, training, model registry, and deployment; see the [official documentation](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/machine-learning).

### Was Vertex AI renamed?

The official renaming page states that with the introduction of Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, "Vertex AI Platform" is now called "Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform" and "Vertex AI" is renamed to "Agent Platform", with several sub-products renamed alongside; see the [official renaming page](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise-agent-platform/vertex-ai-name-changes).

### How does Vertex AI differ from Google AI Studio?

Both are Google AI development entry points with different positioning: Vertex AI is the engineering platform for training and deployment on Google Cloud, while AI Studio is the lightweight web entry point for trying and wiring in Gemini models; see the [Google AI Studio entry](/en/wiki/products/google-ai-studio).

### Whose cloud does Vertex AI belong to?

It belongs to [Google](/en/wiki/brands/google)'s cloud business, [Google Cloud](/en/wiki/brands/google-cloud).
