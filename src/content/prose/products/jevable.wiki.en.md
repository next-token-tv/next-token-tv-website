## What Jevable is

Jevable is a website collecting projects and demos built with Jev, under the tagline "Discover what people build with Jev." It presents a gallery of application demos built with Jev, the structured-decision model from [TypeSafe AI](/en/wiki/brands/typesafe-ai) (see the [Jev entry](/en/wiki/products/jev)); entries can be filtered by categories such as Agents, Games, Productivity and Robotics. Each record links back to the builder's original post on X and credits the builder, and developers can submit their own demos for inclusion. The site does not name an operator; the show refers to it as an enthusiast site.

## The name and discussion in the show

The site's name is a naming joke on [Lovable](/en/wiki/products/lovable) — Lovable with "Lo" swapped for "Jev".

In Weekly #003's chapter "“没有幻觉”不等于判断正确" of the [Chinese transcript](/weekly/003/transcript#chapter-06), Orange brings it up while discussing Computer Use applications of Jev: "他们有一个爱好者网站，收集了各种用法叫 Jevable" ("they have an enthusiast site collecting various uses, called Jevable"), and cites a Live2D demo on the site where the model turns a spoken line into parameters driving a character's motion and expression in real time ([the paragraph](/weekly/003/transcript#quote-2fda6572c140936fe8dc)). Yang Pan confirms: "橘子推荐的网站叫 Jevable.com" ("the site Orange recommended is Jevable.com") ([the paragraph](/weekly/003/transcript#quote-2139193111dd2a33d0cd)); Orange adds "对，他把 Lovable 换成了 Jevable" ("right, he swapped Lovable for Jevable") ([the paragraph](/weekly/003/transcript#quote-d150e1a6ffc1aa5496e6)) — a joke about the site's name, not a discussion of the Lovable product.
