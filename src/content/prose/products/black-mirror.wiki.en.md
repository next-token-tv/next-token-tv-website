## Black Mirror

Black Mirror is a British anthology series created by Charlie Brooker that extrapolates the impact of near-future technology on individuals and society. It premiered on the UK’s Channel 4 on December 4, 2011; from the third season, first released on October 21, 2016, it became a Netflix production. Netflix’s official media page describes it as a “dark, satirical anthology series,” and the season that returned in 2025 consists of six brand-new episodes, including a sequel to “USS Callister.”

## Watching and series structure

As an anthology, each episode of Black Mirror is a standalone story with no continuing plot, so viewers can start anywhere. In terms of broadcast history, the first two seasons and the 2014 special “White Christmas” premiered on Channel 4, while from season 3 onward the series streams on Netflix; the interactive film Bandersnatch was released on December 28, 2018. Through the seventh season in 2025, the series totals 33 episodes plus the interactive film, and an eighth season was commissioned in January 2026. Regional availability and language support should be checked on the [official Netflix page](https://media.netflix.com/en/only-on-netflix/70264888).

## Frequently asked questions

### What kind of show is Black Mirror?

A technology-themed anthology: each episode tells a standalone story about how technology affects people and society.

### Where can I watch Black Mirror?

From season 3 it is a Netflix production available on Netflix; the first two seasons and the Christmas special premiered on the UK’s Channel 4. Check the [official Netflix page](https://media.netflix.com/en/only-on-netflix/70264888) for current regional availability.

### Do I need to watch Black Mirror in order?

No. It is an anthology series with standalone episodes, so you can start with any episode.

### Is there news about Black Mirror season 8?

An eighth season was commissioned in January 2026; release timing and episode count are up to Netflix’s official announcements.
