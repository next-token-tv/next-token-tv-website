## Definition

The Twilight Saga is a young-adult fantasy romance film series adapted from the novels of American author Stephenie Meyer, released in five installments from 2008 to 2012: Twilight (2008), The Twilight Saga: New Moon (2009), Eclipse (2010), Breaking Dawn – Part 1 (2011) and Breaking Dawn – Part 2 (2012). The first four films were distributed by Summit Entertainment and the fifth by Lionsgate Films. The story follows the romance between the human teenager Bella and the vampire Edward, intertwined with the werewolf Jacob.

## Related and confusable terms

"The Twilight Saga" can refer to Meyer's original four-novel series, these five films, and their derivative content. This entry covers the film series only. In casual usage, "Twilight-style" is often shorthand for vampire-and-werewolf young-adult screen stories.

## Frequently asked questions

### How many Twilight films are there?

Five, released between 2008 and 2012, with the Breaking Dawn adaptation split into two parts.

### What is the viewing order of the Twilight films?

The release order: Twilight (2008), New Moon (2009), Eclipse (2010), Breaking Dawn – Part 1 (2011), Breaking Dawn – Part 2 (2012).

### Who wrote the novels the films are based on?

The films are adapted from the Twilight novel series by Stephenie Meyer.
