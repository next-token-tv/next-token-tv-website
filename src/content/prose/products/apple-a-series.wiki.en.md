## Definition

Apple A-series is Apple's system-on-chip (SoC) family for iPhone and other mobile devices, integrating CPU, GPU, Neural Engine and memory on one chip. The first Apple-designed SoC, the A4, launched in 2010 with the original iPad and was later used in the iPhone 4. Apple's description of the current chip is on the [iPhone 18 Pro page](https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/).

## How it works

Each generation combines CPU, GPU, a Neural Engine and memory in one package, providing the local compute and on-device intelligence for the device it powers. Notable milestones: the A7 was the first 64-bit chip in a smartphone (2013, iPhone 5S); the Neural Engine arrived with the A11 (2017); and per the [official page](https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/), the A20 Pro uses "packaging inspired by our M-series chips", has a GPU up to 40 percent faster than iPhone 17 Pro, and introduces a dual 16-core Neural Engine.

## Usage

The A-series chip drives an iPhone's everyday performance, graphics and on-device AI: photo processing, game rendering and on-device speech and vision models all depend on it. A chip is a component of the whole device — actual specifications, thermal behavior and experience also depend on the model that carries it.

## Related and confusable terms

A-series and M-series are two separate Apple chip lines: A-series powers iPhone and other mobile devices, while M-series targets the Mac and related product lines; Apple contrasts the two when describing the A20 Pro's "packaging inspired by our M-series chips". "Apple A-series chips" also has nothing to do with Apple Watch's "Series" naming — the watch line is called Series 9 and so on, not A-series.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who manufactures A-series chips?

Apple designs them; manufacturing is outsourced — Samsung fabricated chips through the A9, after which TSMC took over (see [Wikipedia: Apple silicon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_silicon)).

### How do A-series and M-series chips differ?

They are different Apple chip lines: A-series goes into iPhone and other mobile devices, M-series into the Mac and related products. Apple describes the A20 Pro's packaging as "inspired by our M-series chips" (see the [official page](https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/)).

### Which A-series chip is the latest?

A20 Pro shipped in the iPhone 18 Pro, unveiled on September 9, 2026; for models released since then, check the live information on [Apple's product pages](https://www.apple.com/iphone-18-pro/).
