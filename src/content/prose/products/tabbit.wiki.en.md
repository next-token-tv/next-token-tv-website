## What Tabbit is

Tabbit is a browser that bakes an AI assistant into web browsing. Its site footer names the developer as 北京酷讯互动科技有限公司 (Beijing Kuxun Interactive Technology Co., Ltd.). The product lives at [tabbit.com](https://www.tabbit.com/), positioned around completing the loop of "browse — ask AI — execute tasks" inside one browser.

## Features and boundaries

The official site groups its capabilities into four areas:

- **Context references**: AI chat can draw on page text, screenshots, web pages, and files, with @-references to tabs, tab groups, and local files.
- **Task execution**: the AI can open pages, fill forms, and perform actions for you; official examples include customer-service auto-replies, batch web data collection ("资源捕手"), and generating scripts from a single sentence.
- **Tab management**: a vertical sidebar layout with automatic tab grouping.
- **Presets ("妙招")**: save frequently used prompts for one-click reuse.

Per the official site, Tabbit ships with models from multiple Chinese AI vendors and updates them over time — the current list belongs to its official pages. A Tabbit CLI also exists. For platforms, the site offers downloads for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 12+) and Windows 10+, plus membership and pricing entries. On privacy, the site states that "your data belongs to you."

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Tabbit, and who develops it?

Tabbit is a browser with a built-in AI assistant that can reference pages and files as context and execute web tasks on your behalf. The site footer names the developer as Beijing Kuxun Interactive Technology Co., Ltd. (北京酷讯互动科技有限公司)， and the official site is [tabbit.com](https://www.tabbit.com/).

### Which platforms does Tabbit support?

The official site offers downloads for macOS (Apple Silicon M1+ and Intel, macOS 12+) and Windows 10+; current availability belongs to [tabbit.com](https://www.tabbit.com/).

### What can Tabbit do?

Per the official site: AI chat can reference tabs, screenshots, and local files as context; it can open pages, fill forms, and perform actions such as customer-service auto-replies and batch data collection; the sidebar manages tabs with automatic grouping; and "妙招" presets save frequently used prompts for one-click reuse.

### Which AI models does Tabbit include?

The official site says it supports models from multiple Chinese AI vendors, with the selectable list changing over versions. Check the in-browser model picker or the [official site](https://www.tabbit.com/) for the current list.
