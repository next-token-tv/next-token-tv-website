## What is Microsoft Office

Microsoft Office is Microsoft's family of office software suites, centered on word processing, spreadsheets, and presentations: Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. The suite was announced by Bill Gates on August 1, 1988, at COMDEX, and the first bundle contained exactly those three applications; Office for Windows shipped on October 1, 1990, after a Mac version in 1989 ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Office)). For the company and the operating system, see the [Microsoft entry](/en/wiki/brands/microsoft) and the [Windows entry](/en/wiki/products/windows).

## Composition

The first bundle consisted of Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, all of which had shipped standalone before the suite: the first version of Word was released for MS-DOS in the autumn of 1983, and the first version of Excel was released for the Mac in 1985 ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Office)). Over time the suite added Outlook, OneNote, and other apps, with the Windows version also including Access; the exact app mix varies by subscription plan and platform, and the [official Microsoft 365 page](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/microsoft-office) is the reference.

## Office and Microsoft 365

Microsoft launched the subscription service Office 365 on June 28, 2011; on March 30, 2020, it announced that the consumer plans of Office 365 would be rebranded as Microsoft 365, effective April 21, 2020 ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_365)). Later in 2022, Microsoft announced it would retire the Office brand on the web and in mobile apps: office.com was renamed to Microsoft 365 in November 2022, and the mobile apps followed in January 2023 ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_365)). Today [office.com](https://www.office.com/) states "Office is now Microsoft 365": signing in gives everyone free access to essential Microsoft 365 apps on the web plus 5 GB of cloud storage, while larger storage and desktop apps come with a Microsoft 365 subscription.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do Microsoft Office and Microsoft 365 differ?

Office is the name of the software suite, while Microsoft 365 is the subscription service that includes those apps; the consumer plans of Office 365 were renamed Microsoft 365 on April 21, 2020, and office.com now points to Microsoft 365 ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_365)).

### Is there a free version of Microsoft Office?

Yes. Signed in with a Microsoft account at [office.com](https://www.office.com/), you can use essential Microsoft 365 apps on the web and 5 GB of cloud storage for free; desktop apps and more storage require a subscription (the official page reads: "Everyone gets cloud storage and essential Microsoft 365 apps on the web, free of charge").

### Where do I download Microsoft Office?

The official entry points are [office.com](https://www.office.com/) and the [Microsoft 365 product page](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/microsoft-office); after signing in, follow the on-page prompts to install the desktop apps or use the web version.

### Which apps are in the Office suite?

The original three are Word, Excel, and PowerPoint; Outlook, OneNote, and others were added later, with the Windows version also including Access. The app mix differs by subscription plan and platform — the [official page](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-365/microsoft-office) is the reference.
