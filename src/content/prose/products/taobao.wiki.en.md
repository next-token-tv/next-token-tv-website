## What Taobao is

Taobao is an online shopping platform under [Alibaba](/en/wiki/brands/alibaba) Group; the official page describes it as "an international online shopping platform under Alibaba Group." The marketplace connects merchants and consumers: merchants sell through storefronts, while consumers find and buy products via search, shops, and live-streaming rooms. The official entry point is [taobao.com](https://www.taobao.com/).

## Usage and boundaries

Shopping happens through the web app and the mobile Taobao app. The official page lists sources of product information that include Tmall brand flagship stores, Taobao live-streaming rooms, influencer comment sections, and Taobao Baike; for buyer protection, the mobile app offers escrow-style secured transactions (pay after receiving the goods) plus return and replacement commitments. The page also mentions tools such as product translation; overseas shipping and payment specifics are governed by the official documentation.

Taobao’s shape is that of a marketplace: goods and shops come from merchants, and platform rules govern transactions. Its openness toward apps such as WeChat and Douyin is a matter of inter-platform strategy — discussed in the show — and does not change its basic role as an e-commerce platform.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Taobao?

Taobao is an online shopping platform under Alibaba Group, officially positioned as an international online shopping platform: merchants run shops on it, consumers buy via search, live-streaming, and storefronts, and transactions carry escrow-style protection.

### Where is the Taobao website and web version?

The official site is [www.taobao.com](https://www.taobao.com/); after signing in you can shop on the web. On mobile, use the Taobao app.

### How do I download the Taobao mobile app?

Search for “Taobao” or “手机淘宝” in your phone’s app store, or enter the download from the [official site](https://www.taobao.com/). Be careful to use the official app rather than third-party wrappers.

### How can users outside China use Taobao?

Taobao is officially positioned as an international shopping platform, and its pages include tools such as product translation; overseas shipping and payment specifics are governed by the official documentation.
