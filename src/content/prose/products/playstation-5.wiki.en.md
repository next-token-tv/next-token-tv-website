## What the PlayStation 5 is

The PlayStation 5 (PS5) is a home video game console from Sony Interactive Entertainment, part of [Sony](/en/wiki/brands/sony). It is the fifth generation of the PlayStation brand and belongs to the ninth console generation. Sony announced it in April 2019 as the successor to the PlayStation 4, launched it in North America, Japan, Australia, New Zealand, and South Korea on November 12, 2020, and released it worldwide on November 19, 2020.

## Editions and hardware evolution

The PS5 launched in two editions: a standard model with an Ultra HD Blu-ray disc drive and a lower-cost Digital Edition without one. Both shipped alongside the DualSense wireless controller.

The hardware has since gone through documented revisions. Slimmer models replaced the original consoles in November 2023. The higher-end PlayStation 5 Pro was announced on September 10, 2024 and released on November 7, 2024, with a faster GPU, improved ray tracing, and an AI-driven upscaling technology as its headline changes. Models on sale and their configurations change over time, so the [official PS5 page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/ps5/) is the reference point for the current lineup.

## Usage and boundaries

The PS5 is used to play games on the PlayStation platform, acquired either as physical discs or digital downloads; online services, accounts, and store functionality run through Sony's PlayStation Network. Availability depends on region, account, and system software. The game lineup and prices change over time; the official store and the [official PS5 page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/ps5/) carry the live information.

## Frequently asked questions

### What PS5 editions exist?

The PS5 launched in a standard edition (with an Ultra HD Blu-ray disc drive) and a Digital Edition (without one). Slimmer models followed, plus the PlayStation 5 Pro released on November 7, 2024. The current lineup lives on the [official PS5 page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/ps5/).

### When was the PS5 released?

The PS5 launched on November 12, 2020 in North America, Japan, Australia, New Zealand, and South Korea, and worldwide on November 19, 2020. The PlayStation 5 Pro followed on November 7, 2024.

### How does the PS5 Pro differ from the base PS5?

Sony's September 10, 2024 announcement listed a faster GPU, improved ray tracing, and an AI-driven upscaling technology as the Pro's main changes. It targets players who prioritize image quality and frame rates; the official announcement covers the hardware details.

### How much does a PS5 cost now?

Pricing varies by region and changes with the lineup. Check the [official PS5 page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/ps5/) and your regional PlayStation Store for current pricing.
