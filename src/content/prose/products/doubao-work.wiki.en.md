## What is Doubao Work

Doubao Work is a work-oriented product under the Doubao brand of [ByteDance](/en/wiki/brands/bytedance), with its official entry at [doubao.com/work](https://www.doubao.com/work) and the official tagline "工作新习惯，你说豆包干" ("a new work habit — you tell Doubao, it does the work"). It is positioned not as a chat assistant but as a product you hand concrete tasks to: research, writing, presentations, spreadsheets, scheduled tasks, and computer operation are all within its official scope.

It shares the brand and domain with the Doubao chat assistant but targets work delivery; [the coding-oriented counterpart sits on a separate product line](/en/wiki/products/qoder).

## Use and boundaries

Per the official page, Doubao Work's capabilities fall into several groups:

- Professional task delivery: it invokes matching skills for different professional domains and follows professional workflows to deliver usable results, including deep research, long-form writing, and automation of repetitive tasks.
- Computer operation: it can read, create, and modify local files, open web pages, fill in forms, and complete cross-page flows; tasks can also be dispatched remotely from a phone.
- Creative generation: images, posters, and comics use Seedream 5.0 Pro; video storyboards and finished videos use Seedance 2.5, with iterative revision based on feedback.
- Access and pricing: the official page notes a 30-day subscription benefit for downloading the client; team and enterprise plans are billed per seat, with credit-based usage packages on top. See the [official pricing page](https://www.doubao.com/work/pricing) for current plan structures and prices.

The official page does not market a "persistent cloud computer"; show participants disagreed on whether it has that form — see below.

## FAQ

### What is Doubao Work, and how does it relate to Doubao?

Doubao Work is a product for handing tasks to AI, under ByteDance's Doubao brand, at [doubao.com/work](https://www.doubao.com/work). It shares the brand and domain with the Doubao chat assistant but targets work delivery: research, documents, spreadsheets, scheduled tasks, and computer operation.

### How much does Doubao Work cost?

Per the official pricing page, the team plan is billed per seat (about ¥166 per seat per month when billed annually, or ¥198 per seat per month when billed monthly), with a monthly credit allowance plus a limited-time bonus; the enterprise plan starts at 100 seats on a "seats plus usage packages" model; downloading the client grants a 30-day subscription benefit. As of September 2026, current plans and prices are on the [official pricing page](https://www.doubao.com/work/pricing).

### What can Doubao Work do?

Per the official page: it invokes domain-specific skills for research and work deliverables; it runs scheduled tasks; it reads and writes local files and operates web pages and cross-page flows, with remote task dispatch from a phone; and it generates images and posters with Seedream 5.0 Pro and videos with Seedance 2.5, iterating on feedback.

### Is “豆包办公” (Doubao Office) another name for it?

No. The official product name is Doubao Work (豆包工作). "Doubao Office" is a common miswriting — on Weekly #001, Yang Pan mentioned a newsroom draft that used it and worried the mistake would spread. Use the official name when searching or citing.

### Does Doubao Work get its own cloud computer?

The official page does not market a persistent cloud computer. Show participants discussed this explicitly: Yang Pan grouped it with "given a cloud computer" products ([episode 002](/weekly/002/transcript#chapter-13)), but Guizang, who had used it, found its virtual machines temporary with no persisted context, a different generation from Grok Bot's persistent machines ([the episode 002 chapter](/weekly/002/transcript#chapter-24)). Treat the show content as personal experience and defer to official product documentation.
