## What Qianwen Go is

Qianwen Go (千问Go!) is a Personal Agent product from Alibaba's [Qianwen](/en/wiki/products/qwen), positioned as an AI assistant that handles personal tasks on the user's behalf. According to multiple news reports, Alibaba's Qianwen announced the product on October 13, 2026; it is not a standalone app but an agent embedded inside the Qianwen app, with the official entry point at the [Qianwen website](https://www.qianwen.com/).

## Uses and access

By the reports' accounts, Qianwen Go targets complex personal tasks without fixed workflows. Example tasks include flight-delay compensation claims, shopping price-protection requests, car maintenance bookings, sports venue reservations and shopping price comparison: the agent operates real apps and phone processes for the user, negotiating across multiple turns. These are demo and beta cases from media coverage; actual capability and coverage change as access expands.

Access: at announcement the product was in an experience-application stage, with the official side opening access in batches. Whether it is available to you, and which regions and tasks are covered, is determined by the official entry inside the Qianwen app.

## FAQ

### What is Qianwen Go?

Alibaba Qianwen's Personal Agent product, an AI assistant that runs errands for users, announced on October 13, 2026. Per media reports it lives inside the Qianwen app and can handle tasks such as flight-delay claims and shopping price protection.

### How do I get access to Qianwen Go?

It is not a standalone app: apply through the official entry inside the Qianwen app, with access opened in batches. Current status follows the [Qianwen website](https://www.qianwen.com/) and in-app information.

### What can Qianwen Go do?

Reported hands-on cases include cash claims for flight delays, Taobao price-protection refunds, car maintenance bookings, venue reservations and shopping price comparison — tasks that take multi-turn negotiation. Actual supported scope is whatever the product states officially.

### How does Qianwen Go relate to Qianwen?

Qianwen is Alibaba's AI assistant product; Qianwen Go is the personal agent capability inside it, built on the Qianwen app and Alibaba's open ecosystem. The [Qianwen](/en/wiki/products/qwen) entry covers the broader product context.
