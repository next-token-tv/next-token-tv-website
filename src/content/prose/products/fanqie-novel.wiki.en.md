## Fanqie Novel

Fanqie Novel is an online-literature platform operated by [ByteDance](/en/wiki/brands/bytedance), formally launched in November 2019. It centers on free reading monetized through advertising; from 2022 it expanded investment in original content and added paid-revenue mechanisms, and from 2023 it began developing film, animation, and other adaptations of its titles (per [Chinese Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/番茄小说)). The site is [fanqienovel.com](https://fanqienovel.com).

## Reading and writing

For readers, the platform is mainly free reading with advertising as the commercial base. For writers, it offers a publishing channel and, beyond the original free model, paid-revenue mechanisms; signing and revenue rules are documented at the official author entry. The catalog mixes third-party distribution with original serialized works, some of which move on to film and animation adaptation.

## Discussion in the show

Episode 009, chapter 4 ("软件和游戏网文化与实践" — [the web-novelization of software and games](/weekly/009/transcript#chapter-04)), includes Guizang recounting that he had DeepSeek research how the two web-novel platforms Qidian and Fanqie filter content. He relays the findings: Fanqie's moderation "can't keep up," with hundreds of thousands of books published per day, and both platforms no longer gate publishing but gate distribution — ["they don't recommend you. They gate the distribution, they don't recommend you"](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-dd8bc8eae33f18b3c5bf) (the research retelling starts [in the preceding passage](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-536670957ad5f3f7beb4)). He describes both platforms as leaving publishing un-gated, with the difference lying in recommendation and distribution — publishing is automated, and platforms gate distribution by not recommending. This is one participant's account of a research exercise, not data disclosed by the platform.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Fanqie Novel?

An online-literature platform owned by ByteDance, launched in November 2019, centered on free reading monetized by advertising; see the [official site](https://fanqienovel.com).

### Is Fanqie Novel free or paid?

For readers it is mainly free with advertising; the platform later introduced paid-revenue-related mechanisms around original works. Exact reading and purchasing formats follow the official documentation.

### How do Fanqie Novel and Qidian relate?

They are independent web-novel platforms often compared with each other: Qidian started earlier and is known for paid reading, while Fanqie launched in 2019 and expanded on a free model. In episode 009, Guizang names both together when discussing how platforms filter content once enormous volumes arrive.

### Can writers make money on Fanqie Novel?

Yes — from 2022 the platform increased original-content investment and added paid revenue (per Chinese Wikipedia), and writers earn from their works; signing terms, revenue sharing, and performance rules are documented at the official author entry.

### What does "Fanqie's moderation can't keep up" mean in the show?

It refers to Guizang's relayed research impression: AI brought a flood of new works — hundreds of thousands of books published per day — outpacing moderation. It is a participant's retelling, not an official operating figure from Fanqie.
