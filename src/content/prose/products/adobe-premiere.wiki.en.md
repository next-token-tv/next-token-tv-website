## Adobe Premiere Pro

Premiere Pro is [Adobe](/en/wiki/brands/adobe)’s professional video editing software, part of the Creative Cloud subscription family. Adobe positions it as professional video editing software that sits alongside its imaging and motion tools in a video workflow. Features, naming, and plans change across releases; check the [official product page](https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere.html) before relying on details.

## Usage and boundaries

Premiere Pro covers the editing pipeline from footage organization and timeline work to final delivery. Adobe’s page lists individual, business, and students-and-teachers plans. It handles editing and sequencing; motion graphics and visual effects are usually done together with [After Effects](/en/wiki/products/after-effects). Plan contents, system requirements, and pricing belong on the [official page](https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere.html).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the relation between Premiere Pro and “PR”?

“PR” is a common shorthand for Premiere Pro in Chinese-speaking communities. The official product name is Adobe Premiere Pro.

### Where can I download Premiere Pro?

Install it from the [official product page](https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere.html) or through the Creative Cloud desktop app; follow the official installation steps.

### Is Premiere Pro free, and how is it priced?

It is sold through Creative Cloud subscriptions with individual, business, and students-and-teachers plans. Plan contents and prices are listed on the [official page](https://www.adobe.com/products/premiere.html).

### What is the difference between Premiere Pro and After Effects?

Premiere Pro is primarily for editing and finishing a video; After Effects is primarily for motion graphics and visual effects. They are commonly used together — see the [Premiere Pro](/en/wiki/products/adobe-premiere) and [After Effects](/en/wiki/products/after-effects) entries.
