## What Debian is

Debian is a free, general-purpose operating system created and maintained by the Debian Project, a worldwide volunteer community. The project was initiated by Ian Murdock on August 16, 1993, making it one of the oldest Linux distributions still in active development and the base of many others (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debian)). The official introduction is at [debian.org/intro/about](https://www.debian.org/intro/about).

## Community and governance

Debian is not owned by a company; it is developed by volunteers around the world. In 1997 the project published the Debian Social Contract and the Debian Free Software Guidelines (DFSG), committing the system's components to free software and setting out how the project makes decisions (see the [Social Contract page](https://www.debian.org/social_contract)). Per the official introduction, Debian systems use the Linux kernel (a FreeBSD kernel variant has existed historically), with much of the base tooling from the GNU project — hence the name GNU/Linux.

Many distributions are built directly on Debian, most prominently Ubuntu; these downstream distributions inherit Debian's packaging system.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Debian?

A free operating system maintained by a volunteer community, initiated in 1993 by Ian Murdock, running the Linux kernel and installable on servers and ordinary computers. See the [official introduction](https://www.debian.org/intro/about).

### Is Debian free?

Per the official introduction, every component of the system is free software; the Debian Social Contract and DFSG, published in 1997, set that commitment out (see the [Social Contract](https://www.debian.org/social_contract)).

### How are Debian and Ubuntu related?

Ubuntu is a distribution built on Debian and inherits its packaging system; English Wikipedia describes Debian as "the base of many others."

### Who maintains Debian?

The Debian Project, a global volunteer community. It belongs to no single company, and its governance is documented in the official Social Contract and project documents.
