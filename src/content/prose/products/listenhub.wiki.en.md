## What ListenHub is

ListenHub is an AI content creation platform by the AI application company MarsWave. Its official site is [listenhub.ai](https://listenhub.ai/). Per the site, you start from an idea, a link, or a document and finish with publishable audio, images, slides, and video. The same company also builds the desktop AI assistant [ColaOS](/en/wiki/products/colaos); brand and product information is at [marswave.ai](https://marswave.ai/).

MarsWave's founder is Orange (橘子), one of the four co-hosts of [Next Token Weekly](/en/wiki/products/next-token-weekly); the relationship is recorded on this site's [person entry](/en/wiki/people/orange).

## Main capabilities

The official site groups its capabilities into several areas:

- AI Podcast: turn a topic, link, or document into a finished show with script, voice casting, and voiceover, in one- or two-host formats.
- Audio tools: text to speech, multi-speaker voiceover, audio to text, and voice cloning.
- AI Image: generate covers and product shots from prompts or reference images, with area-selective edits.
- AI video and explainer videos: short films from scripts, photos, or reference clips; the site notes it draws on models such as Seedance.
- Slides: decks with speaker notes, exportable as PPTX, video, or audio.
- Developer tools: integration via CLI, JavaScript SDK, OpenAPI, MCP Server, and Agent Skills.

Current capabilities follow the [official site](https://listenhub.ai/).

## Pricing

The site describes a free-to-start model: signing up grants free credits without a credit card, while paid plans add credits, larger uploads, higher image resolution, more voice-clone slots, and permission for commercial use of generated content. Current plans and quotas are on the [official site](https://listenhub.ai/).
