## What Canva is

Canva is an Australian software company and its online graphic design platform, founded in Perth, Australia, on January 1, 2013, by Melanie Perkins, Cliff Obrecht, and Cameron Adams (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canva)). The platform uses a drag-and-drop interface and templates to create presentations, posters, social media graphics, and other visual content, with photo and video editing built in, on a freemium model. The company overview is at [canva.com/about](https://www.canva.com/about); the edition for mainland China is named 可画 and lives at [canva.cn](https://www.canva.cn/).

## Timeline of AI features

As recorded on English Wikipedia from the company's releases:

- December 7, 2022: Magic Write, the AI copywriting assistant.
- March 22, 2023: the Assistant tool, which recommends graphics and styles matching the user's existing design.
- January 11, 2024: a Canva GPT listed in OpenAI's GPT Store.
- April 10, 2025: Visual Suite 2, including Canva Sheets and the generative coding assistant Canva Code.
- April 2026: Canva AI 2.0 in research preview, adding agentic tools that schedule and perform recurring design tasks in the background.

Per English Wikipedia, in August 2026 Canva lowered its expected annual revenue-growth rate from 30% to 20%, saying the unexpectedly high cost of serving its AI features had led it to slow their rollout while rebuilding the underlying architecture.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Canva?

An online design and publishing platform that builds presentations, posters, social graphics, and video from drag-and-drop templates, run by the Australian company of the same name. See the [official overview](https://www.canva.com/about).

### What is 可画?

The name of Canva's edition for mainland China, at [canva.cn](https://www.canva.cn/). Canva itself was founded in Australia and is not a Chinese domestic product.

### Is Canva free?

It runs on a freemium model: core features are free and advanced features require a subscription; current plans are on the official pricing page.

### What AI features does Canva have?

Per its release history on English Wikipedia: Magic Write (December 2022), Assistant recommendations (March 2023), a Canva GPT (January 2024), Visual Suite 2 and Canva Code (April 2025), and Canva AI 2.0 in research preview (April 2026).

### Is Canva an Australian company?

Yes. It was founded in Perth, Australia, in 2013 by Melanie Perkins, Cliff Obrecht, and Cameron Adams (per English Wikipedia).
