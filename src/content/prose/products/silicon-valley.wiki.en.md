## What Silicon Valley is

Silicon Valley is an American comedy series produced by HBO. The official description credits the show to the offbeat mind of Mike Judge (director of Office Space) and sets it in the world of Silicon Valley tech startups, following the startup Pied Piper. The official page lists the series as premiering in 2014 with six seasons, starring Thomas Middleditch, T.J. Miller, Zach Woods, Kumail Nanjiani, Martin Starr, Jimmy O. Yang and others.

On this site the entry is filed under products and refers to the TV series, not the geographic region of Silicon Valley.

## Main characters and where to watch

The character Jian-Yang is played by Jimmy O. Yang, whose name appears in the official page's starring list. Series information, episode lists, and viewing options are on the [official HBO series page](https://www.hbo.com/silicon-valley); the currently available platform and region follow that page.
