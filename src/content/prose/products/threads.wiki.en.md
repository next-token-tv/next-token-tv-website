## What Threads is

Threads is [Meta](/en/wiki/brands/meta)’s social platform for text sharing and public conversations, built by the Instagram team and launched in its initial version on July 5, 2023, rolling out that day in more than 100 countries on iOS and Android. Meta’s official announcement describes it as "a new app, built by the Instagram team, for sharing text updates and joining public conversations": users log in with their [Instagram](/en/wiki/products/instagram) account, the username and verification carry over, posts run up to 500 characters with links and photos, and videos up to 5 minutes long. On safety, Threads enforces Instagram’s Community Guidelines, and accounts blocked on Instagram are automatically blocked on Threads. All of this comes from [Meta’s official announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/07/introducing-threads-new-app-text-sharing/).

The same page records later updates, including fediverse interoperability via ActivityPub, a web experience, and keyword search. Threads sits alongside [Facebook](/en/wiki/products/facebook) and WhatsApp in Meta’s social portfolio but follows a public, text-first conversation format, which is why it is frequently compared to [X](/en/wiki/products/x) (formerly Twitter).

## Usage and boundaries

Threads is used through [threads.net](https://www.threads.net/) and the mobile apps, and its account system is tied to Instagram — there is no registration flow independent of Instagram. Content is organized around text posts with photos, videos, and links, assembled into feeds of public conversations, replies, and reposts. With fediverse interoperability enabled, posts can move across servers that support the ActivityPub protocol; the exact switches and scope follow in-app settings and official documentation.

Governance and data handling follow Meta’s account system: community rules come directly from Instagram, and account actions are linked across the two apps. Comparisons with X and other platforms — positioning, moderation rules, user scale — are time-sensitive and are not cited here as fact; official data lives at the [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What app is Threads?

Threads is Meta’s text-focused social platform for sharing updates and joining public conversations, built by the Instagram team and launched on July 5, 2023. See [Meta’s official announcement](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/07/introducing-threads-new-app-text-sharing/).

### Do you need an Instagram account to sign up for Threads?

Yes. The official announcement states that you "simply use your Instagram account to log in," and your Instagram username and verification carry over. There is no registration path separate from Instagram.

### How does Threads differ from X (Twitter)?

Both are platforms built around public text conversations, but Threads is operated by Meta, tied to Instagram accounts, and governed by Instagram’s Community Guidelines. Comparisons of user scale depend on the metric and the date; official figures live at the [Meta Newsroom](https://about.fb.com/).

### What is the fediverse on Threads?

The fediverse is a network of social servers connected by open protocols such as ActivityPub. Meta’s official announcement lists fediverse interoperability via ActivityPub among Threads’ later updates; when enabled, Threads posts can interact with other services that support the protocol, subject to in-app settings.

### Where can I get Threads?

The official entry is [threads.net](https://www.threads.net/); the mobile apps are available on the iOS and Android app stores (the official announcement describes the July 2023 rollout on both platforms). Sign-in uses an Instagram account.
