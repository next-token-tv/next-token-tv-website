## What Lovable is

Lovable is a Swedish company and its namesake AI app-building platform: users describe the product they want in natural language, and the platform generates an app or website from it — the official tagline is "If you can describe it, you can build it". The company was founded in Stockholm in 2023, and its origin is the open-source project GPT Engineer: Anton Osika created GPT Engineer in 2023, then with Fabian Hedin built the commercial GPT Engineer App, which was renamed Lovable in December 2024, according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lovable_(company)).

## What it does and where it stops

Per the [official site](https://lovable.dev/), the flow starts from a description, continues with iterating on design and features through conversation, and ends with direct deployment; the platform provides infrastructure such as hosting, SSL, authentication, and a backend, and integrates with existing tech stacks. The company also emphasizes that "Your code and data stay yours" — code and data belong to the user and can be exported into one's own workflows.

It targets the full path from idea to launch: product prototypes, internal tools, and customer-facing sites can all be built with it. Generated results still deserve normal software scrutiny — data protection, permission configuration, and security review remain the user's responsibility, and automatic deployment does not remove those steps.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Lovable?

An AI development platform for building apps and websites from natural-language descriptions: describe the need, generate the product, iterate through conversation, and deploy directly. The official entry point is [lovable.dev](https://lovable.dev/).

### Where is the Lovable company from?

The company was founded in Stockholm, Sweden, in 2023; its predecessor was the 2023 open-source project GPT Engineer, whose commercial version was renamed Lovable in December 2024, per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lovable_(company)).

### How do GPT Engineer and Lovable relate?

GPT Engineer is Lovable's predecessor: the open-source project came first, and its commercial version, the GPT Engineer App, was renamed into today's Lovable. The open-source project continues with its own community ecosystem.

### How much does Lovable cost?

Plans and prices follow the [official pricing page](https://lovable.dev/pricing); the differences in generation usage and features between free and paid tiers change with product strategy.

### How does Lovable differ from coding tools like Cursor or Claude Code?

The positioning differs: Lovable's official description runs from a natural-language description to a complete, hosted application, covering deployment; Cursor and [Claude Code](/en/wiki/products/claude-code) embed into a developer's existing codebase and workflow. Lovable suits starting quickly from an idea, the others suit continued development inside an existing engineering setup.
