## Definition

Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA VI, commonly GTA 6) is an open-world action-adventure game developed and published by Rockstar Games. Its officially announced release date is November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The story follows Jason and Lucia in the state of Leonida, based on Florida, which includes the Miami-inspired Vice City. Pre-release information is maintained on the [official page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI).

## Series and releases

The game is the eighth main entry in the Grand Theft Auto series, following 2013's Grand Theft Auto V. It was formally revealed in December 2023; the release plan was subsequently revised twice, and the officially announced target date is November 19, 2026.

## Usage

Public material describes a third-person, open-world action-adventure game played as Jason or Lucia: players roam Leonida, drive vehicles, explore its different regions and advance the story. Further gameplay details should be taken from Rockstar's official releases.

## Related and confusable terms

GTA VI, GTA 6 and GTA6 all refer to Grand Theft Auto VI; VI is the Roman numeral for six. It is the next mainline entry in the series, not an expansion or update to 2013's Grand Theft Auto V (GTA V, GTA 5).

## Frequently asked questions

### When does GTA 6 come out?

The officially announced release date is November 19, 2026, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S; see the [Rockstar official page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI).

### Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

The platforms Rockstar has officially announced are PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PC version has been announced; such claims are not confirmed by official channels, so follow official announcements.

### Are GTA VI and GTA 6 the same game?

Yes. VI is the Roman numeral for six; both names refer to Grand Theft Auto VI.
