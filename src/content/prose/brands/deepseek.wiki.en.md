## DeepSeek

DeepSeek is the AI company behind the DeepSeek model family, developer APIs, and agent tools. Its official home page lists DeepSeek Web, DeepSeek App, DeepSeek Harness, the API Platform, and API Docs as product and developer entry points.

## Products and developer entry points

Users can access the Web or App through the [official product entry](https://www.deepseek.com/), build applications with the [API Platform](https://www.deepseek.com/platform/), and check current interfaces in the [API documentation](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/). Released models, technical reports, and model cards are collected in the [Transparency Center](https://www.deepseek.com/en/transparency/). Features, prices, and release status should be read from those official pages as they change.

Related entries include [the DeepSeek model family](/en/wiki/products/deepseek) and [DeepSeek Harness](/en/wiki/products/deepseek-harness). The model family page covers model and API boundaries; the Harness page covers a coding-agent framework.

## Frequently asked questions

### What company is behind DeepSeek, and what is its registered name?

DeepSeek develops AI models and related services. Its Chinese website names 杭州深度求索人工智能基础技术研究有限公司 as the company; the [official terms](https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/zh-CN/deepseek-terms-of-use.html) describe that entity and its affiliates as service operators.

### What country is DeepSeek from, and where was it founded?

DeepSeek is a Chinese company founded in Hangzhou in 2023, as reported in the [Associated Press company overview](https://www.ap.org/news-highlights/spotlights/2025/what-is-deepseek-the-chinese-ai-company-upending-the-stock-market/).

### What is DeepSeek’s official company website?

The official website is [deepseek.com](https://www.deepseek.com/), with links to its products, research, developer documentation, and careers. See the [model profile](/en/wiki/products/deepseek) for product access and API questions.
