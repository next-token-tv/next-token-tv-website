## What WaytoAGI is

WaytoAGI, known in Chinese as "通往 AGI 之路" (WTA for short), is an open knowledge base and co-learning community for Chinese-speaking AI learners, creators, and enterprise practitioners. Its official introduction frames the goal as helping more people "少走弯路，因 AI 而强大" (avoid detours and become stronger with AI), organizing scattered AI knowledge into "一条人人都能走、也能一起修的路" (a road anyone can walk and help maintain). It is not a conventional company: the knowledge base is a free, open public asset, while commercial cooperation and training services are run separately by the team and clearly labeled.

## Work and main projects

- **Open knowledge base**: the core public infrastructure is a searchable knowledge base hosted on Feishu, which the official introduction puts at more than 10,000 searchable items; the [main site](https://www.waytoagi.com/) also aggregates a tools directory, daily knowledge-base picks, and an open-source articles site.
- **Co-learning and events**: a nightly 8 p.m. public study session, city chapters, hackathons, AI video competitions, physical spaces, and overseas nodes. Cumulative figures on the official introduction page (as of July 2026) include more than 10 million visitors and over 600 events.
- **How it runs**: the official FAQ describes it as starting as a community, "由创始人与一批共建者、志愿者共同运转" (run by the founder together with co-builders and volunteers).

WaytoAGI was initiated by [AJ](/en/wiki/people/aj). Per the official introduction, AJ began saving ChatGPT-related articles and tools into a Feishu document in 2022, opened it to the public in 2023, and readers began contributing corrections, turning "个人笔记逐渐成为一群人共同维护的知识库" (personal notes into a knowledge base maintained by a group).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is WaytoAGI?

WaytoAGI (通往 AGI 之路) is an open AI knowledge base and co-learning community. Its core asset is a free, searchable knowledge base hosted on Feishu; see the [main site](https://www.waytoagi.com/) and the [official introduction](https://about.waytoagi.com/).

### Is WaytoAGI a company, and who founded it?

It is not a conventional company; the official FAQ says it is run by its founder together with co-builders and volunteers. The knowledge base is free, while training camps, certifications, and enterprise services are labeled as commercial offerings. [AJ](/en/wiki/people/aj) is the initiator; the project grew out of a personal Feishu document started in 2022.

### Where is the WaytoAGI knowledge base?

The main entry is the "WaytoAGI Wiki" link (a Feishu document) on the [official site](https://www.waytoagi.com/), which also carries the tools directory, blog, and event information.
