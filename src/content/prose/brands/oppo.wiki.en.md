## OPPO

OPPO is a Chinese consumer electronics brand. The company was founded on October 10, 2004, and is headquartered in Shenzhen, Guangdong, formally as OPPO Guangdong Mobile Telecommunications Corp., Ltd. ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OPPO)). The official site presents the business as smartphones, IoT devices, and related software services ([oppo.com](https://www.oppo.com/)).

## Business and main product lines

- **Smartphones**: the core business, with the Find series, Reno series, A series, and the Find N foldable line ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OPPO)).
- **Operating system**: ColorOS, an Android-based system, runs on its phones.
- **IoT and accessories**: a smartwatch line starting with the OPPO Watch in March 2020, plus tablets and audio products.
- **Affiliated brands**: OnePlus and realme are brands under OPPO.

Time-sensitive details such as current models, regional availability, and prices should be read from the official store.

## Frequently asked questions

### What company is OPPO, and where is it from?

OPPO is a Chinese consumer electronics brand founded in October 2004 and headquartered in Shenzhen. Its main products are smartphones, alongside wearables, tablets, and audio devices.

### Where is the OPPO website?

The official site is [oppo.com](https://www.oppo.com/), with separate sites per country and region; purchasing, service, and model information are on the regional sites.

### Which phone series does OPPO sell?

According to the Wikipedia article, OPPO's phone lines include the Find series (with the Find N foldable line), the Reno series, and the A series; for currently available models, see the [official store](https://www.oppo.com/).

### What is the relationship between OPPO and OnePlus?

OnePlus and realme are brands under OPPO ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OPPO)).
