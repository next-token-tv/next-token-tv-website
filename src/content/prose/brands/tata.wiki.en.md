## Definition

An India-based business group operating across manufacturing, technology and consumer services. See [official source](https://www.tata.com/about-us).

## Business and main product lines

According to the [group's official introduction](https://www.tata.com/about-us), Jamsetji Tata set up a trading firm in 1868 and the group is headquartered at Bombay House in Mumbai. It operates across ten verticals including technology, steel, automotive, consumer and retail, infrastructure, financial services, aerospace and defence, tourism and travel, telecom and media, and trading and investments; revenues exceeded 180 billion US dollars in 2024–25, and the combined market capitalization of Tata companies was 328 billion US dollars as of March 31, 2025, with 66% of Tata Sons owned by trusts. [Tata Electronics](https://www.tata.com/business/tata-electronics), established in 2020, is the group's electronics and semiconductor manufacturing company, spanning electronics manufacturing services, semiconductor assembly and test, foundry and design services, with operations in Gujarat, Assam, Tamil Nadu and Karnataka.

## Use cases

Consumers encounter Tata through consumer and industrial products (vehicles, steel, IT services) and through the electronics supply chain: Tata Electronics provides manufacturing services for global electronics brands, and the group is a major participant in India's semiconductor manufacturing projects.

## Common confusions

- Tata Group vs. Tata Electronics: the former is the parent group; the latter is its electronics and semiconductor manufacturing company established in 2020. "Indian factories" in the show's topic refers to manufacturing businesses of this kind.
- The certificate incident vs. Tata: the weak-randomness Indian certificate issue discussed in the same chapter concerns certificate issuers and is unrelated to Tata.
