## What Google is

Google is a technology company that started with web search and now spans search, advertising, video, operating systems, browsers, cloud services, and AI products. Since the 2015 Alphabet restructuring, Google has been a wholly-owned subsidiary of Alphabet, and Alphabet replaced Google Inc. as the publicly traded entity. Google's mission, as stated on the official How Search Works site, is to "organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful."

## Product lines and AI footprint

The official product directory groups the business into AI, search, ads and commerce, cloud and developer, hardware, and media and communication:

- AI: [Gemini](/en/wiki/products/gemini), the personal AI assistant, plus products like Notebook and Flow; developers create API keys in [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/) and call the Gemini API.
- Search and information: Google Search (including AI Mode), Google Maps, Google News, and Google Scholar.
- Media and communication: YouTube, Gmail, [Chrome](/en/wiki/products/chrome), and Google Photos; the Alphabet founders' letter also names Android among the billion-user products.
- Cloud and developer: Google Cloud, Firebase, and AI for Developers.
- Hardware: Pixel phones, Pixel Watch, Nest, and Fitbit.

On the research side, Google DeepMind is Google's frontier AI research lab. Its official page states the mission of building AI responsibly to benefit humanity, with outputs including Gemini, Veo, and AlphaFold.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is Google's official website?

The search entry point is [google.com](https://www.google.com/); company information and the product directory are on [about.google](https://about.google/).

### How do Google and Alphabet relate?

After the 2015 restructuring, Google became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Alphabet, and Alphabet replaced Google Inc. as the publicly traded entity. See the [Alphabet founders' letter](https://abc.xyz/).

### What are Google's main products?

Search, YouTube, Android, Chrome, Gmail, Google Maps, Google Cloud, Workspace, Pixel hardware, and Gemini are all listed in the [official product directory](https://about.google/products/), organized by AI, search, cloud, hardware, and other categories.

### What are Google's AI products and developer entry points?

For users: the [Gemini](/en/wiki/products/gemini) assistant at [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com/). For developers: the Gemini API on [Google AI for Developers](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models) and [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/). Enterprise services live on Google Cloud.

### What is Google's mission?

The official How Search Works site states: "Google's mission is to organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful." See the [Our approach to Search page](https://www.google.com/search/howsearchworks/mission/).
