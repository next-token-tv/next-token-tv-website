## Ant Group

Ant Group is a technology company that grew out of [Alipay](/en/wiki/products/alipay) and is headquartered in Hangzhou, Zhejiang. Alipay started in 2004 as Alibaba's payment tool; in 2014, Alipay and its related financial services were formed into the independent company Ant Financial, which was renamed Ant Group in July 2020 (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant_Group)). The company's businesses cover payments and digital daily-life services, expanding into consumer credit, wealth management, and international payments; since 2025 it has released the Ling model family and AI applications such as Lingguang and Ant Afu.

## Business and product lines

- Payments and daily life: [Alipay](/en/wiki/products/alipay) offers payments, mini programs, and other everyday services, and is the company's main business vehicle.
- Credit and wealth management: consumer credit products such as Huabei and Jiebei, wealth services such as Yu'e Bao, and MYbank.
- International: Ant International serves cross-border and overseas scenarios, including payment solutions such as Alipay+.
- AI applications: [Lingguang](/en/wiki/products/lingguang) is Ant Group's multimodal AI assistant; the [official press release](https://www.antgroup.com/news-media/press-releases/1763452800000) shows its app launched on November 18, 2025. [Ant Afu](/en/wiki/products/ant-afu) is an AI health app, renamed from AQ in December 2025. On models, English Wikipedia records that Ant released the Ling-Lite and Ling-Plus models in March 2025.

The company profile is on the [official page](https://www.antgroup.com/en/home).

## Common confusions

- Ant Group vs. Alibaba: two different companies. Alipay was originally created by Alibaba, and the related businesses became the independent Ant Financial in 2014; the two have operated separately since, so attributing the Alipay app to Alibaba is a common misreading.
- Lingguang vs. Qianwen Go: Lingguang is Ant Group's product; [Qianwen Go](/en/wiki/products/qianwen-go) comes from Alibaba. The two products were discussed side by side in episode 006, but they belong to different companies.

## Frequently asked questions

### How are Ant Group and Alibaba related?

They are separate companies. Alipay started in 2004 as Alibaba's payment tool, the related businesses became the independent Ant Financial in 2014, and the company was renamed Ant Group in July 2020 (per English Wikipedia); the two have operated separately since.

### Is Ant Group Alipay's parent company?

Yes. Ant Group grew out of [Alipay](/en/wiki/products/alipay), which remains the main vehicle for its payments and digital daily-life services; the [official company profile](https://www.antgroup.com/en/home) also takes Alipay as the starting point of the business.

### Is Lingguang an Ant Group product?

Yes. [Lingguang](/en/wiki/products/lingguang) is Ant Group's multimodal AI assistant; the [official press release](https://www.antgroup.com/news-media/press-releases/1763452800000) shows its app launched on November 18, 2025. It is often called "蚂蚁灵光" (Ant Lingguang) and is not an Alibaba product like [Qianwen Go](/en/wiki/products/qianwen-go).

### What is the relation between Ant Afu and AQ?

Two names for the same app. [Ant Afu](/en/wiki/products/ant-afu) is Ant Group's AI health app, renamed from AQ in December 2025, offering health Q&A, report explanations, and access to health services.

### Did Ant Group go public?

In November 2020, Ant's planned dual IPO on the Shanghai Stock Exchange's STAR Market and the Hong Kong Stock Exchange was suspended by the Shanghai exchange two days before listing, and the Hong Kong leg was suspended soon after (per English Wikipedia). Listing arrangements should be followed on the [company's official website](https://www.antgroup.com/).
