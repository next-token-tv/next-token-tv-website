## Definition

A video-production team and channel creating filmmaking, technology and creative content. See the [official channel](https://www.youtube.com/@mediastorm6801).

## Business and main product lines

Mediastorm (影视飓风) is a video team publishing on Bilibili and YouTube, covering filmmaking, consumer tech and science content. According to Chinese Wikipedia, its main channel is operated by Hangzhou Xing'ao Media Co., Ltd.; founder Tim Pan (潘天鸿) began posting videos in 2015, returned to China after graduating in 2018 to build his studio, and co-founded the operating company. The team also runs sibling channels such as the science channel "亿点点不一样" (A Little Different), and was named one of Bilibili's "Top 100 Creators" every year from 2019 to 2025. In 2024, its video on declining video-platform compression quality, "清晰度不如4年前！", was taken down platform-wide the day after release, drawing wide discussion. Current channel information is on the [official channel](https://www.youtube.com/@mediastorm6801).

## Use cases

Viewers watch its filmmaking, tech review and science videos on Bilibili and YouTube; its high-production-value videos are also frequently cited as a case study of industrialized Chinese video production.

## Discussion on the show

In episode 007's chapter "自己造剪辑工具：自动化与渲染的等待" (Building your own editing tools: automation and waiting for renders), Guizang says while discussing automation in content production: "我们都说看影视飓风，影视飓风的优势就是它的质量高……但它核心就是它有一些视觉奇观。那这个东西我觉得是会很快被消解，被 AI 消解掉了这部分" ([see the line](/weekly/007/transcript#quote-50b4f807615ccc7fc12d) in the Chinese transcript). This is a host's personal view of industry trends, not a statement by Mediastorm; see [episode 007's chapter](/weekly/007/transcript#chapter-05), linked transcript is in Chinese.

## Common confusions

- Mediastorm vs. [Mediastorm Tim](/en/wiki/people/mediastorm-tim): the former is the team and channel; the latter is founder Tim Pan, listed separately on this wiki.
- Main channel vs. sibling channels: "亿点点不一样" and similar channels belong to the same team; this wiki has no separate entries for them.
