## Xinhua News Agency

Xinhua News Agency (新华通讯社) is the state news agency of the People's Republic of China, headquartered in Beijing. Its predecessor, the Red China News Agency (红色中华通讯社), was founded in 1931 and renamed Xinhua in 1937. The agency supplies real-time text news, photos and graphics, video reporting, and economic information services to media organizations, and operates its website and news portal [news.cn](https://www.news.cn/).

## Services

Xinhua's core business is news supply to media organizations: text wire copy, photos and graphics, audio and video content, and economic information services for institutions. Its reporting and style standards are widely referenced across Chinese newsrooms. The news.cn portal aggregates its reporting and information services.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Xinhua News Agency?

Xinhua is the state news agency of the People's Republic of China, providing text, photo, video, and economic information services to media organizations in China and abroad, per [news.cn](https://www.news.cn/).

### What is the history of Xinhua?

Its predecessor, the Red China News Agency, was founded in 1931 and was renamed Xinhua News Agency in 1937; the agency is headquartered in Beijing.

### What is the Xinhua website?

The official website and news portal is [news.cn](https://www.news.cn/), which carries Xinhua's text, photo, and video reporting.
