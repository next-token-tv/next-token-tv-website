## GeekPark

GeekPark (极客公园) is a Chinese technology media and community organization founded in 2010; its own site calls it “the home base for China’s innovators.” It is operated by Beijing Qianyan Jike Management Consulting Co., Ltd. and based in Beijing. GeekPark runs three business lines — technology news media, conference and PR services, and startup services — serving technologists and founders.

## Business and main events

According to the [official About page](https://www.geekpark.net/about/):

- **News media**: geekpark.net publishes front-line technology reporting with its own editorial team, plus vertical brands such as 极客之选, which covers smart hardware.
- **Conference and PR services**: planning and production of product launches and summits for technology companies.
- **Startup services**: 极客加速 (Geek Accelerator), launched in 2015, offering fundraising and incubation support for early-stage projects.

Offline, GeekPark has run a series of event formats since 2010: the annual industry summit 极客公园创新大会 (GeekPark Innovation Festival, GIF), the 奇点·创新者峰会 held since summer 2014, the monthly 未来头条 that spotlights innovative products, and the 极客公开课 series held once or twice a month. The official About page lists past speakers including Steve Wozniak, Eric Schmidt, Elon Musk, Robin Li, and Lei Jun.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is GeekPark?

GeekPark is a Chinese technology media and community organization founded in 2010. It runs geekpark.net, reports on innovative companies and products, and connects founders with industry figures through events such as its innovation conference.

### Where is GeekPark’s official website?

The official website is [geekpark.net](https://www.geekpark.net/); the company introduction is on the [About page](https://www.geekpark.net/about/).

### What is the GeekPark Innovation Festival?

The GeekPark Innovation Festival (GIF) is GeekPark’s annual technology summit, which the official introduction describes as gathering leading internet figures from China and abroad. Details of past editions are on the [GeekPark website](https://www.geekpark.net/).
