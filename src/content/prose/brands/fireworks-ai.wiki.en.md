## Company overview

Fireworks AI is an AI infrastructure company serving developers and enterprises. Its business spans training, inference and deployment of open models, providing infrastructure and services for putting models to work in business applications. [Official website](https://fireworks.ai/)

## Founding team

Co-founder and CEO Lin Qiao previously led PyTorch at Meta. The founding team also includes people who worked on PyTorch core maintenance, compilers, machine-learning infrastructure and Google Vertex AI. [Founding team](https://fireworks.ai/team)

## Business focus

The company provides training and inference infrastructure to help development teams apply open models and their own data to specialized business needs. Its portfolio also includes Fireworks Nexus, a model-routing product. These business areas support companies building and running AI applications. [Official website](https://fireworks.ai/)

## Discussion in the show

In Weekly #004’s chapter “Harvey: Open-Source Models and Product Moats,” [Yang Pan notes that many Silicon Valley companies are moving to their own models, “including Fireworks pushing these efforts,” in the Chinese transcript](/weekly/004/transcript#quote-be20aa68704a5ecebd0b). His argument: swapping frontier models for open ones to cut costs suits top companies like Harvey that have already validated their business loop and market position, not every startup; Guizang adds that closed verticals such as law carry stronger barriers. This is participant judgment about industry practice, not an assessment of Fireworks’ products.
