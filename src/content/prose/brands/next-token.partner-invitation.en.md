We welcome new spaces for conversation. If you run a bookstore, café, bar, community space, or innovation hub and would like to bring people together around AI, products, and creativity, we would love to explore a venue partnership with you.

A collaboration could begin with a Next Token Weekly roundtable recording. We also welcome ideas for gatherings that suit your space. The format, timing, and venue arrangements can be discussed together.

Reach out through our co-hosts’ public contact channels. Please include your city, a description or photos of the space, its capacity, and the kind of collaboration you have in mind so we can continue the conversation.
