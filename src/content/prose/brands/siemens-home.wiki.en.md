## Siemens Home Appliances

Siemens Home Appliances is a home appliance brand run by the German company BSH Hausgeräte GmbH. BSH was founded in May 1967 as a joint venture between Robert Bosch GmbH and Siemens AG under the name Bosch-Siemens Hausgeräte; in September 2014, Bosch agreed to acquire Siemens' 50% stake, and BSH became part of the Bosch Group. The Siemens Home Appliances website footer states "BSH Group is a Trademark Licensee of Siemens AG" — the brand is operated by BSH under license from Siemens AG.

## Product lines

BSH's product range covers large appliances for cooking, dishwashing, laundry (washing, drying, and folding), and refrigeration and freezing, plus a range of small appliances; the Siemens Home Appliances brand includes lines such as studioLine, with product entry points on the [international site](https://www.siemens-home.bsh-group.com/). Bosch home appliances sit under the same BSH umbrella, sharing manufacturing and service infrastructure while the brands are positioned separately.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the relationship between Siemens Home Appliances and Siemens AG?

The Siemens Home Appliances brand is run by BSH, a home appliance company owned by the Bosch Group; the brand's website footer states that BSH is a trademark licensee of Siemens AG. In other words, the brand uses the "Siemens" trademark, but the operating company is BSH.

### What is the relationship between Siemens Home Appliances and Bosch home appliances?

Both come from BSH: founded in 1967 as a Bosch–Siemens joint venture, BSH became a wholly owned Bosch Group subsidiary after Bosch agreed in September 2014 to buy Siemens' 50% stake, and it runs the Bosch and Siemens appliance brands alongside others.

### Where is the Siemens Home Appliances website?

The international site is [siemens-home.bsh-group.com](https://www.siemens-home.bsh-group.com/); regional sales and service entry points are reached by selecting a country or region from that page.
