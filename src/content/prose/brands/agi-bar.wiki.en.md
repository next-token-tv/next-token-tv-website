## AGI Bar

AGI Bar is an in-person gathering space for AI practitioners and founders, with venues in Beijing and Shanghai, and is a venue partner of Next Token. The official site positions it as "全球首家 AI 主理酒吧" (the world's first AI-managed bar) and describes it as "an AI store by day, a bar by night" ([agi.bar](https://agi.bar/)). The Beijing Zhongguancun venue is its first permanent space, on Zhongguancun Startup Street in Haidian; [Next Token's venue partner page](https://nexttoken.tv/partners/agi-bar) records its opening in June 2025.

## Venues and activity formats

- **Beijing Zhongguancun**: on Zhongguancun Startup Street, Haidian West Street, Haidian District; open daily 15:00–23:00 ([official venue page](https://agi.bar/)).
- **Shanghai Beiyang**: in Xuhui, Shanghai, located at Sequoia China's innovation accelerator ([Next Token venue partner page](https://nexttoken.tv/partners/agi-bar)).
- **Activity formats**: the official site lists six, including product launches and cold starts, industry exchange, after parties, event IPs, a mobile bar for external events, and embodied-AI showcases.

Next Token's materials describe the space as an industry social network that is "an AI store by day and a bar by night," where practitioners, founders, and investors get immediate in-person feedback.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is AGI Bar?

AGI Bar is an in-person gathering space for AI practitioners and founders, operating as an AI store by day and a bar by night, hosting launches and industry events, with venues in Beijing and Shanghai.

### Where is AGI Bar located?

The Beijing venue is on Zhongguancun Startup Street in Haidian District, open daily 15:00–23:00; the Shanghai venue is in Beiyang, Xuhui. Addresses and transit details are on [Next Token's venue partner page](https://nexttoken.tv/partners/agi-bar).

### When did AGI Bar open?

The Beijing Zhongguancun venue, AGI Bar's first permanent space, opened in June 2025 ([Next Token venue partner page](https://nexttoken.tv/partners/agi-bar)).

### Which Next Token episode was recorded at AGI Bar?

Next Token Weekly episode 002 was recorded at AGI Bar in Shanghai; both the opening and closing chapters of that episode thank the venue.
