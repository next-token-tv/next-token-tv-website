## What Samsung is

Samsung is a South Korean technology brand spanning consumer electronics, mobile communications, and semiconductors. In the context of this page, the operating entity is Samsung Electronics. Its official company information states that Samsung Electronics was founded in 1969 and renamed Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd. in 1984. The page tag is "brand"; product entries such as Galaxy devices are maintained separately.

## Business and main product lines

Officially, the business is organized around two divisions. The Device eXperience (DX) Division covers mobile devices and consumer electronics: Galaxy smartphones (official history credits the 2019 Galaxy Fold with creating the foldable category in mobile devices), a TV business that has led the global market for years, and home appliances such as refrigerators, washers, and air conditioners. The Device Solutions (DS) Division covers semiconductors: memory (DRAM, V-NAND), system chips, and the foundry business — official history records the 1974 acquisition of a 50% stake in Korea Semiconductor to enter semiconductors, the 2017 establishment of a foundry business unit independent from the System LSI division, and the start of 3nm GAA chip production in 2022.

Beyond hardware, official pages also note that Samsung announced its generative AI model "Samsung Gauss" at the Samsung AI Forum in 2023. Current product lines and offerings can be read from the [Samsung China website](https://www.samsung.com.cn/) and the [global website](https://www.samsung.com/).

One point relevant to the show's context: foundry is the part of Samsung's semiconductor business that manufactures chips designed by other companies, putting Samsung in the same chipmaking tier as TSMC. This page does not go into process-node details or capacity figures.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is Samsung's official website?

The Samsung China website is [samsung.com.cn](https://www.samsung.com.cn/) and the global website is [samsung.com](https://www.samsung.com/). Both provide product information and company profiles.

### What company is Samsung?

"Samsung" in everyday usage usually refers to Samsung Electronics, whose official company information states it was founded in 1969, with businesses across consumer electronics, mobile communications, and semiconductors under the DX (Device eXperience) and DS (Device Solutions) divisions. The official pages do not cover Samsung-group history, so this entry does not either.

### What is Samsung Foundry?

Foundry is the part of Samsung's semiconductor business that manufactures chips for other companies. Official history records the establishment of a foundry unit independent from the System LSI division in 2017 and the start of 3nm GAA chip production in 2022. Current process and capacity information can be read from the [official company information page](https://www.samsung.com/us/about-us/company-info/).

### What are Samsung's main product lines?

Officially described product lines include Galaxy smartphones (including the foldable Galaxy Fold series), TVs, home appliances such as refrigerators and washers, and semiconductor businesses covering memory, system chips, and foundry. Current models per line can be read from the [Samsung China website](https://www.samsung.com.cn/).
