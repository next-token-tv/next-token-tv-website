## Amazon

Amazon is an American technology company founded in July 1994 by Jeff Bezos and headquartered in Seattle. It started as an online retailer and later expanded into cloud computing, digital content, and devices (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_(company))). Andy Jassy has served as CEO since July 2021.

## Business and product lines

- E-commerce: the consumer retail platform amazon.com, along with logistics and the Prime membership program.
- Cloud computing: [AWS](/en/wiki/brands/aws) (Amazon Web Services), offered as a public cloud infrastructure service since 2006 and one of the company's main profit engines; see the [AWS entry](/en/wiki/brands/aws).
- Digital services and devices: Prime Video, Amazon Music, Kindle, the Alexa voice assistant, and Echo devices.

The corporate overview and business entry points are on the [official page](https://www.aboutamazon.com/).

## Relationship with the AI company Anthropic

According to the joint announcement of November 2024, Amazon's cumulative investment in Anthropic reached 8 billion US dollars, and Anthropic named AWS its primary cloud and model-training partner (per the [English Wikipedia entry on Anthropic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic)). This makes Amazon one party in the web of capital and compute relationships around Anthropic.
