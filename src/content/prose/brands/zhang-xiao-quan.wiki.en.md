## Zhang Xiao Quan

Zhang Xiao Quan (张小泉) is a knife and scissors brand founded in 1628, the first year of the Chongzhen era of the Ming dynasty, in Hangzhou. It is operated by Zhang Xiao Quan Co., Ltd., headquartered in Hangzhou, Zhejiang. Starting from scissors and cutlery, the brand now covers knives, scissors, kitchen hardware, and household tools; its website is [zhangxiaoquan.cn](https://www.zhangxiaoquan.cn/).

## History and credentials

Per the [official brand history page](https://www.zhangxiaoquan.cn/culture/history): in the Wanli era, Zhang Sijia opened a shop named "Zhang Dalong" in Yixian, Huizhou, moving to Dajing Lane in Hangzhou around 1610; in 1628 Zhang Xiaoquan took over the shop and renamed it after himself. According to Wikipedia and other public sources, the brand won an award at the 1915 Panama–Pacific International Exposition, and in 2006 its scissors-forging technique was included in the first batch of the national intangible cultural heritage list; it also holds the Ministry of Commerce "China Time-honored Brand" designation. The Hangzhou and Shanghai companies that long disputed the trademark were merged in 2014.

In 2021, Zhang Xiao Quan Co., Ltd. listed on the ChiNext board of the Shenzhen Stock Exchange under code 301055; the knife-making smart manufacturing center project settled in Yangjiang, Guangdong in 2018 and was completed in 2021. See the [official website](https://www.zhangxiaoquan.cn/) for the brand history and product lines.
