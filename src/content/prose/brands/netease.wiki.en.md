## NetEase

NetEase, Inc. is a Chinese internet technology company founded in June 1997 by Ding Lei and headquartered in Hangzhou. It listed on NASDAQ on July 1, 2000 (ticker NTES) and completed a secondary listing on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in June 2020 (ticker 9999) ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NetEase), [NetEase official website](https://www.netease.com/)).

## Main business lines

- **Games**: NetEase Games entered online games in 2001, develops and operates its own titles, and also publishes games from partners such as Blizzard ([NetEase official website](https://www.netease.com/)).
- **Youdao**: the business brand for learning, productivity, and intelligent tools, independently listed on the NYSE in October 2019; see the [NetEase Youdao entry](/en/wiki/brands/netease-youdao).
- **NetEase Cloud Music**: a music community platform launched in April 2013, listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on December 2, 2021 (ticker 9899).
- **Other businesses**: Yanxuan (curated e-commerce, launched 2016), NetEase News (started 1998), and enterprise services.

Current status of each business should be read from the official pages.
