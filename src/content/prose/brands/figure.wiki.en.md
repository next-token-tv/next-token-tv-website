## Figure

Figure (Figure AI) is an American AI robotics company that describes itself as "the first-of-its-kind AI robotics company bringing a general purpose humanoid to life." It develops general-purpose humanoid robots and the embodied AI models that control them. The company was founded in 2022 by Brett Adcock and is headquartered in San Jose, California (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure_AI)).

## Robots and models

Figure’s hardware has iterated through three generations: Figure 01 took its first steps in May 2023, Figure 02 improved hand dexterity and integrated a torso battery for workplace deployment, and Figure 03 was redesigned for home environments (all three per the [official company page](https://www.figure.ai/company)). On the software side, Helix is Figure’s model family for humanoid perception and whole-body control; Helix 2.5, announced on September 17, 2026, achieved zero-shot generalization across 30 unseen Bay Area homes on tidying living rooms, folding towels, and making beds, and the company says it has committed $3.5 billion of compute to training Helix through a partnership with Nscale (see the [official announcement](https://www.figure.ai/news/helix-2-5-zero-shot-30-home-generalization)). See the [Helix entry](/en/wiki/products/helix).

A manufacturing-deployment partnership with BMW was announced in January 2024; in February 2024 Figure raised $675 million from investors including OpenAI, Microsoft, and NVIDIA, and its model partnership with OpenAI ended roughly a year later (per English Wikipedia).

## Frequently asked questions

### What company is Figure?

Figure is an AI robotics company founded in 2022 and headquartered in San Jose, California. It builds the Figure 01/02/03 humanoid robots and the Helix model family that controls them.

### Where is Figure’s official website?

The official website is [figure.ai](https://www.figure.ai/); the company overview is on the [company page](https://www.figure.ai/company) and major announcements on the [news page](https://www.figure.ai/news).

### What is Helix, and how does it relate to Figure?

Helix is Figure’s in-house embodied-AI model family for humanoid perception and whole-body control; Helix 2.5 is the version announced on September 17, 2026. See the [Helix entry](/en/wiki/products/helix) and the [official announcement](https://www.figure.ai/news/helix-2-5-zero-shot-30-home-generalization).

### What is Figure’s relationship with OpenAI?

Figure received investment from OpenAI in February 2024 and collaborated with it on speech models for robots; per English Wikipedia, that partnership ended about a year later as Figure moved to develop its own control models.
