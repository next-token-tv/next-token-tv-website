## Alibaba Cloud

Alibaba Cloud is the cloud computing and AI services brand of Alibaba Group, founded in September 2009 and headquartered in Hangzhou, with international operations registered and headquartered in Singapore. Its international site describes the company as a provider of "AI and Cloud Computing Services" for enterprises and AI builders ([official website](https://www.alibabacloud.com/)).

## Business and main product lines

- **Cloud infrastructure**: Elastic Compute Service (ECS), Object Storage Service (OSS), databases, CDN, container service (ACK), and security offerings, billed pay-as-you-go or by subscription.
- **AI and large models**: the Platform for AI (PAI) for machine learning, and Model Studio for building generative AI applications with the Qwen model family. Qwen is Alibaba Cloud's open-weights model family; the official page lists text, vision, multimodal, image, and video generation models among its offerings. Developers enter through the [official AI product pages](https://www.alibabacloud.com/).
- **Industry partnerships**: official cloud partner of the Olympics since January 2017 ([Wikipedia article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alibaba_Cloud)).

Time-sensitive details such as model versions, prices, and free tiers should be read from the official pages.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Alibaba Cloud, and which company owns it?

Alibaba Cloud is Alibaba Group's cloud computing and AI services brand, founded in September 2009. It provides cloud infrastructure, data storage, and AI development platforms.

### Where is the Alibaba Cloud website?

The international site is [alibabacloud.com](https://www.alibabacloud.com/); Chinese users can switch to the China site from the official site. The search suggestion "阿里云国际" refers to the international business.

### What is the relationship between Qwen and Alibaba Cloud?

Qwen is Alibaba Cloud's open-weights model family, offered to developers through platforms such as Model Studio. For model and API specifics, see the product entry and official documentation.

### What is Alibaba Cloud's AI developer entry point?

The Platform for AI (PAI) and Model Studio for generative AI applications are both listed on the [official website](https://www.alibabacloud.com/), where developers can reach each product page.
