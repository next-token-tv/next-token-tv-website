## SAIC Motor

SAIC Motor (上海汽车集团股份有限公司) is a Chinese automotive group headquartered in Shanghai, operating across six segments: vehicles, components, mobility and services, finance, international operations, and innovation technology. Its shares trade under code 600104, and the group completed a whole-group listing in 2011 (see the [official company profile](https://www.saicmotor.com/chinese/gsgk/gsjs/index.shtml)). Per that profile, SAIC ranked 125th on the Fortune Global 500 published in July 2026 — its 22nd time on the list.

## Brands and businesses

Per the official profile, SAIC’s vehicle brands fall into two groups:

- Own brands: Roewe (荣威), MG (名爵), Rising Auto (飞凡), IM Motors (智己), SAIC Maxus (上汽大通), Yuejin, Wuling, and Baojun, among others.
- Joint ventures: SAIC Volkswagen, Škoda, SAIC Audi, SAIC-GM (Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet), SGMW (SAIC-GM-Wuling), Naveco, SAIC Hongyan, and Sunwin buses.

Beyond vehicles, the group owns parts maker Huayu Automotive, operates overseas vehicle plants and KD factories, and sells products and services in more than 170 countries and regions (per the official company profile).

## Frequently asked questions

### What company is SAIC Motor?

SAIC Motor is an automotive group headquartered in Shanghai, engaged in vehicle and parts development and manufacturing, mobility services, finance, and international operations. It is listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange (600104) and completed a whole-group listing in 2011.

### Where is SAIC Motor’s official website?

The official website is [saicmotor.com](https://www.saicmotor.com/), with the company profile, brand list, sales bulletins, and investor-relations entries.

### What brands does SAIC Motor own?

Own brands include Roewe, MG, Rising Auto, IM Motors, SAIC Maxus, Wuling, and Baojun; joint-venture brands include SAIC Volkswagen, SAIC Audi, and SAIC-GM (Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet) — see the [official company profile](https://www.saicmotor.com/chinese/gsgk/gsjs/index.shtml).

### Is SAIC Motor a Fortune Global 500 company?

Per its official company profile, SAIC has been on the Fortune Global 500 for many consecutive years; on the list published in July 2026 it ranked 125th, its 22nd appearance.
