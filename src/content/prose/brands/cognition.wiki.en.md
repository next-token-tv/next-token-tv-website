## Cognition

Cognition (Cognition AI, also known as Cognition Labs) is an American AI company headquartered in San Francisco that builds agents for software engineering, including Devin. According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognition_AI), it was founded in August 2023 by Scott Wu, Steven Hao, and Walden Yan, all gold medalists at the International Olympiad in Informatics. The [official website](https://cognition.com/) calls Devin "the first autonomous software engineer."

## Business and products

Cognition's core product, [Devin](/en/wiki/products/devin), targets real software engineering work: the company says it "plans, writes, tests, and ships production code on its own" inside a team's existing codebase and tools. Product surfaces include Devin Desktop, the CLI, and Devin Fusion, with a Windsurf integration; the site also lists customers such as Mercedes-Benz, Goldman Sachs, and Infosys.

In July 2025, Cognition signed a definitive agreement to acquire Windsurf, the agentic IDE (per Wikipedia, after Google's $2.4 billion "acquihire" of Windsurf's CEO and other senior staff). The site also introduces FrontierCode, an evaluation method asking whether code is "genuinely mergeable" — "Would a human maintainer actually merge this pull request?"
