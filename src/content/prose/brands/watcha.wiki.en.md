## Definition

Watcha (观猹) is an AI media and community brand focused on product discovery and creator conversations. Its activities include an online community and the in-person Watcha Conference. See the [Watcha website](https://watcha.cn/) and the [conference website](https://dahui.watcha.cn/).

## Business and main product lines

Per its own website metadata, Watcha.cn describes itself as a gathering place for frontier-tech enthusiasts ("前沿科技爱好者聚集地") with the tagline "玩 AI，上观猹！" (For AI, go to Watcha), and its social-sharing description calls it "新一代前沿科技爱好者的内容平台" (a content platform for a new generation of frontier-tech enthusiasts). Its activities include online content and community plus an offline event named Watcha Conference (观猹大会); the conference site is currently titled "观猹大会 2026". No reliable public sources describing its organizers, team or event schedules were found, so this entry does not go further.

## Use cases

AI enthusiasts follow Watcha for product news and creator discussions; practitioners use events such as the Watcha Conference to demo products, try new devices and meet the community. The conference was taking place around the recording of episode 007, and the hosts mention custom hardware handed out at the event.

## Common confusions

- Watcha (观猹) vs. the Watcha Conference: the former is the media and community brand, the latter is its offline event; this wiki uses one entry for both.
- Other services named "Watcha": this entry refers to the Chinese AI community brand at watcha.cn, identified by its domain and Chinese name.
