## What is Tencent

Tencent is a Chinese internet and technology company founded in 1998 and headquartered in Shenzhen. Its official English About page describes it as "a world-leading internet and technology company" whose services help people stay connected with friends and family, get around, pay for daily expenses, and have fun, guided by the principle "to use technology for good."

## Business groups and main product lines

The official About page organizes the business by groups:

- Weixin Group (WXG): builds and operates the [WeChat](/en/wiki/products/wechat) ecosystem, including Official Accounts, Mini Programs, Weixin Pay, WeCom, and search, plus QQ Mail and WeRead.
- Interactive Entertainment Group (IEG): research, development, and operations for games and esports.
- Cloud & Smart Industries Group (CSIG): drives [Tencent Cloud](/en/wiki/brands/tencent-cloud) and the industry-internet strategy for retail, healthcare, education, and transportation.
- Platform & Content Group (PCG): social platforms such as QQ and QZone alongside news, video, and streaming.
- Corporate Development Group (CDG): fintech, advertising, and related businesses.
- Technology & Engineering Group (TEG): technology infrastructure.

On the AI side, Tencent has the [Hunyuan](/en/wiki/products/hunyuan) model family, and inside WeChat, [Xiaowei](/en/wiki/products/xiaowei) is a native AI assistant in testing. Current features and entry points live on the official websites.

## Frequently asked questions

### What kind of company is Tencent?

Tencent is an internet and technology company founded in 1998 in Shenzhen, covering social communication, content, gaming, fintech, and cloud computing. See the [official About page](https://www.tencent.com/en-us/about.html).

### What are Tencent's main products and businesses?

By its official groups: the WeChat ecosystem (Official Accounts, Mini Programs, Weixin Pay, WeCom), QQ and content platforms, games and esports, Tencent Cloud and industry internet, plus fintech and advertising. See the [official About page](https://www.tencent.com/en-us/about.html).

### What is the relationship between Tencent and WeChat?

WeChat (Weixin) is Tencent's product, developed and operated by the Weixin Group, spanning messaging, social content, Mini Programs, and payments. See the [WeChat entry](/en/wiki/products/wechat).

### What is Tencent Cloud?

Tencent Cloud is Tencent's cloud computing, data, and AI services brand, owned by the Cloud & Smart Industries Group. It has its own entry: [Tencent Cloud](/en/wiki/brands/tencent-cloud).
