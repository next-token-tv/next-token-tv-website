## Patagonia

Patagonia is an American outdoor clothing brand founded in May 1973 by the rock climber Yvon Chouinard in Ventura, California, where the first store, Great Pacific Iron Works, opened the same year (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patagonia,_Inc.)). Starting from climbing, the brand now makes clothing and equipment for activities including climbing, surfing, skiing, and trail running.

## Environmental commitments and corporate governance

Environmental commitment is central to the brand: since 1985, the company has pledged 1 percent of total sales to environmental groups, and founder Chouinard was a founding member of the organization 1% for the Planet (per English Wikipedia). In September 2022, Chouinard announced a new ownership structure: the family's voting stock (about 2 percent of all shares) moved to the Patagonia Purpose Trust, and all nonvoting stock moved to the nonprofit Holdfast Collective, so that company profits fund climate action and land protection. Brand information is on the [official site](https://www.patagonia.com/).

## Discussion in the show

Episode 010 mentions Patagonia in the chapter "上周买了啥：遮光贴、羽绒服与 GTA 6" ([Last week's purchases: blackout stickers, down jackets, and GTA 6](/weekly/010/transcript#chapter-28); Chinese transcript), where Orange uses it as a fill-power reference for down jackets: he says ordinary jackets such as Bosideng run about 650 fill power, better ones such as Patagonia ("巴塔" in his shorthand) and Arc'teryx about 800, while the Montbell jacket he bought is 1000 (see [the passage](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-0ee1067cdcc1112620b2)). No Patagonia product was bought or tested in the episode; these fill-power numbers are Orange's personal account, not official Patagonia specifications. Background is in the [Montbell entry](/en/wiki/brands/montbell).

## Frequently asked questions

### What brand is Patagonia?

A US outdoor clothing brand founded by Yvon Chouinard in Ventura, California in 1973, making clothing and equipment for climbing, surfing, skiing, and other activities; see the [official site](https://www.patagonia.com/).

### What is Patagonia's "1% for the Planet" commitment?

Since 1985 the company has pledged 1 percent of total sales to environmental groups; founder Chouinard later became a founding member of the organization 1% for the Planet (per English Wikipedia).

### Who owns Patagonia?

Since September 2022, the voting stock has been held by the Patagonia Purpose Trust and the nonvoting stock by the nonprofit Holdfast Collective, with profits directed to climate and land protection (per English Wikipedia).

### What did the show mean by "Patagonia down jackets are 800 fill power"?

Fill power measures the volume occupied by a given weight of down. In episode 010, Orange cites about 800 fill power for Patagonia jackets as a comparison point with Bosideng (about 650) and Montbell (1000). That is his personal account; actual fill power of products on sale belongs to [official product pages](https://www.patagonia.com/).
