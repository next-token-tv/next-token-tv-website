## What OpenAI is

OpenAI is an artificial intelligence research and deployment company founded in 2015 and headquartered in San Francisco. Its official About page states the mission as ensuring "that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity" and describes the structure: the nonprofit OpenAI Foundation governs the for-profit OpenAI Group, which operates as a public benefit corporation.

## Products and developer platform

The main product lines listed on the official site are:

- [ChatGPT](/en/wiki/products/chatgpt): an AI assistant for individuals and organizations, with ChatGPT, ChatGPT Business, ChatGPT Enterprise, and ChatGPT for Education plans shown on the site.
- [Codex](/en/wiki/products/codex): a coding agent for software development that works across editors, terminals, desktop apps, and the cloud.
- [The GPT model family](/en/wiki/products/gpt): the model series behind these products; current versions belong to the official site and documentation, not this page.
- API platform and developer resources: developers connect through the [OpenAI API platform](https://platform.openai.com/docs/overview) and read documentation on [OpenAI Developers](https://developers.openai.com/); the site also lists Open Models and the Apps SDK.

Product-level details and boundaries live in the product entries; this page covers only the company and how its product lines relate.

## Frequently asked questions

### What company is OpenAI?

OpenAI is an AI research and deployment company founded in 2015, headquartered in San Francisco. The official About page states the mission of ensuring that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity and describes governance by the nonprofit OpenAI Foundation over the for-profit OpenAI Group, a public benefit corporation. See the [official About page](https://openai.com/about/).

### Where is OpenAI's official website?

The company site is [openai.com](https://openai.com/); the ChatGPT entry point is [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com/); developer documentation lives at [developers.openai.com](https://developers.openai.com/). All three are official domains.

### How do developers access the OpenAI API?

Create an account and API key on the [OpenAI API platform](https://platform.openai.com/docs/overview) and follow the official overview documentation. The API is billed separately from ChatGPT subscriptions; current features and limits belong to the platform docs.

### What are OpenAI's main products?

[ChatGPT](/en/wiki/products/chatgpt) for individuals and organizations; the coding agent [Codex](/en/wiki/products/codex) for software engineering; and the API platform with the [GPT model family](/en/wiki/products/gpt) for developers. The site also lists Open Models and the Apps SDK.

### How do ChatGPT and OpenAI relate?

ChatGPT is one product from OpenAI; the company also builds models, the API platform, and developer tools. Product usage details are in the [ChatGPT entry](/en/wiki/products/chatgpt); company background is on this page.
