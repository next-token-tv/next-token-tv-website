## GCORES Industries

GCORES Industries (吉考斯工业) is an apparel brand launched in 2017 by the games media organization [GCORES](/en/wiki/brands/gcores), producing collaboration apparel with game brands (per [Chinese Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9C%BA%E6%A0%B8)). The brand sells clothing, accessories and related merchandise and announces new releases through its official page on GCORES (see the [official page](https://www.gcores.com/users/519272/content)).

## Products

The brand focuses on game-themed collaboration merchandise. Per its official page on GCORES, collaborations and items released in 2026 include the [Phantom Blade Zero](/en/wiki/products/phantom-blade-zero) collection (launched online alongside the game's release in October 2026) and autumn/winter items for [Elden Ring](/en/wiki/products/elden-ring): Nightreign, as well as a collaboration keyboard with mechanical keyboard maker NuPhy (see the [official page](https://www.gcores.com/users/519272/content)).

## Discussion in the show

In episode 009, in the chapter "创始人、组织与消费" ([Founders, organizations and consumption](/weekly/009/transcript#chapter-05)) in the Chinese transcript, Guizang, discussing how actively GCORES' CEO Simon uses AI, mentions that "he also has an apparel brand called GCORES Industries," and says GCORES' product selection, ad buying, reporting and analytics workflows — including the brand's — have all been automated (see [the passage](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-b971729eaffca7f92fd6)). This is the participant's retelling of a third-party podcast and his personal evaluation.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is GCORES Industries?

GCORES Industries is the apparel and merchandise brand launched by GCORES in 2017, mainly selling game-themed collaboration clothing and accessories; new releases are announced on its [official page on GCORES](https://www.gcores.com/users/519272/content).

### What kinds of collaborations does GCORES Industries do?

Mainly game IP collaborations for apparel and accessories, plus hardware collaborations such as keyboards; per its official page, 2026 releases include the Phantom Blade Zero collection, Elden Ring: Nightreign autumn/winter items and a NuPhy collaboration keyboard.
