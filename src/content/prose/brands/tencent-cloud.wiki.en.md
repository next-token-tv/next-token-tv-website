## Tencent Cloud

Tencent Cloud is [Tencent](/en/wiki/brands/tencent)’s cloud computing and AI services brand. Its China site is [cloud.tencent.com](https://cloud.tencent.com/), and its international site is [tencentcloud.com](https://www.tencentcloud.com/). The Chinese site carries the slogan “腾讯云 产业智变·云启未来” ([Tencent Cloud official site](https://cloud.tencent.com/)); the international site describes its technology as coming from the Tencent ecosystem behind WeChat and QQ ([Tencent Cloud international site](https://www.tencentcloud.com/)).

## Business and main service lines

Per the [Tencent Cloud international site](https://www.tencentcloud.com/):

- **Compute and containers**: Cloud Virtual Machine, Lighthouse lightweight servers, GPU and bare metal, Tencent Kubernetes Engine, and Serverless Cloud Functions.
- **Storage and databases**: Cloud Object Storage (COS), TencentDB (MySQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, and more), and the TDSQL distributed database series.
- **AI and large models**: the LLM service TokenHub, plus face recognition, speech recognition, translation, OCR, digital humans, and eKYC.
- **Media and communication**: Cloud Streaming Services, Video on Demand, Real-Time Communication (TRTC), and Chat SDK.
- **Networking and security**: CDN, EdgeOne edge security and acceleration, DNS, and SSL certificates.

The international site lists over 3,200 global CDN nodes, 24 regions, and 69 availability zones. Service availability and coverage figures should be read from the official pages.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Tencent Cloud, and which company owns it?

Tencent Cloud is Tencent’s cloud computing and AI services brand, offering cloud infrastructure, databases, media services, and AI capabilities for businesses ([official site](https://cloud.tencent.com/)).

### What is Tencent Cloud’s official website?

The China site is [cloud.tencent.com](https://cloud.tencent.com/), and the international site for overseas users and English-language content is [tencentcloud.com](https://www.tencentcloud.com/).

### What services does Tencent Cloud provide?

Main lines include cloud servers and containers, object storage and cloud databases, LLM and AI services, media and real-time communication, and CDN and edge security; the full catalog is on the [international site](https://www.tencentcloud.com/) or the [China site](https://cloud.tencent.com/).

### What is the difference between Tencent Cloud’s China site and international site?

Both are official Tencent Cloud sites: the China site serves users and workloads in mainland China, while the international site targets overseas regions with an English interface and non-China regions. Available products and regions differ; read each site’s pages for specifics.
