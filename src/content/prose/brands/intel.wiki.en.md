## Intel

Intel is an American semiconductor company founded in July 1968 by Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore and headquartered in Santa Clara, California (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intel)). It has long been one of the main designers of x86 processors and also operates chip manufacturing.

## Business and product lines

- Client processors: Core and Core Ultra series CPUs for personal computers.
- Server processors: the Xeon series for data centers and servers.
- Wafer foundry: the Intel Foundry business, manufacturing chips for external customers.

The company overview is on the [official page](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/company-overview/company-overview.html). On management: Pat Gelsinger departed in December 2024, and Lip-Bu Tan has served as CEO since March 2025 (per English Wikipedia).

## Frequently asked questions

### What company is Intel?

An American semiconductor company founded in 1968 that designs x86 processors for PCs and servers and also runs a wafer foundry business; see the [official company overview](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/company-overview/company-overview.html).

### What are Intel's main products?

By use case: Core and Core Ultra series for personal computers, Xeon for servers and data centers, plus the Intel Foundry business for external customers.

### How are Intel and AMD related?

Both are x86 processor makers competing in PC and server markets over decades; the [AMD entry](/en/wiki/brands/amd) records more of the shared history.

### Who is Intel's CEO?

Pat Gelsinger departed in December 2024, and Lip-Bu Tan has served as CEO since March 2025 (per English Wikipedia).
