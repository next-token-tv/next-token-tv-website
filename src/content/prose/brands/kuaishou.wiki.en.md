## Kuaishou Technology

Kuaishou Technology is an internet company that operates short video and livestreaming platforms, headquartered in Beijing. It was founded in 2011 by Su Hua and Cheng Yixiao; its predecessor was GIF Kuaishou, a tool for making and sharing GIF pictures launched in March of the same year, and in 2013 it became a short-video community (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuaishou)). In February 2021, Kuaishou Technology listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange under the stock code 1024.HK.

## Business and product lines

- Short video and livestreaming: the Kuaishou app's short-video community and live streaming, with livestreaming revenue including virtual gifts viewers send to streamers.
- Online marketing: online marketing services for advertisers and merchants.
- Commerce: livestream e-commerce and related business, where sellers sell through livestreams and short videos.
- Overseas products: Kwai is the company's product name in many markets outside China, along with SnackVideo.

The business categories are described in the [Kuaishou investor FAQ](https://ir.kuaishou.com/investor-resources/investor-faqs/).

## Common confusions

- Kuaishou (app) vs. Kuaishou Technology: the app is the user-facing product; Kuaishou Technology is the company that operates it and the Hong Kong-listed entity.
- Kuaishou vs. Douyin: competing products from separate companies. Douyin is operated by [ByteDance](/en/wiki/brands/bytedance) (see the [Douyin entry](/en/wiki/products/douyin)), while Kuaishou belongs to the listed Kuaishou Technology; English Wikipedia notes that, compared with Douyin, Kuaishou relied more on e-commerce revenue than on advertising revenue.
- Kwai vs. Kuaishou: Kwai is Kuaishou Technology's product name in overseas markets, belonging to the same company as the domestic Kuaishou app.
