## Cyber Zen

Cyber Zen (赛博禅心) is a technology content brand focused on artificial intelligence, published mainly through the WeChat account of the same name, covering AI technology, products, and industry commentary. The brand shares its name with its principal, profiled separately as [Cyber Zen the person](/en/wiki/people/cyber-zen), whose public alias is "Da Congming" (self-styled "金色传说大聪明"). This page covers the brand and its content identity; see the person profile for the individual.

## Content and public identity

According to a [self-description republished on Huxiu](https://www.huxiu.com/article/4093731.html) (originally posted in March 2025), the author calls himself a "media person with a technical background" whose output centers on technical-report breakdowns and hands-on product testing; the post claims the account was first to publish analyses of the DeepSeek V3 and R1 technical reports and a hands-on teardown of Manus. These are the author's own statements; the actual archive lives on the "赛博禅心" WeChat account.

## Discussion in the show

In Weekly #001's chapter "Token Is New Money: 买 Mac Studio 像在家里'铸币'" (Token is new money), Orange mentions ["Da Congming's bar," where "the bar has all kinds of models, free to use"](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-9bb484146be023c2f4ac) (Chinese transcript). In the opening of Weekly #002, Xiangyang Qiaomu thanks ["Da Congming and Nan Qiao for inviting us to record at the AGI Bar in Shanghai"](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-beaa798a9959760c2231). Both remarks show the show's connection to Da Congming and the [AGI Bar](/en/wiki/brands/agi-bar) venue; the venue's own details belong to its official profile.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Cyber Zen?

Cyber Zen (赛博禅心) is a technology content brand, published mainly through its WeChat account of the same name, focused on AI technology, products, and industry commentary.

### Who is "Da Congming" in relation to Cyber Zen?

Da Congming is the public alias of Cyber Zen's principal; the WeChat posts are signed "金色传说大聪明." See the [Cyber Zen person profile](/en/wiki/people/cyber-zen).

### Where can I read Cyber Zen's content?

Mainly on the WeChat account "赛博禅心." Note when searching that results for "大聪明" are mostly unrelated (credit cards, movie lines, and the like); use "赛博禅心" as the keyword instead.

### How are AGI Bar and Cyber Zen related?

In the opening thanks of Next Token episode 002, the host credits "Da Congming" for inviting the show to record at AGI Bar in Shanghai, and in episode 001 Orange calls it "Da Congming's bar." The business relationship itself is described in AGI Bar's official profile — see the [AGI Bar profile](/en/wiki/brands/agi-bar).
