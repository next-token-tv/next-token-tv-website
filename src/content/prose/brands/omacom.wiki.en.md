## What the Omacom Foundation is

The Omacom Foundation is the nonprofit that funds the development, maintenance, and spread of [Omarchy](/en/wiki/products/omarchy). Omarchy is the "Beautiful, fun & agentic Linux" distribution created by David Heinemeier Hansson ([DHH](/en/wiki/people/dhh)) and incubated at 37signals, with code maintained in the [omacom/omarchy GitHub repository](https://github.com/omacom/omarchy). The foundation is the organizational entity behind the distribution; see its [official page](https://omarchy.org/foundation/).

## Organization and how it works

Per the official foundation page, its self-description is "The nonprofit funding the development, maintenance, and spread of Omarchy." It employs people full time on Omarchy, supported through three arrangements:

- **Patrons**: the people and companies funding Omarchy's development, maintenance, and spread; prospective founding and distinguished patrons are directed to david@omarchy.org.
- **Sponsorships**: multi-year funding for upstream projects Omarchy depends on (the page names Hyprland and mise), so their maintainers can work "without thinking about fundraising."
- **Artists in Residence**: a program paying artists a six-month stipend to work freely on themes, plugins, and other aesthetic work.

The foundation also organizes community structures (Discord, meetups, teams) and a merch channel; hosting is provided by Cloudflare and compute by DigitalOcean.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the Omacom Foundation?

It is the nonprofit funding the development, maintenance, and spread of Omarchy, employing people full time on the distribution; see the [official page](https://omarchy.org/foundation/).

### How do Omacom, DHH, and 37signals relate?

Omarchy was created by DHH and incubated at 37signals; Omacom is the foundation behind the distribution, and the official page marks Omarchy as a trademarked name. Foundation contacts use the david@omarchy.org and foundation@omarchy.org domains.

### How can I support Omarchy's development?

The official page's channels are becoming a patron (individual or company), the foundation-arranged upstream sponsorships, and the artists-in-residence program; partnership inquiries go to foundation@omarchy.org.
