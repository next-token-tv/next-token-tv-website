## Sandisk

Sandisk (brand spelling: SanDisk) is a storage brand founded on June 1, 1988 as SunDisk by Eli Harari, Sanjay Mehrotra, and Jack Yuan, headquartered in Milpitas, California (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SanDisk)). It started from flash memory technology, and its products cover memory cards, USB flash drives, and solid-state drives.

## Business and company history

- Founded in 1988 as SunDisk, later renamed SanDisk, and a longtime developer of flash storage products.
- Acquired by Western Digital in 2016.
- In February 2025, Western Digital spun off its flash business as the independent Sandisk Corporation, trading on Nasdaq under the ticker SNDK, with the SanDisk brand passing to the new company (per English Wikipedia).

Product and company information is on the [official website](https://www.sandisk.com/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Sandisk?

A brand and company focused on flash storage products, including memory cards, USB flash drives, and solid-state drives, headquartered in Milpitas, California; see the [official website](https://www.sandisk.com/).

### How is Sandisk related to Western Digital?

Western Digital acquired SanDisk in 2016 and in February 2025 spun the flash business off as the independent Sandisk Corporation (Nasdaq ticker SNDK), with the SanDisk brand passing to the new company (per English Wikipedia).

### What products does Sandisk sell?

By category: memory cards, USB flash drives, portable SSDs, and internal SSDs — flash storage products.

### Which spelling is correct, SanDisk or Sandisk?

Both refer to the same brand: SanDisk is the long-standing brand spelling, while Sandisk is the name of the company that took over the flash business in the February 2025 spin-off. Product and company information should come from the [official website](https://www.sandisk.com/).
