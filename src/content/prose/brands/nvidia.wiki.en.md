## NVIDIA

NVIDIA is a technology company centered on GPUs and accelerated computing. It was founded in 1993 by Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem, and is headquartered in Santa Clara, California. According to [public references](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nvidia), it went public in January 1999 (Nasdaq: NVDA) and later that year released the GeForce 256, the first product marketed as a GPU. The [official overview](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/) states that "NVIDIA pioneered accelerated computing to tackle challenges no one else can solve" and describes the company as engineering "the most advanced chips, systems, and software for the AI factories of the future."

## Business and main product lines

NVIDIA's products span consumer gaming to AI infrastructure; its investor page summarizes its markets as gaming, professional visualization, data center, and automotive.

- Gaming: GeForce GPUs and RTX technology; drivers are on the [official driver page](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/drivers/).
- Data center and AI: accelerated computing chips, systems, and software for large-scale AI training and inference.
- CUDA: described by [NVIDIA](https://developer.nvidia.com/cuda-zone) as "NVIDIA's platform for accelerated computing, providing the software layer that enables applications to harness the power of GPUs."
- Omniverse: the official platform "for industrial digitalization."
- Automotive: computing platforms for intelligent vehicles.

In August 2026, NVIDIA reportedly agreed to acquire Hugging Face, as reported by The Information and carried by [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/nvidia-talks-acquire-hugging-face-13-billion-deal-business-insider-reports-2026-08-27/) (see the [Hugging Face entry](/en/wiki/brands/hugging-face)); as of those reports the deal had not closed, and official company announcements take precedence for later developments.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does NVIDIA do?

NVIDIA builds GPUs and accelerated computing platforms across gaming (GeForce/RTX), professional visualization, data center, and automotive, and supplies the chips, systems, and software behind AI; see the [official overview](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/about-nvidia/).

### What is CUDA?

CUDA is NVIDIA's accelerated computing platform. The official description says it provides "the software layer that enables applications to harness the power of GPUs," usable through languages such as C++ and Python or via accelerated libraries; see the [CUDA page](https://developer.nvidia.com/cuda-zone).

### Where is NVIDIA listed?

NVIDIA went public on Nasdaq in January 1999 and trades as NVDA; see [public references](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nvidia).

### Where can I download NVIDIA drivers?

The official driver page is [nvidia.com/drivers](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/drivers/), with manual product search or automatic updates through the NVIDIA App; Linux drivers are in the Unix Driver Archive.

### Did NVIDIA acquire Hugging Face?

According to media reports in August 2026 (reported by The Information and carried by [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/nvidia-talks-acquire-hugging-face-13-billion-deal-business-insider-reports-2026-08-27/)), NVIDIA agreed to acquire Hugging Face; as of those reports the deal had not closed. Later developments should be confirmed against official company announcements.
