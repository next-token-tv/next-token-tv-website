## Yizhi Yanhua Community

Yizhi Yanhua (一支烟花, "a firework") is a Chinese-language technical community focused on AI, developer tools, and enterprise AI applications. Per [its podcast home page on Xiaoyuzhou](https://www.xiaoyuzhoufm.com/podcast/658cea5d10888b10f7670b0e), the community was founded in 2023, runs more than 40 AI-themed online groups, and publishes through a WeChat official account (“一支烟花AI”). The community operates the Yizhi Yanhua AI podcast.

## Podcast and content

The Yizhi Yanhua AI podcast is hosted on the Xiaoyuzhou platform, with hosts listed as “一支烟一朵花” (Yizhi Yiduohua), “吵爷” (Chao Ye), and “新一学AI” (Xinyi Xue AI). Episodes include AI paper and product walkthroughs (engineering topics such as RAG and KV cache), practitioner interviews, industry-news commentary, and community discussions under the “烟花夜话” (firework night talks) format. Listening and subscription start from the [podcast page on Xiaoyuzhou](https://www.xiaoyuzhoufm.com/podcast/658cea5d10888b10f7670b0e).

Online groups are the community’s main structure; listeners can join through announcements on the podcast page.
