## Qualcomm

Qualcomm is an American semiconductor company founded in 1985 in San Diego, California (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qualcomm)). It develops mobile communications and computing technologies, licensing patents to and selling chip platforms for handset makers and other customers; its main products are the Snapdragon line of mobile platforms.

## Business and product lines

- Snapdragon mobile platforms: SoC series for smartphones and tablets, with the 8 series positioned as the flagship line. The Snapdragon 8 Elite, released in October 2024, adopted the custom Oryon CPU core, a design that came with the Nuvia team Qualcomm acquired in 2021 (per the [English Wikipedia entry on Oryon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oryon)).
- Radio frequency and beyond: RF front-end, automotive cockpit and connectivity, and IoT platform businesses.

Snapdragon platform information is on the [official page](https://www.qualcomm.com/snapdragon/smartphones).

## Frequently asked questions

### What company is Qualcomm?

A semiconductor company founded in 1985 in San Diego that licenses communications patents to and sells Snapdragon chip platforms for handset makers; see the [official Snapdragon page](https://www.qualcomm.com/snapdragon/smartphones).

### What is Snapdragon?

Qualcomm's mobile platform brand, covering SoCs for smartphones and tablets, tiered into the flagship 8 series and the 7 and 6 series.

### When was the Snapdragon 8 Elite released?

The Snapdragon 8 Elite was released in October 2024, the first 8-series flagship platform to use the custom Oryon CPU core (per English Wikipedia).

### What is the "Snapdragon 8 Gen6" mentioned in episode 008?

The wording used when a participant relayed Geekerwan's comparison, referring to Qualcomm's flagship mobile platform at the time; the "significant regression" claim is the participant's retelling.
