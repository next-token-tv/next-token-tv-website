## What SpaceXAI is

SpaceXAI is SpaceX's artificial-intelligence brand, formerly xAI, developing the Grok model family and related consumer and enterprise AI products. The official company page states its mission as "We build AI to understand the universe" (accelerating human scientific discovery), and the site footer reads "© 2026 SpaceXAI LLC".

## Company history

The timeline on the official company page records: xAI issued its company announcement on July 12, 2023; Grok-0 followed on August 18, 2023, Grok was announced on November 3, 2023, and Grok on X launched on December 7, 2023. In 2024 the company open-released Grok-1 and shipped Grok-1.5 and Grok-2; in 2025 it released Grok 3 and Grok 4 and established xAI for Government. On infrastructure, the official page notes the Colossus cluster was built with 200K GPUs in 122 days.

On February 2, 2026, SpaceX announced that it had acquired xAI — the [official announcement page](https://x.ai/news/xai-joins-spacex) reads "SpaceX announced today that it has acquired xAI" — and the company now operates within SpaceX under the SpaceXAI name. This page does not go into the brand's model details or product forms: the model family is covered in the [Grok entry](/en/wiki/products/grok), and the agent product in the [Grok Bot entry](/en/wiki/products/grok-bot).

Current product entry points (Grok apps, API, console) are on the [official website](https://x.ai/) and in the [developer documentation](https://docs.x.ai/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is xAI, and is it still called xAI?

xAI is the AI company behind the Grok model family, founded in 2023. Its official announcement page records that SpaceX announced it had acquired xAI on February 2, 2026, after which the company operates as SpaceXAI (site footer: SpaceXAI LLC).

### What is the relationship between SpaceXAI, SpaceX, and X (the social platform)?

SpaceXAI belongs to the SpaceX organization following the acquisition announced on February 2, 2026; Grok is available on the X platform (the official timeline records Grok on X launching in December 2023). The announcement does not mention any change of ownership for the X platform, so this page makes no inference.

### Where are the xAI/SpaceXAI website and Grok's website?

The company website is [x.ai](https://x.ai/), and the Grok product entry is [grok.com](https://grok.com/) (the site navigation also lists iOS, Android, and Grok on X); developer documentation is at [docs.x.ai](https://docs.x.ai/).

### Where do I create an xAI API key?

Developer documentation is at [docs.x.ai](https://docs.x.ai/), providing API documentation and console entry points for Grok models. Model-version coverage is in the [Grok entry](/en/wiki/products/grok).
