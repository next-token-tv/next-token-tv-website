## Massachusetts Institute of Technology

The Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) is a private research university in Cambridge, Massachusetts, chartered on April 10, 1861 and founded by William Barton Rogers (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology)). The school overview is on the [official page](https://web.mit.edu/about).

## Discussion in the show

In episode 009, in the chapter "模型、算力与安全" ([Models, compute and safety](/weekly/009/transcript#chapter-03)) in the Chinese transcript, Orange relays a research story attributed to MIT: that "MIT posted a study on Twitter" in which Astra was used to edit 0.016 percent of the weights of a [Qwen](/en/wiki/products/qwen) model, raising its math scores about sixfold; he also points out that the wording was deliberately attention-grabbing, since the model actually had a bug where the thinking input length exceeded its output limit (see [the passage](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-e4cb23b47830777ef3ce)). Guizang relays the second experiment from the same set, which he considers more significant: having GPT-6 directly "write" a neural network that plays old arcade games, which played better than a model trained for 10,000 steps (see [the passage](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-a38c362c394c1d16dc5c)). These are the participants' retellings of content circulating on social media and their personal readings; the study's actual provenance should be checked against the institutions' own publications. [Simon Willison](/en/wiki/people/simon-willison)'s replication of the weight-reading experiment is also mentioned in that chapter by Xiangyang Qiaomu.

## Frequently asked questions

### What school is MIT?

MIT is a private research university chartered in 1861 and located in Cambridge, Massachusetts, known for engineering and scientific research; see the [official school page](https://web.mit.edu/about).

### Are MIT and the Massachusetts Institute of Technology the same?

Yes. MIT is the English abbreviation of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.
