## LEGO

LEGO is a construction-brick and toy brand owned by the LEGO Group (LEGO System A/S), based in Billund, Denmark. The company's own introduction says it was founded in 1932 by Ole Kirk Kristiansen and, built on the iconic LEGO brick, is one of the world's leading manufacturers of play materials.

## Business and product lines

The core business is construction toys: interlocking plastic bricks that extend into boxed sets, minifigures, and themed series. Related initiatives include [LEGO Education](https://www.lego.com/en-us/education) and the [LEGO House](https://legohouse.com/) experience center in Billund. The [LEGO Foundation](https://www.legofoundation.com/about-the-lego-foundation) is an independent foundation that owns 25% of the LEGO Group, rather than a business operated by the group. The company is headquartered in Billund, and the site footer names LEGO System A/S as the operating entity.

Brand, product, and building-instruction information should be read from [LEGO.com](https://www.lego.com/) and the [About Us page](https://www.lego.com/en-us/aboutus).

## Frequently asked questions

### What country is LEGO from?

LEGO is a Danish brand. The company's introduction places its headquarters in Billund, Denmark, and the site footer names LEGO System A/S as the operating entity.

### Who founded LEGO, and when?

LEGO was founded in 1932 by Ole Kirk Kristiansen, as described in the [official company introduction](https://www.lego.com/en-us/aboutus).

### Where is the official LEGO website?

The official website is [lego.com](https://www.lego.com/), with company introduction, history, and foundation links collected on the [About Us page](https://www.lego.com/en-us/aboutus).
