## MUJI

MUJI (Japanese: 無印良品, "Mujirushi Ryōhin," meaning "no-brand quality goods") is a Japanese lifestyle brand that started in December 1980 as a private label of the Seiyu supermarket chain and has been run independently by Ryohin Keikaku Co., Ltd. since 1989 (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muji)). The company is headquartered in Tokyo and listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange.

## Business and product lines

- Household goods and lifestyle items: storage, stationery, furniture, and bedding — the brand's most representative categories.
- Apparel: basic clothing for men and women.
- Food: snacks, seasonings, and processed foods.
- Extended formats: Café&Meal MUJI restaurants, MUJI HOTEL, and more.

Brand and product information is on the [official website](https://www.muji.com/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is MUJI?

A lifestyle brand that started in Japan in 1980, selling household goods, apparel, and food, operated by Ryohin Keikaku since 1989; see the [official website](https://www.muji.com/).

### What does MUJI mean?

MUJI is the romanized abbreviation of the Japanese 無印良品 (Mujirushi Ryōhin), meaning "no-brand quality goods" — quality that does not rely on a logo.

### Which country is MUJI from?

Japan: it began as a Seiyu private label in 1980 and has been run since 1989 by Ryohin Keikaku, headquartered in Tokyo (per English Wikipedia).

### What does MUJI sell?

By category: household goods, apparel, and food, plus extended formats such as cafés and hotels.
