## Red Hat

Red Hat is an American software company that provides open-source software products and related services to enterprises. It was founded in 1993, is headquartered in Raleigh, North Carolina, and has operated as an independent subsidiary of IBM since July 2019 (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Hat)).

## Business and products

- Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL): the company's flagship enterprise operating system.
- Fedora: the upstream community distribution sponsored by Red Hat, where new technologies typically mature before entering RHEL.
- Other product lines: the OpenShift container platform, the Ansible automation platform and more (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Hat)).

Red Hat contributes code to free software projects such as the [Linux](/en/wiki/products/linux) kernel; per English Wikipedia it was the third-largest corporate contributor to the Linux kernel in 2025. The company overview is on the [official page](https://www.redhat.com/en/about).

This is the participant's analogy drawing on the distribution model represented by Red Hat and [Debian](/en/wiki/products/debian), not a statement from Red Hat.

## Frequently asked questions

### What company is Red Hat?

An American enterprise open-source software company founded in 1993, best known for Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL) alongside product lines such as OpenShift and Ansible; it has been an IBM subsidiary since 2019. See the [official company page](https://www.redhat.com/en/about).

### How are Red Hat and Linux related?

Red Hat builds enterprise products on Linux: RHEL is its paid enterprise distribution, it sponsors the upstream Fedora community distribution, and it contributes to the Linux kernel over the long term.

### When did IBM acquire Red Hat?

IBM announced a 34 billion dollar agreement to acquire Red Hat in October 2018; the deal closed on July 9, 2019, after which Red Hat has operated as an independent subsidiary of IBM (per English Wikipedia).
