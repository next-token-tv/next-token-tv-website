## LG Electronics

LG Electronics Inc. is a South Korean consumer electronics and home appliance company headquartered in Seoul, part of LG Corporation. It began as GoldStar, founded in October 1958, and was renamed LG Electronics in March 1995; the "LG" name comes from the merger of the Lucky and GoldStar businesses, and the brand slogan is "Life's Good." Alongside Samsung Electronics it sits in the top tier of Korean consumer electronics — see the [Samsung entry](/en/wiki/brands/samsung) for comparison.

## Business areas and product lines

LG Electronics organizes its business into four units: Home Appliance Solution (HS — refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioning), Media Entertainment Solution (MS — TVs, audio, and displays), Vehicle Solution (VS — automotive components and telematics), and Eco Solution (ES — HVAC and energy). Beyond finished products, LG licenses its webOS smart TV platform and runs the LG ThinQ smart home platform; its product lines also include LG gram laptops and monitors. Current products and business scope are on the [global site](https://www.lg.com/global/) and the [company overview](https://www.lg.com/global/about-lg).

## Frequently asked questions

### What country is LG from?

LG Electronics is a South Korean company headquartered in Seoul, part of LG Corporation; its predecessor GoldStar was founded in 1958.

### What does "LG" stand for?

The name comes from the 1995 renaming of Lucky-Goldstar, after the merger of the Lucky and GoldStar businesses. LG has also popularized the slogan "Life's Good," but the official origin of the name is the merger of the two companies.

### What is the relationship between LG Electronics and LG Corporation?

LG Electronics is the electronics business of LG Corporation (the LG Group), which also includes sister companies such as LG Chem and LG Household & Health Care.

### What is LG's smart home platform?

LG Electronics runs LG ThinQ, a platform for connecting its appliances and devices; the entry point is on the [LG global site](https://www.lg.com/global/). For the smart home compatibility topic raised in episode 006, see the "Discussion in the show" section above.
