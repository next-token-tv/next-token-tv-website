### Keep the complete name

The official name in every language is Next Token｜词元之外, with the English name first and the Chinese name second. Use the full-width vertical bar (｜) without surrounding spaces. Do not translate the Chinese name into an English program title.

### Use the official artwork

The visual wordmark is Next Token. Use the downloadable files and scale them proportionally. Do not retype the logo or alter its lettering, orange square placement, or proportions. The SVG contains outlined lettering and does not require a font installation.

### Preserve the background and composition

All logo files have an opaque paper-colored background and no guides. For circular use, center the circle on the original square canvas and use its side length as the diameter, tangent to all four sides. Crop only the corners; preserve the lettering, orange square, proportions, positions and internal spacing without enlarging or shifting the logo. On Warm Paper, add a 1px inset Soft Ink Border line along the circumference.

### Distinguish the brand from the show

Next Token｜词元之外 is the parent brand. Next Token Weekly is its flagship weekly show. Weekly is not part of the parent brand name and does not replace the official program name on publishing platforms.
