## Angry Miao

Angry Miao (怒喵科技) is a desktop hardware brand. Its official store describes it as founded in 2019 by Li Nan (see the [Li Nan entry](/en/wiki/people/li-nan), formerly Senior VP / CMO of Meizu) and positioned as a "Future Art Community" — a high-end tech brand that brings products to consumers through close community interaction. Its products center on mechanical keyboards and wireless desktop peripherals.

## Product lines

The official store sells keyboards and mice: keyboard models include the AM Infinity, AM AFA R2, AM Relic 80, Retro 63 RT, and ATM 98, alongside the AM HATSU and accessories. The brand also runs a Discord community and describes its audience as "Gamers & Keeb Enthusiasts." Current models and prices are on the [official store](https://store.angrymiao.com/).

## Discussion in the show

Weekly #006's chapter “REA 与 AI 逆向：给怒喵鼠标补一个 macOS 驱动” (REA and AI reverse engineering: building a macOS driver for an Angry Miao mouse) centers on Guizang's Angry Miao mouse. [He describes it as designed for gaming: high DPI, with shortcuts bound to game macros, but with good Bluetooth and a light weight](/weekly/006/transcript#quote-b55c73e9c9d26550fb59). [Because the vendor mainly targets Windows gamers and offers none of the side-button shortcut software he was used to from Logitech, he had AI reverse-engineer the device and write a native macOS driver](/weekly/006/transcript#quote-11e4e6daec30ddd6bfb3); [the driver was written by AI in about an hour one evening while he worked on the newsletter, needed only a UI pass the next morning, and supports DPI switching, key remapping, and lighting effects across 2.4G dongle, USB wired, and Bluetooth connections](/weekly/006/transcript#quote-b79ec8065580ad28d3a6) (paragraphs in the [Chinese transcript](/weekly/006/transcript#chapter-02)). The chapter's framing topic is the open-source reverse-engineering tool [REA](/en/wiki/products/rea).

Note: the macOS driver described above is software the host built with AI for his own use, not an official Angry Miao product; the usage impressions are the host's own descriptions.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who founded Angry Miao?

Angry Miao was founded in 2019 by Li Nan; the official store bio says he was previously Senior VP / CMO of Meizu. See the [Li Nan entry](/en/wiki/people/li-nan).

### What does Angry Miao make?

Angry Miao positions itself as a community-driven high-end tech hardware brand, with mechanical keyboards and wireless desktop peripherals (including gaming mice) as its main products; current models are on the [official store](https://store.angrymiao.com/).

### Where is Angry Miao's website?

The brand site is [angrymiao.com](https://www.angrymiao.com/) and the official store is [store.angrymiao.com](https://store.angrymiao.com/); the community runs on Discord.

### Does the Angry Miao mouse have a macOS driver?

In episode 006, Guizang, unable to find ready-made macOS configuration software, built a native driver himself with AI (see "Discussion in the show" above); that is personal, homemade software. For Angry Miao's official software and support, use the [store's Support section](https://store.angrymiao.com/).
