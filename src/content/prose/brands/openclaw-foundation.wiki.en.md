## What the OpenClaw Foundation is

The OpenClaw Foundation is the independent nonprofit organization stewarding [OpenClaw](/en/wiki/products/openclaw), the open-source personal AI assistant. Per the [official site](https://openclaw.ai/), it is an independent US 501(c)(3) nonprofit that employs the core team, signs every release, and "keeps the whole product MIT licensed," with no enterprise edition and no paid version. The OpenClaw project was created by Peter Steinberger and grew from a weekend project into one of the most-starred repositories on GitHub.

## Organization and funding

- **Funding**: donors listed on the official site include the University of Michigan, OpenAI, Amazon, Red Hat, Offline Holdings, and the Lobster Computer Company. The site states that donors do not own, control, or direct the project, and that no model provider receives privileged treatment in the code.
- **Relationship with OpenAI**: the site's wording is "OpenAI is a donor and Peter Steinberger works there; OpenClaw is not an OpenAI product."
- **Official entry points**: the project site [openclaw.ai](https://openclaw.ai/), the [documentation](https://docs.openclaw.ai/), and the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the OpenClaw Foundation?

It is the independent US 501(c)(3) nonprofit stewarding [OpenClaw](/en/wiki/products/openclaw). It employs the core team, signs releases, and keeps the product MIT licensed; see [openclaw.ai](https://openclaw.ai/).

### What is the OpenClaw Foundation's relationship with OpenAI?

OpenAI is one of its donors, and OpenClaw's creator Peter Steinberger works at OpenAI. The official site states plainly that OpenClaw is not an OpenAI product and that donors do not control the project.

### Is OpenClaw free, and who maintains it?

The software stays under the MIT license with no enterprise or paid edition, maintained by the foundation's core team and the community. Costs come from your own hardware and the API usage of whatever model provider you connect; the code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw).
