## ZTE

ZTE Corporation is a Chinese telecommunications equipment and solutions company, founded in 1985 in Shenzhen, where it is still headquartered. It listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in 1997 (ticker 000063) and on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in December 2004 (ticker 0763), according to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZTE). Its global site positions the company as a provider for carriers, homes and enterprises, and consumers ([official website](https://www.zte.com.cn/global/index.html)).

## Business and main product lines

The official global site divides the business into several areas ([official website](https://www.zte.com.cn/global/index.html)):

- **Carrier networks**: wireless access, fixed access, optical transport, IP networks, core networks, and intelligent energy.
- **Home and enterprise**: smart home, campus networks, and networks for micro and small enterprises.
- **Consumer products**: smartphones, mobile internet devices, and smart home products.
- **Services**: digital network deployment, digital operations, and 5G services.

The company also works on 5G and 5G-Advanced, AI, and server and storage products. Current product availability and business figures should be read from the official pages.

The smartphone brand [Nubia](/en/wiki/brands/nubia) grew out of ZTE: it was established as a wholly owned ZTE subsidiary in 2012, became an independent company in 2015, and ZTE reduced its stake to 49.9% in 2017 ([Wikipedia: Nubia Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nubia_Technology)).
