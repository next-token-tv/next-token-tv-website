## Anker

Anker is a consumer electronics brand founded in 2011 by Steven Yang and headquartered in Changsha, China (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anker_Innovations)). The company started by selling laptop replacement batteries online and has since expanded into charging, audio, smart home, and other categories, with sales focused on overseas markets.

## Business and product lines

- Charging and power: Anker-branded wall chargers, power banks, and cables, plus home energy storage and portable power stations.
- Audio: earbuds, headphones, and speakers under the soundcore brand.
- Smart home: robot vacuums, security cameras, and other devices under the eufy brand.
- Other categories: automotive accessories and more.

Product and purchase entry points are on the [official website](https://www.anker.com/).
