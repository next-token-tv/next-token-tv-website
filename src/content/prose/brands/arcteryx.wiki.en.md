## Arc'teryx

Arc'teryx is a Canadian outdoor equipment brand founded in 1989 in North Vancouver, British Columbia, originally under the name Rock Solid and renamed Arc'teryx in 1991; the name and logo come from the Archaeopteryx fossil, chosen to suggest accelerating evolution (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arc%27teryx)). The brand began with climbing gear and now belongs to Amer Sports, headquartered in Helsinki, Finland.

## Business and products

Arc'teryx started out making climbing equipment: the Vapor harness built with heat-lamination technology, the Bora pack in 1993, and its first Gore-Tex apparel in 1996. Its current lines cover clothing and gear for climbing, skiing, hiking, and trail running, alongside sub-lines such as Veilance (founded 2009, for business wear), LEAF (law enforcement and military), and PRO (ski patrol and search-and-rescue). On ownership: the brand was sold to the Salomon Group in 2001 and passed to Amer Sports in 2005 with Salomon; in 2019 a consortium led by Anta Sports acquired a controlling stake in Amer Sports. The brand page is on the [official Amer Sports site](https://www.amersports.com/brands/arcteryx/).

Episode 010 mentions Arc'teryx in the chapter "上周买了啥：遮光贴、羽绒服与 GTA 6" ([Last week's purchases: blackout stickers, down jackets, and GTA 6](/weekly/010/transcript#chapter-28); Chinese transcript), where Orange uses it as a fill-power reference for down jackets: he says ordinary jackets such as Bosideng run about 650 fill power, better ones such as Patagonia and Arc'teryx about 800, while the Montbell jacket he bought is 1000, "the highest grade" (see [the passage](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-0ee1067cdcc1112620b2)). No Arc'teryx product was bought or tested in the episode; these fill-power numbers are Orange's personal account, not official Arc'teryx specifications. ## Frequently asked questions

### What brand is Arc'teryx?

A Canadian outdoor equipment brand founded in North Vancouver in 1989 that started with climbing gear and now focuses on high-end outdoor apparel and equipment; see the [official Amer Sports page](https://www.amersports.com/brands/arcteryx/).

### Who owns Arc'teryx?

It was sold to the Salomon Group in 2001 and has belonged to Amer Sports since 2005; in 2019 a consortium led by Anta Sports acquired a controlling majority of Amer Sports (per English Wikipedia).

### What does the Arc'teryx name mean?

It comes from Archaeopteryx, the fossil regarded as a transitional form between dinosaurs and birds; both the name and the logo reference it, conveying the idea of accelerating evolution.

### What did the show mean by "Arc'teryx down jackets are 800 fill power"?

Fill power measures the volume occupied by a given weight of down. In episode 010, Orange cites about 800 fill power for Arc'teryx jackets as a comparison point with Bosideng (about 650) and Montbell (1000). That is his personal account; actual fill power of products on sale belongs to [official product pages](https://arcteryx.com/).
