## Beijing Daily

Beijing Daily (北京日报) is a regional daily newspaper based in Beijing, founded on October 1, 1952. It serves as the organ of the Communist Party of China Beijing Municipal Committee and is supervised by Beijing Daily Group. Beyond the print newspaper, Beijing Daily publishes digital journalism through its website bjd.com.cn (京报网), the Beijing Daily mobile app, and accounts on Weibo and WeChat.

## Newspaper and digital media

According to the bjd.com.cn pages, Beijing Daily Group's publications include Beijing Daily, Beijing Evening News (北京晚报), Beijing Sub-center News (北京城市副中心报), Beijing Business Today (北京商报), and Beijing Youth Daily (北京青年报). The group also runs the Beijing Daily app, the takefoto.cn site, and converged-media studio brands such as 长安街知事 and 艺绽.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Beijing Daily?

Beijing Daily is a regional daily newspaper for Beijing, founded on October 1, 1952. It is the organ of the CPC Beijing Municipal Committee, supervised by Beijing Daily Group, and covers local, national, and international news.

### Where can I read Beijing Daily online?

The digital entry point is [bjd.com.cn](https://www.bjd.com.cn/), which aggregates electronic editions of Beijing Daily and Beijing Evening News; there is also a Beijing Daily mobile app, with download details on the site.

### What is Beijing Daily Group?

Beijing Daily Group supervises Beijing Daily and publishes several other papers, including Beijing Evening News, Beijing Business Today, and Beijing Youth Daily, per [bjd.com.cn](https://www.bjd.com.cn/).
