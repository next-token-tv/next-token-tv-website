## What MiniMax is

MiniMax is a general AI technology company whose official introduction says it was founded in early 2022 with the mission to "co-create intelligence with everyone." Per Wikipedia, its Chinese name is 稀宇科技 (Xiyu Keji), it is headquartered in Shanghai, it was founded by former SenseTime researchers including Yan Junjie, and it listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on 9 January 2026. This entry covers the company; the application layer is covered separately in the [MiniMax products entry](/en/wiki/products/minimax).

## Work and how the products relate

MiniMax's business has two layers: models underneath, and the applications and platforms that serve them on top:

- **Model family**: covering text, audio, images, video, and music. The official site lists the MiniMax M3 language model (sparse-attention architecture, 1M context), the MiniMax H3 video model (open weights, omni-modal generation), Speech 2.8 for speech, and Music 3.0 (open weights). Open-sourcing is a notable strategy — H3 was officially open-sourced on 3 August 2026 as the company's "next-generation general-purpose video model."
- **Applications and open platform**: the models reach users through MiniMax Agent, Hailuo AI, MiniMax Code, MiniMax Design, MiniMax Audio, and Talkie, while developers call text, video, speech, and music APIs through [platform.minimax.io](https://platform.minimax.io/). Product positioning is covered in the [MiniMax products entry](/en/wiki/products/minimax).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is MiniMax?

MiniMax is a general AI technology company founded in early 2022 (Chinese name 稀宇科技, per Wikipedia) with a multimodal model family covering text, audio, images, video, and music, plus products and an open platform for individuals, enterprises, and developers; see [minimax.io](https://www.minimax.io/).

### What country is MiniMax from?

It is a Chinese company headquartered in Shanghai (per Wikipedia), which listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on 9 January 2026.

### What is the relationship between MiniMax, Hailuo AI, and Talkie?

Hailuo AI (video and image creation) and Talkie (AI character chat) are both MiniMax products carrying its video and language models; the full product line is covered in the [MiniMax products entry](/en/wiki/products/minimax).

### What is MiniMax H3?

H3 is MiniMax's video generation model, officially described as an open-weight "next-generation general-purpose video model," open-sourced on 3 August 2026; details are in the [official H3 announcement](https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-h3-open-source).
