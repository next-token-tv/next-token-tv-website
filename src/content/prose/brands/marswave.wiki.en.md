## What MarsWave is

MarsWave is an AI application company whose site self-describes as an "AI Application Company." It has two product directions: [ListenHub](https://listenhub.ai/) for content creation, and [ColaOS](/en/wiki/products/colaos) for personal assistance. Per the show's participants and public information, co-host Orange ([Orange](/en/wiki/people/orange)) is the founder of MarsWave.

## Work and how the products relate

- **ListenHub**: officially positioned as "One platform for AI content creation" — starting from an idea, link, or document and finishing with publishable audio, images, slides, and video. Features include AI podcast generation, text to speech, multi-speaker voiceover, voice cloning (from one recording across 12 languages, per the official page), AI image and video generation, plus developer access through an SDK, CLI, OpenAPI, and an MCP server. The ListenHub footer reads "© 2026 MarsWave," with contact email on the marswave.ai domain.
- **ColaOS (Cola)**: MarsWave's desktop AI assistant, centered on long-term memory and personal context, with macOS and Windows desktop apps plus iOS and Android mobile apps. Product details are in the [ColaOS entry](/en/wiki/products/colaos); the official entry point is [colaos.ai](https://colaos.ai/).

Both products share an application-layer positioning: MarsWave does not build its own foundation models — ListenHub integrates third-party image and video models (the official page lists Nano Banana Pro, Seedance, Wan, MiniMax H3, and others), while ColaOS connects models to personal context and task execution.

## Discussion in the show

Because Orange is a co-host and MarsWave's founder, statements about Cola in the show are product-owner accounts or his retellings. In Weekly #001's chapter "当所有模型都差不多，用户该如何选择," Orange said [Cola had been offering free models daily since that week and argued that direct user feedback beats benchmarks](/weekly/001/transcript#quote-b6c4393c01ec9960a865). In Weekly #002, he answered that [users were less enthusiastic about new models, mainly because of pricing](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-9f76755f7c62d7ce5229) and described [agent products needing simplicity, with Cola's desktop app hard to grow while mobile spread far more easily](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-40021a3531cadcb2de65). In the health-data chapter, he told a [story of a user who reported ten years of symptoms to Cola, got a medication direction, and found it confirmed by a doctor](/weekly/002/transcript#quote-a83d7732c2f569dab6e9) — a product-owner account whose outcome is unverified. See the [episode 002 chapter](/weekly/002/transcript#chapter-13) in the Chinese transcript.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is MarsWave?

MarsWave is an AI application company whose products are the AI content-creation platform ListenHub and the desktop AI assistant ColaOS; see [marswave.ai](https://marswave.ai/).

### What products does MarsWave have?

Two main ones: [ListenHub](https://listenhub.ai/) (AI podcast, voiceover, voice cloning, and image/video generation for content creation) and [ColaOS](https://colaos.ai/) (a memory-centered AI assistant for computers and phones).

### Who founded MarsWave?

Per the show's participants and public information, co-host Orange is the founder of MarsWave, ListenHub, and ColaOS; see the [Orange profile](/en/wiki/people/orange).

### How do ListenHub and ColaOS relate?

Both are MarsWave products: ListenHub targets content creation and publishing, ColaOS targets personal assistance. ListenHub runs in the browser; ColaOS is installed on computers and phones.
