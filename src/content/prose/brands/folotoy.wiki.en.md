## FoloToy

FoloToy is a brand that develops conversational AI toys: plush companions that talk, plus the companion app and services behind them. Its [official website](https://folotoy.com/) describes the products as "a collection of AI Conversation toys" built with recent AI technology.

## Products and how they work

Users press the toy's paw to start a conversation; voice, personality, and conversation topics can be customized through the companion app. The website lists toys including Fofo, Cactus, Panda-Momo, and Teddy-Kumma, and also sells a "magic chip" that can turn an ordinary plush toy into a talking companion.

The same capability is open to developers and tinkerers: the site publishes open-source server code, documentation, and a web tool for self-hosting, along with a parent dashboard for customization and monitoring. The brand also runs an English X account (@folotoy) and a beta testing program.

The founder is [Wang Le](/en/wiki/people/wang-le). According to his PAGC 2025 speaker profile, he is FoloToy's founder and CEO, a serial entrepreneur whose team "opened a new category of conversational AI toys in China."
