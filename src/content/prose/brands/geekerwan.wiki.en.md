## Geekerwan (极客湾)

Geekerwan (极客湾, channel name 极客湾Geekerwan) is a Chinese tech review team focused on technology and digital hardware, founded by Chu Yunfei (褚云飞). The team is known for reviews and tests of digital devices, chips, and computing hardware; its YouTube channel describes itself as "a hands-on, hardcore tech channel," and the official channel is on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCeUJO1H3TEXu2syfAAPjYKQ).

## Content focus

Geekerwan is best known for performance testing of phones and computing devices, including mobile chip benchmarks and comparisons (such as its annual smartphone SoC comparisons), battery life tests, and PC and hardware DIY build videos. Current uploads live on the [official YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCeUJO1H3TEXu2syfAAPjYKQ).

## Discussion in the show

In episode 008, in the chapter "Anthropic 上市话题：资本泡沫与 AI 价值" ([the Anthropic IPO: capital bubbles and the value of AI](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-02)), while discussing memory and chip price increases, Guizang mentions ["Geekerwan does its annual smartphone chip comparison every year"](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-1c91d538f1fca3aff46b), and says that the [Snapdragon](/en/wiki/brands/qualcomm) 8 Gen 6 regressed sharply — across overall, single-core, multi-core, and floating-point results — making many new phones switch back to the previous-generation chip. These are Guizang's references to Geekerwan's content and his own views on the chip, not official conclusions by Geekerwan (Chinese transcript).
