## Alibaba Group

Alibaba is a technology group spanning e-commerce, cloud computing, and digital services. According to its [official introduction](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US/about-alibaba), the group was founded in 1999 in Hangzhou by 18 people led by Jack Ma, with the mission "to make it easy to do business anywhere," and now describes itself as a global technology company focused on "AI + Cloud and commerce."

## Business and product lines

Following the official introduction's segmentation, the group's businesses include:

- E-commerce: Taobao, Tmall, 1688, AliExpress, Lazada, Idle Fish (Xianyu), Ele.me, and other retail and instant-commerce platforms.
- Cloud and AI: [Alibaba Cloud](/en/wiki/brands/alibaba-cloud) provides cloud and compute services; the [Qwen](/en/wiki/products/qwen) model family powers the group's enterprise solutions and consumer platforms, including a Qwen-based shopping assistant inside Taobao and the Qwen App.
- Logistics and local services: Cainiao, Amap, Fliggy, and others.
- Digital media and entertainment: Youku, Quark, Damai Entertainment, and others.
- Research and technology: DAMO Academy and the T-Head chip business; enterprise collaboration is served by DingTalk.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Alibaba Group?

Alibaba is a technology group founded in 1999 in Hangzhou by 18 people led by Jack Ma. Its businesses cover e-commerce, cloud computing and AI, logistics, local services, and digital entertainment, with an official focus on "AI + Cloud and commerce."

### Where is Alibaba Group's official website?

The group website is [alibabagroup.com](https://www.alibabagroup.com/), with company information, business segments, and investor relations. Individual services live on their own sites (Taobao, Alibaba Cloud, Quark, and so on).

### How is Qwen related to Alibaba?

Qwen (Tongyi Qianwen) is Alibaba's model family; the official introduction says Qwen models power the group's enterprise solutions and consumer platforms. See the [Qwen profile](/en/wiki/products/qwen).

### How is Alibaba Cloud related to Alibaba Group?

Alibaba Cloud is the group's cloud computing business, providing cloud and compute services. See the [Alibaba Cloud profile](/en/wiki/brands/alibaba-cloud).

### What are Alibaba's main businesses?

Per the official introduction: e-commerce (Taobao, Tmall, AliExpress, Idle Fish, Ele.me), cloud computing (Alibaba Cloud), logistics (Cainiao), local services (Amap, Fliggy), digital entertainment (Youku, Quark), plus research arms such as DAMO Academy.
