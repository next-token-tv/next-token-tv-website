## Volcengine

Volcengine (火山引擎) is ByteDance's cloud services and AI platform brand, launched by ByteDance in June 2020 and operating as an independent business unit of the company since November 2021 ([Chinese Wikipedia article](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%81%AB%E5%B1%B1%E5%BC%95%E6%93%8E)). The official site positions itself as "你的 AI 云" (your AI cloud), described as covering "models, tools, and applications down to the cloud base — one-stop enterprise AI" ([official website](https://www.volcengine.com/)).

## Business and main product lines

- **Cloud infrastructure**: cloud servers, TOS object storage, cloud database MySQL, plus categories for AI compute and networking, containers and middleware, big data, video and edge, and security ([official website](https://www.volcengine.com/)).
- **Large models and agent development**: Volcano Ark (火山方舟) is the one-stop LLM platform offering the Doubao model family — ByteDance's self-developed models, originally named "Skylark" (云雀); the official site also lists AgentKit, HiAgent, and other agent development and operations tools.
- **Group services**: according to the Chinese Wikipedia article, ByteDance products including Douyin, Feishu (Lark), and Doubao serve the outside world through Volcengine.

The market-share figure on the official home page is the company's own marketing claim; time-sensitive details such as model versions and invocation prices should be read from the official pages.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Volcengine, and which company owns it?

Volcengine is ByteDance's cloud services and AI platform brand, launched in June 2020. It provides cloud infrastructure, large-model services, and agent development tools.

### Where is the Volcengine website?

The official site is [volcengine.com](https://www.volcengine.com/); the console, product documentation, and developer entry points are all reachable from it.

Volcano Ark (火山方舟) is Volcengine's one-stop LLM platform, through which the Doubao model family is offered. For the current model list and invocation details, read the official pages.
