## Vibe Friends

Vibe Friends is a community for solo AI creators, operated by Beijing Gediao Huoban Culture Technology Co., Ltd. (北京格调伙伴文化科技有限公司); its website is [vibefriends.com](https://www.vibefriends.com/). Per the site, the community helps individuals turn their unique "Vibe" into actual income with AI, connecting "super individuals" who create with AI through online content and in-person events.

## Formats

Per the official site, the community runs:

- **Vibe Thursday**: a weekly Thursday community night for sharing AI creations and learnings.
- **Vibe Friends Meetup**: monthly in-person gatherings.
- **VibeHacks**: quarterly hackathons that turn ideas into working projects.
- **999 Club**: a core community for heavy AI users.

Event details and sign-up are at [vibefriends.com](https://www.vibefriends.com/).

## Discussion in the show

In episode 008, in the chapter "AI 短剧：个人与小团队的创作机会" ([AI short dramas: creative opportunities for individuals and small teams](/weekly/008/transcript#chapter-04)), the hosts discuss opportunities for individuals and small teams making AI content. Orange notes that at hackathons in the Jiangsu–Zhejiang area, roughly six in ten participants chose to make short dramas; Yang Pan adds, ["like Yin Ming and their Vibe Friends — the hackathon they ran was about making games"](/weekly/008/transcript#quote-33296d41121c944d7e58) ([Yin Ming](/en/wiki/people/yin-ming)). This is a host's relay of the hackathon theme; themes of specific events are set by the community's own announcements (Chinese transcript).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Vibe Friends?

Vibe Friends is a community for solo AI creators, connecting individuals who create with AI through Vibe Thursday sharing nights, monthly Meetups, and quarterly VibeHacks hackathons, per [its website](https://www.vibefriends.com/).

### Who runs Vibe Friends?

The website footer names Beijing Gediao Huoban Culture Technology Co., Ltd. (北京格调伙伴文化科技有限公司) as the operating entity.

### How can I join Vibe Friends events?

Event introductions and sign-up are at [vibefriends.com](https://www.vibefriends.com/); check the site for current arrangements.
