## What Anthropic is

Anthropic describes itself as "an AI safety and research company" aiming to build reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. It is a Public Benefit Corporation founded in January 2021 by former OpenAI members, including siblings Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei, and is headquartered in San Francisco. Governance combines a board of directors with a Long-Term Benefit Trust.

## Products and developer platform

- [Claude](/en/wiki/products/claude): Anthropic's model and assistant family; the consumer entry point is [claude.ai](https://claude.ai/). The official company page lists the Mythos, Fable, Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku model families.
- [Claude Code](/en/wiki/products/claude-code): the coding agent for software engineering; the company page also lists Claude Cowork, Claude in Chrome, and Claude for Microsoft 365.
- Developer platform: integrate through the [Claude API overview](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/api/overview), with available models in the [models documentation](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/about-claude/models/overview); cloud partners include AWS, Google Cloud Vertex AI, and Microsoft Foundry.
- Official releases: Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 on September 1, 2026, and Claude Opus 5 on July 24, 2026 (see the [Anthropic Newsroom](https://www.anthropic.com/news)).

Product-level details and boundaries live in the product entries; this page covers only the company and how its product lines relate.

## Frequently asked questions

### What company is Anthropic?

Anthropic is an AI safety and research company organized as a Public Benefit Corporation, founded in 2021 by former OpenAI members and headquartered in San Francisco. The [official company page](https://www.anthropic.com/company) states its goal of building reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems.

### Where is Anthropic's official website?

The company site is [anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com/); the Claude entry point is [claude.ai](https://claude.ai/); developer documentation is at [docs.anthropic.com](https://docs.anthropic.com/).

### How do I get Anthropic's API access?

Create an account and API key on Anthropic's developer platform and integrate through the [Claude API overview](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/api/overview); model capabilities and versions are in the [models overview](https://docs.anthropic.com/en/docs/about-claude/models/overview). The API is separate from claude.ai subscriptions.

### How do Anthropic and Claude relate?

Claude is the model and product family Anthropic develops, spanning the consumer assistant, the API, and the [Claude Code](/en/wiki/products/claude-code) coding agent. Company background is on this page; product details are in the [Claude entry](/en/wiki/products/claude).

### How do Anthropic and OpenAI differ?

Both develop frontier models and offer consumer assistants and developer APIs, but their official self-descriptions differ: Anthropic emphasizes AI safety and research, while OpenAI's mission is to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity (see the [OpenAI entry](/en/wiki/brands/openai)). Practical differences should be judged from each company's products, APIs, and workflows rather than slogans.
