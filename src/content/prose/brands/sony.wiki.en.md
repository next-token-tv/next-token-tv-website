## Sony

Sony (Sony Group Corporation, ソニーグループ株式会社) is a multinational conglomerate headquartered in Minato, Tokyo. Per [Wikipedia's entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony), it was founded on May 7, 1946, in Nihonbashi, Tokyo, by Masaru Ibuka and Akio Morita under the name Tokyo Telecommunications Engineering Corporation, renamed Sony Corporation in 1958, and renamed Sony Group Corporation in 2021.

## Business structure

The Sony Group spans several fields, organized as subsidiaries and business segments:

- **Games**: PlayStation consoles and network services, covering the home-console line described in the [PlayStation 5 entry](/en/wiki/products/playstation-5).
- **Music**: recorded music and publishing under Sony Music, including Sony Music Entertainment and Sony Music Publishing.
- **Pictures**: film and television businesses (Sony Pictures).
- **Electronics**: consumer products such as cameras, televisions (BRAVIA), and audio equipment; historically known for the Trinitron color television and the Walkman portable audio player, and a major player in developing and commercializing CD, DVD, and Blu-ray.
- **Imaging & sensing / semiconductors**: components such as image sensors.

Sony completed the partial spin-off of its financial services business in October 2025; see the [completion announcement](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/IR/news/20251001_E.pdf).

Current segment structure and operating information should be read from [sony.com](https://www.sony.com/en/) and the group's IR materials.

## Frequently asked questions

### What kind of company is Sony?

Sony is a Japanese multinational conglomerate founded on May 7, 1946, by Masaru Ibuka and Akio Morita in Tokyo as Tokyo Telecommunications Engineering Corporation. It is now Sony Group Corporation, with businesses spanning games, music, film, consumer electronics, and imaging and sensing; the financial services business was partially spun off in October 2025.

### What is Sony's full company name?

The current name is Sony Group Corporation (Japanese: ソニーグループ株式会社). From 1958 to 2021 the company was named Sony Corporation.

### What are Sony's main businesses?

Games (PlayStation), music (Sony Music), pictures (Sony Pictures), consumer electronics (cameras, TVs, audio, and more), and imaging and sensing; the financial services business was partially spun off in October 2025. Official entry points are on [sony.com](https://www.sony.com/en/).

### What is Sony's relationship to PlayStation?

PlayStation is Sony's gaming business, covering home consoles, handhelds, and network services; console details are in the [PlayStation 5 entry](/en/wiki/products/playstation-5) and on [playstation.com](https://www.playstation.com/).

### Is Sony Music part of Sony?

Yes. Sony Music (Sony Music Entertainment, Sony Music Publishing, and more) is the music arm of the Sony Group; its website is [sonymusic.com](https://www.sonymusic.com/).
