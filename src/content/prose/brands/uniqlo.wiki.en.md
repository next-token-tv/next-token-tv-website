## UNIQLO

UNIQLO is a Japanese apparel brand owned by Fast Retailing. Its first store opened in June 1984 in Fukuro-machi, Naka-ku, Hiroshima, run by Fast Retailing's predecessor Ogori Shoji (renamed Fast Retailing in September 1991), with Tadashi Yanai leading the operation (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniqlo)). The brand name abbreviates "unique clothing"; a widely told story holds that during brand registration work in Hong Kong in 1988, the letter C was misread as a Q, and Yanai kept the result.

## Business and the LifeWear concept

UNIQLO operates a specialty-store-retailer-of-private-label-apparel (SPA) model, planning, producing, and selling its own clothing across categories for men, women, children, and home wear. The brand describes its design approach with the LifeWear concept: everyday clothing combining practicality, quality, and simplicity, improved continuously through customer feedback. The company overview is on the [official Fast Retailing page](https://www.fastretailing.com/employment/en/uniqlo/about.html).

## Discussion in the show

Episode 010 discusses UNIQLO in the chapter "上周买了啥：遮光贴、羽绒服与 GTA 6" ([Last week's purchases: blackout stickers, down jackets, and GTA 6](/weekly/010/transcript#chapter-28); Chinese transcript). Orange recounts that a Uniqlo padded jacket he had bought earlier "broke after just a few months of wear — the padding all migrated," which he calls terrible, and that this pushed him to stop buying that kind of product and instead buy a Montbell 1000-fill down jacket (see [the passage](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-3fa5ae31195653936550)). This is his judgment about one jacket he owned; the durability problem of a single item does not reflect on UNIQLO's whole product line. Background is in the [Montbell entry](/en/wiki/brands/montbell).

## Frequently asked questions

### What brand is UNIQLO?

A Japanese apparel brand owned by Fast Retailing, started in Hiroshima in 1984, offering everyday clothing under its LifeWear concept; see the [official Fast Retailing overview](https://www.fastretailing.com/employment/en/uniqlo/about.html).

### Where was the first UNIQLO store?

In Fukuro-machi, Naka-ku, Hiroshima, opened in June 1984 by Fast Retailing's predecessor Ogori Shoji (per English Wikipedia).

### Where does the name UNIQLO come from?

It abbreviates "unique clothing"; the story goes that a C was misread as a Q during 1988 registration work in Hong Kong and Tadashi Yanai decided to keep it (per English Wikipedia).

### What does LifeWear mean?

UNIQLO's own term for its clothing philosophy: everyday clothing emphasizing practicality, quality, and simplicity, refined through customer feedback.

### Was the show's take on Uniqlo positive?

In episode 010, Orange gives a negative account of one Uniqlo padded jacket he had bought earlier, saying it broke after a few months and led him to switch to another brand's down jacket. It is his personal experience with a single item; the episode does not evaluate UNIQLO's product line as a whole.
