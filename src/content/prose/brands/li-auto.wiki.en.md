## Li Auto

Li Auto (理想汽车) is a Chinese automaker that develops and sells smart electric vehicles for families. It was founded in 2015 by Li Xiang, is headquartered in Beijing, and manufactures in Changzhou, Jiangsu (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Auto)). Its tagline is “理想，给车和家赋予生命” ("Li, giving life to the car and the home"), per the [official website](https://www.lixiang.com/).

## Product lines

Li Auto’s first model, the Li One, was unveiled in April 2019 as a range-extended SUV; it was followed by the L-series SUVs (L9 in March 2022, L8 and L7 in September 2022); the MEGA, the company’s first battery-electric vehicle, a full-size MPV, followed in November 2023; and a battery-electric i series launched in 2025 (i8 in February 2025, i6 in September 2025). This timeline is per English Wikipedia. Models currently listed on the [official site](https://www.lixiang.com/) include the Li L7, L8, L9, and MEGA.

The company listed on Nasdaq in July 2020 (ticker LI) and later also listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (per English Wikipedia).
