## What Huawei is

Huawei is a technology company providing ICT (information and communications technology) infrastructure and smart devices, founded in 1987 in Shenzhen. Its official corporate information states that the company is 100% privately owned by employees, with approximately 213,000 employees operating in more than 170 countries and regions, serving more than 3 billion people worldwide.

## Business and main product lines

The business areas divided on the official site include: carrier networks (communications networks for telecom operators), enterprise business (networking, optical, wireless, data storage), consumer devices (smartphones, PCs, tablets, wearables, audio, routers), Huawei Cloud, digital power, and intelligent automotive solutions. "Huawei phones" in everyday consumer contexts belong to the devices business, which is only part of the company.

Official corporate information also gives the R&D scale: 2025 R&D spending reached CNY 192.3 billion, about 21.8% of annual revenue. Current products per line can be read from the [Huawei website](https://www.huawei.com/); consumer products have a separate entry point at [consumer.huawei.com](https://consumer.huawei.com/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is Huawei's official website?

The corporate website is [huawei.com](https://www.huawei.com/); consumer products are on [consumer.huawei.com](https://consumer.huawei.com/), and enterprise and cloud entry points are in the site navigation.

### What company is Huawei?

Huawei is a technology company founded in 1987 in Shenzhen, officially described as a leading global provider of ICT infrastructure and smart devices, 100% privately owned by employees (see the [official corporate information page](https://www.huawei.com/en/corporate-information)).

### Does Huawei only make phones?

No. Consumer devices are only one block in the official business divisions; the company also runs carrier networks, enterprise business, Huawei Cloud, digital power, and intelligent automotive solutions. Descriptions of each are on the [Huawei website](https://www.huawei.com/).

### What country is Huawei from?

Huawei was founded in Shenzhen, China. Its official corporate information states the business spans more than 170 countries and regions; governance and headquarters information is on the [official corporate information page](https://www.huawei.com/en/corporate-information).
