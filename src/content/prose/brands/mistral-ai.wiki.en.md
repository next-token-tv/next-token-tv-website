## What Mistral AI is

Mistral AI is a French AI company founded on 28 April 2023 in Paris by Arthur Mensch (CEO), Guillaume Lample, and Timothée Lacroix, three former Google DeepMind and Meta researchers (per Wikipedia). Its home page tagline is "Frontier AI. In your hands," and its enterprise self-description is "help organizations build tailored AI systems to solve the world's hardest problems." It is widely treated as the flagship of the European sovereign-AI route: cloud serving is hosted in the EU, and self-hosted deployment is offered.

## Work and how the products relate

Mistral AI's business spans three layers — models, products, and infrastructure:

- **Model family**: the official site lists Mistral Medium 3.5, Mistral Small 4, Mistral OCR 4, Voxtral TTS, and the safety model Shieldstral. Many models ship as open weights (early models like Mistral 7B under Apache-style licenses), while others (such as the Medium and Large lines) are proprietary — this mixed open-and-commercial route is a defining trait of the company.
- **Product layer**: Le Chat, the chat assistant launched in February 2024, was renamed Vibe at the end of May 2026 (per Wikipedia) and is now positioned as an "AI agent for long-horizon work." The product line also includes Vibe for code, Studio for building and running agents, and Forge for training custom models.
- **Infrastructure layer**: the site lists AI Cloud ("Frontier-scale infrastructure for training and inference"), and in August 2026 the company announced European infrastructure for sovereign AI.

On 8 September 2026, the [official news page](https://mistral.ai/news) announced a €3 billion raise at a post-money valuation above €21 billion, framing "sovereign, open-weight AI" as the technology frontier.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Mistral AI?

It is a French AI company founded in Paris in 2023, with a model family that mixes open-weight and proprietary releases, agent products (Vibe, Studio, Forge), and AI Cloud infrastructure; see [mistral.ai](https://mistral.ai/).

### What is the relationship between Le Chat and Vibe?

Le Chat was Mistral's chat assistant launched in February 2024. Per Wikipedia it was renamed Vibe at the end of May 2026 and is now an AI agent for long-horizon work. Current entry points are on the [official site](https://mistral.ai/).

### Are Mistral's models open source?

It is a mixed route: early models such as Mistral 7B and Mixtral released open weights under Apache-style licenses, while flagship Medium and Large models are proprietary. The license for each model is stated on its [official model pages](https://mistral.ai/).

### What country is Mistral AI from?

France, headquartered in Paris, founded by three former Google DeepMind and Meta researchers (per Wikipedia). EU-based hosting and self-hosting options are part of its sovereign-AI positioning.
