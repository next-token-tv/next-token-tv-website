## Montbell (モンベル)

Montbell (written モンベル in Japanese; the company is mont-bell Co., Ltd.) is a Japanese outdoor equipment brand founded in August 1975 by Isamu Tatsuno and headquartered in Nishi-ku, Osaka (per [Japanese Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%83%A2%E3%83%B3%E3%83%99%E3%83%AB)). The company plans, manufactures, and sells outdoor sports equipment, with product lines covering outdoor clothing, packs, tents, and sleeping bags.

## Business and products

Montbell sells gear and clothing under its own brand for mountaineering, hiking, and camping; the official site offers a product catalog, store locator, and repair services — see [montbell.com](https://www.montbell.com/jp/en). Down jackets are one of its representative clothing categories. A common measure of down quality is fill power: the volume occupied by a given weight of down, where higher numbers mean loftier down and, usually, better warmth per gram. Down jackets on the mass market commonly fall between 600 and 800 fill power; higher numbers sit in more specialized, more expensive territory.

## Discussion in the show

Episode 010 discusses Montbell in the chapter "上周买了啥：遮光贴、羽绒服与 GTA 6" ([Last week's purchases: blackout stickers, down jackets, and GTA 6](/weekly/010/transcript#chapter-28); Chinese transcript), in the recurring "last week's purchases" segment. Orange describes the Montbell 1000-fill down jacket he bought: he says it was ["very expensive — the store in China priced it absurdly, but it's more reasonable in Japan"](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-45bde7684009d758e1fd), and that outdoor bloggers who bought it generally call it good value, saying humanity "has pushed the down jacket to its absolute peak." When Xiangyang Qiaomu asks for details, Orange explains fill power: ordinary down jackets like Bosideng sit around 650, better ones like [Patagonia](/en/wiki/brands/patagonia) ("巴塔") and [Arc'teryx](/en/wiki/brands/arcteryx) around 800, and 1000 is, in his telling, ["the highest grade"](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-0ee1067cdcc1112620b2). He explains that a [Uniqlo](/en/wiki/brands/uniqlo) padded jacket he bought last year fell apart after a few months and pushed him to switch; on arrival, he describes the jacket as ["light as a phone"](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-439b95a6bbf5318e2d99), "huge and super puffy," and says it is "a bit like an iPhone, combining technology and good looks extremely well." These are Orange's judgments based on his own purchase; the comparisons with Bosideng, Patagonia, Arc'teryx, and Uniqlo are also his personal account, not statements from Montbell.
