## Unitree Robotics

Unitree Robotics (宇树科技) is a Chinese robotics company founded by Wang Xingxing and registered in Hangzhou (Binjiang District) in 2016. It develops high-performance quadruped and humanoid robots; its official development-history page calls it a world-renowned civilian robotics company and says it was the first company to start public retail of high-performance quadruped robots ([official history page](https://www.unitree.com/about)).

## Business and main product lines

Per the [official history page](https://www.unitree.com/about) and [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitree_Robotics), Unitree’s products cover:

- **Consumer quadruped robots**: Go1 (2021), Go2 (2023), and related models.
- **Industrial quadruped robots**: B1, B2 (2023), A2 (2025), and related models.
- **Humanoid robots**: H1 (2023), G1 (2024), R1 (2025), H2 (2025), plus robotic arms, motors and reducers, and motion-control algorithms.
- **Other**: the rideable robot GD01 (2026) and robotics AI models.

Model availability and specifications should be read from the [official website](https://www.unitree.com/).

In 2026, Unitree filed to list on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in March and completed its IPO in August (ticker 688836), with a large first-day share-price jump ([Wikipedia: Unitree Robotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitree_Robotics)).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Unitree Robotics?

Unitree is a Chinese robotics company founded in 2016 in Hangzhou, developing quadruped robots, humanoid robots, and core components ([official history page](https://www.unitree.com/about)).

### Who founded Unitree, and when?

Wang Xingxing founded Unitree; he built the XDog quadruped prototype during his master’s studies and registered the company in Hangzhou in 2016 ([Wikipedia: Unitree Robotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitree_Robotics)).

### Did Unitree go public?

Yes. Unitree filed to list on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in March 2026 and completed its listing in August 2026 under ticker 688836 ([Wikipedia: Unitree Robotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitree_Robotics)).

### What products does Unitree sell?

Quadruped robots in the Go (consumer) and B/A (industrial) series, humanoid robots including H1, G1, R1, and H2, plus arms, components, and robotics AI models ([official history page](https://www.unitree.com/about)).

### What is Unitree’s official website?

The official website is [unitree.com](https://www.unitree.com/), with product information and the company history.
