## What Nous Research is

Nous Research is an AI research company developing open language models and related tools. Its official home page states the mission as "advance human rights and freedoms by creating and proliferating open source language models," emphasizing support for their "unrestricted availability and use." Its applied research spans model architecture, data synthesis, fine-tuning, and reasoning, alongside infrastructure for coordinating distributed training.

## Work and main outputs

- **Hermes model family**: a family of open-weight research models; the official site lists Hermes 4 as its flagship model page. Models are distributed through Hugging Face and [GitHub](https://github.com/NousResearch).
- **Hermes Agent**: an open-source agent that runs locally or in the cloud and executes tasks through tools and skills; the entry is [Hermes Agent](/en/wiki/products/hermes-agent) and the official entry point is [hermes-agent.ai](https://hermes-agent.ai/).
- **User and developer entry points**: the [official site](https://nousresearch.com/) lists Nous Chat, Simulators, Nous Portal, and a shop; the community gathers on the official Discord.

## Frequently asked questions

### What kind of company is Nous Research?

It is a research company centered on open language models. Its official mission is to advance human rights and freedoms by creating and proliferating open models, with outputs including the Hermes model family and tools like Hermes Agent; see [nousresearch.com](https://nousresearch.com/).

### What is Nous Research's Hermes?

Hermes is its open-weight model family (flagship: Hermes 4). [Hermes Agent](/en/wiki/products/hermes-agent) is a separate open-source agent product from the same company that shares the name — the former is a family of models, the latter a tool that executes tasks.

### Where can I download Nous Research's models?

Open models are distributed via [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/NousResearch) and [GitHub](https://github.com/NousResearch); for a hosted chat entry point, see Nous Chat on the official site.
