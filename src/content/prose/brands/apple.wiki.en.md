## What Apple is

Apple is a technology company that designs personal computing devices and the software platforms they run on. The company boilerplate in official press releases says Apple "revolutionized personal technology with the introduction of the Macintosh in 1984" and today "leads the world in innovation with iPhone, iPad, Mac, AirPods, Apple Watch, and Apple Vision Pro." It was founded on April 1, 1976, by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, and is headquartered at Apple Park in Cupertino, California.

## Product lines and services

- Hardware: [iPhone](/en/wiki/products/iphone), Mac (including MacBook, [Mac mini](/en/wiki/products/mac-mini), and [Mac Studio](/en/wiki/products/mac-studio)), iPad, [Apple Watch](/en/wiki/products/apple-watch), AirPods, and [Apple Vision Pro](/en/wiki/products/apple-vision-pro).
- Software platforms: the official developer site lists iOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS, visionOS, and watchOS, plus system features such as [Siri](/en/wiki/products/siri).
- Services: the App Store, Apple Music, Apple Pay, iCloud, and Apple TV, among others.

Official September 2026 releases include the [iPhone Duo](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/) (the first foldable iPhone, announced September 9 and on sale October 23 in 70+ countries from $1,999 in the US), [Apple Watch Series 12](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/introducing-apple-watch-series-12-with-the-all-new-health-sensing-system/) (Health Sensing System; Apple says it measures heart rate every five seconds all day; in stores September 18 from $399 in the US), and the September 14 [Siri AI update](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/). Product details should be read from the official pages as they change.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is Apple's official website?

The global site is [apple.com](https://www.apple.com/), with local sites for individual countries and regions. Company information is available through the [Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/) and the About Apple links in the site footer.

### When was Apple founded, and by whom?

Apple was founded on April 1, 1976, by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne. The [Wikipedia entry on Apple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apple_Inc.) summarizes the company's history; official press releases highlight the 1984 Macintosh as the starting point of its modern identity.

### Where is Apple headquartered?

At Apple Park in Cupertino, California, United States.

### What are Apple's main products?

Hardware: iPhone, Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, AirPods, and Apple Vision Pro. Platforms: iOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS, visionOS, and watchOS. Services: the App Store, Apple Music, Apple Pay, iCloud, and Apple TV. This framing comes from the company boilerplate in the [iPhone Duo press release](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/apple-unveils-iphone-duo/).

### Where can developers find Apple's developer resources?

Start at [Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/), which hosts documentation, SDK downloads, Xcode resources, and Developer Program information for distributing apps on the App Store.
