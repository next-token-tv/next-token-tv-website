## What Garmin is

Garmin is a brand providing GPS navigation, wearables, and related technology, founded in 1989 by Gary Burrell and Min Kao — the brand name combines the two names. The official profile states that the company has shipped more than 200 million GPS devices over 30-plus years, employs more than 23,000 people, and runs a vertically integrated model covering R&D, manufacturing, sales, and after-sales service.

## Business and main product lines

The official profile summarizes its markets as automotive, aviation, marine, outdoor, and fitness: cockpit communication and navigation systems in aviation; radar, sonar, and autopilot systems in marine; navigation and infotainment including OEM work in automotive; and handheld GPS and sports watches in outdoor and fitness. On wearables, the official profile notes the first GPS sports watch, Forerunner, launched in 2003, with current lines including Forerunner, fēnix, Instinct, MARQ, Descent watches and inReach satellite communicators. Current products and models can be read from the [Garmin China website](https://www.garmin.com.cn/) and the [global website](https://www.garmin.com/).

Garmin, [Apple](/en/wiki/brands/apple) watches, and [Oura Ring](/en/wiki/products/oura-ring) share the consumer health-wearable context, but each has a different positioning; this brand page makes no buying recommendation.
