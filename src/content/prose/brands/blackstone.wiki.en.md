## Blackstone

Blackstone is an alternative investment management firm headquartered in New York. It was founded in 1985 by Stephen Schwarzman and Peter Peterson, initially as a mergers and acquisitions advisory business. The firm is listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE: BX) and manages assets across private equity, real estate, credit and insurance, infrastructure, hedge fund solutions, and growth equity.

## Business lines

Per the firm's website and its Wikipedia entry, its business falls into several lines:

- **Private equity**: buyout-focused funds; per its Wikipedia entry, Blackstone ranks among the world's largest private equity investors.
- **Real estate**: fund series investing in commercial real estate worldwide.
- **Credit and insurance**: credit investing for corporations and insurers.
- **Multi-asset investing**: infrastructure, hedge fund solutions, growth equity, and related strategies.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Blackstone?

Blackstone is an alternative investment management firm founded in New York in 1985. Its businesses include private equity, real estate, and credit and insurance; it is listed on the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE: BX), per [its website](https://www.blackstone.com/).

### Are Blackstone and BlackRock the same company?

No. Blackstone and BlackRock are separate, unrelated firms: Blackstone focuses on alternative assets such as private equity and real estate, while BlackRock is known for index funds and ETFs. The similar names are a frequent source of confusion and comparison in search queries.

The website is [blackstone.com](https://www.blackstone.com/); the firm introduction and business segments are on the [About Us page](https://www.blackstone.com/about-us/).
