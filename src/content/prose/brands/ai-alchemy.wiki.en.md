## AI Alchemy

AI炼金术 (AI Alchemy) is a Chinese-language podcast co-hosted by Xu Wenhao (徐文浩) and Ren Xin (任鑫). Its official description positions the show as a place to discuss AI and entrepreneurship: inviting founders, product people, and researchers to talk about how AI reshapes industries, how to build AI-native products from zero to one, and business opportunities in the AI era. It belongs to the same space of Chinese AI podcasts as [Next Token](/en/wiki/brands/next-token), the show behind this site.

## Cadence and listening

The [Apple Podcasts page](https://podcasts.apple.com/cn/podcast/ai%E7%82%BC%E9%87%91%E6%9C%AF/id1671502201) lists the show as publishing roughly weekly ("一周一更"), with episodes built around conversations between the two hosts and guest interviews. The listening entry points are the show's pages in podcast apps.
