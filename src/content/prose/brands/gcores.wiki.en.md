## GCORES

GCORES (机核) is a Chinese media organization and community focused on video games and related culture. It was started in May 2010 by Zhao Xia (known online as Simon) and other game players as an internet radio project, and has operated as a company since 2014 (per [Chinese Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%9C%BA%E6%A0%B8) and the [official site](https://www.gcores.com/jobs)). The site describes itself as sharing gamers' lives and discussing game-related culture since 2010.

## Business and products

- Content and community: the GADIO podcast, an article and video submission community, and the GCORES app (per the [official site](https://www.gcores.com/jobs)).
- Offline events: the "核聚变" (Genrebusters) gaming convention series, held since 2016 (per Chinese Wikipedia).
- Game creation and publishing: the BOOOM community game jam launched in 2019; GCORES Publishing, founded in 2020 to help jam participants release games commercially, published its first game on Steam under that name in July 2022 (per Chinese Wikipedia).
- Membership and publishing: the GPASS membership service introduced in 2022, plus the 核市奇谭 audiobook and print publishing brand (per Chinese Wikipedia).
- Merchandise: the apparel brand [GCORES Industries](/en/wiki/brands/gcores-industries), launched in 2017 (per Chinese Wikipedia).

## Discussion in the show

In episode 009, in the chapter "创始人、组织与消费" ([Founders, organizations and consumption](/weekly/009/transcript#chapter-05)) in the Chinese transcript, Guizang mentions that podcast host Zhongqing (重轻) interviewed "the CEO of GCORES, Simon" for his new show, and says that although Simon comes from a design background and rarely talks about technology, his study of AI is very active: starting from [OpenClaw](/en/wiki/products/openclaw), GCORES automated its product selection, ad buying, reporting and analytics workflows, and "the productivity gains a small company gets are huge" (see [the passage](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-8924ce119be20399d645)). This is the participant's retelling of a third-party podcast and his personal evaluation.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is GCORES?

GCORES (机核) is a games culture media and community founded in 2010, producing podcasts, articles and videos and running the Genrebusters convention, the BOOOM game jam and GCORES Publishing; see [gcores.com](https://www.gcores.com/).

### What is Genrebusters (核聚变)?

Genrebusters is the offline gaming convention series organized by GCORES since 2016 (per Chinese Wikipedia).

### How are GCORES Industries and GCORES related?

GCORES Industries is the apparel and merchandise brand GCORES launched in 2017; see the [GCORES Industries entry](/en/wiki/brands/gcores-industries).
