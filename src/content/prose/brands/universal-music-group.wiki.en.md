## Universal Music Group

Universal Music Group (UMG) is the world’s largest music company, working across recorded music, music publishing, merchandising, and audiovisual content in more than 60 territories, with a catalog stretching back over a century. The legal entity is Universal Music Group N.V.; corporate headquarters are in Hilversum, Netherlands, with operational headquarters in Santa Monica, California ([official Company page](https://www.universalmusic.com/company/)).

## Business and main labels

Per the [official Company page](https://www.universalmusic.com/company/):

- **Recorded music**: labels include Interscope Geffen A&M, Republic Records, Def Jam, Capitol Music Group, Island, Motown, Verve Label Group, and Virgin Music Group, plus regional labels such as Polydor, Decca, and Deutsche Grammophon.
- **Music publishing**: Universal Music Publishing Group (UMPG) administers over 5 million copyrights.
- **Merchandising**: the Bravado brand-merchandise business.

UMG listed on Euronext Amsterdam on September 21, 2021 ([Wikipedia: Universal Music Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Music_Group)).

## Relationship with AI music

On September 10, 2026, UMG and the AI audio company [ElevenLabs](/en/wiki/brands/elevenlabs) announced a multi-year strategic agreement whose first project is a licensed AI music creation platform that lets fans co-create with licensed music from participating artists; it is ElevenLabs’ first partnership with a major label ([official announcement](https://www.universalmusic.com/universal-music-group-and-elevenlabs-announce-multi-year-strategic-agreement-beginning-with-a-new-licensed-ai-music-creation-platform/)). For generative music models such as [Suno](/en/wiki/products/suno), see the product entries; licensing relationships should be read from the parties’ official announcements.
