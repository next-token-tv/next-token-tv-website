## OnePlus

OnePlus is a smartphone brand founded in December 2013 by Pete Lau and Carl Pei and headquartered in Shenzhen (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OnePlus)). Smartphones are its core product, extended by earbuds, tablets, and other consumer electronics. OnePlus belongs to the same system as [OPPO](/en/wiki/brands/oppo) and uses OPPO's manufacturing and supply chain resources.

## System and product relationships

Since July 2021, OnePlus's OxygenOS and OPPO's ColorOS have shared a common codebase: global models keep the OxygenOS branding, while OnePlus phones sold in China run ColorOS (per English Wikipedia). That is why the episode 008 discussion says a OnePlus phone "runs OPPO ColorOS" — it refers to the Chinese-market system.

The OnePlus 13T is a compact flagship phone released in China in April 2025; product information is on the [official page](https://www.oneplus.com/cn/13t).
