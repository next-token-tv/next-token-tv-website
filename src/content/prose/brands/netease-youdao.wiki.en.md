## NetEase Youdao

NetEase Youdao is [NetEase](/en/wiki/brands/netease)'s business brand focused on learning, productivity, and intelligent tools. It started as search — the Youdao search beta launched at the end of 2006, with the formal release in December 2007; the company announced its move into online education in 2014; and it listed on the New York Stock Exchange in October 2019 under the ticker DAO, becoming the first NetEase business to list independently ([Chinese Wikipedia article](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%BD%91%E6%98%93%E6%9C%89%E9%81%93), [Youdao official site](https://www.youdao.com/)).

## Business and main product lines

- **Dictionary and translation**: Youdao Dictionary and Youdao Translate are its earliest products, supporting language pairs such as Chinese–English, Chinese–French, Chinese–Korean, and Chinese–Japanese ([Youdao official site](https://www.youdao.com/)).
- **Learning and courses**: Youdao Premium Courses, China University MOOC, Youdao Lingshi, and Youdao Smart Education.
- **AI hardware and tools**: the official site lists Youdao AI hardware, along with newer AI tool products such as Youdao Lobster (LobsterAI).
- **Enterprise services**: the Youdao AI open platform (Youdao Zhiyun), human translation, Youdao Zhixuan, and Youdao Ads.

Time-sensitive details such as features and availability should be read from the official pages.

## Frequently asked questions

### What company is NetEase Youdao?

NetEase Youdao is NetEase's business brand for learning, productivity, and intelligent tools, offering Youdao Dictionary, Youdao Translate, courses, AI hardware, and enterprise services.

### What is the relationship between NetEase Youdao and NetEase?

Youdao was launched by NetEase as its subsidiary; it listed independently on the NYSE in October 2019 (ticker DAO), the first NetEase business to do so.

### Where is the NetEase Youdao website?

The official site is [youdao.com](https://www.youdao.com/); the dictionary, translation, courses, and AI hardware entry points are all reachable from it.

### Where can I download Youdao Dictionary?

The official entry points for Youdao Dictionary and Youdao Translate are on [youdao.com](https://www.youdao.com/), with web and client downloads.
