## Logitech

Logitech is a Swiss peripherals company founded in 1981 in Apples, Vaud, Switzerland, and now headquartered in Lausanne, by Pierluigi Zappacosta, Giacomo Marini, and Daniel Borel; the [official company page](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/about) lists Hanneke Faber as CEO. The company describes its products as "software-enabled hardware solutions" spanning work and play, and states that its products are available in nearly every country, with more than 3.5 million items shipped each week.

## Business and product lines

Product categories include mice, keyboards, webcams, and headsets and speakers; the lineup is split into the MX and Ergo series and a For Mac line for productivity and creative work, the Logitech G brand for gaming, and Ultimate Ears. On the software side, Logitech offers the [Logi Options+](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/software/logi-options-plus.html) app to customize supported mice, keyboards, and other devices, with downloads for Windows and macOS 14 or later; configuration software for gaming devices sits under Logitech G. Product and software entry points are on the [official site](https://www.logitech.com/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What country is Logitech from?

Logitech was founded in 1981 in Apples, Vaud, Switzerland, and later expanded to Silicon Valley; its global headquarters is now in Lausanne. See the [official company story](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/about).

### Where do I download Logitech's drivers and configuration software?

Logitech's customization software is [Logi Options+](https://www.logitech.com/en-us/software/logi-options-plus.html), with official downloads for Windows and macOS; software for gaming devices is under the Logitech G section of the site.

### What is the relationship between Logitech and Logitech G?

Logitech G is Logitech's gaming product brand, covering gaming mice, keyboards, and headsets; the main Logitech brand (MX, Ergo, and more) targets productivity and creative use.

### Do Logitech mice work with macOS?

Logitech maintains a For Mac product line, and Logi Options+ offers a macOS download; per-model compatibility is documented on the [official product pages](https://www.logitech.com/).
