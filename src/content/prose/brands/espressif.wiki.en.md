## Espressif Systems

Espressif Systems (乐鑫) is a semiconductor company headquartered in Shanghai that develops wireless communication chips, modules, and development tools for IoT and edge AI devices. Its [official overview page](https://www.espressif.com/en/company/about-espressif) describes the company, led by founder and CEO Teo Swee Ann, as pioneering "full-stack platforms powering the next generation of intelligent, energy-efficient IoT and edge AI devices." According to [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Espressif_Systems), the company was founded in 2008 and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange STAR Market on July 22, 2019.

## Business and product lines

Espressif's business spans several layers from silicon to cloud:

- Chips and modules: Wi-Fi/Bluetooth MCUs led by the [ESP32 family](/en/wiki/products/esp32) (including the ESP32-S, C, H, and P4 sub-families) and the earlier ESP8266. The official overview page reports more than 1.5 billion IoT SoCs shipped globally.
- Software and developer tools: the ESP-IDF framework, ESP-IoT-Solution, and AI toolchains such as ESP-SR, with support for third-party ecosystems like Arduino and Zephyr.
- Cloud services: device-side offerings such as ESP RainMaker and ESP Insights.

The company emphasizes open-source development; per Wikipedia, public reporting in 2022 noted its move to the RISC-V instruction set architecture.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Espressif?

Espressif Systems is a Shanghai-headquartered semiconductor company that makes wireless communication chips, modules, and development tools for IoT and edge AI. It was founded in 2008 and listed on the STAR Market in July 2019.

### Where is Espressif's official website?

The official website is [espressif.com](https://www.espressif.com/), with products, documentation (docs.espressif.com), developer community links, and purchasing channels.

### How are ESP32 and Espressif related?

ESP32 is Espressif's chip family, spanning multiple Wi-Fi/Bluetooth MCU models. See the [ESP32 profile](/en/wiki/products/esp32) and the [official product page](https://www.espressif.com/en/products/socs/esp32).

### Why does my Wi-Fi network show an "Espressif device"?

Many smart-home and small IoT devices use Espressif's ESP32 or ESP8266 chips, and network scans may display the chip vendor's name. It does not mean Espressif made the device — only that a device contains its chip.

### Is Espressif publicly listed?

Yes. Per Wikipedia, Espressif listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange STAR Market on July 22, 2019. For current stock information, check the exchange or a market data platform.
