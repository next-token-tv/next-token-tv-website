## ModRetro

ModRetro is a company developing retro gaming hardware and publishing games, founded by Palmer Luckey, the creator of Oculus VR. Luckey co-founded the ModRetro Forums community in 2009, centered on "portabilizing" old hardware, and reestablished ModRetro as a video game console company in June 2024. Its first product after the relaunch was the Chromatic handheld, which plays original Game Boy games; its second is the M64, a recreation of the Nintendo 64.

## Business and product lines

The official store lists three kinds of business:

- **Hardware**: Chromatic is a cartridge-based retro handheld, covered in the [ModRetro Chromatic entry](/en/wiki/products/modretro-chromatic); the [M64 console](https://modretro.com/products/m64) is compatible with original Nintendo 64 cartridges and controllers, and its product page describes HDMI video output at up to 4K.
- **Game publishing**: the store sells newly developed games on physical cartridges (such as Tetris, Rayman, and Earthworm Jim) plus licensed re-releases (such as the Atari '90s Rewind Collection), some as 25th-anniversary editions.
- **Accessories**: the Mod Kit, Link Cable, rechargeable power core, and more; the product page states that Chromatic "was designed with modders in mind."

Product information should be read from the current store pages at [modretro.com](https://modretro.com/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Who founded ModRetro? Does Palmer Luckey own it?

ModRetro was founded by Palmer Luckey: he started the ModRetro Forums community in 2009 and reestablished ModRetro as a console company in June 2024, as covered in [Wikipedia's Palmer Luckey entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmer_Luckey) and reflected on the [official store](https://modretro.com/).

### What products does ModRetro make?

The store sells the [Chromatic handheld](https://modretro.com/) (plays original Game Boy cartridges), the [M64 console](https://modretro.com/products/m64) (compatible with original Nintendo 64 cartridges and controllers), and a large catalog of physical cartridge games and accessories. See the [ModRetro Chromatic entry](/en/wiki/products/modretro-chromatic) for the handheld's details.

### Where is ModRetro's official website?

The official website is [modretro.com](https://modretro.com/), and its hardware, games, and accessories are sold through the store there.
