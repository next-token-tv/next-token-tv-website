## SpaceX

SpaceX (full name Space Exploration Technologies Corp.) is an American space company founded in 2002 by [Elon Musk](/en/wiki/people/elon-musk) that develops and operates rockets, spacecraft, and satellite communications networks. In 2024, the company moved its headquarters from Hawthorne, California, to Starbase, Texas (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpaceX)).

## Business and product lines

- Falcon rockets: per English Wikipedia, Falcon 1 reached orbit on its fourth launch attempt on September 28, 2008; Falcon 9 achieved the first landing recovery of a first-stage booster in December 2015; Falcon Heavy flew its inaugural mission on February 6, 2018.
- Dragon spacecraft: in May 2020, Crew Dragon carried two NASA astronauts on a crewed flight.
- Starlink: a satellite internet constellation; SpaceX launched the first large batch of 60 satellites in May 2019 and has kept building the network since.
- Starship: the next-generation launch system.
- AI business: [SpaceXSI (formerly xAI)](/en/wiki/brands/xai) developed the [Grok](/en/wiki/products/grok) model family, and SpaceX announced the acquisition of xAI on February 2, 2026 (see the [official announcement](https://x.ai/news/xai-joins-spacex)).

The company and mission overview is on the [official page](https://www.spacex.com/mission/).

## Common confusions

- SpaceX vs. its AI brand: SpaceX is the space company; [SpaceXSI (formerly xAI)](/en/wiki/brands/xai) develops the Grok product line, so the relation is company and owned brand.
- SpaceX vs. NASA: SpaceX is a commercial space company and NASA is the United States government space agency; the two cooperate under commercial contracts, for example the crewed flight of May 2020.
- Starlink vs. SpaceX: Starlink is the satellite internet business operated by SpaceX, not a separate company.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who founded SpaceX?

SpaceX was founded in 2002 by [Elon Musk](/en/wiki/people/elon-musk), who serves as its CEO and chief engineer (per English Wikipedia).

### What is Starlink?

A satellite internet network operated by SpaceX. Per English Wikipedia, SpaceX launched the first large batch of 60 satellites in May 2019 and has continued launching since.

### What is SpaceX's reusable rocket about?

It is a design in which the first-stage booster returns to land after launch and can fly again. Per English Wikipedia, SpaceX achieved the first landing recovery of a Falcon 9 first-stage booster in December 2015, and Falcon Heavy flew its inaugural mission in February 2018.

### What is the relation between its AI brand and xAI?

Its AI brand (formerly xAI) is the brand name after the acquisition of xAI. SpaceX announced the acquisition of xAI on February 2, 2026 (see the [official announcement](https://x.ai/news/xai-joins-spacex)), and Grok-related business has since operated under that brand; see the [entry for its AI brand](/en/wiki/brands/xai).

### How are SpaceX and NASA related?

NASA is the United States government space agency and SpaceX is a commercial space company; the two cooperate under commercial contracts. Per English Wikipedia, the crewed flight in May 2020, with Crew Dragon carrying two NASA astronauts, is one result of this cooperation.
