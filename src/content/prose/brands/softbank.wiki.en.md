## SoftBank Group

SoftBank Group Corp. is a multinational investment holding company headquartered in Minato, Tokyo, founded in 1981 by Masayoshi Son, focused on investment management in technology companies (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SoftBank_Group)). Son is the group's founder and largest shareholder.

## Investments and business

- Vision Fund: the large technology investment fund established in 2017; per English Wikipedia it is the world's largest technology-focused venture capital fund.
- Arm: SoftBank acquired the British chip design company Arm in 2016, and Arm remains part of the group (see the [Arm entry](/en/wiki/brands/arm)).
- Other investments: per English Wikipedia the group holds long-term stakes in companies including Alibaba, with investments spanning internet, telecommunications, artificial intelligence and other areas.

The group overview is on the [official page](https://group.softbank/en).

## Frequently asked questions

### What company is SoftBank?

### Who founded SoftBank?

The founder and largest shareholder is Masayoshi Son, who has led the group since founding it in 1981 (per English Wikipedia).

### How are SoftBank and Arm related?
