## What is Stripe

Stripe is a technology company providing online payments and financial infrastructure for businesses. It was founded by the Irish brothers Patrick Collison (CEO) and John Collison (president) — Wikipedia's infobox dates it to 2009, while the article body describes the founding in Palo Alto in 2010 — and operates dual headquarters in the San Francisco Bay Area and Dublin, Ireland, with the Dublin headquarters opened in October 2025. The official About page calls it "a technology company focused on improving the conditions for economic growth and prosperity" building "programmable financial infrastructure," used by more than 5 million businesses processing trillions of dollars a year (the official figure is roughly 1.7% of global GDP).

## Business and main products

Stripe's core is a developer-facing payments API, extended by product lines including Billing for subscriptions and invoicing (2018), the Radar risk and fraud-prevention tools (April 2018), Atlas for incorporating a US company (February 14, 2016), Issuing for creating cards (July 2018), Terminal for in-person payments (generally available in the US on June 11, 2019), Link for one-click checkout autofill (May 2021), automatic tax calculation with Tax (June 2021), and Capital, Treasury, and stablecoin accounts. Availability and current rates live on the [official pricing page](https://stripe.com/pricing) and in the [official documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/).

Stripe has leaned into the AI economy over the past two years: it completed the acquisition of the billing company Metronome on January 14, 2026, and on August 19, 2026 announced the acquisition of [OpenRouter](/en/wiki/products/openrouter), an AI gateway that routes and optimizes token usage across 400+ models from more than 80 providers. The press release quotes Patrick Collison: "Tokens are the central currency for companies building with AI," with Stripe building "the economic infrastructure for AI."

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Stripe payments?

Stripe is an online payments and financial infrastructure platform for businesses: companies integrate Stripe's APIs to accept payments, run subscription billing, and fight fraud, while consumers most often encounter Stripe at checkout. See the [official About page](https://stripe.com/about).

### How does Stripe charge?

Stripe charges per-transaction fees that vary by country, payment method, and product; current rates are on the [official pricing page](https://stripe.com/pricing), which this page does not freeze into specific numbers.

### What is the relationship between Stripe and OpenRouter?

Stripe announced its agreement to acquire OpenRouter on August 19, 2026, to help businesses route and optimize token usage across 400+ models. See the [official press release](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-agrees-to-acquire-openrouter) and the [OpenRouter entry](/en/wiki/products/openrouter).

### Is paying through Stripe safe?

Stripe provides merchants with the Radar risk and fraud-prevention tools and handles the compliance and technical implementation of payment processing; the official description of these mechanisms is in the [documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/). Risks in any individual transaction still depend on the specific merchant and context.
