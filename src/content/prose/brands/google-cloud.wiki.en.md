## Google Cloud

Google Cloud is Google's cloud computing business and service brand (Google being a subsidiary of Alphabet), offering compute, storage, databases, analytics, networking, and AI infrastructure, with Thomas Kurian as its CEO (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Cloud)). The official site is [cloud.google.com](https://cloud.google.com/).

## History and main services

- In April 2008, Google App Engine was announced in preview — Google's first cloud service; it reached general availability in November 2011.
- Services such as Cloud Storage and BigQuery (preview in 2010) and Compute Engine (preview in 2012, GA in 2013) followed, gradually forming Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
- At Google I/O in May 2021, Google announced Vertex AI, its machine learning platform and one of the platforms on Google Cloud serving model APIs such as Gemini (see the [Vertex AI entry](/en/wiki/products/vertex-ai)).
- Since at least 2022, Google has used "Google Cloud" as the successor name for "Google Cloud Platform."

Beyond infrastructure, Google Cloud also carries enterprise AI and data analytics services such as BigQuery, Cloud TPUs, and a range of machine learning APIs.

## Discussion in the show

Episode 010 discusses Google Cloud in the chapter "模型之外的门槛：Harness、产品体验与云服务接入" ([Beyond the model: harnesses, product experience, and cloud access](/weekly/010/transcript#chapter-18); Chinese transcript), where the participants bring up Google Cloud repeatedly while discussing how to reach a new model. Yang Pan says it "is still B2B — you have to go through Google Cloud" (see [the passage](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-1fcea51e0e746e7875cf)); Guizang reports that going through Google Cloud and AI Studio both "[接不上，全是 Bug](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-8248b4cc91f3f881f9af)" (would not connect, all bugs), and that he [couldn't even find the page](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-f0049c34d18e6afda57f); Xiangyang Qiaomu interjects that it is "离谱" (absurd); Orange explains that AI Studio and Vertex "are two separate things," that Vertex works better but is very B2B, and that they integrated through Vertex (see [the passage](/weekly/010/transcript#quote-cc394b8c5afb96eb44f4)). These are the participants' judgments based on their own integration experience with specific model channels at the time, not a verdict on Google Cloud's overall service quality.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Google Cloud?

Google's cloud computing business and service brand, offering compute, storage, databases, analytics, and AI infrastructure; see [cloud.google.com](https://cloud.google.com/).

### How are Google Cloud and Google Cloud Platform related?

Google Cloud Platform (GCP) is the earlier platform name; since at least 2022 Google has used "Google Cloud" for the whole business (per English Wikipedia).

### When did Google Cloud start?

Google's first cloud service, App Engine, was announced in preview in April 2008 and became generally available in November 2011; the current Google Cloud business grew out of that expansion.

### How is Vertex AI related to Google Cloud?

Vertex AI, announced in May 2021, is a machine learning platform running on Google Cloud that serves APIs for models such as Gemini; see the [Vertex AI entry](/en/wiki/products/vertex-ai) for more.

### Why did the show say Google Cloud was hard to use?

In episode 010, the participants report that a newly announced model could only be reached through Google Cloud's Vertex platform, that AI Studio would not connect and threw many errors, and that the relevant console page was hard to find. Those are their personal experiences with specific access paths at the time; for current behavior, check the [official Google Cloud documentation](https://cloud.google.com/docs).
