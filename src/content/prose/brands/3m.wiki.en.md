## 3M

3M is a diversified American manufacturing company founded on June 13, 1902 in Two Harbors, Minnesota as the Minnesota Mining and Manufacturing Company, and headquartered in Maplewood, Minnesota (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3M)). The company is known for adhesives and materials science; its product lines cover adhesives and tapes, abrasives, personal safety equipment, window films, and optical films.

## Business and representative products

- Tapes and adhesives: Scotch tape is one of the company's longest-running consumer brands.
- Office supplies: Post-it Notes were introduced under the Post-it name in 1979 and sold nationwide in the US from April 1980.
- Safety equipment: 3M developed the N95 particulate respirator, which was approved in 1972; the portfolio also includes eyewear and hearing protection.
- Films: automotive and architectural window films and optical films for displays; Scotchgard stain repellent was discovered in 1952 and sold from 1956.

The company overview is on the [official page](https://www.3m.com/3M/en_US/about-3m/).
