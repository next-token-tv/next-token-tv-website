## Xiaomi

Xiaomi Corporation is a consumer electronics and smart manufacturing company founded by Lei Jun on April 6, 2010, and listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on July 9, 2018 (1810.HK). The [official company overview](https://www.mi.com/global/about/) describes it as "a consumer-centered electronics and smart manufacturing company with smartphones, EV and smart hardware connected by an IoT platform at its core," organized around a "Human × Car × Home" smart ecosystem.

## Business and main product lines

Xiaomi's product lines cover personal devices, the home, and mobility, with in-house AI woven into an official "AI × OS × Chips" technology strategy.

- Smartphones and personal devices: phones, tablets, and wearables, which the company frames as the personal computing center.
- Operating system and chips: the cross-device [Xiaomi HyperOS](https://www.mi.com/global/about/) and the in-house XRING O1 chip announced in 2025; the chip platform is part of its stated long-term strategy.
- Smart home and IoT: TVs, wearables, routers, and home storage devices such as [Xiaomi Smart Storage](/en/wiki/products/xiaomi-smart-storage), connected through its AIoT platform.
- Electric vehicles: the SU7 electric sedan launched in 2024, followed by the SU7 Ultra and the YU7, its first electric SUV, in 2025.
- AI: the self-developed [MiMo](/en/wiki/products/mimo) foundation model family covers reasoning, coding, agentic, and multimodal tasks, with the official site at [mimo.mi.com](https://mimo.mi.com/); the voice assistant on HyperOS is Super XiaoAi (超级小爱).

Product availability, configurations, and prices change; rely on the official store and product pages for current details.

## Frequently asked questions

### What country is Xiaomi from, and is it publicly listed?

Xiaomi is a Chinese company founded by Lei Jun in 2010. It went public on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on July 9, 2018, under ticker 1810.HK, per the [official company overview](https://www.mi.com/global/about/).

### What EVs does Xiaomi make?

The official overview lists the SU7 electric sedan launched in 2024, the SU7 Ultra, and the YU7, Xiaomi's first electric SUV, launched in 2025. See the [company overview](https://www.mi.com/global/about/) for current models.

### What is the Xiaomi NAS?

"Xiaomi NAS" usually refers to [Xiaomi Smart Storage](/en/wiki/products/xiaomi-smart-storage), a home device for centralized photo, video, and file storage, backups, family sharing, and remote access. The official listing is on the [Xiaomi store](https://www.mi.com/shop/buy/detail?product_id=21425).

### What are Xiaomi's AI model and voice assistant?

Xiaomi's self-developed foundation model family is [MiMo](/en/wiki/products/mimo), with the official site at [mimo.mi.com](https://mimo.mi.com/); the voice assistant on HyperOS is Super XiaoAi. This answer is an editorial addition based on official pages, not a search-demand term.
