## Plantower

Plantower (Nanchang Plantower Technology Co., Ltd.) is a Chinese company that develops, manufactures, and sells air-quality sensors. Its website states that the company was founded in Beijing in 2014 and moved to Nanchang, Jiangxi, in 2018, and that its Plantower-series sensors are used in air-quality monitoring, environmental monitoring, automotive electronics, and smart-home applications.

## Product lines

The product section of the official website lists:

- **Laser particle sensors**: the PMS series, with model numbers including PMS1003, PMS3003, PMS5003, PMS6003, PMS7003, PMS9003M, and PMSA003. The PMS series are low-cost laser PM2.5/PM10 sensor modules; the [air-quality sensor research page of the World Air Quality Index project](https://aqicn.org/sensor/) lists them among the representative products in this class.
- **Other air sensors**: infrared dust sensors, formaldehyde sensors, carbon-dioxide sensors (DS-CO2-20, DS-CO2-30), multi-in-one gas sensor modules, and automotive sensors.

Sensor model numbers, parameters, and selection guides should be read from [plantower.com](https://www.plantower.com/). In the hardware-tinkering community, PMS-series sensors are often paired with microcontrollers such as [ESP32](/en/wiki/products/esp32) in DIY air-quality monitors.

### What is the Plantower PMS5003?

The PMS5003 is Plantower's laser particle-sensor module for measuring PM2.5/PM10. The [World Air Quality Index project's sensor research page](https://aqicn.org/sensor/) lists it, alongside the PMS3003 and PMS7003, among laser-based PM2.5/PM10 sensors; detailed specifications live on [the official site](https://www.plantower.com/) and the corresponding datasheets.
