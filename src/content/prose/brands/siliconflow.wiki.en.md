## What is SiliconFlow

SiliconFlow (Chinese name 硅基流动) is a Chinese company providing generative-AI computing infrastructure and model services. It was founded in August 2023 according to the milestone timeline on its [official site](https://siliconflow.cn/about); its operating entity is Beijing SiliconFlow Technology Co., Ltd., based in Haidian, Beijing. The Chinese site describes the company as an "independent token supply platform" with the mission of "accelerating the era of AGI for everyone"; the international site at [siliconflow.com](https://siliconflow.com/) states the goal of becoming one of the world's most influential providers of AI infrastructure.

## Business and main services

Per the official sites, SiliconFlow's business includes:

- Model-as-a-service APIs: one-stop model APIs across language, speech, image, and video, billed by usage. The platform aggregates models from multiple providers; the current model list lives in the [official documentation](https://docs.siliconflow.cn/) and the models page.
- Inference acceleration: a self-developed full-stack inference engine that the official site says substantially cuts latency and raises throughput, with cross-chip deployment including domestic GPUs.
- Reserved instances and private deployment for enterprises, plus AI compute operation services.
- Open-source inference tooling such as OneDiff for diffusion models and BizyAir (see [siliconflow.com/about](https://siliconflow.com/about)).
