## CXMT (ChangXin Memory Technologies)

CXMT (ChangXin Memory Technologies, 长鑫存储) is a Chinese memory-chip company that designs, develops, produces, and sells DRAM. It was founded in 2016 and is headquartered in Hefei, Anhui. The entity named on its official pages is 长鑫科技集团股份有限公司 (CXMT Technology Group Co., Ltd.), with the securities code 688825 (see the [official website](https://www.cxmt.com/)).

## Product lines

Per the official product pages, CXMT’s DRAM products include:

- LPDDR6: a new generation of low-power memory for mobile terminals.
- DDR5 and memory modules: for PCs and servers.
- LPDDR5/5X: focused on speed and packaging innovation.
- LPDDR4X: focused on low power.

Application areas listed on the site cover mobile terminals, PCs, servers, virtual reality, and IoT.
