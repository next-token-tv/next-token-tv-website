## Seamate (出海同学会)

Seamate (出海同学会, roughly "Going-Global Study Group") is an industry community initiated by Grand View Capital (大观资本) for Chinese companies expanding overseas. The website frames its mission as "let Seamate help Seamate" — connecting founders of global-bound companies, heads of internationalization at large companies, specialized funds, and industry researchers through closed-door discussion and deep networking.

## Format and content

Per the site's own description, Seamate has held more than 100 deep exchange sessions since 2018, with over 2,000 people contributing insights. Formats include offline events (in China and abroad), online closed-door sessions, and livestreams. After each discussion the community publishes writeup articles and maintains a Feishu-hosted knowledge base of accumulated content and globalization resources. Current events and writeups live at [seamate.cn](https://seamate.cn/).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Seamate (出海同学会)?

Seamate is an industry community for companies going global, initiated by Grand View Capital; it organizes closed-door discussions, deep networking, and online sessions for global-bound founders and practitioners, per [its website](https://seamate.cn/).

### Who runs Seamate?

The site states that the community was initiated by Grand View Capital (大观资本).

### How can I follow or join Seamate events?

Event introductions, past writeups, and the knowledge base are available at [seamate.cn](https://seamate.cn/); for how to take part, read the current instructions on the site.
