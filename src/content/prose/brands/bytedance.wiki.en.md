## ByteDance

ByteDance is a global technology company founded in 2012 by a team led by Zhang Yiming and Liang Rubo, with the mission "Inspire Creativity, Enrich Life," according to its [official introduction](https://www.bytedance.com/en/). Starting from content platforms, its product lines now span feeds, short video, enterprise collaboration, AI assistants, and cloud services.

## Business and product lines

The official introduction lists these main products:

- Content platforms: Toutiao (launched August 2012), Douyin (launched September 2016 in China), and TikTok (launched internationally in 2017 and merged with Musical.ly in 2018).
- Enterprise collaboration: [Feishu](/en/wiki/products/feishu) (known internationally as Lark).
- Creative tools: [Jianying](/en/wiki/products/jianying) (CapCut internationally).
- AI assistants and models: [Doubao](/en/wiki/products/doubao) (doubao.com) and other AI applications.
- Cloud and enterprise services: [Volcano Engine](/en/wiki/brands/volcengine) (volcengine.com).

The company site reports more than 150,000 employees across nearly 120 cities worldwide.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is ByteDance?

ByteDance is a global technology company founded in 2012 by Zhang Yiming, Liang Rubo, and their team. Its products include Toutiao, Douyin, TikTok, Feishu (Lark), Jianying (CapCut), and Doubao.

### Where is ByteDance's official website?

The official website is [bytedance.com](https://www.bytedance.com/), with company information, product listings, and news. Product-level questions belong on each product's own site (e.g., doubao.com, feishu.cn).

### Is Doubao a ByteDance product?

Yes. Doubao is ByteDance's AI assistant, with the official entry at [doubao.com](https://www.doubao.com/). See the [Doubao profile](/en/wiki/products/doubao).

### How are Feishu and Jianying related to ByteDance?

Feishu (Lark internationally) and Jianying (CapCut internationally) are both ByteDance products, for enterprise collaboration and video creation respectively. See the [Feishu](/en/wiki/products/feishu) and [Jianying](/en/wiki/products/jianying) profiles.

### What is the relationship between TikTok and Douyin?

Per the official introduction, Douyin launched in China in September 2016, TikTok launched internationally in 2017, and TikTok merged with the acquired Musical.ly in 2018. Both belong to ByteDance.
