## Ricoh

Ricoh Company, Ltd. (株式会社リコー) is a Japanese imaging and electronics company headquartered in Tokyo. It started on February 6, 1936, as Riken Kankoshi (Riken Sensitized Paper) and grew from sensitized paper and office equipment into a corporate group spanning office and imaging businesses.

## Business and product lines

The official site organizes the business into Workplace Services, Office Printing, Commercial Printing, Industrial Printing, Industrial Products, and Solutions for Society, with technology covering printing, optics and image processing, and software — corresponding to copiers, multifunction printers, and production printing equipment. On the imaging side, Ricoh owns two camera brands, Ricoh and Pentax: on October 1, 2011, Ricoh acquired the Pentax imaging-systems business (now Ricoh Imaging), whose GR series compact cameras are well regarded among street photographers. Details are on the [official company page](https://www.ricoh.com/about).

## Frequently asked questions

### What country is Ricoh from?

Ricoh is a Japanese company founded on February 6, 1936, and headquartered in Tokyo.

### What does Ricoh do?

Ricoh's business spans office equipment and imaging: office printing (copiers and multifunction printers), commercial and industrial printing, workplace services, and cameras under the Ricoh and Pentax brands.

### Who makes Ricoh GR cameras?

The GR series of compact cameras belongs to Ricoh: after acquiring the Pentax imaging business in October 2011, Ricoh formed Ricoh Imaging, which handles the GR line.

### Where is Ricoh's official website?

The global site is [ricoh.com](https://www.ricoh.com/), with company information under [About Ricoh](https://www.ricoh.com/about).
