## HeyGen

HeyGen is a generative AI video company founded in 2020 by Joshua Xu and Wayne Liang, headquartered in Los Angeles, California (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HeyGen)). The company was originally named Surreal, later rebranded to Movio and then to HeyGen.

## Business and products

- AI avatar videos: users submit photos and videos to create photorealistic avatars that can recite scripts in multiple languages; the platform also offers a library of pre-made avatars and voices (per [English Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HeyGen)).
- Video translation: converting existing videos into other languages with lip sync.
- [HyperFrames](/en/wiki/products/hyperframes): HeyGen's open-source video rendering framework, described in its repository as "Write HTML. Render video. Built for agents." The GitHub repository was made public in March 2026 (see [heygen-com/hyperframes](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes)); it is designed so that AI agents can generate and render videos from HTML.

The company overview is on the [official page](https://www.heygen.com/about).

## Discussion in the show

Episode 009 discusses HeyGen and HyperFrames in the chapter "Harness 与插件生态" ([Harness and the plugin ecosystem](/weekly/009/transcript#chapter-01)) in the Chinese transcript. Xiangyang Qiaomu notes that HyperFrames bundles components from Remotion and other underlying projects into a framework packaged by HeyGen, and that within "less than half a year" it became the consensus first choice for rendering videos from HTML (see [the passage](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-d54f0ea51c642151a619)). Guizang mentions that when using [Claude](/en/wiki/products/claude) to make videos, Claude calls the built-in HyperFrames first (see [the passage](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-6f9b68be1b07750b38c0)), and says HyperFrames existed long before HeyGen put up its own website "last month," describing the approach as "growing toward agents" (see [the passage](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-10d0c8874ddbf500641c)). Xiangyang Qiaomu also guesses that HyperFrames itself is not built to make money, since HeyGen's main business is the revenue driver (see [the passage](/weekly/009/transcript#quote-3c0a9fc400ab28aae69e)). These are the participants' observations and judgments at the time, not official statements from HeyGen.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is HeyGen?

A generative AI video company founded in 2020, focused on avatar videos with scripted speech and multilingual video translation; it also open-sources the agent-oriented video rendering framework HyperFrames. See the [official company page](https://www.heygen.com/about).

### How are HyperFrames and HeyGen related?

HyperFrames is HeyGen's open-source video rendering framework; the repository lives under the heygen-com GitHub organization with the description "Write HTML. Render video. Built for agents." and was made public in March 2026. See the [HyperFrames entry](/en/wiki/products/hyperframes).

### Is HeyGen free to use?

HeyGen offers free sign-up for trying the product; continued use works on subscription plans, with current pricing on the [official pricing page](https://www.heygen.com/pricing).
