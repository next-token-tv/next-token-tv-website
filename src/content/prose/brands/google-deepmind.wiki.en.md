## What is Google DeepMind

Google DeepMind is Google's artificial intelligence research organization, formed by combining two labs: DeepMind, founded in 2010, and Google Brain, which started at Google X in 2011. The official About page says it "brings together two of the world's leading AI labs" into one team led by co-founder and CEO Demis Hassabis, with the mission to "build AI responsibly to benefit humanity." Its relationship to the parent company is straightforward: Google DeepMind is the frontier AI research arm inside [Google](/en/wiki/brands/google).

## History and main achievements

- The DeepMind line: it pioneered deep reinforcement learning from 2010, with the DQN program learning 49 Atari games from raw pixels; in 2015 AlphaGo became the first computer program to defeat a Go world champion, followed by AlphaZero, MuZero, and AlphaStar; WaveNet text-to-speech and AlphaFold (2020, protein structure prediction) also came from this line.
- The Google Brain line: started at Google X in 2011, created the TensorFlow and JAX tools, and published the Transformer architecture in 2017 — per the official page, it "underpins almost all large language models"; other outputs include BERT, LaMDA, and PaLM.
- Models and systems after the merger: the [Gemini](/en/wiki/products/gemini) model family, Gemma open models, Veo video generation, Lyria music, Imagen images, the Genie 3 world model, Gemini Robotics, and science systems including AlphaFold, WeatherNext, and AlphaEvolve.

Current model versions and entry points live on the [Google DeepMind site](https://deepmind.google/) and in the [Gemini API documentation](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs).

## Frequently asked questions

### What kind of organization is DeepMind?

DeepMind now means Google DeepMind, Google's AI research organization developing models such as Gemini and scientific systems such as AlphaFold; it is not an independent company. See the [official About page](https://deepmind.google/about/).

### What is the relationship between DeepMind and Google?

DeepMind (founded 2010) and Google Brain (started at Google X in 2011) merged into Google DeepMind, led by Demis Hassabis within Google. See the [official About page](https://deepmind.google/about/).

### What is the relationship between DeepMind and Gemini?

Gemini is the model family developed by Google DeepMind; developers access it through the Gemini API. See the [Gemini entry](/en/wiki/products/gemini) and the [Gemini API documentation](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs).

### What are DeepMind's most notable achievements?

AlphaGo (the first program to beat a Go world champion, in 2015), AlphaFold (protein structure prediction, 2020), the Transformer architecture (2017, from Google Brain), and model lines such as Gemini, Gemma, and Veo. See the [official About page](https://deepmind.google/about/).

### Who is DeepMind's CEO?

Demis Hassabis, co-founder of DeepMind and CEO of the merged Google DeepMind. See the [official About page](https://deepmind.google/about/).
